## Recap

The session resumed mid-combat, exactly where Session 25 broke off: the party scattered at the winery's edge under a needle blight ambush, already two rounds deep.

**The retreat inside.** Dip pushed through the doors into the winery and hollered back for Odibli to follow. Luci, cut off from the others, burned a focus point on Step of the Wind to disengage and dash, hurled a bullseye lantern through a window to break it, then vaulted through, landed on barrels stacked on a cart, and rejoined Dip deeper in the building. Iggy, cornered by roughly nine needle blights, disengaged and ran — and mid-flight caught a glimpse of something swooping down out of the air to snatch two of the blights, one of which died. Kellan framed it so Iggy convinced himself his eyes were playing tricks. Odibli turned and threw a firebolt at the pursuing blights, rolled a natural 20, and incinerated one for 16 damage, then slammed the door behind him.

An NPC accompanying the party was clawed by a needle blight during the retreat (??) — the transcript renders the name three different ways across two minutes ("Adrian", "Elvir", "Trevor"), so who this was is unresolved.

**The fermentation room.** The party barred the door with barrels and took in the chamber: the rich smell of fermenting wine, a two-story space dominated by four enormous casks eight feet wide and twelve feet tall, a central wooden staircase climbing to a ten-foot balcony with four windows set at balcony level, old empty barrels branded *Wizard of Wines* stacked underneath, and — along the thick rafters overhead — scores of ravens, quietly gathered, watching with great interest.

**The druid.** A creak drew their eyes to a wild-looking hunched figure on the balcony above the westernmost cask, pouring a flask of thick syrup into it: a woman in a gown of animal skins and a goat-horned headdress, hair long and unkempt. A tiny creature made of twigs skittered from under the stairs and vanished beneath the easternmost cask.

Dip called up to her — who was she, what was she doing, what did she know about the blights outside. She was unbothered: the blights "won't come inside… they are only to keep the ground safe, they are security, if you will," and asked what brought travellers all this way. The party said they had been sent by the White Wizard (??) — likely a mishearing of Wizard of Wines or a reference to Davian Martikov. Dip pressed on the flask: what was that liquid you were pouring into the wine? She answered only that they would find out — "works like a charm every time."

**The fight.** The druid cast Entangle across a twenty-foot square, catching Dip and Odibli while Iggy and Luci broke free immediately, then spoke a word in Druidic. Twig blights — a different creature than the needle blights outside — poured out of the cask nearest the party, and initiative was rolled.

The fight ran long and attritional, because the blights kept replacing themselves: as fast as one died, another crawled out from under the barrel to take its place. Iggy opened with Bardic Inspiration to Dip and a Faerie Fire over the cluster, lighting up part of the group for advantage. Odibli, restrained by vines around his legs but with hands free, cast Chromatic Orb for 13 fire damage and leapt it to a second target for 16, killing two at once. Luci activated an aura of radiant energy that burned everything within ten feet each turn — including allies, at half to Iggy — then later ran up a wall, cleared the blights' reach, and landed on top of a cask to fight from above, throwing a radiant strike down at the druid.

Overhead, the ravens kept intervening. Swarms dropped from the rafters repeatedly through the fight to tear twig blights apart, unprompted and on the party's side.

The druid fought with Shillelagh, multi-attacking Dip for 10 bludgeoning, and later dropped a Thunderwave on Dip and Luci — both made their Constitution saves, taking half and staying put. Odibli, cornered and down to around 10 HP, dropped an Acid Splash into the middle of the cluster that caught Dip in the blast for 4 and killed three blights.

Dip broke the Entangle with a Strength save, raged, closed on the druid, and killed her with a single greataxe turn: 25 damage stacked from Divine Fury and Iggy's inspiration die, described as hacking her apart limb by limb "like cutting down a tree" and finishing by cleaving her in half.

The twig blights kept coming after she fell. Iggy asked to read the situation and perceived that they were not in fact infinite — the herd had been thinned enough that what stood in front of them were likely the last. The party ground the remainder down, with the ravens still helping, until Kellan called it: "congratulations, you finally ran out of little blights."

**The aftermath.** Searching the druid's body, they found the animal-skin gown, the horned headdress, and a necklace of human teeth, which Dip took. They recovered the flask she had been emptying into the vats. Dip made an Arcana check on it; Kellan's read was that it is a magic potion, and floated that it might turn the drinker into a frog (??) — unclear whether that was a genuine result or a joke, since the party then established the wine is poisoned.

Odibli cast detect magic (?) over the room on a 27 Arcana and found magic hanging in the air: the blights are not natural and "don't exist of their own accord" — something is controlling them. He could not locate the source, but the flavour was druidic, implying at least one more druid, powerful enough to animate and command all of them, still unaccounted for.

Luci bottled a portion of the tainted wine — about a quarter cask — for later inspection. Odibli tasted the wine directly and was poisoned, which confirmed what the flask had done. The party floated finding a priest to cleanse it and save the townsfolk, and called the night there.
