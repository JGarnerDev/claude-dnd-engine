## Recap

### Scene 1 — The Yard (0:07–0:54)

- Minutes after the raid, Halsby stepped out of hiding, kicked a dead raider, and told the party he'd found the bandits a little after they did.
- Luci vibe-checked him with a pointed question about how deep he'd gone into shadow (Insight ~21 (?)). Halsby answered honestly: *"The fox sent me. Not everything in the shadows is darkness of the soul — sometimes it's just the absence of light."* The DM ruled he wasn't deceiving at all.
- Iggy offered a handshake. Halsby took it loosely, then remembered it was a custom and committed to it harder as it went on.
- Halsby told them the fox had spoken to him in the forest and sent him here. Odibli explained that they'd been ripped from another reality and fell from what looked like the stars. He also said he felt drawn in one direction, northwest (?). **The citadel behind that pull is player-side only; no character knows it** (see Open questions).
- Halsby to Odibli, with the faintest smile: *"You've come a long way, young one."*
- Luci freed Sable, with Dip's help. Dip had come down from his rage, but the fire and the assaulted people stirred his old trauma.
- Sable's first move was to put out the fire, one small pail at a time.
- **The fire:**
  - Luci's psychokinetic storm (Wisdom) fanned it up the right-hand wall instead of helping.
  - Odibli and Halsby both had the same idea and cast Prestidigitation. They tried to find a "synergy" and failed the check, but the rubble they shifted still smothered about two-thirds of the fire.
  - Dip brought the last pail and finished it.
- Luci read Sable (Insight 14): confident, capable, sincere, hard-working, with a very old hurt muted by time.
  - Luci then took Sable by the shoulder and announced to Odibli that she was like no one in Barovia: "untouched by the darkness", the first person touched by light since they'd arrived.
  - Sable leaned back and side-eyed Odibli: *"What's Barovia?"*
  - She said she's a tiefling, three or four generations removed. She's seen Aasimar before, just none as "extreme" as Luci. She finds the party exotic, not hostile.
- Sable invited them to dinner, in what was left of the building.
- **Loot:**
  - About **150 gp** off the bodies.
  - Mundane, beaten-up arms and armor.
  - A leather-corded necklace on every raider, with a crude metal-wrapped crystal. Luci gave Halsby guidance and he rolled Arcana 19: they let people communicate across distance without speaking.
  - Halsby ripped them all off, handed one to Luci, kept one, and dumped the rest on the table: *"Grab one."*
  - DM named them **earshot crystals**: touch to speak telepathically, past any language and carrying intent, to one holder you can see or to all holders within 300 ft. All identical.
- Odibli refused to go near the dead mage. He has an unexplained in-character hesitation around other magic users.
  - Halsby, smiling faintly, searched the mage himself (Perception, low). He found its arcane focus, a twig-and-twine human figure ("Blair Witch"). Nothing special.

### Scene 2 — What the Raid Left (0:54–1:43)

- The party moved inside to the only undamaged room, in a half-built, half-destroyed would-be tavern full of crates.
  - It had been busy for maybe a week (??), see Open questions.
  - Sable heated chicken soup and handed out hardtack.
- Townsfolk arrived by torchlight:
  - **Mara** (sheriff's star; older and soldierly, "a veteran")
  - **Dunny**, a hurt deputy (heard as "Dunny Marsh")
  - **Silas** (heard as "Crow" / "Crumb" / "Crub"; canon Silas Crubb), portly, finer clothes, organizing people
- **Mara's account:**
  - The raid wasn't unique. Coordinated groups hit every street at once, part of a much larger raider force.
  - The last raid was about three months ago, and the raiders are getting more organized; "they've got plans for us".
  - They were originally camped across the river, about a day out, and are now split up.
  - She saw a great ray of light from the sky an hour or two earlier and asked if that was them. They said it could have been.
  - She thanked them for saving Sable. Sable is newer to town (??), and with fighting on every street, no one could reach her.
- Silas introduced himself as acting mayor and market holder. He runs the general-goods shop and is organizing the town's construction until there's a town hall.
  - Luci asked him who Strahd von Zarovich is. He'd never heard of him. Luci jumped and shook her fists: *"It is the best news I've heard all year that you don't know this man."* Dunny laughed.
- **Healing:** Luci and Iggy healed Dunny, with Iggy's Lay on Hands and Luci's Aasimar healing hands. Green glows; his cracked bones knit and he stood again. Townsfolk murmured at the door: magic is rare here.
- **The copper:**
  - Silas kicked the ruined **brewing copper**, one sheet hammered round by hand. Maybe three smiths on this side of the river could do the work, and it can't be patched. The bandits didn't steal it, they just broke it.
  - Then to Sable: *"They broke your copper. It's not going to affect the money that you owe me."*
  - Iggy offered to heat the metal. Silas said no: you need a specialist, and "certainly not from that piece of slag".
- **The debt (??):**
  - Sable arrived about a week ago to open the first tavern in Ashford, and borrowed about **1,000 gp** from Silas to set up.
  - She still owes **about 650 gp**. Her stolen first stock was meant to pay that off and carry the bar through its first month.
  - Opening was planned for next week. Without the stock she can't pay, and without paying she likely loses her permit to operate.
- **The 150 gp:** the table decided that **Dip** pocketed it, and that Dip, being suspicious of everyone, won't donate it. This was decided in Ben's absence; Ben can overturn it.
- **The smiths:** asked for names, Silas gave "George, Ringo and Zach". After the break, the players added that it's really **George Ringo**, a half-troll Liverpudlian, and **Zach** (Zacharius?), an Aasimar. That makes it two smiths, not three (??).
- **Over soup, town talk** (part fact, part fable):
  - **Old Tobin** the ferryman lost his only boat, burned early in the raid. He knows everyone who comes through.
    - He described **Wick**: an elf, one of a couple of raider commanders, with a mark on his hand like a brand but neater, "like someone did it with paperwork". He was taking careful note of the houses, as if looking for something.
  - Someone said **Harrow Bane**, and the room went quiet, almost superstitious. Someone gave old **Agnes** a look to stop her carrying it further.
  - Mara: this was the third attack of the season. The raiders call themselves independent and don't report to anyone, but their boots are all made by the same cobbler, so she suspects one organizing force provisions them.
  - **Jory**, the 14-year-old stable boy: *"The woman on the cart seat, bow across her knees, cool as you like, took the southeast track, and the ruts are deep. She's not hauling nothing."*

### Scene 3 — The Mistake (1:43–2:26)

- Sable relit the hearth.
  - Odibli kept side-eyeing it. He's afraid of fireplaces in this new realm, and more afraid of who might hear him through one.
  - The hearth burns without anyone feeding it: the logs are spent to ash, and the fire is healthier than it should be.
- Odibli rolled Arcana 23 on the hearth.
  - It is magical, but that isn't all of it: it's **linked to another entity that is not currently present**. Its aura is tied to the area within about 100–200 ft.
  - It fits Odibli's own practice of speaking to other realms through fireplaces, but it doesn't behave like Barovia's. The link here is much stronger, "like fiber".
  - Miguel will send the DM notes on why Odibli suspects it.
- Sable poured **The Mistake** without asking: *"That's the Mistake. It's the one people come back for."*
  - Deep red, a little cloudy, with a pink head that foams and fades.
  - Sour cherry at first, sharp, then a round honeyed middle. It finishes sweet and colder than the mug.
- **Sable's offer:**
  - Nella, one of the raider commanders, took the stock to a nearby camp.
  - If the party gets it back, Sable can pay Silas and put the Golden Lioness on its feet.
  - In return: a room each upstairs, free meals, and a stay of a month or two.
  - Then a stake: **10% of earnings** (rising over time), with no hard requirement to stay. They can even name the new sections of the building ("We shall call it Enron").
  - She needs protection, and they need a home: "partial owners, I guess".
- **Odibli's financial review:**
  - He told Sable she's a bad business owner selling dreams, and that the party should want a majority stake.
  - Sable nearly chugged her drink: *"I just got my thing stolen from me and you're telling me I have bad financial planning?"*
  - Asked if he was the group's financial planner, he offered his hand: "Warren O'Ghibli Buffett". She stared at the hand: *"No. I'm the shitty financial planner."*
  - She rolled her eyes so hard they suspected newt anatomy.
- **The mayor pitch:**
  - Odibli pitched Silas on rebundling the town's debt (Charisma 19). Silas half-followed it and is open to financial advice "later".
  - Halsby then pitched him an investment: 250 gp buy-in, "10, 20, 30x" returns, for **shade on command**. In effect, awnings, sold as a movable roof for bridges and patios. Odibli played hype man.
  - Silas thought he was being sold a boat, decided they were "alien enough not to be applicable to this town's finances", and left after his second drink. He committed to nothing.
- **The party accepted Sable's job** (?), at least to get the stock back. Odibli: "I'll do it for the adventure."
  - Luci proposed guerrilla tactics: ambush the raiders when they leave camp rather than assault it.
  - Halsby (?) worked out that the long-haired archer on the cart is Nella.
- **The song:**
  - The hearthlight through the red Mistake was gorgeous to Luci, and she demanded a celebration.
  - Iggy played his viola, slow and bittersweet at first, then a sing-along. Luci danced: juggling glassware before the fire for kaleidoscope light, backflips across the tables, spinning townsfolk into pirouettes, including old Agnes, who drank more than expected and did a little fists-up old-lady dance.
  - Then Iggy's tune wandered somewhere foreign. **The hearth went completely still for about ten seconds**: no flicker, a constant, inorganic light. Iggy came out of a trance and found the hook again, the fire eased back, and Sable glanced once at the hearth, then went back to pouring.
  - **Iggy reached level 5.**
- The DM will plug in a Dip moment at this point later, as a flashback.
- **Sergei and Bosco:**
  - Odibli heard a familiar bark and rushed out to find a muddy Sergei, with Bosco riding him "holding on for dear life".
  - Speak with Animals, Sergei: *"So late. I came to the forest. Raccoon. Raccoon Bosco. Nearby. We sniff. We look. We hear clashing of metal. We come shortly after. Once safe. Now here."*
  - Odibli gave treats. Both animals rolled 18–19 and joined the dance, wolfing down a shared bowl of soup and spinning on their hind legs. Sable has a soft spot for pets and let them sleep by the fire.
- Mara and most townsfolk were leaving as play stopped.
