# Silas's Road Sketch — the party's first map

The first map the party gets of Aethryn is not a world map. It is a merchant's sketch of the roads
around one ford-town, drawn by a man who thinks in days and prices. Session 29 established it as
the **base layer** of a handout that grows over time: Silas draws it, Sable adds the road she came
in on, and later Elowen, Halsby, Mara and Richmond each add their own layer.

- **Kind:** overview map, built in Inkarnate (see `maps/CLAUDE.md` → *Two kinds of map*). No grid
  and no tokens.
- **Spoiler check:** safe for the table. Everything on the player version is something Silas or
  Sable would say out loud. DM-only facts are listed separately at the bottom and stay off the
  map.
- **Scope:** `maps/locations/silas-region-scope.png` (local render, gitignored) shows how much ground the map covers. Build frame B, Ashford to the bay, as one Inkarnate map, and export frame A from it as Silas's sheet.
- **Status:** draft. Anything tagged *(proposed)* is new and is not canon until Jeff confirms it.

## How it reaches the table

- **Silas's layer, Session 30, Scene 4 (`S2_HORSES`).** He hands it over with the horses, folded
  into a saddlebag: *"So my horses come home by a road I know. Ruts go southeast, so you'll want
  the bottom half."*
- **Sable's layer: the first time someone asks where she's from.** That is likely Scene 3 or after
  the ride home, never forced. She takes the map, draws one line east, and hands it back. She
  answers the question asked and not the one behind it, so she gives no reason for leaving.
- **Each layer is a separate Inkarnate export** (or a separate Roll20 handout image), so a new
  layer can be revealed without redrawing the earlier ones.

## Layer 1 — Silas's sketch

He draws in days, not miles, and marks prices and debts where a mapmaker would put landmarks.
Labels are in his hand: plain, a little smug.

### Must include

- **Ashford** in the middle of the sheet, small, with the creek through it and **the ford**
  marked on the south side. Label: *"Ashford. Mine, sort of."*
- **The creek**, unnamed in canon, coming down from the northeast through Ashford to the
  Moorthistle. Silas just writes *"the creek"*.
- **The Moorthistle**, running east from a lake past Ashford, then looping south past dot 9 to
  where it meets a western branch and becomes **the big river**. Label it by name. It is the
  border: Velmora north and east of it, Riolgatha south and west. It is a tributary, not the
  river that reaches the sea.
- **The Broadwend**, the big river from where the rivers meet down to the bay. It is the
  red/navy border on the political overlay. Silas's label: *"Broadwend. Barges go all the way
  down."*
- **Ashford's jetty**: a few flatboats at the creek mouth, enough for local freight. The real
  barges load at Orrend.
- **Orrend** (`data/locations/cities/Orrend.md`): on the Moorthistle's bend, a day and
  a half south (registry dot 9). Label: *"Orrend. Market's Cael's, the water's Marlow's,
  and you pay both."*
- **Down the Moorthistle and the Broadwend to the bay**: the river mouth at the bottom of the sheet. Label it with the
  line Session 29 established, word for word: *"Tassad — three weeks by road, or ten days if
  you catch a barge down the Moorthistle and a ship from the bay."*
- **The north road** to Lord Cael's hall, running off the top edge. Label: *"Lord Cael — 3–4
  days. Bring a letter. He likes letters."* The hall stays unnamed, as canon has it.
- **The drover's road**, southeast out of the ford, through rolling scrub. Label: *"Drover's
  road. Cattle, mostly."* The party is riding this road in Session 30.
- **Duskwater**: the drover's road runs into it at the southeast edge, greener country along
  the river. Label: *"Duskwater — Marlow land. Good grass. Good prices. I trade there."* Silas
  is neutral, so he adds nothing pointed.
- **Riolgatha**: the far bank of the Moorthistle, drawn as hills and left mostly blank. Label:
  *"Riolgatha. Highlands. Don't."*
- **Velmora**: a word written large across the top half, with nothing under it. Label:
  *"Velmora — the crown's up north somewhere, for all the good it does."*

### Distances Silas would write

- Ashford to Orrend: a day and a half's ride *(proposed)*
- Ashford to Lord Cael's hall: 3–4 days' ride *(canon)*
- Ashford to Tassad: three weeks by road, or ten days by water *(canon)*
- Ashford down the drover's road: *"a long day to anywhere worth stopping"* *(proposed; matches
  Session 30's "a long day's ride" to the waystation without marking the waystation)*
- Ashford to Duskwater's edge: 2 days southeast *(proposed)*

### Must NOT include

- **The waystation, the dusk fork or the camp.** The party finds those in the chase. Marking them
  would give the chase away.
- **Raid routes or raid marks.** Those are Mara's layer.
- **The old battle ground** on the Moorthistle. That is Richmond's layer.
- **Anything linking Marlow to the raids.** Silas would never write it down.
- **The witch's house, Asher, or the aquifer.**
- **World-scale labels** (Calenmere Basin, Erevast, the Ardenwall). Silas doesn't think at that
  scale. Halsby's layer can bring them in.

### Leave room for

Leave the **west** of the sheet sparse, and the **east** blank past a day's ride. Sable fills the
east. Elowen's out-of-date war map covers the river country, so leave the Moorthistle banks
uncluttered for her. Halsby's route to Tassad will run off the edge.

## Layer 2 — Sable's road east *(all proposed)*

When asked where she came from, Sable draws one line due east from Ashford, into the woodland
that fills Velmora's south past the Moorthistle's bend, and writes three short notes along it. She
came on foot. She won't say why she left, who hired her to clear the Lioness, or why she stayed.

> **Terrain check (2026-10-08, `maps/locations/silas-region-scope.png`):** five days east on foot
> is about 150 km, and that lands in the forest east of the river, not on open plains. The first
> draft sent her through Serhari and Grey Mothers country. That country is plains, and none of it
> is within five days of Ashford, so it's dropped here. If you want her to have come from the
> plains, make the walk weeks long instead.

- **Coldwell**, five days east, at the end of the line. A woodland town built around one very deep
  well. Her label: *"Coldwell. Deep wells. Priests who don't like me."* This is where she worked
  before Ashford. It could graduate one of the purple-region registry dots east of Ashford when the
  Calenmere `/region` pass happens.
- **The Stones**, a day and a half east: a ring of standing stones in a clearing where the road
  crosses a spring, and where travellers leave bread. Her label: *"Good water. Leave bread."* She is
  a water warlock and notices water first. Everything she marks is a place to drink.
- **The road itself**, labelled once: *"Old salt road. Five days. On foot. Don't."*

**Why east works:** Silas covers north (Cael), southeast (Duskwater) and south (the Moorthistle and
the bay). East is the empty quarter. It opens a second direction to explore without crossing the
river into Riolgatha.

**Optional hook (Jeff's call):** Sable's file says Ashford's river is "going bad." She could add one
more mark: a short line across the creek below the town with *"Not below here."* It is a clue
about the fouling that she won't explain. Leave it off if the fouling isn't ready to surface.

## Later layers (reserved, not built)

- **Elowen:** a guild war map, decades out of date. Old borders, a fort that may no longer exist,
  and the Moorthistle drawn wrong somewhere.
- **Halsby:** his traveller's route toward Tassad, which is the first world-scale context (the bay,
  the region's name).
- **Mara:** marks where the raids have hit. They will cluster along the drover's road side.
- **Richmond:** the old battle ground on the Moorthistle.

## DM-only (off the map)

- The raids run up the drover's road from Duskwater, under House Marlow, through cutouts.
- The waystation is a long day's ride southeast on the drover's road. Its mileage is unset until a
  region pass sets it.
- Which river is the Moorthistle on the world master is still an open item (`todo-worldmap.md` →
  Calenmere). The handout is local scale and doesn't depend on that decision.

## New canon this would set (confirm or strike)

1. Orrend, a day and a half south: Cael's market, Marlow's wharves (entity drafted)
2. Duskwater's edge is 2 days southeast by the drover's road
3. Sable came from **Coldwell**, five days east through the woods, on foot
4. The Stones, a day and a half east on the old salt road
5. The old salt road runs east from Ashford through the woods
6. *(optional)* Sable's "not below here" mark on the creek
