Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

**From:** [[The Matron]]

**What it does:** Takes 1 minute to set up anywhere: a ledge, a storm, mid-dungeon. Creatures inside can finish a Long Rest, and nothing outside can find, harm or enter it. Afterward it folds into a snowflake and melts. Works once.

**How to earn it:** Earn her favor with honesty, paid debts and respect for the ship. Being kind to [[Mitzi]] counts too.
