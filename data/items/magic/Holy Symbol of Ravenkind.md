The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind is one of three sacred relics hidden across Barovia — alongside the [[Sunsword]] and the [[Icon of Ravenloft]] — each representing a path to defeating Strahd von Zarovich.

Blessed at the founding of the Barovian faith, it empowers its wielder to turn undead with unusual force and break dark curses afflicting those under Strahd's influence. It is one of the few items in the domain capable of granting necrotic resistance.
