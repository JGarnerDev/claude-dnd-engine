A matched pair of smooth stones — one for each party. Once per day, the holder of one stone can speak up to 25 words, heard instantly by whoever holds the other. Range is unlimited, crossing even planar boundaries.

Commonly used by merchants, military commanders, and adventurers who need to coordinate across great distances. Particularly valuable in Barovia, where the Mists prevent conventional long-distance travel.
