# Burner

*The same iron-banded clay egg as the Breaker, with a red-glazed cap. It is warm to hold.*

The fire version of the [[Breaker]]. It hits for less when it bursts, but the burning pitch
sticks to whatever it lands on and keeps burning.

## Mechanics

- **Throw.** As an action, twist the cap and throw it at a point within **60 feet**. It bursts
  when it lands.
- **Blast.** Each creature within **10 feet** of that point makes a **DC 12 Dexterity** save.
  It takes **2d6 fire** damage on a failure, or half on a success.
- **Clinging fire.** A creature that **fails** the save catches fire. It takes **1d4 fire**
  damage at the start of each of its turns. Any creature can use an action to put the fire out,
  and so can rolling in water, mud or sand.
- **Burning ground.** Flammable objects in the blast that aren't worn or carried catch fire.
  The 10-foot area burns until the end of the thrower's next turn. A creature that enters it or
  ends its turn there takes **1d4 fire** damage.
- **Single use.** The Burner is destroyed when it bursts.

Fire-resistant creatures blunt it, and it has no special effect on constructs. That is the
Breaker's job.
