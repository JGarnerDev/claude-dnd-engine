Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

**From:** All four food stalls together

**What it does:** When Kirsty's Candle, Oil That Lasts, Hari's Diya and the Burrow Hood are brought to the core together, their lights merge. Once, the party can dim or brighten the Inner Sun for 1 minute. Dimming it switches off Sees Its Shadow, and is one way to break Groundhog Claus's loop.

**How to earn it:** Earn all four food-stall gifts.
