A cloth bag with an extradimensional interior capable of storing hundreds of pounds of equipment while weighing only 15 lbs. A staple piece of adventuring gear with obvious practical value for any traveling party.

Note: the bag can be destroyed — piercing the exterior or interior tears it open and scatters all contents across the Astral Plane, permanently and irretrievably.
