The Sunsword is one of three sacred relics hidden across Barovia — alongside the [[Holy Symbol of Ravenkind]] and the [[Icon of Ravenloft]] — each representing a path to defeating Strahd von Zarovich.

Its blade of pure radiance was separated from its crystal pommel long ago. When restored, it becomes one of the only weapons in Barovia whose light counts as true sunlight — lethal to vampire spawn and capable of driving back the eternal dark of Ravenloft.
