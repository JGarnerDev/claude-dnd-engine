The Icon of Ravenloft is one of three sacred relics hidden across Barovia — alongside the [[Sunsword]] and the [[Holy Symbol of Ravenkind]] — each representing a path to defeating Strahd von Zarovich.

Ironically, it was commissioned by Strahd himself as an act of devotion before his transformation into a vampire lord. When the Morninglord refused to answer his prayers, he locked it within Castle Ravenloft. It carries the power to both commune with Strahd and imprison him in his own coffin.
