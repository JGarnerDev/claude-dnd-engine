Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

**From:** [[Hari]] (Hindu stall)

**What it does:** As an action, light it for 1 minute: it sheds bright light in a 30-ft. radius, dispels magical darkness there, and allies in the light have advantage on saves against being Frightened.

**How to earn it:** Help finish her rangoli, or listen to her story of light over darkness.
