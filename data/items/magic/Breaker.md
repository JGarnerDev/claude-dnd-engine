# Breaker

*A fat clay egg wrapped in iron bands, with a twist-cap on top. Shake it and something inside
ticks.*

A thrown grenade built to break things that were made, not born. The blast hurts flesh, but its
real work is on stone, timber, iron and anything put together and set moving.

## Mechanics

- **Throw.** As an action, twist the cap and throw it at a point within **60 feet**. It bursts
  when it lands.
- **Blast.** Each creature within **10 feet** of that point makes a **DC 12 Dexterity** save.
  It takes **3d6 bludgeoning** damage on a failure, or half on a success.
- **Built things break.** **Constructs and objects** in the blast take an extra **2d6
  bludgeoning** damage, halved on a successful save along with the rest.
- **Loud.** The burst can be heard up to **300 feet** away.
- **Single use.** The Breaker is destroyed when it bursts.

Roughly a DMG bomb in power and price (about 150 gp).
