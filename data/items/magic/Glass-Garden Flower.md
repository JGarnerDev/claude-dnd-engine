Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

**From:** The glass gardens of the Hollow Mountain

**What it does:** A crystal flower that hums and holds a memory of spring. Shown to Groundhog Claus, it counts as an automatic success when talking him down. Sister Suzanna wants one.

**How to earn it:** Pick one in the glass gardens: INT (Nature) or DEX (Sleight of Hand) DC 13; on a fail it shatters, and finding another is WIS (Perception) DC 15.

Related: [[Sister Suzanna]], [[The Hollow Mountain]]. All the gifts: [[Earning the Gifts]].
