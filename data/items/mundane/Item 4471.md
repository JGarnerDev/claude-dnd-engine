Held by: [[The Knowledge Bank]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

A cracked slate tablet in a velvet-lined box at [[The Knowledge Bank]]. [[Ebling Mis]] sells it as a Rare secret (100 gp). It gives the order of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]] but not the ending.

### Handout

> *KNOWLEDGE BANK OF CINTO. Item 4471. Fragment, Rimefolk script, carved on slate. Translated by the guild. Appraised: Rare. Not for resale.*
>
> ...and Sartori of the Rimefolk set the river in a cradle of ice, that his road might reach the deep.
>
> The first year, Celestine wept, and her tears ran salt through the stone.
>
> After, Celestine slept, and a hundred winters passed over her like a single night.
>
> At the last, Celestine sang, and the stone remembered it was only stone, and broke.
>
> Then Sartori came down to the water and *[the rest has crumbled away]*
>
> *In the margin, in Mis's neat hand: "Order of verses confirmed against two other sources. Ending lost. Will pay handsomely for it. E.M."*
