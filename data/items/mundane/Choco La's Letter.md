Held by: Shaw | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Choco La's letter to his half-brother Shaw (both monks and PCs), handed to [[Pip Post]] at [[The Buckle Post Office]] and carried by [[The Post]] until Shaw, riding with Harrow Bane's band, robbed the mail and found his own name on it (Session 35). It is still sealed with the Post's small closed envelope pressed into the wax. Robbing the Post may get him blacklisted (see [[The Post]]). It introduces [[Hobbes]]. Give it to Tyler before the first session, or have Shaw read it aloud in [[Cinto]].

- **Worth:** to [[Ebling Mis]] it's a Rare secret (100 gp), because it says where a pilgrim tunnel really ends. Selling it spreads it like any sold secret.
- **The second letter:** Pip is holding a later one that never got out because [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] closed the bridge; see [[The Buckle Reopens]].

### Handout

> Shaw,
>
> It's been a long time, and you probably don't remember much of me beyond a name. It's your brother. Half of one, anyway: Choco La. I've been looking for you for years, and the postmaster here swears his couriers can find anybody, so here's hoping.
>
> I'm writing from the Buckle, which is a whole town built round an iron ship frozen in the ice, deep under the Deorum Sanctum. That's what the pilgrims call it. The monks here call it the Deorum Ostium and won't say why.
>
> The monks in the cupola say that if you're very still, you can hear the oldest place, the one underneath everything. They have a word for it: Zahareena. I sat with them for an hour and heard something else. Scratching. Something is digging, far down.
>
> I've made a friend. Hobbes is a halfling scholar in a red knitted hat who talks more than anyone I've ever met, and he wants to go down and say hello to whatever is digging. Someone has to keep him out of trouble, so I'm staying until he's back. He says he'll be back after breakfast. He says that a lot.
>
> Something is happening up here, Shaw. If you don't hear from me again by Midwinter, come and find me. Bring the good cocoa.
>
> Your brother,
>
> Choco La
>
> P.S. A sailor in the bar says the bonfires on the ice bridge have started lighting themselves. Probably nothing.
