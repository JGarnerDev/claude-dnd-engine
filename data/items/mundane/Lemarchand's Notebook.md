Deposit box 117 at [[The Old Bank]], unclaimed for longer than [[Mendelson Shape]] has worked there. It has the ending of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]] and a map of [[The Rimefolk Maze]].

### Handout

Deposit box 117, unclaimed for longer than Mendelson has worked there. Inside is a water-stained leather notebook wrapped in oilcloth. Most pages are measurements (in fongs, naturally) and sketches of gears, chains and a great wheel. Folded in the back is a hand-drawn map of the first pilgrim tunnels, which is the maze, with the words *"Turn always as the river turns: to the right."* The last written page reads:

> Master Sartori says the bridge must remember what he learned, so we have carved it into the wheel. The builder stands proud, and the river waits. Only one who bows as he bowed can turn it.
>
> I was there. When she sang, the dam broke and we all ran, but he did not run. He came down to the water, and the builder bowed. She forgave him. She turns his wheel still.
>
> I carved her face on the hub myself. I hope I got it right.
>
> *Lemarchand, apprentice mason*

Mendelson, peering over their shoulder: "Oh, how lovely. I always wondered what was in that one. Do keep it, nobody's coming back for it."
