The northern wall of the [[Calenmere]] basin — cold grey stone, older than anything settled on it, catching the first snow of the year while [[Ardenwall]] to the south still holds the afternoon sun. Dwarvish-named: *orn* (elder, grey) + *grath* (hewn stone) — "the elder stone." The dwarves cut it first; the name outlived them.

Richest metal country in the region: iron, coal, and silver seamed through the rock, worked from clan-holds and mining towns strung along the passes and foothill valleys. The mining nation **Vaskgrath** — *vask* (iron) + the same *grath* — took its name from the range, not the other way around. Its one artery to market is the pass down at the lake on the mountains' southern foot.

Difficult in any season, sealed in none — the passes filter who attempts them. Rivers drain south and southeast off the range toward the bay, and every load of Orngrath metal follows that water down through country the range does not control — the toll grievance that defines the mountain's politics. Deeper in, older holds stand dark and sealed; what emptied them is the range's own buried question.
