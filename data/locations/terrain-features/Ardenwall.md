The northern mountain range running along [[Solmara]]. Ancient stone, impassive — older than anything settled on either side. The afternoon sun catches the peaks before it fades; travelers coming south first see [[Solmara]]'s forests in that warm light.

Difficult to cross in any season, but not sealed. The passes filter who attempts them rather than blocking passage entirely.

Part of Miguel's revised contribution. Named for the burning quality of afternoon light against old stone — *ardens*, the burning wall.
