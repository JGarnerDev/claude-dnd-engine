The mountain range that crowns [[The Pale Reach]] — pale as bone and cold as the grave. [[The Pale Spire]] is permanently glaciated; no seasonal thaw softens it. It crowns the southern landmass in permanent ice and defines the skyline of everything south.

Impassable. No known passes. It is the visual terminus of the world going south. Part of Miguel's **pale** cluster.
