The mountain range at the center of the world — the line that splits the desert south from the forested north. [[The Divide]] cuts the continent clean in half. Everything changes at its feet: climate, culture, trade patterns, power.

Whoever holds the passes holds the continent's throat. Every major war in the region has eventually been decided by who controls these chokepoints.
