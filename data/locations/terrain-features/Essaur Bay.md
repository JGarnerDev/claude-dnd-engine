Large warm bay on the southern coast of [[Erevast]], near the edge of [[Azel Samarat]]'s influence. Merchants and pilgrims use the same docks. The culture that grew up around the bay reflects both — commercial and devotional, simultaneously pragmatic and sacred. The pilgrimage trade is itself a commercial industry here.
