Lower-left island group near the southwestern volcanic landmasses. Ash from the volcanic chain reaches them on the prevailing wind — the stone is dark, the air occasionally sulphurous. Stepping stones toward the volcanic west; they catch the last light before everything beyond them goes dark.

Named via questionnaire; not yet detailed. Awaits a `/region` pass on the southwestern waters.
