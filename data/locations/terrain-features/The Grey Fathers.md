The resource mountains of the northern tribal peoples. Smaller than the great ranges further south, but indispensable — stone, ore, shelter, game. The name is worn down by centuries of use around fires. These are not legendary mountains; they are familiar ones. The tundra peoples named them as they name anything they depend on: like elders, like providers.
