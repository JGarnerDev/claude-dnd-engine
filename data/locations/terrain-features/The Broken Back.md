A fractured, eroded mountain range running through the lower-center of the map — one wall of the enclosure that traps [[The Hidden Desert]] inside the sub-continent. The desert has had centuries to work on these peaks and it shows. Rock falls. Crumbling passes. The spine of a sunken land.

Navigable but unremarkable — the interest here is what they hide, not the mountains themselves.
