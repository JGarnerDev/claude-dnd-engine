Cold, isolated island group north of The Reach. Pale stone, pale ice, pale water — the color washes out of everything this far north. The grey water around them is the memorable part; the islands themselves offer little. Strategically marginal, atmospherically oppressive.

Named via questionnaire; not yet detailed. Awaits a `/region` pass on the northern waters.
