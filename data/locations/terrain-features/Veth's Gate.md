The southwestern channel between the main continent and the southwestern island. Colonial traders named it after Veth — a lizard-tongue word absorbed into the trading pidgin spoken along this route. It marks the primary contact zone between colonial merchant interests and [[Karath Ut'll]]'s tribal peoples. Both sides use the gate; neither owns it.
