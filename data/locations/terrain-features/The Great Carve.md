The outer wall of the wound. [[The Great Carve]] forms the rim of [[The Caldera]]'s Struck Side — the closest peaks to [[Astra]]'s impact zone that still stand above the hellscape. Ash has coated them for millennia; the stone itself has changed color.

Below and deeper into the Struck Side lies [[Hell's Reign]], the inner wall of the original crater.
