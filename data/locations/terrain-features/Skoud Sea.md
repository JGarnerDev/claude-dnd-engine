Cold in a way that exceeds the physical. The Skoud Sea's cold is not merely temperature — sailors who spend time on it report difficulty feeling positive emotions, an inability to remember warmth, a cognitive fog that lifts only well after leaving. Small and isolated. The eastern island barely makes it worth crossing. Most avoid it entirely.
