Volcanic bay on the far west, surrounded by an island chain of active volcanoes. Ash falls into the water year-round — the surface is often grey with it. Nothing good comes from this bay except [[Coffee]].

[[Cindermouth Bay]] is the nearest sea access to [[The Ashreaches]] — the port through which every sack of the Black eventually travels. House Rogarre's trade route control begins here. Part of Miguel's **cinder/amber** cluster, and the geographic anchor linking his two submissions.
