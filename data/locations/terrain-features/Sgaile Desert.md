Not the burning kind of desert. The [[Gordrathi Mountains]] block enough sun to keep this place cold and shadowed by desert standards — named for that shadow, *sgaile*. The cold here is a desert cold: dry, biting, and disorienting to anyone who came expecting heat. Sand and frost coexist in the morning hours.
