Incredibly fast-moving water. Unprepared boats don't make it through — the current churns them apart. The Vetta Swains are the only people who know the crossing: their methods are proprietary, passed down, and not shared freely. Upper center-right [[Erevast]], adjacent to [[Frisian Bay]]. Any captain who needs to cross pays the Vetta Swains, or doesn't cross.
