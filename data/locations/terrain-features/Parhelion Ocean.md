Named for an optical phenomenon: as the sun transits east to west, rising over the far volcanic island and setting over the western coast, a parhelion — a second sun — appears near the horizon. Two suns in the sky, one real, one light-born. The legend holds that the volcanic island or the Astra impact itself is the land star the second sun circles. Vast and uncharted beyond the island horizon. Few who sail east come back with new information.
