The northern mountain range running along [[The Greyback]]. Storms break against [[The Stormwall]] every season without exception — grey clouds pile against the peaks and shed their weight there. Passes are open roughly three months of the year, closed the rest.

Visible from two days out. The grey of it matches the grey of everything in [[The Greyback]] — the stone, the sky, the water.
