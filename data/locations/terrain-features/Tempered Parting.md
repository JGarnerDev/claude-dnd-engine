The channel separating [[Watler's Way]] from the Struck Side volcanic coast. [[Tempered Parting]] is the water gap between the island that hosts [[The Ashreaches]] and the continental mass where [[The Caldera]] sits.

Navigable but not a major trade lane — traffic here is local, serving the coffee export economy centered on [[Cindermouth Bay]]. Ash from the Caldera clouds the water, and the Kernings that herald every Whatwill Showering echo strangely off the channel walls.
