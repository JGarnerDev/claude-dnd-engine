Slow-moving. Easy crossing. Almost too easy — sailors comment on it. The [[Karath Ut'll]] peninsula channel that should, given its geography, be more dramatic than it is. The torpor is genuine; currents barely move. Nothing happens here in a way that makes experienced sailors slightly uneasy. It is calm in the way a held breath is calm.
