The last land before the eastern ocean swallows everything. [[Caldera's Brace]] sits at the far eastern edge of the world — across the map's wrap seam, the same meridian as [[The Caldera]] on the far west.

Formed by magma splash: molten ejecta flung by [[Astra]]'s impact, too heavy to reach orbit and too displaced to stay, cooled where it fell in the [[Parhelion Ocean]]. The name marks the islands as the Caldera's bracing on the world's far arc — debris from the wound, come to rest a world away.
