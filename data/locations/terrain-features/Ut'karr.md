The deep interior of [[Karath Ut'll]]. Distinct from the outer inhabited territories where the tribes live. Named in the same lizard-tongue tradition as the continent itself — Ut' prefix marks it as belonging to the same cultural family. What makes it distinct from the rest of Karath Ut'll is undefined; whether it is sacred, forbidden, or simply more lethal is not yet determined.
