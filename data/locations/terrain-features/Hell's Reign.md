The inner wall of the original impact crater. [[Hell's Reign]] sits deeper into the Struck Side than [[The Great Carve]], close enough to [[The Caldera]] that volcanic influence is severe and the stone is visibly altered. The Caldera's glow is visible from these peaks at night.

No expedition has returned with a full account of what lies past them.
