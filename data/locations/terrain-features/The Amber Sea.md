The inner sea enclosed by the main continent's arms — warm, landlocked, and calm by comparison to the open ocean. Desert sediment washing in from the east stains the shallows gold. Merchants call it the most forgiving water in the known world.

Its sheltered nature makes it a commercial artery. Whatever conflicts press in from outside, trade on [[The Amber Sea]] tends to continue. Part of Miguel's **cinder/amber** cluster — the silt-stained shallows are the tell.
