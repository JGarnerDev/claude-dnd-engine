The channel separating [[Islands A]] from the Struck Side volcanic coast. [[Channel I]] is the water gap between the island that hosts [[The Ashreaches]] and the continental mass where [[The Caldera]] sits.

Navigable but not a major trade lane — traffic here is local, serving the coffee export economy centered on [[Cindermouth Bay]].
