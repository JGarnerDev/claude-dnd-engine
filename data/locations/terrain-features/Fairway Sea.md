The sea in the middle of everything. Known trade routes run through it; calm conditions make it reliable. The culture of the Fairway is transactional — you can get what you need, but nothing is free. Sailors who need help get it, and they pay for it. No sentiment, no charity. The name implies a fair crossing, which is true: fair, not free.
