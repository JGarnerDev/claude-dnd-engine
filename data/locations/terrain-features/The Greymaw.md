The primary sea approach to [[The Greyback]] — grey skies, grey water, dangerous at the best of times. [[The Greymaw]] earns its name. Sailors lose fingers here in winter. The entrance is treacherous enough that it filters who attempts it.

Whatever commerce reaches [[The Greyback]] overland or by this bay has earned its way in. Part of Miguel's **grey** cluster.
