Southwest sea flanked by volcanic and mountainous terrain. Volcanic debris drifts in from the western chain; geothermal vents warm the water below. The surface runs grey with fine ash on heavy weather days — hence the name. Navigation is possible but demands attention; debris fields and unpredictable currents are common near the volcanic flanks.

Named via questionnaire; not yet detailed. Awaits a `/region` pass on the southwestern waters.
