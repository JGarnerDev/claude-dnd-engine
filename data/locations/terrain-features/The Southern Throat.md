The only sea route between the main landmass and [[The Pale Reach]]. [[The Southern Throat]] is narrow, freezing, and littered with iceberg debris and rock scatter from [[The Pale Isles]]. Most ships do not attempt it. Those that do have reasons they don't discuss.

A brief summer window makes transit possible — not safe, possible. The rest of the year it is effectively closed. Part of Miguel's **pale** cluster — pale stone, pale water, permanent cold.
