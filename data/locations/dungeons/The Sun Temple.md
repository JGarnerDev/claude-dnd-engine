[[Luci]] remembers waking in a temple devoted to the Sun, built on an inconceivable, labyrinthine scale and abandoned by its worshippers. Her form there was a mote of divine energy. She explored it alone, reading the tongue of the **Dead Sun Worshippers** by genetic memory, among chiming prayer bells and spinning solar-powered machinery. A Void Serpent crashed into it and scattered her into the Astral Sea.

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Paul's Roll20 sheet and backstory answers (ingested 2026-10-25). State is `unknown` on purpose: whether Luci was ever truly here, or remembers it only through genetic memory, is an open hook Paul wants answered in play.

## The Walls

Words cover the walls, wheeling in script and meaning, each fighting for the reader's attention. The light is exalted and revered and feared and condemned, all in one breath. Every etched sigil filled Luci with the worship and the horror of the people who carved it, as the light envelops them. Within her, the perpetual light illuminated all of those peoples' ideas of their place in the universe: motes dancing in an infinite, harsh expanse, drifting and flaring and winking out, pulling life in with them. A dance that feeds, that burns, that twirls.

## How Paul Wants It Played

A **pocket dimension the party can hack**, like Shadowrun's cyberdeck security realm. Putting a piece on the board shifts the board. At its core is [[Aedra]], a powerful dark entity trying to find the light, watched over by [[Sabathiel]].
