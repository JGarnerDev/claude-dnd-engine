Location: [[Deorum Ostium]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

A tangle of pilgrim tunnels in the deep ice, adapted from the Tomb of the Unknown King maze in Final Fantasy VIII. Turning right always leads on; turning left leads back. The route runs Entrance, Fallen Sword, Statue Room, Dam Room, Wheel Room and back to the entrance, after which the straight path leads over the lowered drawbridge to [[The Ice Bridge]].

The dam and the wheel were built by [[The Rimefolk]] around [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]]. The full puzzles, rolls and encounter table are in the mission [[Only the Righteous Find the Way]].

**Treasure:** Sartori's own mason's mallet, jammed in the wheel, worth a Rare secret in [[Cinto]].

## Inside

Every corridor between the rooms looks the same: smooth blue ice, a low ceiling, a drip somewhere you can't see. The rooms are where it changes.

- **The Entrance:** a round chamber with a domed ceiling, carved with thousands of pilgrim names. A pile of old bedrolls and a fire ring, warm enough to rest in. Three passages: left, right and straight. Straight ends at the frozen moat until the drawbridge is down. *Poke at:* "Only the righteous find the way," carved over the first fork. *Hidden:* a pilgrim's chalk marks, some leading the wrong way on purpose.
- **The Fallen Sword:** a narrow room where a dead pilgrim sits against the wall, frozen solid, sword across his knees. *Poke at:* the blade, with three worn [[The Rimefolk|Rimefolk]] marks under the frost: a teardrop, a closed eye, an open mouth. *Hidden:* a dropped letter from the brothers Post near his boots, which tells the party they're on the right track.
- **The Statue Room:** a tall hall with an empty stone plinth in the middle and scorch marks across the floor, as if something burned here and then got up and walked away. *Poke at:* the plaque on the plinth: "Sartori, who bowed." *Hidden:* half-melted carvings around the walls, chewed at the edges: [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] fed here before it moved to the bridge.
- **The Dam Room:** a cavern with a wall of frozen water held back by an old dam of ice and stone. A walkway runs along the base, and three chains hang from the sluice gate, each ending in a carved stone weight (a teardrop, a closed eye, an open mouth). *Poke at:* the teardrop chain, worn smooth from use. *Hidden:* a drainage channel that carries the meltwater to the Wheel Room once the sluice opens.
- **The Wheel Room:** a deep shaft with a great wooden water wheel, three times a person's height, its hub carved with a proud stone builder facing a woman made of waves. A frozen bundle of [[The Rimefolk|Rimefolk]] tools is jammed in the gears. *Poke at:* the hub figures; the builder will press down. *Hidden:* Sartori's mallet in the bundle of tools.
- **The frozen moat:** straight ahead from the Entrance, a trench of black ice with the drawbridge raised on the far side. When the wheel turns, chains clank and the bridge comes down, and warm air drifts back from [[The Ice Bridge|the Ice Bridge]] beyond.

**Encounters:** at each fork, roll a d6. On a 1, roll on this table. Each link between rooms has 3 forks.

- **1.** Encounter: 4 Skeletons. XP: 200.
- **2.** Encounter: 2 Ghouls. XP: 400.
- **3.** Encounter: 1 Minotaur Skeleton. XP: 450.
- **4.** Encounter: 2 Gargoyles posing as statues. XP: 900.
