The wound left by [[Astra]]. When the celestial body struck the Struck Side of the dwarf planetoid 6.7433 millennia ago, the impact gouged this massive Caldera into the surface. The same event dislodged the Arc Side's islands, cast [[The Slices]] into orbit, and spun off the trillions of shards that became [[Whatwill]].

The Caldera is described uniformly as a fiery hellscape. Its interior is largely unknown — no expedition is recorded as having fully explored it and returned.

## Story Hooks

- Venture into the Caldera — what survives at the point of Astra's original contact with the planet?
- Something down there is connected to Whatwill in ways not yet understood
