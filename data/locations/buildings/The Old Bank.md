Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

An old, decrepit stone bank sits off to the side of what used to be [[Cinto]]'s original town square, long since abandoned for the busier streets near the port. Its columns are cracked, its sign has lost half its letters, and almost no one goes in. Since knowledge became the currency, nobody needs a bank for gold.

- **The banker:** [[Mendelson Shape]] still runs the till alone. He happily explains that currency means nothing anymore and that it's been a lovely place to retire.
- **The vault:** it's still full of gold no one wants. [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]] will change coins, store valuables or give out loans on very relaxed terms.
- **The deposit boxes:** a few boxes have gone unclaimed for decades, left by pilgrims who went into the deep ice and never came back. One could hold an old map of the first pilgrim tunnels.

**The sign** (half its letters fell off years ago): *C NTO SA  NGS & L AN*. [[Mendelson Shape]] calls it "the Cinto Sangs and Lan" and won't hear otherwise.

Deposit box 117 holds [[Lemarchand's Notebook]], with the missing ending of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]].

## Inside

A grey stone building with cracked columns on the empty old square. The sign reads *C NTO SA  NGS & L AN*. Inside, every sound echoes.

- **The banking hall:** a marble floor with a crack down the middle, a long counter with brass grilles, and one window open. Dust on everything except the stretch of counter [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]] polishes every morning. *Who's here:* [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]], humming, making coin-clinking sounds when he's pleased. *Poke at:* a brass bell on the counter that rings a different note every time.
- **[[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]]'s office:** a desk buried in ledgers, a cold cup of tea, and a cushion on the windowsill where he naps in the afternoon. *Hidden:* the ledgers list every deposit box and who opened it, which is how the party can find box 117 (INT (Investigation) DC 14).
- **The vault:** a round steel door that's always open, because nobody wants the gold inside. Gold bars stacked like firewood, some of them holding the door open.
- **The deposit boxes:** a narrow room lined floor to ceiling with small locked boxes, each with a brass number. Box 117 is high up, and the ladder squeaks. *Hidden:* [[Lemarchand's Notebook|Lemarchand's notebook]] in box 117. Other unclaimed boxes can hold whatever you need: a pilgrim's last letter, a carving tool, a key to nothing.
- **The party's box:** if [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]] opens one for their stories, it sits on the counter, and he adds new clippings while they watch.
