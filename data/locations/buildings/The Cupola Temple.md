Location: [[The Buckle]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Carved into the cupola of the chamber and run by [[Aurelian Sellenor]] and [[The Monks of the Ostium]]. It's reached by precarious stairs or by climbing the mast (DC 13 STR (Athletics)), which pilgrims treat as part of the pilgrimage. Fail by 5 or more and you fall 10 ft. The stairs need no roll, just a lot of complaining.

The Archhierophant listens here for the worlds grinding together. The slow scratching he hears is really [[Groundhog Claus]] digging (DM-only).

## Inside

- **The way up:** either the precarious stairs cut into the cavern wall (no roll, a lot of complaining) or the climb up the ship's mast and across a rope bridge to the cupola (STR (Athletics) DC 13).
- **The listening hall:** a round chamber carved into the cavern's ceiling, with the ship below, visible through a wide opening in the floor. Rows of cushions face inward. It's so quiet you can hear your own pulse. *Who's here:* [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]], glowing faintly, and a few monks in silence. *Poke at:* the hush; sit and listen, and the cracks and pops of the worlds are there.
- **The carving walls:** every monk carves a line a day here, and the walls spiral upward with them.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]'s cell:** a bare room with a bed, a lamp and a single carving he has worked on for forty years: a door, slightly open. *Hidden:* his notes on the scratching, marking where it's loudest. They all point down.
