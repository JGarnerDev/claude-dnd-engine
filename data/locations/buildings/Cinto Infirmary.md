Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Run by [[Susan Calvin]], with [[Nurse Dors]] as her right hand. It treats frostbite, exhaustion and the odder ailments pilgrims bring back from the deep ice. Patients from the deep ice talk in their sleep in languages no one knows, and some wake up remembering lives they never lived. The doctor suspects the veil is thinning.

- **Hook:** the doctor is out of a rare frost-moss salve that only grows near [[The Buckle]], and pilgrims keep coming back with frostbite.
- **Helping out:** a DC 12 WIS (Medicine) check earns a Potion of Healing.
- **The sleep-talkers:** a DC 15 INT (Arcana) check recognises scripts from the deep ice.

## Inside

A long whitewashed hall near the port, built over an old bathhouse, so the floor is always warm.

- **The front room:** a bench, a stove with a kettle always on, and a slate where Dors writes who's in which bed. *Poke at:* a cot by the door labelled "THE GNOME."
- **The ward:** twelve beds in two rows under high windows, with curtains on rails. Half are full of frostbitten pilgrims. *Who's here:* [[Nurse Dors]], knitting between patients, humming. *Poke at:* the patients back from the deep ice, who talk in their sleep in scripts no one knows (INT (Arcana) DC 15).
- **[[Susan Calvin|Doctor Calvin]]'s surgery:** a scrubbed table, shelves of jars, and a chalkboard of notes on "veil cases" in tiny handwriting. *Hidden:* an empty jar labelled "frost-moss salve, [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]," the reason for her hook.
- **The bath:** the old bathhouse pool, still fed by a warm spring, where patients thaw out. It's the warmest room in [[Cinto]], and the staff pretend not to know that pilgrims sneak in to use it.
