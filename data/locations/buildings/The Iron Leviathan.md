Location: [[The Buckle]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

A WW2-style battleship, always described to the players in D&D terms, never as modern technology.

- **The hull:** as long as a cathedral, with no sails or oars, made of riveted iron plates so perfectly matched they seem forged by one hand.
- **The Thunder Towers:** iron towers on the deck holding giant black iron tubes. Scholars argue whether they're siege engines, wands or a god's organ pipes. Pilgrims call them the Pointing Fingers.
- **The Castle on its Back:** a tall iron keep lined with round glass windows like the eyes of a giant insect.
- **The Bronze Flowers:** huge petalled bronze shapes frozen under the stern, purpose unknown.
- **The Bell:** a brass bell and plaque in an unknown script. Comprehend Languages could hint at the ship's name.

**Theories:** dwarven smiths say it was made without magic, which they find more frightening. Wizards say it's a warship from a world where the gods died. The monks say it proves the veil is thin. See [[The Arrival of the Iron Leviathan]].

**Rolls:** INT (Arcana) DC 12 finds no magic in it at all; INT (History) DC 15 says the rivets match no forge in this world; reading the bell plaque is INT (Investigation) DC 18, or Comprehend Languages, for a fragment of the ship's name.

[[The Matron's Inn]] fills its main body.

## Inside

- **The gangway:** a ramp of planks and rope up to the main deck, with a brass bell at the top. Ring it and [[Mitzi]] comes running.
- **The main deck:** iron underfoot, with the Thunder Towers rising either side and their black iron tubes pointing at nothing. The deck stalls sit between the towers. *Poke at:* the house rules nailed by every hatch. *Hidden:* "Don't touch the Pointing Fingers" is rule 3 for a reason: the tubes hum faintly in the cold.
- **The Castle on its Back:** the tall iron keep with round glass windows. Its lowest floor is the inn's dining hall: long tables, iron walls hung with pilgrims' banners, and a stove the size of a wagon. *Who's here:* [[The Matron|the Matron]] at the end table with her ledger, and [[Mitzi]] with the cocoa pot. *Poke at:* the dining hall's "no paper airplanes" rule, added just for this party.
- **[[The Matron]]'s office and vault:** behind the dining hall, an iron room with one desk, one chair and no decoration. The vault door is set into the wall behind her, and anything left in her care below the ice stays safe there. *Hidden:* what's in the vault is up to you; [[Shadwell]]'s hook says something worth stealing.
- **Below deck, the cabins:** narrow iron corridors with pipes overhead and riveted doors. *The Gun Deck Suite:* the best room, a long cabin with a sealed gun port turned into a window onto the ice. *The Boiler Cabin:* tiny and stiflingly hot, next to the old boilers, which [[The Matron|the Matron]] keeps lit for warmth; she won't give it to [[Luci]]. *The bunk rooms:* rows of bunks for pilgrims who can't pay much.
- **The deep hold:** the bottom of the ship, half full of ice. Nobody goes down here. *Poke at:* the Bronze Flowers, frozen under the stern and visible through the ice of the hold's floor. *Hidden:* the scratching is loudest here.
- **The bell:** on the bow, a brass bell and plaque in an unknown script (Comprehend Languages hints at the ship's name; reading it is INT (Investigation) DC 18).
