Cinto is the pilgrim port outside the glaciers, a melting pot of faiths and commerce that never sleeps. Shops, temples, guilds, a port, an infirmary, homes and an inn crowd together, and the town swells during festivals. Even monks under vows of silence struggle to keep quiet in the excitement.

## Knowledge is currency

**Knowledge is currency.** It's worth more than gold, though gold still has its place. Players can pay with a secret, and the merchant judges how rare it is:

- **Gossip.** Worth: 1–5 gp. Example: Who's feuding with whom at the inn.
- **Useful.** Worth: 25 gp. Example: A safe route through the outer glacier.
- **Rare.** Worth: 100 gp. Example: Where an old pilgrim tunnel really ends.
- **Forbidden.** Worth: 500+ gp. Example: Something carved where no light reaches.

**The catch:** once a secret is sold, it spreads through Cinto and soon becomes worthless, so players learn to guard what they know.

- **Festival crowds** push up prices and draw pickpockets who steal notebooks, not purses.
- **Guilds** buy, sell and verify knowledge, like banks for secrets.

## People of Cinto

- **Archivist [[Ebling Mis]] (guild head).** Who they are: Head librarian, runs [[The Knowledge Bank|the knowledge bank]]. Hook: Appraises secrets and wants anything copied from the mountain.
- **[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]] (riddle master).** Who they are: Answers only in riddles. Hook: Sells hints about [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]].
- **[[Cal Mooney]] (innkeeper).** Who they are: Runs [[The Last Fire|the Last Fire]], gives free soup to pilgrims who can't pay. Hook: The warm opposite of [[The Matron|the Matron]], and maybe an old friend of hers.
- **[[Shadwell]] (thief).** Who they are: Steals notebooks instead of purses. Hook: Can be hired to steal a secret, or caught stealing theirs.
- **Captain [[Hober Mallow]] (sailor).** Who they are: Ferries pilgrims north. Hook: Has seen lights under the sea ice that match the Inner Sun.
- **[[Malingo]] (tailor).** Who they are: Makes cold-weather gear. Hook: His furs give advantage on saves against extreme cold.
- **[[Jerichau]] (candlestick maker).** Who they are: Makes candles that work in thin reality. Hook: Sells rare [[Shade Candles|shade candles]] that give off darkness, a tool for dimming the Inner Sun.
- **[[Bayta Darell]] (baker).** Who they are: Bakes impossibly good cookies. Hook: Her cookies give advantage on Milk and Cookies.
- **[[Preem Palver]] (farmer).** Who they are: Grows food in vent-warmed greenhouses. Hook: Hasn't seen a real spring in years.
- **[[Sister Suzanna]] (gardener).** Who they are: Tends the monks' ice garden. Hook: Wants a [[Glass-Garden Flower|glass-garden flower]], which can convince [[Groundhog Claus]] that spring is safe.
- **Doctor [[Susan Calvin]] (doctor).** Who they are: Runs [[Cinto Infirmary|the infirmary]], treating frostbite, exhaustion and the odder ailments pilgrims bring back from the deep ice. Hook: Her patients from the deep ice talk in their sleep in languages no one knows, and some wake up remembering lives they never lived. She suspects the veil is thinning.
- **[[Nurse Dors]] (nurse).** Who they are: An orc and [[Susan Calvin|Doctor Calvin]]'s right hand. She's huge, gentle, knits between patients and can carry two of them at once. Hook: Sings lullabies to frightened patients, and knows every pilgrim who has gone into the deep ice and not come back.
- **[[Mendelson Shape]] (banker).** Who they are: The only banker left at [[The Old Bank|the Old Bank]], happily retired at the till. Hook: Holds unclaimed deposit boxes from pilgrims who never came back, and has no idea what's in them.

**Captain [[Hober Mallow]]'s line:** "We traveled far up north, going a speed of 48 blusts. If you're not a seafarer, that's the speed of the fastest galegull in a headwind, times itself, and a bit!"

## Local measurements

Cinto's locals use their own units, which never quite match the party's.

- **Shein.** Equals: 5.7 fongs. In real terms: About 7.7 feet (2.35 m).
- **Fong.** Equals: 18 manymeters. In real terms: About 1.35 feet (41 cm).
- **Manymeter, or KWN.** Equals: The length of King Wellend's Nail. In real terms: About 0.9 inches (2.3 cm).
- **Blust.** Equals: A seafarer's speed: the fastest galegull in a headwind, times itself, and a bit. In real terms: Deliberately unclear.

**Quick conversions:** a 5-foot square is about 3.7 fongs. A speed of 30 feet is about 22 fongs, and 60 feet is about 44 fongs. In sheins, 30 feet is about 4 sheins and 60 feet is about 8.

## Getting here

Pilgrims reach the Deorum Ostium by [[The Deorum Ostium Way]] overland, or by sea across The Still Cold. Cinto is the port where the sea approach lands.
