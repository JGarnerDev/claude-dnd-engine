Crater town of [[Masamune]], built around a shallow [[Whatwill]] impact site on the outer Struck Side. [[Kernholt]] sits approximately two days' travel from the Caldera rim on the main extraction road — the first substantial settlement most travellers reach when approaching from the east.

The crater pool at the center reflects a face that is not present. It appears from every viewing angle in the same position, always looking up. The [[The Verdicants]] call it the Bowl Witness. [[Extraction Guilds]] call it reflective interference. Three visitors last season stopped being able to name their mothers after a night near the water.

[[The Verdicants]] control crater wall access. Guilds hold extraction licenses for the outer ring. [[The Wattlers]] hold the impact rim informally and resent both.

Named NPC: [[Sable Mourne]] (Verdicant Mirror Keeper).
