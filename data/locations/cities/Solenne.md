Solenne is the human seat of **[[The Marrow Concord]]** — a young settler port on the warm south shore, its name carrying the *sol-* root of the sunlit south, elegant and a little self-important: a frontier city that already thinks of itself as a metropolis. It faces the bay mouth, and directly across that water sits Cyan's **south jaw**, the strait fort that taxes every hull leaving for open sea.

It is the one leg of the Concord that swells. Immigrants arrive every season, and the city grows to meet them — which makes Solenne both the Concord's engine and its stress fracture. Its two hungers are the twin strains on the whole nation. The first is for a **toll-free sea door**: Solenne, alone among the three powers, cares about Cyan's strait, and schemes to buy, build, or take the south jaw for itself — a fight the forest powers have no stake in, so every step toward it drags the interior toward a war it never chose. The second is for **land**: a growing city needs ground, and the ground inland belongs to **[[The Orrow]]**. Solenne's settlers clear forest the elves need whole, and neither side quite sees the other's terms — to the settlers it is empty frontier; to the Orrow it is the slow murder of their sleeping god.

There is a quieter drift, too. The settlers brought the thin coastal pantheon with them, but the longer they live beside the forest the more they lean toward **[[Orrowan]]** — a slow, uneasy conversion the Orrow deeply distrust, settlers taking up worship of the very god whose home they are felling.
