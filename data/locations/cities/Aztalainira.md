The floating metropolis of [[Arclight's Seat]] and the political heart of [[The Climbers]]' world. The city is structured around [[Cathedral ad Astra]], a building as much landmark as institution. The Arclight Council governs from here. The Opticon convenes here.

The Vitrian calendar engine rests in Aztalainira — a miraculous mechanism that ticks forward at each Opticon, tracking approach of a Glistening. The Lifted regard its accuracy as sacrosanct.

## Story Hooks

- The Vitrian calendar engine has misfired — what does a false Opticon signal mean?
- Reach the Arclight Council to negotiate on behalf of Landborne peoples
