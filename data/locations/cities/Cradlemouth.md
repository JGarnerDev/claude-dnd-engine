Monastic settlement built into the inner wall of the great Caldera in [[Masamune]], accessible via a switchback road that descends from the rim. The descent takes most of a day. The ascent takes longer. People who go to [[Cradlemouth]] tend to go deliberately.

Home of [[Caldera Monks]] and the oldest functioning observatory on Masamune. The observatory's predictions are not published, but shared with anyone who arrives honestly asking. The Monks predicted the Sixth Glistening eleven months in advance — the first public prediction in recorded history. The Lifted and Guild authorities did not acknowledge it.

Six months ago the monks began hearing a new sound from beneath the old mirrorglass at the crater floor: not a descending whistle from above but a lower, slower resonance rising from beneath. [[Brother Orn]] suspects it changes pitch during Kernings. The sound has been getting louder. No other faction has been told.

Contains a small community of Latticed individuals who arrived and found no reason to leave.
