The Diver capitol — a subterranean sprawl spanning the entirety of [[Darkling's Perch]]'s main Islift interior. Not a city in the surface sense: it's a cave network that grew into a civilization, passages linking chambers linking districts linking command nodes. Outsiders who enter without a guide do not return.

[[Ordir'qur]] serves as a heavily militarized satellite, linked to the main mass through means the Divers don't publicize.

## The Stalactite Problem

Formations are growing on the underside of the main Islift. If they reach critical mass, the imbalance threatens a launch arc-ward — a catastrophic event that could Staple Darkling's Perch entirely. Whether this is natural geology or Diver engineering with a dangerous miscalculation is unknown.

## Story Hooks

- Navigate Ordir'qurzk to reach the Diver leadership — without a guide
- Determine whether the stalactites are natural or deliberate
