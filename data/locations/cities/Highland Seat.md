Location: [[Solmara]] | Realm: [[The Ardvellyn Reach]]

The mountain capital of [[The Ardvellyn Reach]], high on the east slopes of the [[Ardenwall]] — dot (8) on the political crop. Old ground, defensible, cold, and pointedly *not* the coast. Here sits the crown, and the Ridge-Council that grants the crown its authority.

## The Seat and the Council

The crown is held by one house, but it holds it at the pleasure of the Ridge-Council — the mining clans and wool-holdings that each control a pass, a vein, or a valley and send one voice to the Seat, weighted by what they can withhold. The crown speaks for the Reach *outward* — treaties, war, the coast-fees, the garrisoning of the passes. It cannot touch anything *inward*: it cannot open a vein, move a clan, or tax a holding on its own ground. Try, and the pass above the capital quietly closes.

## Why it stays on the rock

The money is at [[Outer-Coast Port]], down on the ocean, and everyone here knows it. That is exactly why the Seat won't move there. To rule from the wharf would be to admit the coast is the country — so the crown stays in the mountains and sends down a governor to the water, always someone the Seat can afford to lose.
