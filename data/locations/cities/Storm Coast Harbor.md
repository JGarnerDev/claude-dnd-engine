Location: [[Solmara]] | Coast: [[The Weatherlee]]

The [[The Weatherlee|Weatherlee]]'s one real harbor, north of [[Outer-Coast Port]] on the storm-exposed outer coast of [[Solmara]], screened from the rest of the region by the [[Ardenwall]]. A new marker — dot (29), no ID yet on the political crop. Winter closes it for months. What passes for the seat of a people who would rather not have one.

## What it is

A hard cove town: fishers, timber crews, the captains who own the biggest boats, and the cliff-scavengers who work the [[Stormglass]]. Poor across the board — wealth here is counted in seaworthy hulls, not coin. When the fishing fails and the harbor is iced shut, the town does what the coast has always done: it raids, and it moves things through the back door.

## What it sits on

The cliffs above the harbor are where [[Stormglass]] forms, and the shore below is the one door into the region that pays no strait toll. Both are worth killing for; both are hidden only by how little anyone thinks to look here. The lee-folk tell outsiders the cliffs are haunted — the drowned made brittle — and let them believe it. Nobody sails toward a hard place with a haunted shore and nothing on the map.

Governed, when governed at all, by a **Tide-Word**: the captains gather, bind the coast for one season, and disperse. No seat outlasts the weather that called it.
