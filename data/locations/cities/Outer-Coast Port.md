Location: [[Solmara]] | Overlord: [[The Ardvellyn Reach]]

Ocean-facing port on the outer west coast of [[Solmara]], its wharves looking out past the islands of [[The Myriad]] and the mountains of the [[Ardenwall]] rising at its back. Dot (28) on the political crop. This is Red's one real ocean harbor — and the first place [[Coffee]] touches land coming up the southwest sea lane.

It is *not* [[Calenmere]]'s port. Calenmere's hub sits inside the bay at the river mouth; Outer-Coast Port faces open ocean. Two different harbors, two different waters — worth keeping straight.

## The coffee wharf

Coffee lands here first, off the lane, and re-ships from here to the bay towns that drink most of it. [[The Ardvellyn Reach]] takes a landing-fee, a warehouse-fee, and a re-ship-fee at each stage — which reads as wealth, though the Reach only owns the wharf, never the price. The [[Coffee]] factor-house sits somewhere on this waterfront. Whether it is the Houses' guest or the Houses' garrison is an open question the town has learned not to ask out loud.

## Who runs it

Red rules the Port through a coast-governor, [[Garryn Vale]], sent down from the [[Highland Seat]] — and, as always, chosen from a house the Seat wouldn't grieve to lose. The coast-town majority pays the wharf fees because the wharf is Red's, and rejects everything past it. The governor collects, and knows exactly why he was the one sent to collect it.

*Seed:* this is the natural place for a sea-lane hook to come ashore — a foreign captain off the lane, a ship that docked itself, something washed up that shouldn't have been on the water at all.
