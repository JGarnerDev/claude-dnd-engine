The lower of the two great orbital Slices, torn from the Arc Side when [[Astra]] struck the dwarf planetoid 6.7433 millennia ago. Larger than any Islift. No expedition is recorded as having reached it. Its counterpart at higher orbital position is [[Opticon's Fade]]; together they are the two bodies of [[The Slices]].

## Astra's Sorrow

The Stellar Daughter bleeds water constantly — freshwater from the interior, saltwater from its edges. The runoff saturates the Islifts below. The Lifted call this phenomenon Astra's Sorrow.

## The Witness

A glass lens of unknown origin sits centered on the Slice's surface. It catches the water of Astra's Sorrow and throws miraculous rainbow light downward through the falling droplets, visible from the Islifts below on clear days. Who made it, or why, is unknown.

- What lies at the surface of The Stellar Daughter — and who put The Witness there?
