The far north's only claim to significance. Rumored to be the last place the gods touched before sealing the world from the ethereal realms. The Ostium — a structure of ice, constantly being extended — receives pilgrims from every religion. The rule is simple: carve something. Some leave murals. Some carve complete works of literature. Some add a single line to an existing passage. Chambers run deep enough that some carvings haven't been seen in centuries. There are rumors of incantations buried in the walls — placed deliberately, waiting for someone to read them aloud in the dark.
