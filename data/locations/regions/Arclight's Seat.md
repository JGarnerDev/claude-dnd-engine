Low-altitude Islift cluster and the seat of [[The Climbers]]' power. Lush and heavily cultivated — sustained by [[Aztalaina]]'s extraordinary horticultural output. The air here is clean, the population dense, and the trade volume between Landborne and Lifted flows through this cluster more than any other.

[[Aztalainira]], the floating metropolis at the heart of the cluster, is where the Opticon convenes, where the Vitrian calendar engine ticks, and where the [[Cathedral ad Astra]] stands.

## Ord

A covert [[The Divers|Diver]] observation post operates somewhere within the cluster. Its position is uncertain — likely moved frequently on an untraceable basis. Its purpose: monitoring Landborne-Lifted trade flows to facilitate interception.

## Story Hooks

- Locate and expose [[Ord]] before the Divers intercept a critical shipment
- The Opticon is called — what Showering formed a new Islift, and what are its implications?
