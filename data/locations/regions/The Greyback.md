Cold, forested sub-continent on the world's left edge, below the main continent's western arm. Grey stone, grey sky, grey water — the color drains from things here. Dense taiga forest covers most of the interior, broken by mountain ridges.

[[The Stormwall]] runs along the northern edge. [[The Greymaw]] is the primary sea approach — cold, dangerous, and aptly named.

Part of Miguel's naming cluster: the **grey** group — cold, overcast, northern.
