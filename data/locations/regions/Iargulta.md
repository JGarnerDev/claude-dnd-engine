Remote and mountainous southwestern island cluster. The soil was seeded by volcanic ash from the Astra impact millennia ago — fertile in unexpected ways. The most notable product is the lemon: exceptional quality, tied to the mineral-rich volcanic earth. Nothing about Iargulta invites visitors; everything about it sustains those who stay.
