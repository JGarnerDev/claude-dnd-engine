Elevated, mountain-backed, claw-shaped. The dominant cultural identity is industrial and mercantile — dwarven craft, sky pirate operations, competing trade guilds, and mage towers coexist in uneasy productive tension. The [[Watchwall Mountains]] back it and serve as mines, launch points, and guild territory simultaneously.
