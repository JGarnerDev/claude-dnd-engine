The lower-right landmass. Kethara's interior is desert — relentless heat and sand broken only by the [[The Scorched Rim]] to the south, which forms a hard wall where the continent ends and the ocean takes over.

Warm and exotic. The heat here is not merely uncomfortable — it reshapes everything. Architecture, trade, culture, survival. Sun-bleached ruins and spice routes suggest a civilization that learned to live with the desert rather than fight it.
