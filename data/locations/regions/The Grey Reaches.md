Mid-altitude Islift cluster strung between [[Arclight's Seat]] below and [[Darkling's Perch]] above. Neither [[The Climbers]] nor [[The Divers]] fully control it — the heterogeneous population here enforces fair play simply because neither side trusts the other enough to let one dominate.

Trade happens here in precarious swap meets. Parlays between Climbers and Divers happen here too — specifically on [[Aztala]], declared neutral ground. [[Ordir'q]] is the Divers' main piracy staging point; [[Aztal]] is the Climbers' defensive countermeasure, armed with a mirror-lens array capable of pinpoint luminous strikes.

[[Ordi]], locked to [[Aztala]]'s underside, operates as a Temple to Abandon and Diver propaganda engine. The Arclight Council is not aware of how effectively its views are being shaped from below.

## Story Hooks

- A parlay at [[Aztala]] is sabotaged — which side is responsible?
- [[Aztal]]'s lens array fires on a Landborne ship — accident or provocation?
