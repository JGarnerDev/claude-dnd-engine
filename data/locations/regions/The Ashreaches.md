The only terrain in the world where [[Coffee]] (ashwake) grows. The volcanic ash soil of the Ashreaches is the missing ingredient — no other terrain takes. [[House Gilgane]] owns and builds the terraces and parcels out the grower-settlements to bonded labor.

Located near [[Cindermouth Bay]] on [[Watler's Way]], the volcanic island chain of the far west, separated from the Struck Side coast by [[Tempered Parting]]. [[The Cinder Plain]]'s volcanic geography extends into this region.

The Ashreaches were taken by massacre generations ago. The original growers were wiped out and replaced with bonded labor. [[House Tomsen]]'s ledger still quietly records it.
