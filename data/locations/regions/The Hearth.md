The lowest of the right-side Islifts and the closest named Islift to the surface. [[The Landing]] sits directly above it. The Hearth is the natural threshold between the ground world and the Lifted's aerial society on the eastern side of the map — the warmest, most accessible point of contact.

Beyond its position and altitude, details are unknown.
