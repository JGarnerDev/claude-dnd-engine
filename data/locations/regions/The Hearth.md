The center islift cluster, mid-altitude — the warmest and most central of the Lifted communities. [[The Hearth]] functions as the political hub for [[The Lifted]]: the fulcrum of sky governance, the place where disputes are brought and factions negotiate.

Its warmth is meaningful at altitude. That one feature draws people and keeps them.

Paired with [[The Landing]] — which handles the world below, [[The Hearth]] handles the world within the sky.
