Masamune is a dwarf planetoid defined by [[Whatwill]], a meteor-origin glass produced by the ancient collision of an astral body called the Astra with the planet. The collision gouged the Caldera into the Struck Side, dislodged landmasses into the Arc Side, and filled orbital paths with invisible glass shards that fall during unpredictable Showerings.

The current era is called the Nearing by the Verdicant order: [[Paleloft]] and other Islifts are losing altitude, old craters are answering Kernings, and shared dreams spread among populations with Whatwill exposure.

Settlements: [[Kernholt]], [[Paleloft]], [[Wattling Cross]], [[Cradlemouth]], [[Mirrorfield Station]].
