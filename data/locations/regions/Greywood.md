Eastern island-continent. Greywood is almost entirely forested — the tree cover runs from the shoreline to the peaks of [[The Elderspine]], which forms the continent's backbone. There is almost no open land.

The kind of place where the trees are older than the gods, and the shadows between them are older still. Mountain ranges run the full length of the continent, making interior travel difficult. Everything lives in the forest or doesn't live here.
