The lower main landmass. Caldrath's interior is desert-heavy — relentless heat and sand broken only by the tallest mountain range on the map, which flanks its borders and creates violent weather where the cold altitude meets the basin heat.

Hot, rugged, and difficult. The land resists settlement, and those who make it work here are shaped accordingly.

Part of [[Miguel]]'s naming cluster: the **cinder/amber** group — volcanic ash, desert silt, heat haze. Tied geographically to [[The Cinder Plain]] and [[Cindermouth Bay]].
