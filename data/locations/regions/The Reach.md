Upper-right sub-continent. The Reach faces west across the water toward the main continent — visible on a clear day, a long sail in any weather. [[The Greymast]] runs along its eastern edge, a wall between the sub-continent and the open ocean beyond.

The kind of place that has always watched from the edge. Whatever happens in the main continent arrives here last and leaves first.
