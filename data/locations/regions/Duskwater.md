Controlled by [[House Marlow]] and ruled from here by [[Lady Sorcha Marlow]]. The
neighboring purple fief to [[Stonemarch]] (Cael's), sitting to its southeast, nearer the
red/purple border-river — on the **Calenmere political map**, the purple southern edge.
Its stretch of the border-river is both its wealth and its weapon: the overland routes
its raiders take run up from here, crossing the tributary at the Ashford ford.

Duskwater is the secure aggressor's seat, not a bled frontier. Under the new, callous
generation of [[House Marlow]] it quietly courts **red** — the off-screen external enemy
its grandfathers once died stopping — and gives deniable backing to the land bandits
raiding [[Ashford]], run through a cutout so the house can disavow them. This is the near
side of "the shield that turned."
