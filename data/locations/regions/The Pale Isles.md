Cold, scattered islands sitting between the main landmass and [[The Pale Reach]]. Pale stone, pale water — halfway to the ice and not much warmer. Sailors feel the cold before they see them.

Nominally a stepping-stone approach to [[The Pale Reach]], though few make that journey intentionally. No known controlling power. Part of Miguel's **pale** cluster.
