The sub-continent between the two main landmasses. [[The Narrows]] is neither here nor there — squeezed between greater powers, with desert pressing in from the east. It has changed hands too many times to count and belongs to no one for long.

Transitional terrain: scrubland giving way to desert in the east, more temperate toward the western coast. Every power in the region has a historical claim. None hold it securely.
