A populated orbital Slice that sits in a dense band of the [[Whatwill]] shard field. Showerings are frequent but yield almost entirely Dust — no Lodes, few Pearls. [[The Vitricants]] never came. [[The Partenariats]] assessed it once and left. What filled the vacuum:  [[The Mintners]], who built a dose-economy around the endless cheap Dust supply.

The entire island is threaded with Lensing Zones — alien vegetation, carnivorous growth, phantasmic entities at every district margin. The inhabitants did not learn to avoid the Zones. They mapped them, named the entities, and built their theology around them. Dust, they say, lets you hear what the entities are saying.

The name is colloquial — borrowed Vitrian calendar language applied to a place that technically predates any Opticon. Nobody on Opticon's Fade cares about calendar accuracy.
