Scattered islands in the western ocean, below [[Tempered Parting]] which separates them from the Struck Side's volcanic western coast. Frontier territory — no known controlling power. Close enough to [[Cindermouth Bay]] and [[The Ashreaches]] to matter strategically; far enough that nobody has formally claimed them.

Whatwill Showerings strike here more than anywhere — Lensing Zones form without warning, and Watlers and Dust Scrapers work the shores after every fall. The name marks the danger as much as the place.
