Upper-left islift. The Watcher drifts higher than most surface islifts — the air here is thin and cold, and on clear nights [[The Slices]] dominate the sky directly overhead. Named for its orientation: it always seems to be looking up.

Nearest of the named islifts to Slice territory. Whether that makes it sacred, dangerous, or merely inconvenient depends on who you ask.

Named via questionnaire; not yet detailed. Awaits a `/region` pass on the sky layer.
