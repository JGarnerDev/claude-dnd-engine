The large orbital island masses flung into orbit when [[Astra]] struck the dwarf planetoid 6.7433 millennia ago. The impact dislodged many islands from the Arc Side — the larger ones became the Slices, casting themselves into distant orbits; the smaller Islifts wander at lower altitudes and are inhabited by [[The Lifted]].

The Slices are higher, larger, and entirely frontier territory. No known permanent inhabitants. Whether they have been reached at all is unclear.

Showerings — when the planet passes through the orbital shard band — may also strike the Slices, with unknown consequences. Whether a Slice can be Stapled (brought back to ground) by a Showering is unknown.
