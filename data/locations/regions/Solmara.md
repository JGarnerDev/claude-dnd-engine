Warm, forested sub-continent on the world's left edge, below the main continent's western arm. Dense oak and pine cover the hills, breaking where river valleys cut down to the sea. Warm stone, good farmland in the inland plains, productive coast.

[[Ardenwall]] runs along the northern edge — catching the afternoon sun before it fades. [[Calenmere]] is the primary sea approach, where the rivers converge at the bay mouth into a busy trading port. About eight settlements scattered through the interior and coast; the bay-mouth hub is the largest.

Economy: timber, fishing, coastal trade, some agriculture in the inland river plains. Part of Miguel's revised naming cluster — warm coast, Mediterranean character.
