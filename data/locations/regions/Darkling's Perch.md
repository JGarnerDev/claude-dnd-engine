The high-altitude Islift cluster and seat of [[The Divers]]' power. Massive cave structures run the entirety of the underside — nearly impassable to outsiders at night without running into ambush. [[The Divers]] hollowed out the interior to build [[Ordir'qurzk]], their sprawling subterranean capitol.

[[The Climbers]] hold one surviving outpost here: [[Az]], sparsely populated, heavily mobile, defended by net throwers. A portal to [[Opticon's Rest]] is rumoured to exist somewhere within it.

The stalactites forming on the underside of the main mass are growing. If they reach sufficient mass, they threaten a deadly launch arc-ward — a catastrophic Stapling or worse.

## Story Hooks

- The stalactites are accelerating — is it natural formation or Diver engineering?
- Find the rumoured portal inside [[Az]] before the Divers discover it
