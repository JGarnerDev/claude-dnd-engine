The crossroads between the two great continents. Whoever controls these islands controls the trade routes. In practice nobody fully controls them — pirates dominate, merchant kings operate behind them, and a navigation wizard guild charts the currents and sells the information to whoever pays. The law that exists is the law you enforce yourself.
