Islands scattered below the main continent's southwestern reach. Warm by comparison — which matters more than it sounds given what surrounds them. Currents bring drifting things here and take them away again. Nobody claims them.

Distinct from [[The Pale Isles]] in character — temperate where those are frigid — but equally uncontrolled.
