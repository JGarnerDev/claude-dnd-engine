The southernmost landmass. [[The Pale Reach]] is permanently glaciated — tundra on the margins, ice peaks throughout, nothing habitable at its core. It reaches south until the map runs out.

[[The Southern Throat]] is the only viable sea approach from the north. [[The Pale Isles]] break the water between The Pale Reach and the main landmass. The dominant mountain range here is [[The Pale Spire]].

Part of Miguel's naming cluster: the **pale** group — pale stone, pale water, bone-white cold.
