Location: [[Cinto]] | Proprietor: [[Cal Mooney]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Cinto's inn and the warm opposite of [[The Matron's Inn]] under the ice. [[Cal Mooney]] gives free soup to pilgrims who can't pay. Captain [[Hober Mallow]]'s sailors sing "The River Daughter" here (see [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]]), and the whole room joins in on the chorus.

In the "All of Cinto" ending, the party gets free drinks here for life.

## Inside

Two storeys of black timber leaning on the warm side of the harbor wall. The front door sticks; Cal fixes it every winter and it sticks again.

- **Common room:** one long room around a stone hearth big enough to stand in, with the stew pot hanging over it all day. Long benches, a few small tables in the corners for people with secrets to trade. *Who's here:* pilgrims thawing out, sailors from [[Hober Mallow|Captain Mallow]]'s crew, and Cal behind the bar. *Poke at:* the stool by the fire with the sign "RESERVED FOR THE VIOL PLAYER." *Hidden:* the old coin and Gossip secret behind a loose brick in the hearth (for [[Odibli]], who looks up chimneys).
- **The Soup Corner:** a bench by the kitchen door where anyone who can't pay eats free. Cal never asks, and nobody else sits there.
- **Kitchen:** copper pots, a bread oven, and a back door to the alley. Cal's cook is a nervous halfling who has never once looked up from the onions.
- **Upstairs rooms:** eight small rooms off one creaking corridor, each with a bed, a washbasin and a window that frosts on the inside. The best room has a hot-water pipe from the hearth running under the floor. Cal gives it to whoever looks coldest.
- **The cellar:** barrels, root vegetables, and a shelf of letters from [[The Matron|the Matron]] going back years, tied in ribbon. If the party is kind to Cal, he shows them the last one: it's months old.
