## Description

Two-storey corner building, narrow frontage but wider inside than it looks. Warm sandstone and dark timber, worn but well-kept. Iron sign with a seated lioness. Ivy on the upper floor. Lanterns at the door, always lit. Smell of hops hits before you see it.

Inside: long bar, low ceiling, mismatched chairs — comfortable in an accumulated way, not designed. Central hearth always burning; everything slightly warm. Music stage in one corner, nothing fancy. Walls covered in years of posted notices, sketches, a few mounted antlers. [[Bree Tealeaf]] knows what every one of them means.

## Staff

**[[Sable Wren]]** — tiefling warlock, proprietor. Got the building by doing a favor: hired to exorcise something, made friends with it instead. Patron: ancient hearth spirit (unnamed), tied to fire, shelter, and gathering places. The patron nudged her to this location; she stopped asking why.

**[[Lok Thuliaga]]** — goliath, security and heavy work. Left something behind before settling here. Doesn't talk about it. The way he watches a room isn't just bouncer instinct.

**[[Bree Tealeaf]]** — halfling bartender. Knows people she shouldn't, in cities she's never mentioned visiting. The chatty act is real, and also useful.

## Regular Performer

**[[Oswin]]** — old man, plays regularly, looks like nothing special. Genuine folksy charm. Can make the room dance on special occasions.

## Brews

- **Lioness's Pride** — golden blonde, flagship. Crisp, long finish.
- **The Standing** — English bitter, house pour. Name implies permanence.
- **Pale Crossing** — light ale, the entry point.
- **Embertide** — amber ale, warm and reliable.
- **Ironhold** — dark stout. [[Lok Thuliaga]]'s drink.
- **The Mistake** — fruit ale. Sable tried something, it became the bestseller. She kept the name.

## Kitchen

Specialties: pot pies, rotisserie, stonecap mushrooms (dense, earthy, sourced from somewhere specific). Rest of the menu varies — kitchen cooks what they feel like.

## Regulars

Working locals, occasional families, small tradespeople — not wealthy. Also draws academic and bureaucratic mages. Good live music. Staff enforce civility; fights happen anyway.

## Origin

[[Sable Wren]] was hired to exorcise something from the building. She made friends with it instead and received the building as payment.

## Story Hook

The Lioness is the safest place in the city — something about Sable's arrangement with the building makes it genuinely protected. The party may not realize this until something bad can't follow them through the door. Also: whatever [[Lok Thuliaga]] and [[Bree Tealeaf]] left behind will find them eventually.
