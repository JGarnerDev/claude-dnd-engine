Location: [[The Buckle]] | Proprietor: [[Pip Post]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Run by the brothers Post, [[Pip Post]] and [[Barnaby Post]], with [[Stamp]] the puffin. Their motto: "Nothing stops the letters from getting through." They hold [[Dip's Letter]], which can never be delivered. The pile of letters to "the Claus" at the core came from here.

Gift: the Return-Receipt Parcel (see [[Earning the Gifts]]).

The Buckle's branch of [[The Post]], the Blackmarket guildsect that delivers anything anywhere; the post office is its honest front.

## Inside

Carved into the ice wall beside the ship to look like a little stone building, with a painted sign and a lamp always lit.

- **The front counter:** a wooden counter with brass scales, a stamp rack and a bell. *Who's here:* Pip behind the counter, whistling, and [[Stamp]] glaring from the satchel on its hook.
- **The sorting room:** pigeonholes floor to ceiling, one for every pilgrim station in [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]]. *Poke at:* the sack of letters for "the Lightning One" and the fan mail for "the Viol Player." *Hidden:* a pigeonhole labelled "UNDELIVERABLE" with [[Dip's Letter|Dip's letter]] in it, and a growing pile labelled "THE CLAUS." Pip doesn't know where to send those, so he sends them down, and they always go.
- **Barnaby's corner:** an armchair, a blanket and the dead courier's coat on a peg. Barnaby is usually in the armchair.
