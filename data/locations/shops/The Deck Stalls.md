Location: [[The Buckle]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Four stalls between the Thunder Towers. Each keeper has their own file; [[Sister Kirsty]] is listed as proprietor only because the schema wants one name.

**The food stalls** all celebrate a winter festival of light, which fits the light-and-shadow finale. These faiths are small in this world.

- **Christian.** Food: Gingerbread and mulled cider. Character: [[Sister Kirsty]], who leads carols around the mast. Hook: Gives travelers a candle "for the dark below".
- **Jewish.** Food: Latkes and sufganiyot. Character: [[Joseph Schwartz|Joseph]] and [[Bella Schwartz]], an old couple with a menorah lit on the counter. Hook: Their lamp oil never runs out down here, and they wonder why.
- **Hindu.** Food: Ladoo, jalebi and chai. Character: [[Hari]], who lines her stall with diyas and draws rangoli in colored ice dust. Hook: Tells the story of light winning over darkness.
- **Silly.** Food: "Shadow Snacks". Character: [[Prophet Boone]] of [[The Order of the Burrow|the Order of the Burrow]], in a burlap groundhog hood. Hook: Worships a great burrowing beast and predicts "six more weeks." He's right.

**Prophet Boone's pamphlet** (pressed into every hand on deck):

> THE ORDER OF THE BURROW WELCOMES YOU!
>
> Have you seen your shadow today? The Great Burrower has. He sees it every day. That is why it is still winter.
>
> SIX MORE WEEKS.
>
> *Hoods available at the stall. Shadow Snacks, 2 cp.*

## Inside

Four stalls between the Thunder Towers, under strings of lanterns.

- **[[Sister Kirsty]]'s stall:** gingerbread and mulled cider, with a hand-painted sign and a stack of candles "for the dark below." Carols start here and go round the mast.
- **The Schwartzes' stall:** latkes and sufganiyot, with a menorah lit on the counter and a lamp whose oil never runs out. Joseph and Bella argue about why, cheerfully, all day.
- **[[Hari]]'s stall:** ladoo, jalebi and chai, lined with diyas, with rangoli drawn on the iron deck in coloured ice dust. *Poke at:* the half-finished rangoli she'd like help with.
- **[[Prophet Boone]]'s stall:** "Shadow Snacks" under a burlap awning shaped like a groundhog's head, with hoods hanging from the front and pamphlets on every surface.
