A covert [[The Divers|Diver]] observation post embedded inside [[The Climbers]]' home cluster of [[Arclight's Seat]]. Its exact location is deliberately uncertain — moved frequently by a small Diver cell using routes unknown to Climber patrols. What it does: watch trade flows between Landborne peoples and the Lifted, transmit intelligence to [[The Divers]]' raiders, and facilitate interception.

The Climbers do not know it exists.

## Story Hooks

- Track Ord down before the Divers intercept a shipment the party cares about
- Capture the Diver cell operating Ord — how far back does the surveillance go?
