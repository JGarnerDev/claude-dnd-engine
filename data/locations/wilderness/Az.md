The last [[The Climbers|Climber]] outpost inside [[The Divers]]' home cluster of [[Darkling's Perch]]. Sparsely populated, heavily mobile — the residents here don't stay in one place long enough for the Divers to pin them down. Net throwers form their primary defense.

A portal to [[Opticon's Rest]] is rumoured to exist somewhere within Az. If true, it represents an escape route, a supply line, or a strategic asset of enormous value — which explains why Az is still defended despite its isolation.

## Story Hooks

- Reach Az and locate the rumoured portal before the Divers do
- Reinforce Az — it's the Climbers' only intelligence window into Diver territory
