A [[The Climbers|Climber]] defensive waystation in [[The Grey Reaches]], positioned to deter Diver aggression. Its defining feature is a mirror and lens array — a weapon capable of focusing and redirecting light into pinpoint luminous strikes. Not a siege weapon in the conventional sense; more a precision deterrent.

The technology mirrors, in miniature, what [[Aztalaina]] does with growing conditions — leveraging altitude and aerial optics for an effect unreplicable on the surface.

## Story Hooks

- The lens array has been sabotaged or redirected — who did it, and what's the real target?
