A floating Diver bastion in [[The Grey Reaches]] — the primary staging point for piratical operations against Landborne-Lifted trade. Ships and goods intercepted across the cluster's altitude band trace back here.

The Climbers know Ordir'q exists; eliminating it has proven difficult. Its position in the middle-altitude contested zone means any military action risks triggering broader conflict in [[The Grey Reaches]]' fragile neutral order.

## Story Hooks

- Disable Ordir'q's raiding capacity without igniting open war in The Grey Reaches
