A heavily militarized satellite Islift in [[Darkling's Perch]], linked to the Diver capitol [[Ordir'qurzk]] through means [[The Divers]] haven't made public. It functions as the outer military shell of the Diver political center — the thing you have to get through before you can reach the city inside.

The linking mechanism is notable. Physically, the two masses shouldn't be so tightly coordinated. Whether it's Whatwill-based propulsion, a staple of some kind, or something stranger is unknown.

## Story Hooks

- Understand the link between Ordir'qur and Ordir'qurzk — it's the key to bypassing the defenses
