Region: [[Deorum Ostium]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

> **What happens here:** the mini-boss fight. At [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]]'s exit, [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] guards the ice bridge into [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. Beating it, ideally without wrecking the bridge, reopens [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] and resolves every opening hook.

**Read aloud:** *Warm air hits you first, then the smell of woodsmoke, impossible this deep in the ice. The tunnel opens onto a chasm spanned by a single bridge of glassy ice. Beside it, crackling happily, sits a log the size of a wagon, wreathed in flame and wearing what might be a festive ribbon. It pops at you. Somehow, you know it was an insult.*

[[The Yule Log]] lives at [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]]'s exit, burning beside an ice bridge that spans a chasm into [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. It keeps itself going by feeding on carvings and offerings left by lost pilgrims.

- **Fire next to ice:** each time it uses Yule Roll on the bridge, the bridge cracks. After three cracks, a section collapses, so the party must choose between fighting on the bridge or drawing it away.
- **Encounter:** [[The Yule Log|the Log]] and 2 Twig Blights with 4 players (2,950 XP, about Moderate). With 5 players, add 4 Magmins (3,350 XP).
- **Extras:** Marshmallow Moment lets players toast snacks for temporary HP, and Crackling Commentary means everyone understands its rude pops and hisses.

Full stats are in the Holiday Boss Stat Blocks files.
