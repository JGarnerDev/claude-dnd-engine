A paradisiacal horticultural Islift — intensively cultivated, lush even by aerial standards, and capable of producing enough food and medicinal plants to sustain millions. It is the agricultural engine behind [[Arclight's Seat]]'s population density and the reason [[Aztalainira]] can exist at the scale it does.

The growing conditions here are unlike anything surface-bound agriculture can replicate — the combination of altitude, aerial moisture from [[The Stellar Daughter]]'s Astra's Sorrow runoff, and centuries of cultivation have produced plant varieties found nowhere else.

## Story Hooks

- Harvest a medicinal plant available only at altitude — Aztalaina is the source
- Something is threatening the harvest; the Climbers need it contained quietly
