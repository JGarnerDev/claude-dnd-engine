Neutral territory in [[The Grey Reaches]]. All official parlays between [[The Climbers]] and [[The Divers]] happen here. Both sides declared it off-limits for military action, and the heterogeneous population of the cluster enforces that rule by mutual interest.

What the Climbers don't know: [[Ordi]] is physically locked to the underside of Aztala. The Temple to Abandon operates beneath the very ground where the Arclight Council negotiates. Diver propaganda filters upward through the Islift's structure.

## Story Hooks

- A negotiation is scheduled — expose Ordi before the Climbers' position is shaped by it
