An Islift physically attached to the underside of the neutral parlay ground [[Aztala]]. The Climbers know Aztala. They do not know Ordi.

It operates as a Temple to Abandon — a Diver religious and propaganda institution that transmits influence upward through the Islift's structure into every negotiation held on Aztala above. The Arclight Council believes its worldview is its own.

## What Ordi Does

- Cultivates ideological drift in the Climbers' negotiating representatives
- Provides a religious framing ([[The Divers]]' "Abandon" theology) that makes Climber concessions feel spiritually correct
- Operates continuously — not just during parlays

## Story Hooks

- Expose Ordi and watch the Arclight Council confront how much of its recent position was manufactured
- Destroy Ordi — but doing so at [[Aztala]] will collapse the neutral zone
