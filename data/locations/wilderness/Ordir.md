A haven for the underbelly of [[The Lifted]]'s society. Gamblers, cheats, and criminals operate openly in [[The Grey Reaches]]' middle-altitude neutral zone — neither [[The Climbers]] nor [[The Divers]] police it seriously, because both extract value from what flows through it.

[[The Divers]] draw recruits and intelligence from Ordir. The Climbers use it when they need deniability.

## Story Hooks

- Someone the party needs is hiding on Ordir — extraction won't be clean
- A gambling debt from Ordir is being called in at the worst possible time
