The Sesh'kar are the lizardfolk of the deep — the reclusive people of the Marrow's inmost river-forest and swamp, and the deep-forest power of **[[The Marrow Concord]]**. Their endonym is their own, sibilant and untranslated; they keep to the depths and the depths keep them, which is why the growing world of the coast barely knows they are a power at all.

What makes them matter is seniority. The Sesh'kar are **[[Orrowan]]**'s *first* people. They knew the sleeping god and named it before the elves ever walked the wood — they call it **Orro'ssha**, the elder first-tongue word that the smoother elvish *Orrowan* was worn down from. So while **[[The Orrow]]** front the god's worship and drive the project of the waking, it is the Sesh'kar who hold the older understanding of what the tree is — and, quite possibly, the piece of the waking-lore the elves have never actually possessed. Their guardianship runs quietly through **[[The Circle of Orro'ssha]]**: behind the Circle's elven face sits a core of Sesh'kar elder-keepers who hold the founding rites the Orrow only half-grasp.

Between the two forest peoples lies the Concord's oldest, gentlest fault. There is no war in it, only a question neither speaks aloud: *whose god is Orrowan?* The elves believe they are the wakers; the Sesh'kar know they are the ones who could actually wake it. A shared god, split by seniority — and a reckoning the whole Concord has managed, so far, not to have.
