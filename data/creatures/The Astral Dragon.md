One of two impressions [[Luci]] kept from the drift: **an Astral Dragon**, a draconic being of the Aether rivaled only by its cousins, the Time Dragons. Astral Dragons are keepers of balance, cold, calculated, and punishingly wise. Their neutrality seems unimpassioned because they forever bear witness to the overreaches of primordial forces in their domain, coiled to strike.

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Paul's backstory answers (ingested 2026-10-25). Whether this is one dragon or a kind, and whether it noticed Luci, is open. Stat block is the DM's.

- It arrives to weigh Luci, or what she means to do.
- Freeing [[Aedra]] is exactly the kind of overreach it exists to answer.
