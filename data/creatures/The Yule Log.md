Location: [[The Ice Bridge]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

A log the size of a wagon, wreathed in flame and wearing what might be a festive ribbon. It lives at the maze's exit beside [[The Ice Bridge]], feeding on carvings and offerings left by lost pilgrims, and its presence is why nothing has come out of [[The Buckle]] in weeks.

- **Encounter:** the Log and 2 Twig Blights with 4 players (2,950 XP, about Moderate). With 5 players, add 4 Magmins (3,350 XP).
- **The bridge:** each Yule Roll on the bridge cracks it; after three cracks a section collapses.
- **The last ember:** finding it is a DC 13 WIS (Perception) check. Leaving it burning is a crack toward the worst ending.
- Play notes and rolls: [[The Log at the Bridge]].

### Stat block

Full stat block files: `Desktop\DnD Bosses\Holiday Boss Stat Blocks.docx` and `.xlsx`.

- **Mini-boss, Huge Plant, CR 7 (XP 2,900)**
- **Armor Class** 15 · **Hit Points** 130 · **Speed** 30 ft. · **Proficiency Bonus** +3
- **STR** 20 (+5) · **DEX** 8 (-1) · **CON** 18 (+4) · **INT** 6 (-2) · **WIS** 12 (+1) · **CHA** 10 (+0)
- **Defenses** Immune to Fire damage

#### Traits

- ***Hearth Aura.*** Creatures that start their turn within 10 ft. take 5 (2d4) Fire damage.
- ***Extinguishable.*** Any Cold damage or a bucket's worth of water stops Hearth Aura until the end of its next turn.
- ***Marshmallow Moment (Each creature, once per fight).*** A creature within Hearth Aura can use a Bonus Action to toast a snack, gaining 1d6 + 2 Temporary Hit Points.
- ***Crackling Commentary.*** It can only communicate by popping and hissing, but everyone somehow understands that it's being rude.

#### Actions

- ***Multiattack.*** Makes two Burning Branch attacks.
- ***Burning Branch.*** +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning + 3 (1d6) Fire. Melee attack against one target.
- ***Yule Roll (Recharge 5-6).*** DC 15 DEX save. Damage: 23 (4d8 + 5) Bludgeoning + 7 (2d6) Fire. Rolls up to 30 ft. in a line, passing through creatures. On a failed save, a creature takes the damage and is knocked Prone. On a success, it takes half damage and isn't knocked Prone.

#### Special

- ***Burning Down (When first Bloodied).*** It splits open and Hearth Aura increases to 3d4. Two Twig Blights crawl out wearing tiny scarves, and it immediately recharges Yule Roll.

#### Minions

2 Twig Blights appear through Burning Down. With 5 players, also add 4 Magmins as flying embers.
