Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

One of two impressions [[Luci]] kept from the drift: **a Rider of the Black Sun**, a gravitic gravekeeper raiding the realms of light to snuff them out. Its name is **Farzrig**.

> **Reliability:** The Rider is from Paul's backstory answers (ingested 2026-10-25). Farzrig's name, titles and nature are Paul's later write-up (2026-11, quoted below). Its master [[The Nul|Nul]] and its role at Threnai are Jeff and Paul's shared late-game decision (2026-11-27). Pool entity: stat block, numbers and timing are open.

## Paul's Write-up

> Farzrig, Huzzar of the Void, Herald of Nul
>
> This primordial entity of consumption, negation and ravenous hunger has been distorted, manifesting as a riding conflagration; in a state of émission, remission and absorption of the énergies surrounding it. It crosses the Astral plane effortlessly, expediently, pulling in the almost-matter of that plane and nullifying it utterly. It has lost its meaning, originating as a balancing force against the potential for spontaneous creation inherent to the Astral Realm. Repurposed by [Jeff BBEG], Farzrig occupies the post of Herald of Nul; an agent of their design for the absolution of creation by its negation. They seek the end of a cycle of what they designate "a mockery of entropy," a clinging to a life within an afterlife; the gods themselves existing in an after-afterlife and pulling to their bosom that which should end.

Jeff's BBEG is [[The Nul|Nul]].

## The Rider and the Steed

Farzrig rides. Its steed is the **Void Serpent**, the immense, translucent creature that crashed into [[The Sun Temple]] and scattered Luci into the Astral Sea. Luci saw them separately while she drifted and never put them together. The serpent is the part that strikes. The rider is the part that eats.

> **DM-only:** The Void Serpent was hunting the temple's door, not Luci. She was in the way.

## Threnai

> **DM-only:** [[Sah]] called Farzrig to Threnai. The sealed order [[Iggy]] carried was the summons: the plan for the Failed Intervention, to set an old balancing power on the first Returned, a dead thing that refused to stay ended. Sah did not know Farzrig had already bent. It came, ate the Returned, and kept eating. It was what came up the road behind Iggy at Caelion Marr's bridge, and the blank road on his courier run home was the first touch of it, the world going unfinished where it had fed. The Correctors' ritual broke Threnai's spine. Farzrig ate what was left.
>
> Sah is guilty, not villainous. He reached for the wrong tool in a war he was losing, and the old woman who burned the order knew it might go wrong: *"If this goes wrong, someone should know we chose it."*

## Why It Wants Luci and Iggy

> **DM-only:** Two reasons, both true. They offend it: each is a leftover that refused to end, Iggy the last of a plane it finished and Luci a mote of light that slipped its grip. And what they carry could undo Nul's work: Iggy's unfinished song still holds power Sah kept alive past Threnai's end, and Luci is bound to [[Aedra]], the creation Farzrig was made to balance and now hunts.

## Fida

> **DM-only:** The door to [[The Sun Temple]] is Fida, Aethryn's broken moon. The Astra impact broke it, but the shards that still drift off it are Farzrig feeding on the door from the far side. Each shard that falls away is the door getting thinner.
