Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

*Zahareena* (said zah-hah-REE-nah) comes from the Basque word zaharrena, "the oldest." Every ancient people who reached the mountain had their own word for the same place, and the party will find all of them. This is the one that stuck.

**What it is:** the Zahareena is the place outside everything. It's older than the worlds and older than the gods: the stillness that all the grinding worlds rest on. Nothing lives there, and nothing ends there. The mountain is where the Zahareena touches this world, like a thumb pressed into clay from the other side. That's why it's older than time, and why it points both up and down.

#### What the party can learn

- The Inner Sun isn't a sun. It's a pinhole into the Zahareena, and the light coming through is the oldest light there is.
- Every relic layer was left by a people trying to reach it. None of them did.
- The fissure [[Groundhog Claus]] darts into at the end leads toward it, and so do the five messages.
- [[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]] has spent his life listening for it beneath the static.

**Traces in each layer:** every layer has one carving of the same symbol, a small circle drawn outside a larger one, with the Zahareena's name in that people's script.

- **Outer shell:** the giants carved it as a door too big even for them, labeled "the Hall Before Halls."
- **Drowned halls:** the murals show a deep beneath the deepest sea, "the Water Before Water." When no one is looking, the painted fish all swim toward it.
- **Glass gardens:** asked about it, the crystal plants hum a single note and show a memory of somewhere perfectly still, "the Light Before Light."
- **Shrine ring:** among the hundreds of altars, one has no name worn away, because it never had one. It's the altar where the gods themselves came to pray, to the Zahareena.
- **The core:** the Inner Sun itself. Anyone who looks straight into it makes a DC 15 CON save or is Blinded for 1 minute. Either way, for a moment they see nothing at all, and it's the most peaceful thing they've ever seen.

**Rolls:** spotting that the symbol repeats is a DC 15 INT (History or Arcana) check, and reveals that every ancient people was chasing the same place. The Zahareena is the deepest layer of the secret of the core (see The final decision).

## Odibli's secret (DM-only)

An optional moment just for [[Odibli]], at the nameless altar in the shrine ring.

- **The sign:** as [[Odibli]] gets close, the ink in his fingertips tingles. He can see writing on the altar that no one else can: fresh, wet ink, in his own handwriting. He has never been here.
- **Reading it:** seeing it takes no roll. Understanding all of it takes a DC 15 INT (Arcana) check, or it comes to him in a dream the next time he rests. No one else can read it, even with Comprehend Languages.
- **What it says:** this one is your call, so it fits [[Odibli]]'s story. Some ideas:
  - His ink was the Zahareena's first: the Oldest wrote the very first word with it, and a drop of it has come down to him.
  - His true name, written here long before he was born.
  - A message from another [[Odibli]], in another world, who got here first and left him a warning, or a kindness.
- **The catch:** this is the one secret in the campaign that can't be sold. If [[Odibli]] tries to sell it, or writes it down for anyone else, the ink fades from the altar and from his memory.
- **The payoff:** at the end, when the ink is pulled from his fingertips onto the imaginary scroll, it rolls up and is carried off to the Zahareena. It's his reply.
