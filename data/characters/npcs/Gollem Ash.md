Gollem Ash has been coming to the treeline posts for sixty years. The factors' fathers dealt with him. He is patient, funny, scrupulously fair on a weight, and the single easiest counterparty in the eastern forest trade — which is why he is the only forest-kin most league men could name, and the only one they would call a friend.

He has never said which band he speaks for.

This is not evasion. Nobody has asked. The post ledgers list him as a broker because a man who brings that many loads must be brokering, and the assumption has been quietly load-bearing for two generations of accounts. Everything the league believes about **[[The Thelven]]** — their numbers, their appetites, what they will and will not sell — traces back through Gollem, because he is the sample.

He answers questions honestly. He simply answers the ones he is asked.

Notable: he is comfortable at the treeline and has never been observed going deeper. Notable also: **[[Vessaine Rook]]** came out of the interior and has not sought him out, and he has noticed that, and has not mentioned it to anyone at the post.

*Kellan's seed. Whether sixty frictionless years are a life or a position is open — and so is what it costs the league if the answer is the second one.*
