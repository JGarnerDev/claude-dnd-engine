Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Race: Dragonborn

Makes candles in [[Cinto]] that work in thin reality, and sells rare [[Shade Candles]] that give off darkness: a tool for dimming the Inner Sun.

- **Opening hook:** the monks always order candles before the festival, but nothing has come this year. Leads to [[The Cupola Temple]].
- **Buying shade candles:** CHA (Persuasion) DC 15, spoken softly, or trade a Rare secret. Raising your voice gives disadvantage.

## How they speak

(The `## Voice` section is added by `/recap` once they've spoken at the table; see `meta/voice.md`.)

- **Core:** Soft and secretive, speaks quietly as if a flame might go out.
- **Catchphrase:** "Every candle burns. Mine choose what for."
