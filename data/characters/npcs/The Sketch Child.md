Location: [[Wattling Cross]] | Guardian: [[Pell]]

An unnamed child in [[Wattling Cross]]. Every morning before sunrise, she draws on whatever surface is available — wall, cloth, ground — and does not remember doing it. The drawings show a consistent multi-level structure, architecturally detailed beyond her age, with an inverted orientation: ground above, building descending.

She has never knowingly handled [[Whatwill]]. No documented exposure history. The nearest lode is seventeen fathoms below the road she walks to the water pump.

[[Mira Dust-Pale]] is trying to determine whether the child is receiving imagery or transmitting it. [[Pell]] watches her and has not decided what her drawings mean for her safety. Three factions have become aware of her. None have moved yet.

The basement drawings are torn out before anyone sees them. Even Pell has not seen them. The sixth version included a door not present in the first five.

Exposure stage: none by any measurable standard. Something is transmitting to her that does not require exposure as a medium.
