Location: [[Cradlemouth]] | Faction: [[Caldera Monks]]

Caldera Monk at [[Cradlemouth]], [[Masamune]]. For six months, [[Brother Orn]] has been hearing a low resonance from beneath the old mirrorglass at the crater floor. It changes pitch during Kernings. Two other monks have independently confirmed it. He has reported to no outside faction.

He holds two-hour pre-dawn vigils at the inner crater wall without a lantern. Has stopped calling this prayer entirely in the last month.

Exposure stage: entering Stage 4 (Latticed). His two-hour vigils are not prayer in the Verdicant sense — he is listening to Stage 5 presence and trying to determine whether it is the planet, the embedded Astra core, or something else.

Corresponds formally with [[Sable Mourne]] through Verdicant-adjacent channels. The correspondence is information-sparse and read by both as a negotiation neither has yet named. He has the observatory data. She has sanctum access and political standing. Neither has made an offer yet.

Met [[Mira Dust-Pale]] once at the Caldera rim. Said one sentence. She has been thinking about it ever since.
