Location: [[The Buckle Post Office]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Firbolg

A former drifter who found a dead courier's coat and bag in the ice and pretended to be a postman for free meals. People believed in him so much that the lie came true. He insists he's a fraud, but comes through at the worst moment. Adopted by [[Pip Post]] as his brother. He owes [[Malingo]] for a coat.

- **Getting him to finish his delivery:** CHA (Persuasion) DC 13. Intimidation works too, but Pip disapproves.
- **Gift:** shares the [[Return-Receipt Parcel]] with Pip.

The dead courier whose coat he found in the ice was a courier of [[The Post]], the Blackmarket guildsect that delivers anything anywhere.
