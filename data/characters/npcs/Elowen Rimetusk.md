Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Half Orc]] | Class: [[Wizard]]

The old mage of **[[Ashford]]** — a half-orc woman, and the only real magic the town has. She
drifts between lucidity and absence. On her good days she is courteous, sharp, and
unmistakably trained; on the others she is somewhere else, kindly, and no help at all.

On the wizard pyramid she belongs to the working tier — guild-trained, the grade that does
construction and military engineering and harvests. What she is doing in a border ford-town
instead of a guild hall is not a question she can reliably answer any more, and it is a real
one: a working wizard is not what a town this size should have.

Her power has outlasted the mind that held it. She is formidable when present, and she stands
beside **[[Mara Tull]]** in the town's defense whenever she is — which is often enough to
matter and never often enough to count on.

Her name is a soft elven given name carried over an orcish battle-name. Half the time she
cannot recall which war the second one is from, and she says so without distress.
