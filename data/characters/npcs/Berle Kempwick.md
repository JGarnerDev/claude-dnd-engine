> **DM-only.** Sourced from Miguel's Citadel knowledge doc (2026-10-25), which replaces the earlier unnamed "Citadel Assistant". Spelled **B-E-R-L-E**; renamed from Elwyn Berlekin because that name blurred with Elspeth. Inspired by Elwyn Berlekamp, a younger Bell Labs researcher influenced by Shannon.

Location: [[The Citadel]]

## The Fuse

Assistant to [[The Original Odibli]]. Loyal to him, but more loyal to [[The Citadel]] and [[Wendrick Schulden]]. He has no real personal connection to the copy and sees it loose in the world as a **security issue**: he wants it destroyed as soon as possible.

He is at a crossroads: betray the Master's trust or the original's. The Citadel's rules say that when a copy runs, the entire Citadel must be told. For now he backs the original. Over time he is likely to go over to the Master. When, and how, is left to the DM.

## What He Has Done

With the original and [[Elspeth Moorlin]], he has tried to shut the copy down remotely, more than once. The shadow [[Odibli]] took in at Session 10 was one of those attempts landing. It failed.
