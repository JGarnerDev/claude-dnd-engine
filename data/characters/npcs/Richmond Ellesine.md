Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Half Elf]]

Blacksmith of **[[Ashford]]**. Long-lived enough to have been at the thing the two houses are
still arguing about, and he put the sword down afterward and picked up a hammer instead.

Warm but weathered. He is friendly and exacting in equal measure, proud of good work, and
quick to warm to anyone who respects the craft — show him you know what you are holding and
you have a friend. Ask him about the war and he goes quiet.

## The witness

He was there. That makes him the one dignified, firsthand source in Ashford for "the shield
that turned": the old alliance between **[[House Cael]]** and **[[House Marlow]]**, the
grandfather who died shielding Marlow's lord, the aegis-banner that was flown marching to
Cael's defense and is now flown against this town.

It costs him to tell it, so it is **earned lore and not a lecture**. The party has to give him
a reason — respect, usually, or a repair job done honestly — and even then he tells it once,
plainly, and does not want to be asked twice.

## The forge

He is the gear-and-repair contact, and he has his own version of the town's problem: the
raiders wrecked his stock, and stock is not easy to replace out here. He is the natural NPC
behind the base-building smithy, and the person who will key a **personalised item** to a PC
who earns his respect — a spotlight beat rather than a shop transaction.
