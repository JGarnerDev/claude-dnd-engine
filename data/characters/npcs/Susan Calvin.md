Location: [[Cinto Infirmary]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Dwarf

Runs [[Cinto Infirmary]], treating frostbite, exhaustion and the odder ailments pilgrims bring back from the deep ice. Her patients from the deep ice talk in their sleep in languages no one knows. She suspects the veil is thinning.

- **Opening hook:** she's out of a rare frost-moss salve that only grows near [[The Buckle]]. Leads to [[The Ice Bridge]] and the Buckle.
- **Helping out:** WIS (Medicine) DC 12 earns a Potion of Healing.
