Location: [[The Matron's Inn]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Goliath

Runs [[The Matron's Inn]]. Nobody knows her real name, and anything left in her care below the ice stays safe. Frost-gray skin. Honesty, paid debts and respect for the ship win her favor; bargaining and rudeness lose it, and losing it is a crack toward the worst ending.

- **Reading her:** WIS (Insight) DC 10 on [[Mitzi]] shows how she really feels. Lying to her: CHA (Deception) against her passive Insight 17.
- **Gift:** [[The Matron's Hearth Tent]]. Being kind to Mitzi counts toward earning it.
- **The Matron's Mercy:** in the worst ending, if the party still has her favor, she holds back the ice for 3 extra rounds.
- **The ending:** told about the core, she guards the way below the ice, and Mitzi glows for a week.
