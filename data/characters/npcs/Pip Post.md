Location: [[The Buckle Post Office]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Race: Halfling

Runs [[The Buckle Post Office]] with his adopted brother [[Barnaby Post]]. Motto: "Nothing stops the letters from getting through." He whistles on every round and carries [[Stamp]] in his satchel. The brothers marked the maze route with FORWARD and RETURN TO SENDER stamps, and they hold [[Dip's Letter]].

- **Gift:** [[Return-Receipt Parcel]] (deliver a lost letter found in the maze, or help Barnaby finish a delivery he's been avoiding).

Postmaster of the Buckle branch of [[The Post]], the Blackmarket guildsect that delivers anything anywhere. He handed Choco La's letter to the Post's best courier.
