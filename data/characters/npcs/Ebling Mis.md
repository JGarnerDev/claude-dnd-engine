Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

Race: Owlin

Head librarian of [[Cinto]] and guild head of [[The Knowledge Bank]]. She appraises secrets and wants anything copied from the Mountain.

- **Opening hook:** the Buckle's copies of new carvings have stopped arriving. Leads to the maze and [[The Buckle]].
- **Holds:** [[Item 4471]], a scrap of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]] with the verses in order but the ending lost. She sells it as a Rare secret (100 gp) and pays handsomely for the ending.
- **Lying to her:** CHA (Deception) against her passive Insight 15. Fail and she marks them as unreliable and pays less from then on.
- **The ending:** sold the secret of the core, she makes the biggest sale Cinto has ever seen, and within a season it's worth nothing.
