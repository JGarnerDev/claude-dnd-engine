## Overview

**Vessa**, [[Dip]]'s twin sister, lost in the fire that destroyed [[Dip's Home Hamlet]]. Presumed dead by the survivors — but not by Dip.

## The Hook

In Dip's visions, many hands guide and push them along. Their sister's hands are neither leading nor helping — and they take that very absence as proof she still exists on this realm in some form. Her true fate is open: alive somewhere, an echo across the planes Dip is attuned to, or genuinely gone and the hands they seek are a grief-shaped hope. A DM decision.

## Three Futures (Ben's Handout, 2026-10-14)

- **Hoped for:** she has grown into someone worth knowing, found her own path, and is proud of it.
- **Feared:** she went beyond the veil and came back as one of the monsters, the bloodlust raiders, that split the twins apart.
- **Never said aloud (DM-only):** there was no twin. Vessa is a figment Dip's broken mind built as a pillar to rebuild itself on.

Dip's belief would only shake if Vessa's hands joined the guiding mass in their visions. Even then, Dip would take it as her pushing them toward a greater cause.

## Open Questions (for the DM / Ben)

- Whether she survived, or existed at all.
- If alive: where, as what, and whether she remembers Dip.
- Her connection, if any, to the "guiding hands" in Dip's visions and their Session 7 death account.
