Vessaine Rook came out of **[[The Inward]]** in the spring and has not gone back. Her band is one of the deepest in the wood; as far as anyone at the treeline can establish, no one from it has left the interior in living memory, and she has offered no account of why she has.

What she does is ask questions. Specific ones, about numbers — what a fur fetched at the post, what the same fur fetched at the bay, what the difference is called and who keeps it. She asks them of factors, of carters, of other **[[The Thelven]]** at the margin, and she asks them in the flat unsoftened register of someone who has never had to be polite to a merchant. She writes nothing down.

The factors find her irritating and are not yet worried. The margin bands find her *interesting*, which is a different and more consequential thing — because the margin has known for a long time that it is being shorted and has never had a reason from deeper in the wood to do anything about it.

**Unresolved:** whether the wood-court sent her, or she came on her own. Both readings are live and they mean opposite things. A court that has quietly begun auditing the league is a court preparing something. A single deep-band speaker acting alone is a person the court may have to disown — or follow.

*Kellan's seed. What her band actually wants, and what she will do with the arithmetic once she has it, are open.*
