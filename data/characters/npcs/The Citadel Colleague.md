## The Cold Spot

A genuine friend of [[Odibli]]'s who knows nothing about any of this. Odibli won't confide in them, because the thing he's hiding he is hiding even from himself, so the friendship carries a permanent, unexplained distance.

Worse (DM-only): the Colleague knew Odibli *before* the split, so their honest memories belong to [[The Original Odibli]] and are subtly wrong for the man in front of them. They keep reaching for a shared past that never quite lines up, and neither understands why. This is the relationship the copy is quietly failing.
