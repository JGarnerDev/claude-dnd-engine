Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Tiefling

Old Kissoon answers only in riddles and sells hints about the maze.

- **Opening hook:** a riddle about "the fire that eats the way" (the Yule Log).
- **The Lay:** she knows the oldest version of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]] and gives it out a verse at a time, each behind a riddle. Her price is a Gossip secret per riddle, or a riddle she hasn't heard. Her six riddles are in [[Only the Righteous Find the Way]].
- **Getting a hint:** INT (Investigation) DC 13 to untangle her answer; a fail gets a harder riddle.
- **About Tesla Bombeck, later:** "Ask me who they were, and I'll ask you who they'll be."
- **The twist:** she can hint that selling a secret and telling everyone end in the same place.
