Location: [[The Cupola Temple]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Aasimar

His Most Radiant Eminence, the Archhierophant Aurelian Valorien Sellenor the Thirteenth, Listener Between Worlds, Warden of the Thinning Veil and Keeper of the Thousand Silences. He leads [[The Monks of the Ostium]] from [[The Cupola Temple]].

He teaches that in the silence below the ice you can hear worlds grinding together: crackles and pops when they collide, and static when they pull apart. Beneath them all lies [[The Zahareena]], the stillness every world rests on, and he has spent his whole life listening for it.

- **Listening meditation:** an hour with him and a DC 15 WIS check hears a clue about what lies ahead.
- **His secret (DM-only):** the slow scratching he hears beneath everything is [[Groundhog Claus]] digging, not the multiverse.
- **Gift:** [[The Fold of Static]] (complete a meditation, or bring proof of what the scratching is).
- **The ending:** told about the core, the monks become its keepers. True to their motto, they keep the door, not the key.
