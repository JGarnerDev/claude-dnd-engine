Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Human]]

Sheriff of **[[Ashford]]**, and in practice the town's whole defense. Her lord,
**[[Lord Corren Cael]]**, is a decent man who cannot come; what covers Ashford instead is
Mara Tull, too few deputies, and **[[Elowen Rimetusk]]** on the days the old mage is
present.

She lost her son and her daughter in the war. She carries it as steel rather than despair,
which is the whole of her character: she has already survived the worst available thing, so
a bandit band does not frighten her and flattery does not move her. She speaks quietly, gives
an order once, and is generally obeyed by people who could not explain why.

## At the table

She is the town's competence, and she is outnumbered. That combination is what makes her
useful: she will take the party seriously the moment they are useful, she has no capacity to
be precious about where help comes from, and she is the one person in Ashford who can tell
them the truth about how bad it is without either panicking or minimising.

She has noticed things about the raiders — that they are kitted alike, that they withdraw too
cleanly, that this is not what independent bandits look like. She has no one to report it to
who will act.

## Her children (DM-only)

Her son and daughter did not die in battle. They were in the company
**[[Elowen Rimetusk]]** moved in one translocation when it was surrounded, and they never
arrived. Mara believes they died in the war, and does not know Elowen was there. Elowen's
help is a repayment Mara has never been told about. Whether the children are still somewhere
in between is deliberately unanswered.
