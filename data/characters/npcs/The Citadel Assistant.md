## The Fuse

Assigned to help [[The Original Odibli]] with the transcription of a fragment of the [[Tome of Strahd]], and left holding the bag when the copy woke and walked out. Knows that a copy *without an ending*, the gravest crime the Citadel knows, is loose, and that the original buried it.

Agonizing over whether to report it to [[The Master of the Citadel]]. The Assistant is the fuse; the Master is the bomb they're deciding whether to light.

They also hold the abandoned, unfinished transcription: the pages [[Odibli]] never finished.
