Location: [[Tassad]]

The **Grand Archon** sits at the head of the [[The Templar Triumvirate|Archon Council]], the highest authority over the three tower-guilds of [[Tassad]] and over the region's registry of recognised Speakers of the Word.

The office is defined; the person is not. Name, race, history, and personal manner are open — a deliberate elaboration slot for the DM or Paul. What is fixed is the seat: whoever holds it commands the Triumvirate and arbitrates between marble, onyx, and malachite.

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Paul's faction concept for [[The Templar Triumvirate]]. Only the office is established — personal details are unconfirmed.

## Notes

- Personal name and character sheet intentionally unset — awaiting DM/Paul.
- The [[The Templar Triumvirate|Archon Council]] itself (the wider body of Archons) is spawnable as its own faction/sub-entity if the story warrants.
