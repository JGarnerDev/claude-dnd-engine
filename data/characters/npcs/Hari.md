Location: [[The Deck Stalls]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Elf

Keeps the Hindu stall on [[The Deck Stalls]]: ladoo, jalebi and chai. She lines her stall with diyas, draws rangoli in colored ice dust, and tells the story of light winning over darkness. [[Bayta Darell]] in Cinto buys her spices.

- **Gift:** [[Hari's Diya]] (help finish her rangoli, DEX DC 12 with painter's or calligrapher's tools if proficient, or listen to her story).
