Location: [[The Golden Lioness]]

Race: tiefling | Class: [[Warlock]]

[[Sable Wren]] runs [[The Golden Lioness]]. She was hired to exorcise something from the
building, made friends with it instead, and received the building as payment. What she found
was **[[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]]** — a singular ancient deep-water being in the
aquifer the tavern's cellar spring drops into, whom she calls **Asher**. She talked to it
instead of banishing it, was not disgusted by it, and has been bringing it the world's small
news and good drink ever since. He nudged her toward this building early on, in a way she
noticed but did not question. She has never been certain how much of the dream was hers. She
decided it does not matter.

What she gets out of it: the cleanest water in a town whose river is going bad, and the
ability to bless and charge deep-water for brewing. What he gets is a clean well used for
life, someone opposing the fouling, and company.

She works alongside [[Lok Thuliaga]] and [[Bree Tealeaf]].
