Location: [[The Golden Lioness]]

Race: tiefling | Class: [[Warlock]]

[[Sable Wren]] runs [[The Golden Lioness]]. She was hired to exorcise something from the building, made friends with it instead, and received the building as payment. Her patron is an unnamed ancient hearth spirit tied to fire, shelter, and gathering places. The patron nudged her here early on, in a way she noticed but did not question. She has never been certain how much of the dream was hers. She decided it does not matter.

She works alongside [[Lok Thuliaga]] and [[Bree Tealeaf]].
