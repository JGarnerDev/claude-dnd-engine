Location: [[The Old Bank]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Kenku

Runs the till at [[The Old Bank]] alone. He happily explains that currency means nothing anymore and that it's been a lovely place to retire. He'll change coins, store valuables or give loans on very relaxed terms.

- **The deposit boxes:** some have gone unclaimed for decades. Box 117 holds [[Lemarchand's Notebook]]. Opening one: CHA (Persuasion) DC 13, or INT (Investigation) DC 14 in his ledgers. Picking a lock: DEX (Sleight of Hand) DC 15; his passive Perception is 9.
- **On box 117:** "Oh, how lovely. I always wondered what was in that one. Do keep it, nobody's coming back for it."
