> **DM-only.** Sourced from Miguel's Citadel knowledge doc (2026-10-25), which replaces the earlier unnamed "Citadel Colleague" (who knew nothing; she now knows everything). Surname spelled **M-O-O-R-L-I-N**. Inspired by Betty Shannon, deliberately not on the nose.

Location: [[The Citadel]]

## The Friend

[[The Original Odibli]]'s true friend inside [[The Citadel]]. She may be his partner rather than just a friend; the exact relationship is to be nailed down later.

She knows the secret fully. She wants the copy destroyed, to protect the original, and like him she doesn't want [[Wendrick Schulden]] to find out. To her the copy is a machine, however alive it acts.

## What She Has Done

With the original and [[Berle Kempwick]], she has tried to shut the copy down remotely, more than once. The shadow [[Odibli]] took in at Session 10 was one of those attempts landing. It failed.
