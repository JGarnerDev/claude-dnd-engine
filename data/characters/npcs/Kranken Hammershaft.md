## Concept

Kranken Hammershaft is *said* to be a dwarven barbarian-bard with a full epic heavy-metal aesthetic — thunderous drums, growled war-ballads, a barbarian's rage poured straight into the performance. Said to be. Every detail is contested. What's certain is only that he is a **well-known bard**: not universally famous, but anyone who follows bards, music, or heroes knows the name on sight — and has a different story to prove it.

He belongs to the [[Homebrew Campaign]] world (tag `homebrew`), seeded now for later play. His exact home, haunts, and how the party crosses him are **not yet decided**.

But the party will hear of him long before they meet him — and no two stories will agree.

## The Brasky Rule (DM device)

Every NPC who mentions Kranken invents new lore on the spot — absurd, contradictory, exaggerated, impossible — and swears it true. None of it can be reconciled. It is not lying; it is legend metabolizing in real time. Play it straight: the NPC believes every word.

- **Escalation.** Each teller tops the last. Bar-toast cadence — "To Kranken Hammershaft!"
- **Contradiction is the point.** One says he's twelve feet tall, the next says he fits in a tankard. One says he's dead, the next drank with him Tuesday.
- **Never confirm or deny.** The DM keeps no canonical Kranken facts — each retelling is disposable. Invent freely.

### The initial pool (as real as Kranken himself — invent more freely)

- Killed a dragon with a rhyming couplet. The dragon agreed the couplet was better.
- Born full-grown, beard first, holding the drum.
- Has forty-seven children, all named Kranken, none his.
- The Gilded Tankard is a scorch mark because he clapped once.
- Fought the ocean to a draw. The ocean bought the next round.
- Not a dwarf. Three halflings. No one's checked.
- Broke his manhood copulating with a bronze dragon — and that's how he got the name.
- Takes his coffee and a fable or two in a cozy huddle piled high from the corpses of his foes.
- Sheds one single tear before every performance. For the fallen homies.
- Has a song that stills the undead. No one's heard it and lived to hum it.

### When they finally meet him

The real Kranken is smaller than every legend and somehow bigger. He neither confirms nor denies a single tale, because to him they're all true enough to sing. He may absurdly *confirm* the dumbest one deadpan, or go silent at the wildest, leaving the party unsure which was real.

> **Reliability:** Table-born concept (grew from a Session 17 running bit that "Metallica is canon"). Canonized as a design seed with `exists: false` — flip to `exists: true` and move to `historian/` when the party actually meets him in the new campaign. Placement (`location`) and relation to the party are open for the DM to define.
