Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Goblin

A thief in [[Cinto]] who steals notebooks, not purses. Can be hired to steal a secret, or caught stealing the party's.

- **Opening hook (shadier):** wants help stealing something from the vault at [[The Matron's Inn]].
- **Catching him:** STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) against his DEX (Acrobatics) +6. If he loses, he offers a secret to be let go.
- **Hiring him:** CHA (Persuasion) DC 13, or pay him a Useful secret.
