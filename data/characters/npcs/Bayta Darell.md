Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Loxodon

Bakes impossibly good cookies in [[Cinto]]. Her cookies give Groundhog Claus disadvantage on his Milk and Cookies save, and count as proof that spring is safe.

- **Opening hook:** her spices from [[Hari]]'s stall have run out, and no new ones have come. Leads to [[The Deck Stalls]].
- **Family:** her granddaughter Arkady wrote [[Arkady's Letter]] to the Claus; Bayta is the grandma who saw spring once.
