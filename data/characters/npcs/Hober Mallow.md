Location: [[Cinto]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Race: Triton

Ferries pilgrims north to [[Cinto]].

- **Opening hook:** pilgrims she ferried north haven't come back, and she's heard there's a monster blocking the passage. Leads to [[The Yule Log]].
- **Her line:** "We traveled far up north, going a speed of 48 blusts. If you're not a seafarer, that's the speed of the fastest galegull in a headwind, times itself, and a bit!" (Understanding a blust: INT DC 25. No one ever succeeds.)
- **Has seen:** [[Lights Under the Sea Ice]] that match the Inner Sun.
- **Her crew** sing "The River Daughter" with two verses swapped "for the tune" (WIS (Insight) DC 13 gets her to admit it).
