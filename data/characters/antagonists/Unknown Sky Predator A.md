*Aerial predator of uncertain classification. What follows is fragmentary observation.*

It is rarely seen clearly because encounters are short. Most accounts are silhouette, impact, and retreat — something large, fast, and already gone. Sky-faring crews have the most complete records: charted no-fly zones marked on private maps, passed between captains, not published.

The consistent detail is speed. There is no warning period. It was not there, and then it was.
