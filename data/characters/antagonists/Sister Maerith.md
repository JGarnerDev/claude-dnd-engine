Location: [[Homebrew World]] (Corveth and the towns downwind of the shadow-wound)

Sister Maerith is the veiled woman [[Halsby]] remembers from dark-moon visits in childhood — *an
old friend from home*, his grandmother called her — who appeared again, unaged, at the
grandmother's funeral. She is a Sharran of [[The Nightveil]], and she serves [[Shar]].

She waited until the other mourners left, then told Halsby three things without pressure: that
his grandmother had been one of theirs; that the rite he'd performed since childhood had a name,
and so did the one it addressed; and that they could tell him what is in his blood whenever he
cared to come and ask. She did not say what it would cost. She did not have to. He went.

She is not a brawler — she is a handler. Her weapon is the one question Halsby will trade anything
to answer, and the ledger she lets him run against it.
