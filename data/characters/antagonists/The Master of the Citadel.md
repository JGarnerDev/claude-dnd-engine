## The Bomb

The institutional threat over [[Odibli]]. The Master does not even know he exists. To the Master a deathless copy is not a person who erred; it is the gravest breach [[The Citadel]] knows, a category to be filed and contained.

The dread is the **indifference**: the moment Odibli registers at all, it is already as a problem to be processed.

Whether the Master ever learns depends on [[The Citadel Assistant]], who knows and is deciding whether to light the fuse.
