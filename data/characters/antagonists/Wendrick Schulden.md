> **DM-only.** Sourced from Miguel's Citadel knowledge doc (2026-10-25), which names the earlier unnamed "Master of the Citadel". A combination of three Bell Labs-era figures: William Shockley (the hands-in-everything boss who takes credit for others' work; only his personality and ego are borrowed, not his real-life views), Norbert Wiener (the credit-conscious rival) and Joseph Doob (the harsh critic). A hands-off Master modeled on Mervin Kelly was rejected as too easy a story.

Location: [[The Citadel]]

## The Bomb

The head of [[The Citadel]]. He does not know [[Odibli]]'s copy exists. By the Citadel's rules, a runaway copy must be reported to the whole Citadel: letting an agent loose is one of the gravest offenses a wizard can commit, and a powerful wizard's copy walking the world without knowing who he is is exactly what the rules exist to prevent.

[[The Original Odibli]] and [[Elspeth Moorlin]] are keeping it from him. Whether he learns depends on [[Berle Kempwick]].
