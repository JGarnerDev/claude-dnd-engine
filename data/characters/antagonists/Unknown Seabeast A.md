Location: unknown | Campaign: agnostic

*Creature of uncertain origin and form. What follows is legend, not record.*

Coastal communities old enough to have a name for it do not use that name freely. Fishermen who have seen it and lived describe contradictory shapes — vast whiteness, wrong angles, movement that does not match any animal they know. The consistent detail is scale. And the silence after.

Some communities have built ritual around it: offerings thrown at the tide-line before long voyages, prayers that are really apologies, taboos against certain words at sea. Whether these rituals work or merely comfort is unknown.

No one has sought it deliberately and returned to report why.
