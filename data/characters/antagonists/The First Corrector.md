Location: unknown | Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

The First Corrector was the primary articulator of the ideology that death is an imposed falsehood — proof, in their reading, that finality was a solvable error rather than a boundary. A catalytic insight (evidence that *something* existed beyond the system of meaning) they interpreted not as a threat but as vindication. Core belief: **"Nothing that must end can be called just."** Their calm certainty drew scholars, archivists, and the grief-stricken into **[[The Correctors]]**, whose ritual broke **[[Threnai]]**.

> **Reliability:** Player-backstory canon for [[Iggy]]. Free (`exists: false`) future antagonist — true name uncertain or lost. Fate deliberately open: never confirmed dead, disappeared during or after the ritual. Whether they survived, and in what form, is DM's call.

## Failure Point

They assumed what lay beyond meaning was neutral or empty, and ignored that the plane's finalizing function may have been protective, not restrictive. What they broke through instead corrupted the returned dead — the **Returned** — and even resisted Sah's direct intervention.
