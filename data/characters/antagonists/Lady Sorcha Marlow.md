Location: [[Duskwater]]

Race: [[Human]]

Ruling lord of **[[House Marlow]]**, holding **[[Duskwater]]** — the purple fief
neighbouring Stonemarch to the southeast. She is "the shield that turned" made flesh: heir
to the house that once marched to **[[House Cael]]**'s defense, now the house extorting its
border trade and quietly taking red's coin to do it.

## How she works

Diplomacy first, and the diplomacy is good. She is courteous, reasonable-sounding, and
genuinely persuasive, which is what makes the switch land — the charm is the weapon, not a
disguise over one. When she stops being diplomatic she does not escalate gradually. She is
merciless and then she is finished.

Her revenge is patient and structural rather than violent. She does not want a battle with
**[[Lord Corren Cael]]**; she wants his trade throttled, his towns bled, his tenants
convinced he cannot protect them, and his fief hers in every way that matters before anyone
formally notices. The raids on **[[Ashford]]** are that project, not a grudge.

## Deniability

She runs the raids through **[[Harrow Bane]]**'s land band, and she does it through an
intermediary — no direct orders, nothing on paper, nothing in her own hand. If the band were
caught tomorrow she could disavow it and be technically telling the truth.

That intermediary is the whole seam. Pulling him is the traitor-reveal, and it is optional:
the party can run the entire act treating the raiders as independent bandits and never be
wrong about anything that matters to them. Marlow is the answer to a question nobody in
Ashford has quite asked out loud.

## Her own account of it

She is not interested in being a monster and does not experience herself as one. Her house
spilled blood for Cael's and received, by her reading, gratitude and nothing else — a
grandfather's grave and a broken betrothal and two generations of being the lesser
neighbour. The extortion is a debt being collected late. She would say so to the party's
face, reasonably, over a decent table, and some of them would find it hard to answer.
