*Collective entity of unknown origin. What follows is observational record.*

They move with shared intention. Not like a swarm — like a decision.

Scholars who have studied encounter reports note one consistent and unsettling detail: insectoid behavior changes after contact with a new species. They *update*. What they do with that update is unknown.

They have not been provoked without response. They have also not been the first to strike — in any confirmed encounter.
