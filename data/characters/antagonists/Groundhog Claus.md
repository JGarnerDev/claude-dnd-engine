## DM-only

The players don't know he's there. A very large groundhog in a very worn red coat, with a red hat, a white beard and whiskers. He is misunderstood and stuck, not evil. He has relived the same day at the core for a thousand years, believing he's guarding the world from spring.

- **The Inner Sun:** its constant light means he always sees his shadow, so winter is always on.
- **The scratching:** [[Aurelian Sellenor]] has heard him digging for years.
- **The letters:** his burrow is piled with letters to "the Claus" from [[The Buckle Post Office]], including [[Arkady's Letter]].
- **The cult:** [[The Order of the Burrow]] worships a great burrowing beast. It's him.
- **Freed:** he gives the whole world an hour of calm, then darts off into a fissure in reality.
- **His opening monologue** (repeated word for word after Same Day Again) is on Ben's prep list.

How to free him, the rolls and each PC's part: [[Freeing Groundhog Claus]].

### Stat block

Full stat block files: `Desktop\DnD Bosses\Holiday Boss Stat Blocks.docx` and `.xlsx`.

- **Final boss, Large Monstrosity, CR 9 (XP 5,000)**
- **Armor Class** 17 · **Hit Points** 150 · **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft. · **Proficiency Bonus** +4
- **STR** 20 (+5) · **DEX** 14 (+2) · **CON** 18 (+4) · **INT** 10 (+0) · **WIS** 14 (+2) · **CHA** 16 (+3)

#### Traits

- ***Sees Its Shadow (Start of its turn, in Bright Light).*** It shouts "SIX MORE WEEKS!" Until its next turn, the ground within 15 ft. is Difficult Terrain, and its attacks deal an extra 3 (1d6) Cold damage.
- ***Milk and Cookies (Each creature, once per fight).*** DC 15 WIS save. A creature within 5 ft. can use an action to offer it a snack. Groundhog Claus makes the save, and on a failure it loses its next Bonus Action eating.

#### Actions

- ***Multiattack.*** Makes three attacks, using Rend or Lump of Coal in any combination.
- ***Rend.*** +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing. Melee attack against one target.
- ***Lump of Coal.*** +9 to hit, range 60 ft., 10 (2d4 + 5) Bludgeoning. Ranged attack against one target. The target can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.
- ***Special Delivery (Recharge 5-6).*** DC 16 DEX save. Damage: 27 (6d8) Bludgeoning. Burrows up to its Speed, then bursts out of the ground. On a failed save, a creature within 10 ft. takes the damage and is knocked Prone. On a success, it takes half damage and isn't knocked Prone.

#### Bonus Action

- ***Naughty List.*** Marks one creature it can see, reading its "crimes" aloud. It has Advantage on attacks against that creature until it marks another.

#### Reactions and Special

- ***Déjà Vu (Reaction, 3/Day).*** When a creature hits it, that creature rerolls and must use the new roll.
- ***Same Day Again (1/Day, at 0 HP).*** When it drops to 0 HP, it regains 60 HP instead. Everyone returns to where they stood at the start of the round, keeping their current HP and spent resources. It then repeats its opening monologue, word for word.

#### Minions

Mimics disguised as presents, which attack anyone who tries to open them or who gets too close. Use 2 Mimics with 5 players, or 4 Mimics with 6 players.
