Location: [[Duskwater]]

Race: [[Goliath]] | Class: [[Druid]]

One of three lieutenants under **[[Harrow Bane]]** in the land band **[[House Marlow]]** uses
deniably against Ashford, alongside **[[Wick]]** and **[[Nella]]**. A male goliath druid, and
the only member of the band whose reason for being there is not money.

## The exile

He was cast out of his circle for a transgression he does not discuss. On the druid pyramid he
sits where the circuit druids and grove wardens sit — the working tier, the agriculture behind
the agriculture — except that the institution that would place him there has disowned him. What
the circle gave him, the band now gives: a place, a function, people who expect him at a fire.
His loyalty runs to the **crew**, not to Marlow's cause, and he would not be able to tell you
what Marlow's cause is.

## Why he rides

The raids buy leverage, and leverage keeps the feud's war off his stretch of wild. That is the
whole calculation. He is not indifferent to Ashford; he simply weighs a grove he has sworn to
against a town he has not.

He also hates what the fighting is doing to the water — the blood at the fords, the fouled
stretches, the sick fish — which puts him, unknowingly, on exactly the same side of one issue
as **[[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]]** beneath the town he is raiding. Neither of them knows.
That is a lever lying in the open for any party that finds both halves of it.

## In a fight

Disciplined and deliberate. He shapes the terrain, blocks pursuit, and gets the crew out. He
does not butcher, does not chase, and will not spend a crew member to win an exchange. Fighting
him feels like fighting someone with somewhere else to be.

## The splinter

Press the grove or press the fouling and his crew-loyalty can be pried away from the cause —
he is the band's weak weld. This is a later-act lever, not a Session-1 beat: he is offstage for
the opener and held for a harder fight.
