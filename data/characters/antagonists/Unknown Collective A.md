Location: unknown | Campaign: agnostic

*Collective entity of uncertain origin and composition. What follows is fragmented record.*

It is not the same thing it was. It is also not different — it contains what it was, along with everything since.

Those who study it disagree on whether to call it one entity or many. It does not appear to care about the distinction.

The name *Velhareth* appears in the oldest accounts. Whether it named itself or was named by something it later absorbed is unresolved.
