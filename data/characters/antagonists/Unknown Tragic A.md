Location: unknown | Campaign: agnostic

*Humanoid antagonist. What follows is partial record — the rest is contested.*

They were something before this. Accounts of what differ, but they agree on the quality: capable, committed, worth knowing. Whatever happened was not gradual. Something broke, or was revealed, or was lost — and what assembled afterward used the same pieces differently.

Their goals are not irrational. That is the problem.

*The Last Architect* is what allies called them. *The Unfinished* is what enemies say now, not always as an insult — sometimes as an acknowledgment that they have not yet done what they came to do.
