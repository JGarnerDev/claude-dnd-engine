Location: [[Duskwater]]

Race: [[Elf]] | Class: [[Warlock]] — a swordlock, Pact of the Blade

Nominally one of **[[Harrow Bane]]**'s three lieutenants, riding beside **[[Jorn]]** and
**[[Nella]]**. Actually an agent of a mage guild, sanctioned to hunt dangerous entities, and
using the band as a way to move through the borderlands without having to explain himself to
anyone.

On the warlock pyramid he belongs to the broker-and-pact-scholar tier — the occult service
economy, the people retained for problems no guild will invoice. In his case the wrinkle is
that a guild *does* invoice it: he is sanctioned, carrying a warrant, which makes him the rare
warlock with institutional backing and makes the other raiders faintly wrong-footed around
him. He takes nothing from a raid that he does not need.

## The errand

His guild detected **a vampire in this region** — presence, no identity. That is the whole
brief, and it is why he is here. The band is transport and cover.

How the hunt runs, once it is live:

- **The flush.** He stages a bled body in **[[Ashford]]** to panic the town and watch who
  moves wrong. The method is dirty; the target is real.
- **The declaration.** Later, with evidence in hand, he publicly names his suspect to the
  village. The accusation is **true** — and partly built on the frame he staged, so exposing
  the fake can drown the real evidence along with it. That is the trap in the beat: the party
  can be completely right about Wick's methods and still be helping a vampire.
- **After.** If she runs, the hunt continues, and where it ends depends on what the party
  chose to do about her.

## Session 1

He hits the far half of the raid and is gone before the party can engage him. What survives is
the townsfolk's account: a strange marked man, moving differently from the rest, who did not
seem to be there for the ale.
