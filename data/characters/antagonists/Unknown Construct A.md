*Construct of uncertain origin. What follows is fragmentary record, not observation.*

No confirmed sighting exists in living memory. What survives are older accounts — carvings, marginalia, one translated passage in a dead language that scholars render loosely as *the deterrent* — that may or may not describe the same thing. No civilization claims to have built it. No ruin matches its described scale.

It is buried. Where, unknown. Whether burial was deliberate — containment, hibernation, defeat — is unknown.

Whether waking it requires something external, or only time, is unknown.
