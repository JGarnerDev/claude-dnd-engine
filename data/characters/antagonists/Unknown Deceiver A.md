Location: unknown — mobile | Campaign: agnostic

*Humanoid antagonist, itinerant. What follows is compiled report.*

He arrives after bad news. Not suspiciously — bad news creates openings, and he is good at finding openings. What he offers is exactly what the situation seems to require.

His names change. *Brother Aveth* in religious communities. *Goodman Tole* among working folk. The face stays the same; no one puts it together until he is already somewhere else.

The product has never been confirmed to work. It has also never been confirmed to harm. That gap is where he lives.
