The world begins with a wound. 6.7433 millennia ago the astral body [[Astra]] struck the Struck Side of the dwarf planetoid, fusing briefly with it before separating in a pirouette. The impact gouged the massive [[The Caldera|Caldera]], dislodged the islands of the Arc Side — flinging the larger ones into orbit as [[The Slices]] and leaving smaller Islifts to wander at low altitudes — and spun trillions of glass shards into Astra's orbital path. Those shards are the source of all [[Whatwill]], the fallen glass that the world's powers now extract, refine, worship, and kill for.

Since the impact, Astra and the planet have traveled separate elliptical trajectories, reconverging less violently every thousand years — an event called a **Glistening**, anticipated and tracked by [[The Vitricants]]. Everything in the present world — the Showerings, the Lifted's floating Islifts, the Whatwill economy — traces back to this single collision.
