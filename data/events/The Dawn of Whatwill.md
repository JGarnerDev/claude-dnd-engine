> **Reliability:** Extrapolated. The corpus establishes that Showerings recur (roughly weekly) but does not name a discrete *first* one. This event treats the beginning of the Whatwill era as a founding moment for the World-tab timeline; confirm framing with the DM before ratifying.

After [[The Astra Collision|the collision]], the planet's orbit first carried it through the band of shards Astra had spun off. Pulled gravitically toward the surface, the shards became luminous and opaque only as they rejoined their mother planet — falling as [[Whatwill]] for the first time, heralded by the first **Kerning**, that haunting whistle with no warning. Indigenous vegetation was devastated where the glass landed; in its place rose the first **Lensing Zones**, dense with phosphorescent, alien, sometimes carnivorous life.

From that first fall grew everything the present world fights over: the [[The Vitricants|Vitricants']] reverence for the great LeyLodes, the [[The Mintners|Mintners']] dissection of glass into anaesthetic tatters, the [[The Partenariats|Partenariats']] clandestine expeditions, and the endless jurisdiction disputes of the [[Courts Superior]]. The Whatwill era had begun.
