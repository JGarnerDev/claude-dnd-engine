Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

**Nul** is not shadow and not darkness. It is the hunger that exists outside of being, the thing that is there before anything is made and after everything is gone. It has no face, so it borrows them.

> **Reliability:** Jeff and Paul's shared late-game antagonist for [[Luci]] and [[Iggy]], decided 2026-11-27. Pool entity. Its arrival is meant for late game (level 14+); how soon it is seen and how the finale plays are open. No player knows its name.

## Three Faces

Nul is one enemy met in layers, from the outside in.

- **[[The First Corrector]], the prophet.** The mortal face. The Threnai scholar who wanted to abolish death looked past the ending, found Nul there, and turned. They now preach the inverted creed: *"Nothing that refuses to end can be called just."*
- **The Dead Sun, the convert.** The god face. A sun god who chose to die, and whose death is the doctrine. The Dead Sun Worshippers who built [[The Sun Temple]] were Nul's first congregation; the walls Luci read, light "exalted and revered and feared and condemned," pulling life in as it winks out, are Nul's scripture in a sun's mouth.
- **Nul, the hunger.** No face at all. What the other two serve, and what [[Farzrig]] rides for.

> **DM-only:** The Dead Sun may need its own deity file once it is played. For now it lives here, as Nul's god-convert.

## What It Wants

Paul's write-up names its aim through Farzrig: the end of "a mockery of entropy," a clinging to life within an afterlife, the gods themselves existing in an after-afterlife and pulling to their bosom that which should end. Every afterlife is an offence to Nul, and every god who keeps the dead is a thief.

## The Shared Secret

> **DM-only:** The secret Luci and Iggy share, and no one else: each has seen the void. Not shadow, or darkness, but the hunger that exists outside of being. Iggy saw it through the blank road and behind Caelion's bridge. Luci saw it in the drift, in the oblivion with no before and no after. Neither has a word for it. When they compare, they find they are describing the same thing, and that it saw them back.

## Its Reach in Aethryn

- **[[The Absolvers]]** preach it openly as release.
- **The Returned are its eyes.** Every crude return the grief society makes is a window Nul looks through, so [[The Correctors]]' heirs feed it without knowing.
- **Fida** is thinning: see [[Farzrig]].

## Open Questions

Left undecided on purpose (Jeff and Paul, 2026-11-27):

- **How soon Farzrig is seen.** The arrival is meant for level 14 or later. The tempo before that (felt early, glimpsed once, or only absences) is open.
- **How the finale plays.** Whether Farzrig is restored to a balancer, slain, or turns on Nul, and how Iggy's song and Luci's freeing of [[Aedra]] land, is open. The leading idea is that a chosen ending is meaning, and meaning is the one thing Nul cannot eat.
