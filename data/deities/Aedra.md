**Aedra** sits at the core of [[The Sun Temple]], the pocket dimension [[Luci]] remembers waking in. She is a powerful dark entity trying to find the light, and she is descending into madness. [[Sabathiel]] is her eternal guardian, ever watchful over what Paul calls "the imprisonment of a sort".

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Paul's backstory answers (ingested 2026-10-25). Pool entity: her nature, tier, and how she came to be held are open. Her link to the Void Serpent that shattered the temple is not stated.

## What Luci Wants

Luci means to liberate Aedra from her descent into madness, hand in hand with Sabathiel. She sees herself as a vehicle for salvation, and "no expense shall be spared". This is what "Closer" may point toward.

## Why It Matters

Aedra is a liability of existential proportions. A being of her temperament could not drift unsupervised through creation without being noticed. Freeing her draws the attention of cosmic powers: the kind Luci glimpsed in the Astral Sea, such as the [[Rider of the Black Sun]] and the [[The Astral Dragon|Astral Dragon]].

## Hooks

- Reaching Aedra means crossing the shifting board of [[The Sun Temple]]; each piece placed shifts it.
- Freeing her may be salvation, or may be exactly what her guardian exists to prevent.
- Once she stirs, something comes looking.
