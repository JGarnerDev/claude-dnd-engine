The **Bay B Circuit** is a multi-port coastal trade loop encircling [[Bay B]] on western Erevast. Four major rivers drain the continent's agricultural heartland into this bay — the Broadwend (fed by the Moorthistle), the [[Bayward River]], [[Deepwend River]], and [[Thelindra River]] — making it the single greatest concentration of export goods on the map.

Ships move in continuous rotation among the river-mouth ports, loading grain, timber, and downriver trade goods. The circuit sees more traffic than any sea route in the world.
