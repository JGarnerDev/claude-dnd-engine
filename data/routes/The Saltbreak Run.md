**The Saltbreak Run** threads south from [[Sub Continent A]] through [[The Saltbreak Isles]], using the island chain as stepping stones to reach [[Continent B]]. Without the isles, this would be a dangerous open-ocean crossing; with them, it is the primary maritime link between two significant landmasses.

The ports of [[The Saltbreak Isles]] exist almost entirely because of this route — their economies built on resupply, harbor fees, transshipment, and the information that flows between ships making the run.
