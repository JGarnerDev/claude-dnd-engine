**The Still Cold Crossing** spans the northern sea from the western [[Ocean A]] coast to [[Frisian Bay]] in the east, cutting across [[The Still Cold]]. It is among the fastest routes connecting west to east — but the arctic conditions, pack ice, and narrow season make it a route for the desperate, the bold, or those carrying cargo too valuable to send the long way around.

Most merchants avoid it. Those who use it regularly hold significant advantages in speed and market timing.
