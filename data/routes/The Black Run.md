Every legal sack of [[Coffee]] that leaves [[Cindermouth Bay]] travels [[House Rogarre]]'s lane. [[The Black Run]] is not a named road — it is a legal monopoly on movement, locked in by an old family compact that gave Rogarre exclusive right to carry the Black to market.

No one has successfully run coffee outside this route in living memory. The combination of [[House Westane]]'s sterile beans (no independent growing) and Rogarre's route control (no independent shipping) means the monopoly has two independent locks.

The compact is now under pressure. The Rogarre succession feud has rival claimants threatening to enforce competing toll claims on the same lane. The other four houses are watching to see whether the route fractures — or whether one claimant emerges strong enough to hold it.

The destination end of the route is deliberately kept vague by Rogarre. Knowing which markets the Black enters first is itself commercial intelligence they treat as proprietary.
