The **Dustbreak Highway** runs east-west across Erevast's heartland, tracing the length of [[The Dustbreak Plains]] from the agricultural river delta country in the west to [[The Irongate]] in the east. It is the backbone overland route of the most populous continent in the world — more goods, soldiers, and travelers move along this road than any other single land corridor.

The plains offer no natural cover; the road is exposed and fast in good weather, impassable in the worst seasons.
