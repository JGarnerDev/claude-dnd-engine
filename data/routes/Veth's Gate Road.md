**Veth's Gate Road** is the only confirmed overland route south from [[Karath Ut'll]] and [[Sub Continent C]] into [[Continent C]]. It passes through [[Veth's Gate]], a named threshold whose character — whether natural pass, fortified crossing, or something stranger — is not yet established.

Traffic here is sparse compared to the great Erevast routes. What moves through Veth's Gate tends to be valuable, secretive, or both.
