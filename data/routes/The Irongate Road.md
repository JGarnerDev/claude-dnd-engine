The **Irongate Road** is the only overland route connecting [[Erevast]] to [[Continent G]] in the east. It passes through [[The Irongate]], a natural and fortified chokepoint where the [[Watchwall Mountains]] pinch the passable corridor to a narrow gap.

Whoever holds [[The Irongate]] controls the flow of goods, armies, and information between the western and eastern halves of the known world. The toll levied here is a source of enormous political tension.
