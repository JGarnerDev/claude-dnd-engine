The **Gordrathi Gap** threads south through a pass in the [[Gordrathi Mountains]], linking [[Erevast]]'s southern settlements to the population centers of [[Continent B]]. The mountains form an otherwise impassable wall; this is the sole land crossing.

Control of the gap means control of the continent's food and goods exchange between north and south. The pass closes seasonally and has been fortified and contested throughout history.
