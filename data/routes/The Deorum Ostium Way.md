**The Deorum Ostium Way** carries pilgrims from [[Erevast]] north through [[Oathmark]] to [[Deorum Ostium]] — the Gateway of the Gods — on the northern coast. It is the most spiritually significant journey in the known world. Pilgrims arrive from every continent, but the primary overland approach runs through the populated heart of Erevast before climbing north toward [[Oathmark]] and the sacred threshold.

Sea approaches also exist — ships can reach Deorum Ostium via The Still Cold — but the overland way is considered the traditional pilgrimage path. The journey's difficulty is likely part of its meaning.
