The **Frisian Bay Lanes** run north from [[Frisian Bay]] on eastern Erevast's coast into [[The Still Cold]] sea. They serve as the maritime outlet for [[Hightalon]] and the broader eastern Erevast region, connecting to the northern crossing routes.

[[The Rip]], a treacherous channel near the bay entrance, is a known hazard that claims ships in rough weather and likely requires local pilots to navigate safely.
