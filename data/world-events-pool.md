# World Events Pool

Possible world events — political, social, economic, military shifts that could reshape the campaign world. Sourced from player ideas and AI generation.

Events here are **not canon**. They graduate to `./data/events/` or `./historian/` only when the DM commits to them via `/event` → commit flow.

---

## Format

Each entry follows this structure:

```
## Event Name
- **Scale:** local | regional | global
- **Contributed by:** paul | ben | miguel | jeff | ai
- **Themes:** comma-separated (e.g. trade, war, religion, plague, succession)

**Setup:** What triggers or begins this event.

**Outcomes:** Likely political, social, economic, or military impact.

---
```

## Events

## Catastrophe During Peace
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** group
- **Themes:** war, politics, catastrophe, contrast

**Setup:** Something terrible happens on a happy day — a festival, a wedding, a moment of genuine collective relief. Could be a political assassination, a magical detonation, or a sudden invasion. The contrast is the point.

**Outcomes:** The peace being celebrated is shattered in a session. Factions realign, trust between parties collapses, and the emotional weight of the loss compounds the political fallout.

---

## Politically Destabilizing Love Affair
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** group
- **Themes:** politics, succession, romance, alliance

**Setup:** A romance forms between two figures whose relationship is forbidden or catastrophic for power structures — nobles, cross-faction leaders, or royals. The relationship becomes known before either party can manage the damage.

**Outcomes:** Alliances fracture, succession lines are thrown into question, or wars begin. The political cost of the relationship reshapes regional power in ways that outlast whether the affair survives.

---

## Plague
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** group
- **Themes:** plague, faction, desperation, power

**Setup:** Disease emerges as a distinct event — an instigator or arc side effect, not background noise. Spreads through trade routes, military movement, or magical contamination. Origin may be unknown.

**Outcomes:** Factions are strained by death toll, resource drain, and desperation. Power shifts toward whoever controls the cure, survives intact, or can weaponize the fear. Can chain with crop failure.

---

## Pestilence / Crop Failure
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** group
- **Themes:** scarcity, trade, politics, hunger

**Setup:** One or more key crops are ruined — blight, magical contamination, drought, or deliberate sabotage. The cause may not be immediately clear. The effect is immediate.

**Outcomes:** Food scarcity reshapes regional politics. Factions with stockpiles gain leverage. Trade routes shift or collapse. Desperation opens conflict over supply lines. Can chain with plague.

---

## Wizard Guild War
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** war, magic, trade, politics

**Setup:** Two rival arcane cartels escalate past internal cold war into open conflict. Assassinations, counterspells embedded in client contracts, and field skirmishes spill into cities caught between them.

**Outcomes:** Magic dries up on the open market as guilds pull supply from neutral clients. Cities between the factions become contested ground. Freelance casters either profit or disappear. One guild may not survive.

---

## Remnant Tech Site Activates
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** ancient mystery, war, politics, remnant tech

**Setup:** A faction excavates a pre-history ruin and something inside turns on. The activation is not hostile — but it is not safe either. The site begins doing things no one understands.

**Outcomes:** Every interested party converges: factions who want to control it, factions who want to destroy it, and factions who want to know what it is. The site becomes a flashpoint. Whatever the relic does, it reshapes the region around it.

---

## Divine Silence
- **Scale:** global
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** religion, mystery, politics, power

**Setup:** A major god stops answering prayers without warning or explanation. Clerics lose spells. Temples lose credibility. The silence is total and offers no cause.

**Outcomes:** Religious factions fracture between those who double down on faith, those who defect, and those who exploit the vacuum. Rival religions gain ground. The silence may be the first visible sign of something larger.

---

## Crow Network Exposed
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** politics, war, espionage, magic

**Setup:** The mercenary wizard guild's crow surveillance operation is uncovered — by accident, by a rival, or by a defector. Cities realize they have been watched for years. The scope of what was gathered is unknown.

**Outcomes:** Panic and political fallout cascade. Leaders who were surveilled scramble to assess damage. Purges begin. The guild faces retaliation it may not be equipped to handle, or may be very equipped to handle.

---

## Insectoid Expansion
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** war, politics, alien logic

**Setup:** A previously contained insectoid population begins pushing outward. Their goals are coherent from their own frame — not mindless, not evil, just wrong. Diplomacy barely functions. Standard threat assessment does not apply.

**Outcomes:** Border settlements are absorbed or abandoned. Military responses fail to dislodge them cleanly. Factions disagree on whether communication is possible or worth attempting. The expansion continues on its own schedule.

---

## Succession Crisis
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** politics, succession, war, alliance

**Setup:** A ruling monarch dies without a clear or uncontested heir. Multiple factions back competing claimants — some legitimate, some manufactured. The window before civil war is narrow.

**Outcomes:** Alliances form and fracture fast. Outside powers pick sides for leverage. The region destabilizes whether or not a winner emerges quickly. Whoever takes the throne inherits a fractured court.

---

## False Prophet Ascends
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** religion, politics, deception, power

**Setup:** A charismatic deceiver builds a following around a manufactured miracle. The cult grows faster than anyone expects. People want to believe — and the prophet is very good at making them want to.

**Outcomes:** Local power structures are threatened as the following absorbs resources, loyalty, and attention. Established religious factions respond with hostility or attempted co-option. The prophet may or may not know what they've started.

---

## Sky Pirate Blockade
- **Scale:** regional
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** trade, war, politics, scarcity

**Setup:** A coalition of sky pirates seizes a critical aerial trade route and holds it. The move is organized — this is not raiding, it is leverage. Someone is backing them, or they have a specific demand.

**Outcomes:** The embargo starves a region of essential goods within weeks. Factions dependent on the route face hard choices. Military relief is possible but expensive. Negotiation requires conceding something real.

---

## The Captured God Stirs
- **Scale:** global
- **Contributed by:** ai
- **Themes:** religion, mystery, politics, ancient mystery

**Setup:** Signs emerge that something divine is imprisoned and struggling — divine magic behaves strangely in certain regions, cult behavior intensifies around inexplicable phenomena, and clerics report dreams they cannot explain.

**Outcomes:** Cults form around the disruption. High-tech factions with something to hide move to suppress or discredit. Religious institutions are shaken. The world does not know what is happening — but it can feel that something is.
