Five creatures so rare that nobody has a collective name for them. You call each by what it is.

## The Five

- **Griffin** — aerial combat and long-range transport. The military prize. Found nesting in the [[Gordrathi Mountains]], where something ancient and non-human named the peaks before civilization arrived.
- **Giant Eagle** — scouting and messenger work. Faster and more maneuverable than a Griffin, less of a weapon. Territory: [[The Pale Spire]], permanently glaciated and impassable, which is why almost no one has ever reached one.
- **Dragon** — the apex. Whoever has one doesn't use it for errands. [[Hell's Reign]] is their domain — inner volcanic crater wall, no expedition has returned with a full account.
- **Nightmare** — fiendish warhorse. Not found in the world; obtained through dark pacts with the Nine Hells. The only exotic mount that isn't caught or tamed in the traditional sense.
- **Displacer Beast** — hunter's mount and assassin's companion. Found in the [[Virethorn Range]], jungle-covered mountains with dinosaur territory. You have to survive the ecosystem to reach one.

## The Bond

Taming any of these creatures requires matching its alignment and earning its respect — the animal is making a choice as much as the rider. The bond is maintained through behavior. Drift from it and the creature senses the betrayal.

If the bond breaks, the mount does not flee or go feral. It turns on the rider with the fury of a betrayed predator. It will attempt to kill you. Whatever made it trust you is now the reason it hunts you.

There is no transferring a bond. No handing one off. You are the right person, or you are not.

Upkeep matches the creature's nature — space and resources scaled to wild animals, not stables. Keeping one in or near civilization is its own logistical problem.

## The Mist

None of these creatures can breach the Barovian mist by strength alone. The aerial ones can fly over or attempt to enter; the mist decides whether to admit them. The Nightmare is the exception — as a fiendish creature it operates on different rules, and the mist's curse does not apply to it. It can pass freely.

Strahd has no interest in exotic mounts. They are mortal concerns.

## Story Activation

These creatures become a story when:

- The party encounters one in the wild and someone decides to try for the bond
- The party needs one — a mission, an escape, a crossing — and has to go find it
- A villain arrives on one and the party sees for the first time what they are dealing with
