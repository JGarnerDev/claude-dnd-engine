[[Masamune]]'s defining resource. Whatwill is meteor-origin glass produced by the ancient Astra impact — semi-opaque, pearlescent, phosphorescent, containing metallic tears reactive to nitrogen on fracture.

**Forms by value:** Dust (cheap, addictive, common) → Pearls (status, ritual, reverie) → Discs (industrial, stabilisation engines) → Shards (dangerous, unstable) → Lodes (strategic, war-provoking) → Hearts (sovereign-scale, rarely seen).

**Exposure progression:** Fixation → missing time → shared dreams → false memory → Latticing (crystalline biological transformation). Not everyone progresses the same way. Symptoms are treated as religious experience, industrial hazard, or social stigma depending on faction.

Controlled by [[Extraction Guilds]] in legal terms. Contested by [[The Verdicants]], [[The Lifted]], and everyone else in practice. [[The Mintners]] inhale dust. [[Caldera Monks]] oppose all extraction. [[The Wattlers]] scavenge what everyone else leaves behind.
