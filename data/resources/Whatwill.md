Resource that falls on [[The World]] from the Astra moon's orbital debris field during Showerings.

**Forms (lowest to highest value):** Dust (almost devoid of metallic compound, lowest denomination) → Pearls (polished, used for display and reverie) → Discs → Lodes.

**Uses:** Beautiful when polished. Revered for falling from the heavens. Addictive — entrancing to gaze upon. [[The Vitricants]] display pieces for onlookers to enter reverie. [[The Mintners]] inhale the dust believing it transports them to another realm. [[The Lifted]] use it as the source of propulsion and geo-stabilisation for their géode islands.

**Origin:** An astral body (the Astra) collided with the planet long ago, fusing briefly then separating — spinning off trillions of glass shards into orbital paths. The planet and Astra continue along elliptical trajectories, reconverging roughly every thousand years (a Glistening). Showerings occur when the planet passes through the orbital shard band. Shards are transparent in space, indistinguishable from background stars, becoming luminous and opaque only on atmospheric entry — making prediction impossible.

**Warning:** Showerings are heralded by a Kerning — a haunting whistle. No reliable way to predict where strikes land.

**Danger:** Fracturing exposes the reactive metallic compound to nitrogen-rich air. Refinement into pearls, discs, and lodes carries significant risk.

**Rarity (implied):** Event-scale scarcity. Showerings are tied to Glistening cycles (~1000-year orbital returns) and unpredictable shard-band passages — not a steady supply. Every piece is effectively accounted for the moment it lands.

**Access (implied):** No monopoly is possible — strikes are random and prediction is impossible. Access is purely contested: whoever arrives first after a Kerning has the advantage. [[The Vitricants]], [[The Mintners]], and [[The Lifted]] all have strong motivation to move fast for different reasons.

**Value (implied):** Priceless across multiple markets simultaneously — sacred to the Vitricants, transcendent to the Mintners, infrastructurally critical to the Lifted, and beautiful to anyone. Value does not come from any single use but from the fact that every faction needs it for a different reason and none can reliably obtain it.
