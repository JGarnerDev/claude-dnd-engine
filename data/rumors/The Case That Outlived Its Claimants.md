## The Rumour

Urbanites fight each other in [[Courts Superior]] over who owns where [[Whatwill]] fell, and deliberation slows activity to a crawl. This rumour is the joke that structure makes about itself: a case so slow that the dispute outlasted the disputants.

The heirs still attend. Counsel is still paid. The site is bedrock.

It is `believed` rather than `spreading` because nobody who has dealt with the courts finds it implausible. That is the damage — the story functions as an argument that the law is not worth using.

## Why It Travels

Every telling is a recommendation. Someone who hears this and has a claim of their own does not file it; they find [[The Partenariats]], who fly no true banner and are prone to turning coat for a higher bidder, and who are the most effective actors in the economy precisely because they never wait for a ruling.

## Story Hooks

- The party is hired as witnesses in a hearing about a place that no longer exists, and someone very much wants the hearing to continue.
- An heir wants the case *ended*, which requires proving the site is gone, which requires naming who took it.
- A Partenariat factor offers the party the same work the courts are still deliberating over, at once, for coin.
