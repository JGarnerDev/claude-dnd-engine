## The Rumour

[[The Lifted]] hold that their floating géode Islifts are stabilised and propelled by [[Whatwill]], and they treat a Stapling — an Islift crashing back to earth — as catastrophic disgrace. Disgrace is handled by not discussing it.

The rumour fills that silence with a saboteur. Someone descended beneath the mass and cut the lode out.

It circulates most freely in [[The Grey Reaches]], where no single faction controls the trade and a heterogeneous population enforces fair play by mutual distrust. That is precisely the population that enjoys this story.

## Why It Travels

Because a Stapling clears airspace, and cleared airspace has an owner afterward. [[The Climbers]] and [[The Divers]] each gain from the accusation landing on the other, so neither has any interest in killing the rumour. A story two rivals both decline to deny is functionally immortal.

## Story Hooks

- A surviving household from the Stapled mass wants someone unaffiliated to go and look at the wreck, because everyone affiliated already has an answer.
- Somebody is quietly buying salvage rights over the impact site before a cause is ever named.
- The party is offered passage in exchange for carrying the accusation somewhere it has not reached yet.
