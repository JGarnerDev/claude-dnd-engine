## The Rumour

[[Opticon's Rest]] is rumoured reachable through a portal inside Az, the last [[The Climbers]] outpost in [[Darkling's Perch]]. Nothing else about the place is known, which is exactly the conditions under which a second rumour grows on top of the first.

This is that second rumour: that [[The Divers]] already walk through it, and that Az's survival deep in the Divers' seat of power is the fee.

## Why It Travels

Because it is useful to both sides and invented by neither. Climbers repeat it to argue Az is being bled and must be reinforced. Divers repeat it to argue Az is a standing insult that should have been condemned years ago. It is `doubted` everywhere and cited constantly, which is the most durable condition a rumour can reach.

In [[Arclight's Seat]] the story arrives with the Climber inflection and the accusation pointed outward.

## Story Hooks

- Somebody wants the portal *photographed*, described, or simply confirmed to exist, and will fund an approach to Az that is not a raid.
- An Az officer knows there is no portal and cannot say so without surrendering the argument that keeps the outpost supplied.
- The party is hired to carry the rumour, not to test it.
