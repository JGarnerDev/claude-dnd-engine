## The Rumour

Told in [[Opticon's Fade]] the way factory towns tell their own bad news — flatly, and with the numbers attached. A refining shift went into a Mint manufactory. The count going in is agreed on. The count coming out is zero.

There was no blast. Refining [[Whatwill]] into pearls, discs, and lodes risks shattering the reactive compound against nitrogen-rich air, and everyone on the Fade knows what that looks like and sounds like. This was quiet.

[[The Mintners]] filed no loss. Pressed, they do not deny the shift is gone — they correct the tense. The shift *arrived*.

## Why It Travels

The Fade runs on the dust economy, which means most families there have somebody inside a manufactory. A rumour that the order considers a vanished worker a success rather than a casualty is not abstract theology to them. It is a question about whether anyone is counting.

## Story Hooks

- Someone's relative was on that shift and wants a body, a name, or an answer — and the order will offer only the third, in a form nobody can use.
- A manufactory foreman will sell the real shift roster, and the price is not money.
- [[The Vitricants]] have their own reading of the disappearance and it flatters no one.
