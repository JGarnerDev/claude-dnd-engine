## The Rumour

[[Deorum Ostium]] takes pilgrims from every religion, and requires each of them to add their own carving to the ice. Monks cut into it continuously. The rumour turns that requirement into a trap: one pilgrim went deeper than the route, found a chamber, and did what the site asks of everyone.

What she says now, nobody at the door can place — and the monks' position is not that she is lying but that there is no such chamber to have found.

## Why It Travels

Because pilgrims carry it home. A rumour seeded at an all-religion pilgrimage site does not stay regional; it disperses along every road that feeds the door, arriving in a hundred places with a hundred different inflections and one intact detail.

[[The Vitricants]] collect versions of it, for reasons they do not explain to pilgrims.

## Story Hooks

- The pilgrim is findable, and will carve rather than answer.
- A party member is required to add a carving and gets to choose where.
- Two religious delegations want the chamber found for opposite reasons, and will each fund the search on condition the other is excluded.
