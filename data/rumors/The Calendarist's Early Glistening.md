## The Rumour

[[The Vitricants]] maintain the Vitrian calendar, which tracks Opticons and foretells Glistenings — the thousand-year elliptical return of [[Astra]]. The calendar is the order's authority made portable, and its arithmetic is not a public document.

The story is that one calendarist carried his working to his superiors rather than to a street, and that both he and the original calendar left the room. What circulates now is a recopy in a hand nobody recognises, agreeing with every past date and nothing else.

It is told as doubted, not believed. That is the interesting part: people repeat it while explaining why it cannot be true.

## Why It Travels

Anyone holding a claim on a landing site is pricing it against a thousand years of quiet. [[The Partenariats]] trade those claims. A rumour that the quiet is shorter than advertised moves money whether or not it is true, which is exactly why the rumour is worth planting if it is not.

It reaches the widest audience in [[The Grey Reaches]], where the mixed population trades with everyone and believes nobody — the one market where a Vitrian scandal can be repeated out loud without consequence.

## Story Hooks

- Someone is selling the calendarist's original working. There will be more than one original.
- A Vitrian with a conscience wants the recopy compared against a surviving fragment, and cannot be seen doing it.
- The party is asked to verify a date they have no means of verifying, by someone who knows that.
