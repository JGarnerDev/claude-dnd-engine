## The Rumour

[[The Ashreaches]] are the only place in the world where ashwake grows, worked in volcanic ash terraces under bonded labour, and controlled by five rival houses that each hold one irreplaceable link in the production chain. That arrangement is stable because no house can move without the other four.

The rumour is the crack in it. Six links, not five. One of them ownerless.

## Why It Travels

It is `believed` because the five houses have spent generations each telling the world that *its* link is the one nothing works without — and five such claims, laid side by side, sound exactly like a count that does not add up.

[[House Apostol]] is the newest and immigrant-risen, and owns the coffeehouses where the Black is drunk. Rooms where strangers of every class sit, talk, and plot are where this story is loudest, and Apostol has less reason than the older houses to shut it down.

## Story Hooks

- Someone will fund an expedition to find the sixth link. The funding is real whether or not the link is.
- A map of the sixth link's location exists in at least four incompatible versions, each with a provenance.
- An older house wants the rumour killed and needs an outsider to be publicly, expensively wrong.
