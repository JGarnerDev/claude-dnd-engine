## The Rumour

[[The Vitricants]] display [[Whatwill]] publicly so onlookers can enter reverie states, and they go to enormous lengths to secure LeyLodes before the structures shatter themselves or are dissected by [[The Mintners]]. Their calendar is supposed to say when a lode's time is up.

One lode's time was up a long while ago. It is still whole, and it is no longer on display.

The second half of the rumour is the part told quietly: that sitting with it is not the ordinary reverie. You bring a question. You leave with a different one.

## Why It Travels

It is a doctrinal problem dressed as a curiosity. A faction whose authority rests on a calendar cannot casually hold an object the calendar was wrong about — so the withdrawal from display reads to outsiders as concealment, whatever it actually was.

[[The Mintners]] have the most direct interest. They dissect LeyLodes into anaesthetic tatters for distribution to bankers, and a lode that refuses to shatter is either the finest material anyone has ever cut or an argument against cutting.

## Story Hooks

- Someone needs the party to get a question *to* the lode, and will not say what the question is.
- A Mintner buyer offers a great deal of money for a theft the order would never survive admitting.
- A Vitrian keeper wants the lode moved somewhere the calendar is not consulted, and needs escorts with no allegiance.
