Circulating in: [[Cinto]], [[The Buckle]] | Source: [[Hober Mallow]]'s crew, among others | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

**The rumour** (what people say, and the party can hear it anywhere): this plane rests on **Pillars**, which sailors call **Beams**, great old supports holding the world up over the dark beneath it. And something is eating them. Not something: someone. A handful of them, the most ruthless there are, patient and hungry, working to bring the whole world down. Nobody agrees on who they are, and the people who say they know tend to stop talking.

**Where the party hears it:**

- **Captain [[Hober Mallow]]'s crew,** on the voyage north: "The Beams are groaning. You can feel it in the hull on a still night."
- **[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]],** in [[Cinto]], as a riddle: "What holds up the roof of the world, and what has teeth for it?"
- **Archivist [[Ebling Mis]]** keeps it as a Forbidden secret (500 gp): a scrap of a list headed "Pillars still standing," with most of the lines crossed out.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]],** in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]: some of the static he hears isn't worlds grinding together. It's a Beam, straining.
- **[[Prophet Boone]]** has his own version: the Great Burrower is holding the Pillar up, and if he ever stops, "six more weeks" becomes "forever."

**The truth (DM-only):** [[The Hollow Mountain|the Hollow Mountain]] is one of the Pillars, a Beam of this universe, pressed up from [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]]. The ones eating the Pillars are real, and the core of [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]] is exactly where they would bite. They have no name yet; that's yours to give them. (This is world-level lore beyond your regions, so check with Jeff how the Pillars sit with Aethryn's cosmology.)

- **If the party does well,** the hour of calm settles [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]], and the Pillar holds. Nobody ever needs to know how close it came.
- **If the party fails** (the worst ending), the Pillar cracks, and the party gets a Meeting with one of the ones eating it: [[Hobbes]] (see The worst ending, and [[Hobbes]]'s real face).
