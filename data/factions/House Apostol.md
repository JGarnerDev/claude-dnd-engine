[[House Apostol]] is the newest of the five — immigrant-risen, the only house that didn't inherit its position. They own the coffeehouses: every room where [[Coffee]] is poured, where strangers of every class sit at the same table and begin to talk.

That is the threat. Wherever Apostol opens a house, people start to plot. Crowns have noticed. The pressure to close the rooms is constant; Apostol walks the line between indispensable commerce and seditious infrastructure.

Their position in the five-house compact is the least secure. The older houses tolerate them because someone has to own the retail end. But Apostol knows their tenure depends on remaining useful — and on the compact holding.
