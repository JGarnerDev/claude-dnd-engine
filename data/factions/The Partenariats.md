The Partenariats are the most effective extractors in the [[Whatwill]] economy — not because they are the most powerful, but because they move first and ask questions later.

When a Kerning sounds, sponsored expeditions depart immediately from the nearest urban centres: heavily laden with extraction equipment, mercenary bands, and hundreds of hangers-on — Watlers and Dust Scrapers desperate for whatever particle they can seize before high-society agents fill their coffers.

They fly no true banner. They operate clandestinely and are even more prone to turning coat, selling to a higher bidder mid-operation. [[Courts Superior]] fights over jurisdiction of landing sites in protracted legal proceedings; the Partenariats have already extracted and departed.

They are not a unified body — individual Partenariats are distinct consortia, sometimes in competition with one another.

## Story Hooks

- Hired by a Partenariat to guard an extraction — then they turn coat mid-mission
- A Partenariat is intercepted by pirates on the return trip with a full load
- Two Partenariats arrive at the same site and the situation deteriorates
