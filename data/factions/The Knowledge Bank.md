Location: [[Cinto]] | Head: [[Ebling Mis]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

The guilds of [[Cinto]] buy, sell and verify knowledge, like banks for secrets. Archivist [[Ebling Mis]] runs the largest. It holds [[Item 4471]], a scrap of [[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]].

**Knowledge is currency.** It's worth more than gold, though gold still has its place. Players can pay with a secret, and the merchant judges how rare it is:

- **Gossip.** Worth: 1–5 gp. Example: Who's feuding with whom at the inn.
- **Useful.** Worth: 25 gp. Example: A safe route through the outer glacier.
- **Rare.** Worth: 100 gp. Example: Where an old pilgrim tunnel really ends.
- **Forbidden.** Worth: 500+ gp. Example: Something carved where no light reaches.

**The catch:** once a secret is sold, it spreads through [[Cinto]] and soon becomes worthless, so players learn to guard what they know.

- **Festival crowds** push up prices and draw pickpockets who steal notebooks, not purses.
- **Guilds** buy, sell and verify knowledge, like banks for secrets.

In the ending where the party sells the secret of the core here, it's the biggest sale Cinto has ever seen, and within a season it's worth nothing and the guild charges admission to the core.

## Every floor behaves like what it keeps

- **The appraisal floor: everything is weighed.** A great brass balance stands in the middle of the floor, and its pans tip whenever someone in the room lies. Anyone watching it has advantage on Insight, and the clerks always watch it. Words spoken in the curtained booths never carry a step outside them.
- **Gossip: the walls repeat.** Anything said aloud here, the walls say again a minute later, a little wrong and a little juicier. The shelves stand at odd angles and are somewhere else every visit, and paper birds flit between them carrying slips of gossip (catch one, DEX DC 12, for a random Gossip secret).
- **Useful: it answers clear questions.** The shelves run on rails. Ask a clear, specific question aloud and the right shelf rolls over to you; ask a vague one and nothing moves, and a clerk sighs. The map table redraws any route asked for.
- **Rare: there's never enough of it.** The floor is bigger inside than the tower is outside (Perception DC 13 to notice), dark, and nearly empty: the glass cases only appear inside a light, and most plinths hold nothing at all.
- **Forbidden: it doesn't want to be known.** Ink fades from any page read aloud here, and whoever reads a Forbidden secret makes an INT save (DC 13) on leaving or forgets one detail of it. The study's window looks onto a street that isn't in [[Cinto]].

## Inside the guild hall

The tallest building in [[Cinto]] that isn't a temple: a narrow stone tower of shelves, five floors high.

- **The appraisal floor:** the ground floor, where people come to sell. A row of booths with curtains, so no one overhears. *Who's here:* clerks with ink-stained fingers, and pilgrims whispering. *Poke at:* the price board, chalked fresh every morning with what kinds of secret are buying high this week.
- **The stacks:** three floors of shelves, ledgers and boxed tablets, reached by a spiral stair. Each floor is a tier: Gossip at the bottom, then Useful, then Rare. *Hidden:* Item 4471, the Lay scrap, in a velvet-lined box on the Rare floor.
- **The top floor:** [[Ebling Mis]]'s study, under the roof. Forbidden secrets are kept here, in a locked cabinet only she opens. *Poke at:* a slim file on her desk with the party's names on it. *Hidden:* her private shelf of secrets she's refused to sell. She dusts it herself.
