Location: [[Homebrew World]] (active in and around Corveth, near the shadow-wound)

The Nightveil is a secretive order of [[Shar]], the Lady of Loss. Their devotion is the giving
away of things in the dark — loss offered freely — and they run it as a long, patient harvest.

Halsby's grandmother was one of theirs, "for a long time, and a good one." Through her they
dedicated [[Halsby]] as a small child, taught him the mourning rite as household manners, and
kept him near the shadow-wound so the fade in his blood would not kill him. From their side he
was never hidden and never lost — only *stored*.

At his grandmother's funeral, [[Sister Maerith]] told him what the rite was, whose name it
addressed, and that they alone could tell him what is in his blood — whenever he cared to come
and ask. He keeps a ledger of what he owes them for the answers. He believes he can close it.
