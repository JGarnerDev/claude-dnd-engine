[[House Westane]] owns the plantation operations, roasting-houses, and — most critically — the sterilizing step. Before any bean leaves the highlands, it is scalded a second time on Westane's order, killing its ability to sprout. Only sterile beans ever reach market.

That one step is the monopoly. Westane built their fortune not by growing [[Coffee]] or controlling its routes, but by ensuring no competitor could ever grow it elsewhere. The process is guarded as closely as any military secret.

The rumor that won't die: somewhere, a smuggler carries seven fertile seeds. Westane's response to this rumor has historically been disproportionate.
