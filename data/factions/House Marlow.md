Holds [[Duskwater]], nearer the Moorthistle, ruled by [[Lady Sorcha Marlow]]. Two
generations back this was the **stronger** house — the shield that marched under its
ancestral aegis-banner to [[House Cael]]'s defense against a coalition of red minor lords,
and held the Moorthistle at the cost of Cael's grandfather's life.

The new, callous generation inherited the leverage without the loyalty. It **broke the
betrothal** that bound the houses — coldly, as insult — and demanded the aegis-banner back.
It turned extortionist, and now quietly courts **red**, the same river-grasping enemy the
grandfathers died stopping, to bleed Cael into submission. In the cruelest turn, Marlow
now re-flies that same aegis-banner, with red's coin, against [[Ashford]].

The raids are Marlow's, but never on paper: they run through [[Harrow Bane]]'s land band
via an intermediary, so the house can disavow them. Pulling that cutout is the thread that
leads back here. This is the predatory side of **"the shield that turned"** — discoverable,
not narrated.
