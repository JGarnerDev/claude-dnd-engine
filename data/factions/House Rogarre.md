[[House Rogarre]] owns the trade routes — every legal shipment of [[Coffee]] that leaves [[Cindermouth Bay]] travels on Rogarre's network or doesn't travel at all. Their grip was locked in by an old family compact that no one has successfully challenged.

Until now. A succession feud is fracturing the house. Rival claimants are courting the other four houses for support, and the compact's legal standing is suddenly under scrutiny. The other houses are watching and waiting.

Rogarre's instability is the most immediate threat to the monopoly's stability. If routes fragment, the whole five-house structure risks collapse.
