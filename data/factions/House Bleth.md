Location: [[Homebrew World]] (Corveth, in Sarmene)

House Bleth is one of the oldest merchant-noble families of **Corveth**, of the **Old Charter**
caste — the elder mercantile line that, during the occupation, climbed *toward* the shadow power
rather than hiding from it. That biography is now a rope: the nation hanged collaborators, and
the house survives only by keeping its history quiet.

The blood belongs to [[Halsby]] by direct descent — he is the last known Bleth heir, though he
has never heard the surname applied to himself. Somewhere in Corveth, ledgers, charter records,
and occupation-era marriage contracts still exist, and somebody would very much prefer the last
Bleth to remain missing.
