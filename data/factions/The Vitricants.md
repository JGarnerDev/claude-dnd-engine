The Vitricants display [[Whatwill]] publicly — polished and precisely lit — so onlookers can become lost in reverie. It is entrancing to gaze upon, and the addiction sets in. At the highest end, Aureum-quality Lodes are exhibited in perfectly controlled environments, with lens arrays focusing light to bestow the full luminous splendor of the piece. LeyLodes, if preserved intact, can induce full reality fracturings in unprepared onlookers.

## The Vitrian Calendar

The Vitricants maintain their own calendar system. When an Opticon is called following the formation of a new Islift, the Vitrian calendar ticks forward, foretelling the approach of a **Glistening** — Astra's brief return to the planet on her thousand-year orbital cycle.

## LeyLode Preservation

When a powerful Kerning heralds the fall of a titanic LeyLode, the Vitricants move to secure and preserve the structure at enormous cost and effort. Two threats drive this urgency: the LeyLode's own deafening hum can eventually shatter it, and [[The Mintners]] will dissect it into anaesthetic tatters for distribution to bankers if given the chance.

## Story Hooks

- Hired by the Vitricants to secure a massive fallen glass structure before it shatters or is stolen
- Steal a fridge-sized Lode from Vitricant holdings
- The Vitricants and Mintners are in direct conflict over a newly fallen LeyLode
