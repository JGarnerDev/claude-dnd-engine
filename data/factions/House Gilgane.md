[[House Gilgane]] owns the land [[Coffee]] grows on — the volcanic ash terraces of [[The Ashreaches]]. They build the terrace infrastructure and parcel settlements out to bonded labor. No other terrain takes the crop; Gilgane's land titles are therefore irreplaceable.

But owning the land is only the first link. Gilgane cannot roast, sterilize, grade, ship, or sell without the other four houses. They hold the most fundamental position in the chain and the least autonomy within it.

The Ashreaches were taken by massacre generations ago. The original growers were wiped out and replaced with bonded labor — a fact recorded quietly in [[House Tomsen]]'s ledger.
