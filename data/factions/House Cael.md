Ruled by [[Lord Corren Cael]]; holds the fief of [[Stonemarch]], which contains
[[Ashford]]. Bound to the distant purple crown, stretched thin, a nominal protector
**failing to defend its own town**. Proud, honest, out-matched.

Two generations back, [[House Cael]] and [[House Marlow]] stood allied against a coalition
of red minor lords pushing to seize the border-river. Marlow was the stronger house — **the
shield** — and marched under its ancestral aegis-banner to Cael's defense. In the last
decisive battle that broke the coalition and held the river, **Cael's grandfather died
shielding Marlow's lord** — a life spent so a Marlow could live. The alliance was sealed in
that blood and a betrothal arranged to bind the houses.

That debt is now Cael's open wound. The new generation of [[House Marlow]] broke the
betrothal coldly, turned extortionist, and quietly courts **red** — the same enemy the
grandfather died stopping — to bleed Cael into submission. Corren cannot fully hate the
house that once died beside his; he remembers heroic-Marlow and the life he is owed. The
grief is both his weakness and his dignity. This is the dignified side of **"the shield
that turned."**
