Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]] | One of [[The Blackmarket Guildsects]]

The Post is the guildsect that carries things: a loose, underground delivery system that runs through the whole world with an efficiency nobody can explain. A letter handed over in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] can be in someone's hand on the far side of Aethryn within days, and couriers find people who don't want to be found. Nobody quite knows how they do it. Their motto: *"Nothing stops the letters from getting through."*

- **How you use it (what people know):** you don't find the Post; it finds you. Leave a sealed letter with a coin under it at a drop (a loose brick, a shrine's offering bowl, a certain barstool), whisper the name it's for, and by morning it's gone. Payment is coin, a secret or a favour owed, and the Post always collects.
- **Postmasters** run the branches, usually behind an honest front. The brothers Post's post office in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] is one: Pip is its postmaster. The dead courier's coat Barnaby found in the ice belonged to one of the Post's couriers.
- **Couriers** deliver by hand. Nobody sees them arrive: a letter is simply put into your hand by someone in a plain coat who is gone before you can ask a question.
- **Their marks:** the FORWARD and RETURN TO SENDER stamps in [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]] are Post marks, and every Post seal carries a small closed envelope pressed into the wax.
- **The one thing they never sell:** what they carry. In [[Cinto]], where every secret has a price, that makes the Post the only people nobody can buy. Archivist [[Ebling Mis]] would pay anything to learn how the Post works: a Forbidden secret, if anyone ever finds out.
- **[[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]'s letter:** Choco La handed it to Pip in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] before [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] closed [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]]. It was on its way to [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] when he and Harrow Bane's band robbed the Post's shipment, and he found his own name on it. He robbed his own mail.
- **Rolls:** knowing the Post exists, and that its couriers are real, is INT (History) DC 12, or automatic for anyone who has lived rough or worked on the wrong side of the law. Finding a drop in a new town takes asking the wrong sort of people: CHA (Persuasion) DC 15, or a Gossip secret.

**[[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] and the Post: a possible blacklisting.** The Post never forgets a robbed sack. Robbing one of its shipments, even riding with Bane's band and even for a letter addressed to himself, has put [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]'s name on a postmasters' list marked "under review." Whether he ends up blacklisted depends on how he handles it.

- **Signs it's coming:** in [[Cinto]], a letter for [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] turns up on his pillow at [[The Last Fire|the Last Fire]], empty except for a RETURN TO SENDER stamp. Anyone he asks about a drop suddenly doesn't know what he means.
- **If he's blacklisted:** no courier carries anything he sends, no drop takes his coin, and anything addressed to him comes back stamped RETURN TO SENDER. The other Blackmarket guildsects hear about it too: prices go up for him in every black market, and he has disadvantage on Persuasion with their members.
- **Pip's problem:** in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], Pip is the postmaster who has to decide. He likes [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]], he's thrilled the letter got through, and he's heartbroken about the sack. Telling Pip the truth and asking for a second chance is CHA (Persuasion) DC 13. Lying is Deception against Pip's passive Insight 12; if it fails, Pip blacklists [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] himself, in tears.
- **Clearing his name:** make the Post whole. Deliver what's left of the robbed sack, if any of it survived; carry the Claus letters down to the core for Pip; or owe the Post a favour, which it will collect at the worst possible moment. Any one of these takes his name off the list, and Pip stamps it FORWARD.
- **Choice ([[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]):** own up to the Post, or keep quiet and hope nobody connects him to the robbery.

## Another sect: the grave-goods trade (DM-only)

When the party learns about the guildsects, let one more sect surface: the one that sells grave goods. Its prize stock is grave markers with no death date, cut from the hull of [[The Iron Leviathan|the Iron Leviathan]]. The ship isn't from this world, and its iron has strange properties: the grief society believes a marker of otherworld iron keeps the dead from ever properly ending. Someone has been quietly cutting neat squares out of the inside of the hull, down in the deep hold where nobody goes, and carrying them off.

- **The buyer:** the sect's best customer is a lord or merchant-prince who buys the markers in bulk and quietly funds the grief society. He isn't grieving; he's afraid of his own story ending (Jeff's "Patron Who Won't Die", for [[Iggy]]). Leave him offstage and unnamed for now.
- **How the party hears of it:** Pip, if [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] is on good terms with the Post, mutters that "the iron lot" ship their crates through the Post and pay double for silence. Or [[Shadwell]] offers to sell the party "a souvenir from the big ship": a small iron tablet, blank where a death date should be.
- **[[The Matron]] doesn't know.** If she finds out someone is stripping her ship, that's a whole new hook in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], and a way to win her favour for good.

## How they're so fast (DM-only, a possibility)

the Post moves letters through the thin places between worlds. A letter slipped into one crack comes out of another. That would explain why Pip's letters to "the Claus" always reach the core, and why [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]] sometimes hears a faint rustle of paper in the static. Keep it or drop it.
