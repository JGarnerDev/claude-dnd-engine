The Mintners inhale [[Whatwill]] dust, dosed via the eyes — trickling a slurry into the tear ducts. They believe the experience transports them to another realm entirely.

## Manufactories

The Mintners operate **Mint manufactories** where Whatwill is processed and refined into distributable form. They will dissect LeyLodes into **anaesthetic tatters** for distribution to bankers and other high-society clients — in direct conflict with [[The Vitricants]], who wish to preserve LeyLodes intact.

Their refinement work carries significant danger: fracturing Whatwill exposes the reactive metallic compound to nitrogen-rich air. The Mintners have, at least once, overestimated their ability to contain a Shard explosion within a manufactory.

## The Other Realm

What realm the Mintners believe the dust transports them to is unknown. Whether it is real, a shared hallucination, or something more dangerous has not been established.
