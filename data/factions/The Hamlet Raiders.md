## Overview

The strangers who arrived at [[Dip's Home Hamlet]] friendly, earned its trust, then turned — sent to slaughter everyone. They favored a stealth approach over open swords, and it partly worked, until the tavern keeper, Brannoc Hale, torched his own roost to trap them and the fire spread to the whole hamlet.

Dip remembers them as friends who turned into monsters, hate hidden behind smiles and laughter over many moons of shared meals, an evil fuelled by **bloodlust**. Whether "monsters" is literal is open. Most burned alive in Brannoc's fire; a few fled. Brannoc's sacrifice stopped their ideology cold in Emberhollow, but not everywhere. Dip's season goal is to finish them off.

Dip fears Vessa went beyond the veil and came back as one of these monsters.

## The Hook

Someone sent them. Who, and why a small hamlet warranted a slaughter contract, is unknown — the thread [[Dip]] would pull to find them. Their fate is equally open: disbanded, still working, or now serving a larger antagonist the campaign can grow into.
