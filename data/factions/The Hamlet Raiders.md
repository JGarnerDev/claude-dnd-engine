## Overview

The strangers who arrived at [[Dip's Home Hamlet]] friendly, earned its trust, then turned — sent to slaughter everyone. They favored a stealth approach over open swords, and it partly worked, until the tavern keeper torched his own roost to trap them and the fire spread to the whole hamlet.

## The Hook

Someone sent them. Who, and why a small hamlet warranted a slaughter contract, is unknown — the thread [[Dip]] would pull to find them. Their fate is equally open: disbanded, still working, or now serving a larger antagonist the campaign can grow into.
