The Circle of Orro'ssha is the druid order at the heart of the Marrow Concord's central forest — the clergy of the sleeping demigod [[Orrowan]] and the hands behind the long, patient work of waking it. Orrowan's dreams *are* the forest's magic, and the Circle is how that magic is read and tended: dream-walkers, forest-shapers, keepers of the vigil that holds the wood whole so the god may one day rise.

Its name tells its secret. **Orro'ssha** is a Sesh'kar word — the lizardfolk's first-tongue name for the Deep Dreamer, older than the elves. The Circle was *founded* by the Sesh'kar, who knew and named Orrowan before the Orrow ever walked the wood. The elves joined generations later, and being many where the lizardfolk are few, they became its face: the visible druids at the tree, the numbers, the daily rites. But those rites are inherited, and the Orrow perform them earnestly without fully grasping what they mean. Behind the elven face sits a quiet core of Sesh'kar elder-keepers who hold the founding lore — and, almost certainly, the last of the waking-knowledge the elves have never actually understood. The Orrow believe they are the wakers. The Sesh'kar know they are the ones who *could* wake it. That reckoning has not yet come.

Outward, the Circle is the gate. Every settler of Solenne who wants timber, every arcane cartel that covets a demigod tree's dream-magic, every pilgrim who wants to stand at the roots — all of them must pass the Circle first, and it does not sell what it guards. It is the anti-guild: uncontracted, unpriced, rooted. Its danger is not gold but conviction, and its deepest fault line runs quietly through its own two peoples.
