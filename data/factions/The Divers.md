The Divers are the piratical faction of the aerial world — the underside to [[The Climbers]]' establishment. They emerged from within [[The Lifted]]'s society as a counter-power, operating from cave networks in [[Darkling's Perch]] and maintaining covert cells throughout [[The Grey Reaches]] and even inside [[Arclight's Seat]] itself.

Their primary enterprise is trade interception: monitoring Landborne-Lifted commerce from observation posts like [[Ord]] and raiding it from bastions like [[Ordir'q]].

## Ordir'qurzk

The Diver capitol is a sprawling subterranean crawl carved into the entire interior of their main Islift. The cave network is vast enough to lose armies in.

## Propaganda

[[Ordi]], locked to the underside of the neutral parlay Islift [[Aztala]], serves as a Temple to Abandon and propaganda engine. The Arclight Council's worldview is being shaped from below without their knowledge.

## Story Hooks

- Expose Ord before a critical trade shipment is intercepted
- Navigate Ordir'qurzk to reach the Diver leadership
