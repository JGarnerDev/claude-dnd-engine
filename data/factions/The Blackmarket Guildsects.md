Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]] | New canon from Ben, 2026-11-25

a loose web of underground guilds, each one a "sect" with a single trade nobody licenses. They have no capital, no charter and no leader anyone can name. They deal through black markets, back rooms and whispers, and keep a few old courtesies: never sell out another sect, never name a member to the law, and pay what you owe in the currency asked, whether that's coin, a secret or a favour. The Post is the first sect the party meets. Any others are yours to add ([[Shadwell]], the notebook thief, could belong to one).

- [[The Post]]: the sect that carries things.
