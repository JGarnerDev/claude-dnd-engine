The Templar Triumvirate is seated at [[Tassad]] — three towers of marble, onyx, and malachite, each roughly half a kilometre across and reaching nearly a full kilometre into the air. The towers converge on a suspended "saucer" structure hanging above them, some two and a half kilometres across, where the Triumvirate's shared business is done.

Each tower houses one of three mage-guilds. Each guild oversees a **limited portfolio of magic schools** — no single tower commands the whole of the arcane. The exact division of schools between marble, onyx, and malachite is not yet fixed.

## The Archon Council

The three guilds answer to the **Archon Council**, headed by the **[[Grand Archon]]**. The Council arbitrates portfolio boundaries between the towers and sets the terms of recognition for casters in the region.

## The Templars

The Templars are the Triumvirate's enforcement arm. They **police magic use** across the region and maintain a record of every recognised **Speaker of the Word** — a licensed, known caster. This makes the Triumvirate the extreme end of the guild-policing spectrum described in [[Magic & the Arcane]]: mage-police, registries, and enforcement, rather than light outreach. An unrecognised Speaker operating in Templar territory is a problem to be found and registered — or dealt with.

## Story Hooks

- The party's caster is stopped and asked to prove recognition as a Speaker of the Word
- A portfolio dispute between two towers spills into open guild rivalry the party can exploit
- Hired to escort, protect, or expose an unregistered caster the Templars are hunting
- Something in the suspended saucer holds a record the party needs — and the towers guard it jealously
