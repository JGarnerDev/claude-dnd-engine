The Lifted live on floating géode islands called **Islifts** — smaller landmasses that orbit at varying altitudes, some a fathom above ground, others hundreds of fathoms up where air is thin. These originated from the Arc Side of the dwarf planetoid, dislodged when [[Astra]] collided with the Struck Side millennia ago.

They believe [[Whatwill]] is the source of propulsion and geo-stabilisation keeping their Islifts aloft.

## Opticon

When a particularly powerful Showering touchdown forms a new Islift, the Lifted call an **Opticon** — a gathering or council of significance. The Vitrian calendar ticks forward at this event, foretelling the approach of a Glistening.

## The Stapling

It is seen as tragedy when a Showering strikes an Islift rather than ground — interpreted as Astra falling out of favour with her chosen peoples. The worst conceivable outcome is a **Stapling**: an Islift brought crashing back to the earth by the impact, forcing total abandonment of the island and all who lived upon it. This is both a material and spiritual catastrophe.
