Secular scientists and academics. The Scholars study [[Whatwill]] as a natural phenomenon — impact chemistry, orbital mechanics, exposure neurology, resonance physics. They reject mystical interpretation while using vocabulary inherited from mystics because no scientific terminology works better.

Public position: Whatwill is natural, even if not yet understood. Not every mystery is divine.

Secret fear: the equations are beginning to resemble liturgy.

Funded by [[The Lifted]], [[Extraction Guilds]], and the state — all of whom expect favorable results. Scholars who publish honestly tend to publish briefly. Some have stopped publishing entirely because their results change after Kernings.

Notable figure: Preva Dorn, whose 14-volume Latticing study began documenting Dorn's own Latticing midway through. Volume fourteen was published posthumously in an unidentified hand.
