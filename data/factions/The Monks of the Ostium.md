Locations: [[Deorum Ostium]], [[The Cupola Temple]] | Leader: [[Aurelian Sellenor]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

A single order of monks oversees the Deorum Ostium. They believe a site this holy can't belong to one faith, so they act only as stewards, helping others find the answers they seek. Their motto: "We keep the door, not the key." They carve continuously, and some carvings in the deep chambers haven't been seen in centuries.

In the ending where the party tells the Archhierophant about the core, the monks become its keepers and open a new, harder inner pilgrimage for the worthy.
