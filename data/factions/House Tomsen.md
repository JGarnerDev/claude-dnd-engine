[[House Tomsen]] owns the ledger. They grade and price every legal bean of [[Coffee]] — quiet, unseen, never touching a sack. No house can sell without Tomsen's grade; no grade exists without Tomsen's say.

They burn surplus after good harvests to keep the price brutal. A sealed crate worth more than a working ox is not an accident — it is policy, maintained deliberately, recorded in Tomsen's hand.

The ledger also records the massacre that founded [[The Ashreaches]]: the original growers wiped out, the land seized, the bonded labor installed. Tomsen has held that record for generations. Whether it is kept as insurance, as guilt, or as threat is unknown — and that ambiguity is itself a form of power.
