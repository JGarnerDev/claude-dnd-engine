Vaskgrath is the mining nation of the north — the polity of the range **[[Orngrath]]**, whose dwarvish name it shares: *vask* (iron) welded to the range's own *grath*. Outsiders shorten it to **the Grath**. It is the richest metal country in the basin and one of the poorest in ready coin, because everything that makes it wealthy — iron, coal, silver, and the rumored gold and gems deeper still — has to leave the mountains the same way, and every step of that road belongs to someone else.

The nation is not one people but an uneasy weld of two. The dwarves cut these holds first and named them; then some clans stayed, some holds went dark, and the dark holds were reoccupied by human mining towns. What governs the result is the **Grath-Moot** — a council of dwarf hold-lords and human town-bosses with no single ruler and no easy agreement. Its one reliable glue is an enemy: **Blue**, the river power downstream, which holds the harbor and the river-fork chokepoint and taxes every load of Vaskgrath metal that comes down the pass at Mountain Gate. Whoever chairs the Moot keeps that grievance hot on purpose, because a coalition this fractured needs somewhere outside to point.

The fracture runs deepest at the sealed dead holds. They are, by every rumor, the deep wealth — the gold and gems that could pay for the arms buildup outright. The human towns want them reopened; they need the coin and do not share the dread. The dwarf clans veto, because they remember why those holds went dark, and treaty holds the line — for now. This is the nation's engine: an outside economic squeeze (Blue's toll) pushing Vaskgrath toward the one act (breaking the seals) that would endanger it from below. The toll fight papers over the dwarf/human fault; the deep-hold question is exactly where that fault reopens. It is where the human town-bosses' hired **[[Nadirium]]** Readers descend, on contracts the dwarves call blasphemy.

Jeff's contribution — the Pink nation of the Calenmere basin: a mountain confederation defined by the tolls that tax it and the sealed holds that divide it.
