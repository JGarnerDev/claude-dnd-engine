Urban legal bodies that adjudicate jurisdiction over [[Whatwill]] landing sites. When a Kerning sounds, urbanites race to the Courts Superior to file competing claims — nobles, merchant houses, civic authorities all asserting primacy.

The process is self-defeating. Deliberation slows to a crawl while [[The Partenariats]] quietly dispatch expeditions and extract. By the time a ruling issues, the site is often already stripped.

- Play lawyer at a Court Superior — argue jurisdiction for a client with everything on the line
