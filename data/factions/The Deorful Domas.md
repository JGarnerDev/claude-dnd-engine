# The Deorful Domas

The quiet hands behind [[Fargrsh]]. They move like the plant itself — they could be the beggar on the corner or the judge in the courtroom, proving truly difficult to find and track. They operate across [[The World]] wherever ground is well-trodden enough for the plant to grow.

## Practice

The Domas disseminate knowledge of the plant and how to prepare it. They appear to hold some mystical ability stemming from *metered* use: they deprive themselves of certain things in order to know the fullness the plant can grant, and existing in both states at once — want and satiation together — seems to be their calling.

## Doctrine

The Domas believe everyone needs to be using the plant, and that the plant will cleanse the earth, ushering in a new holy age.

## Story Hooks

- The plant gives what you do not have — in the right hands it can sway a populace. Whose hands are the Domas guiding it toward?
- A Doma is discovered somewhere impossible to ignore: the judge, the priest, the quartermaster.
- Someone seeks the Domas' tutelage for a ceremony of "great good" — and cannot handle it.
