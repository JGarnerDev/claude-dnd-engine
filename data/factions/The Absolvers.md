Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

The grief society of [[The Correctors]] promises that no goodbye is final. The Absolvers, preachers of [[The Nul|Nul]], promise that every goodbye is a kindness. They sit with the dying, help the old let go, and speak against necromancers, liches and the grief society's "patient ones" as plainly as any temple would. At first they look like the good side of the argument.

> **Reliability:** Jeff and Paul's late-game decision (2026-11-27). "The Absolvers" is a working name. Pool entity; scale, members and first appearance are open.

## The Mirror

- The grief society reads the names of the dead aloud. The Absolvers burn the names so the dead can rest.
- The grief society carves grave-markers with no death date. The Absolvers carve markers with no birth date: only the ending matters.
- Both are answering the same grief. Each thinks the other is the danger, and each is partly right.

> **DM-only:** The two cults are rivals, and Nul wins either way. The Absolvers serve it openly. The grief society feeds it blind, because the Returned are Nul's eyes: every crude return is a window it looks through. The pact-voice under the grief society is its own villain, separate from Nul (Dip's thread stays separate).
>
> Their leader is [[The First Corrector]], who founded the movement that now becomes the grief society. The preachers do not know their prophet's past.

## Hooks

- An Absolver at a deathbed the party is trying to save, sincerely offering comfort.
- Absolvers speak of a rider coming to finish what was left unfinished: [[Farzrig]], whom they await as a saint.
- They condemn [[Sah]] by name, a god who keeps meaning past its end.
- The Absolvers and the grief society fight in the street, and the party is asked to pick a side.
- An Absolver recognizes [[Iggy]] or [[Luci]] as "unfinished" and is gently, terribly kind about it.
