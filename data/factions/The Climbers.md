The Climbers are the establishment faction of [[The Lifted]]'s aerial society — the traders, administrators, and political power behind the Arclight Council. Their seat is [[Arclight's Seat]], their spiritual and political center is [[Aztalainira]], and their highest authority is the Opticon, convened there whenever the Vitrian calendar marks a new Islift formation.

They sustain themselves on Landborne-Lifted trade — the flow of goods between surface peoples and the aerial world. [[Aztalaina]] provides the food and medicinal supply that makes their population density possible.

## What They Don't Know

[[Ord]] — a Diver observation post — operates inside [[Arclight's Seat]], tracking their trade flows. The Arclight Council's political views are being quietly shaped by [[Ordi]]'s propaganda engine in [[The Grey Reaches]].
