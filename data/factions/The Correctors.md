Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

The Correctors began as an intellectual splinter within the Great Library of **[[Threnai]]** — archivists, scholars, and theorists with intimate knowledge of how meaning and preservation functioned. Their **[[The First Corrector]]** articulated the belief that death was not natural but a consequence of being *recorded and finalized*, and that the plane's system had to be broken to free existence from endings. Early, apparently successful returns of the dead — plus grief and fear — built momentum. Their spine-breaking ritual destroyed Threnai instead of saving it.

> **Reliability:** Player-backstory canon for [[Iggy]]. Free (`exists: false`) future antagonist — deliberately open in scale and structure per the backstory's DM-flexibility note.

## Present-Day Hook (Material Plane)

The ideology persists as a subtle, distributed cult of survivors or successors — possibly still steered by hidden elements of the original leadership. It operates on three vectors, any of which a DM can scale from a local cell to a broad conspiracy:

- **Covert cult** — conducting experiments and influence operations around death and continuity.
- **Academic/arcane fronts** — institutions exploring death and continuity, knowingly or not.
- **Memetic spread** — the idea propagating on its own, independent of any center.

Their returns follow a corruption arc (the **Returned**) documented in Iggy's file: Convincing Return → Pale Echo → Intrusion → Degradation.

## In Aethryn: The Grief Society (Player Seed)

Jeff's handout answer for Iggy (2026-10-13). This is a seed, not canon.

It starts as a respectable circle for the bereaved, meeting in a parlor or chapel, promising that "no goodbye is final." The early meetings are gentle: reading the names of the dead aloud, carving grave-markers with no death date, agreeing that endings were only ever something written down. Deeper in, it splits into heresies. Some chase **lichdom**, keeping the self in a vessel, a record that never closes. Others raise the dead crudely and call them "the patient ones." Under both is **pact magic**: something answers when they call into the dark and promises to keep the dead from dying. The Correctors believed what lies beyond the ending was empty. In Aethryn it has a voice and a price, and it is learning to sound like the people they lost.

Three who are tempted, each a thread of one larger arc:

- **A grieving parent** who joined to hear their child's name read aloud and stayed for the voice that answered.
- **Someone powerful and afraid of dying,** a lord or merchant-prince who funds the society quietly and wants the lich-path for himself.
- **A good healer** tired of losing patients, who decided ending death is the only real cure.

> **DM-only:** None of the three knows the others are involved. The homebrew's climax is the moment all three threads meet the thing they've been feeding: a demon, or something older, wearing the Correctors' philosophy as a mask. Already seeded in canon: [[Tassad]]'s spreading fashion for grave-markers with no death date. Unconfirmed link: [[Halsby]]'s open Tassad rumor could lead here.
