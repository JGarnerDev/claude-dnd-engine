The glaciated southern continent. Named by the ancient arctic peoples who have lived here since before written record — the name means approximately "the deep place" or "the below." Something is buried under the ice; the indigenous peoples have built their spiritual life around knowing it is there without naming what it is. Outsider expeditions — scientific and magical — have been arriving with increasing frequency.
