The mountain range that backs and defines [[Hightalon]]. Multi-function: resource extraction fuels the forge culture, altitude provides sky pirate launch elevation, mage guild towers are built into the rock, and the peaks command strategic visibility over sea approaches. Everything [[Hightalon]] does, the Watchwall makes possible.
