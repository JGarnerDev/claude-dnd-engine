An enclosed inner sea flanked by mountain-ringed coasts and narrow channel approaches. Dwarven influence from [[Hightalon]] means access is commercially controlled — everyone pays. Functions as much as a channel as a sea. The name is functional: the iron gate is the toll, the dwarven hand on the passage.
