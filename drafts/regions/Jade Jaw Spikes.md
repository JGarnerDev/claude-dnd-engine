Eastern continent mountain range. Jungle-covered from base to treeline and beyond — the forests crown the peaks rather than bare stone. Dinosaurs inhabit the slopes. No meaningful civilization; the range is simply wild in the oldest sense. The name comes from the view from below: green spikes like the jaw of something enormous.
