Small, scattered, center-left ocean. No single island is notable — the group is the thing. Navigators use them collectively as waypoints, anchoring points, resupply stops. Uncountable in number at a glance, hence the name. Nobody lives there by preference, but they make open-water crossings significantly less lethal.
