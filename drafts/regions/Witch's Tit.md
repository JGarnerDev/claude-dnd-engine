The cold northern strip of [[Erevast]], wedged between the treeline and the arctic. Named bluntly by those who have to cross it. Not quite tundra, not quite arctic — a transitional zone that is unpleasant in a very specific seasonal way. The people who live adjacent to it named it without poetry and without apology.
