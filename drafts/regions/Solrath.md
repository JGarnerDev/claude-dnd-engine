An enclosed desert sub-continent that controls the ocean trade lanes of the central world. Hard to approach, hot, and strategically indispensable. Proximity to [[Karath Ut'll]] gives it cultural complexity — desert peoples from both regions intersect here. Nobody fully owns it; everybody needs it.
