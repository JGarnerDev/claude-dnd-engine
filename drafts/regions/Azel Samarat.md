The sacred desert. Every culture that knows of it has a name for it; Azel Samarat is the cross-cultural term that stuck. It belongs to no one — no nation claims it, no army holds it, no religion owns it exclusively. Pilgrims arrive from everywhere. The desert itself seems aware: old things are buried here, and the sand holds what happened above it. Departure point: [[Essaur Bay]] to the north.
