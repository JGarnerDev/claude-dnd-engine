Small Sand. The name is accurate and unambiguous. Secondary arid zone neighboring [[Azel Samarat]], without the scale or reputation of the larger desert. The locals who named it had no patience for myth-making — it is smaller sand, adjacent to the larger sand. That is the whole of it.
