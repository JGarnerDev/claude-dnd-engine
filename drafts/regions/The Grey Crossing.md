The southern passage between [[Erevast]]'s eastern tail and the glaciated continent of [[Ilurak Qeth]]. Stormy but regularly sailed — scientific expeditions, magic guild vessels, and the occasional supply run to [[Ilurak Qeth]]'s indigenous peoples. The grey is the sky, not the water; the crossing is named for what you see most of the way through.
