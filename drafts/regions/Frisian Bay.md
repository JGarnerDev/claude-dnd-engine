Named for the pack mules discovered along its shores — lean, hardy animals that proved immediately useful for carrying, meat, and hides. The name stuck, and the bay became associated with practical industry rather than trade prestige. Prominent north-facing bay, upper-center [[Erevast]]. Good anchorage, reliable conditions.
