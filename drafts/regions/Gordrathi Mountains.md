The great spine of the continent. Tallest range on the map. Ancient bestial powers inhabit or underlie these peaks — the name itself predates the current civilizations and was given by something non-human. Dark secrets are buried here, but the mountains are not malevolent. They are simply very, very old, and something vast lives in or beneath them.
