Interior bay on [[Erevast]]'s center-left coast, veiled by a fog that doesn't behave like ordinary fog. It can be entered — those who know the approach get through. Those who don't drift or miss it entirely. It has a reputation as a refuge: whatever you've done, Slor Bay doesn't ask. The fog keeps it off most charts.
