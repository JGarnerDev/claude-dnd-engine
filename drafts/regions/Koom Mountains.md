Steep-sided and enclosing. The Koom Mountains press in on travelers from all sides, but not with menace — there's a quality to the enclosure that feels more like shelter than threat. Interior range, landlocked, removed from the coastal drama of larger formations. The valleys here are quiet in a specific, contained way.
