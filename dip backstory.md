Dips Backstory:

Dip comes from the woods, but he didn't always come from there. dip remembers a past life with parents but being shunned by society. from a young age dip seemed more aware of the nuances of society than those around him. he also learned really fast that being vocal as an orc was a bad idea. keeping his mouth shut to save the hassle more often than he would have liked.

dips strongest memories from being a child are when some people came to their town and acted friendly but as it turned out they were actually sent to slaughter them all and instead of just coming in swords swinging decided to try a stealth attack after gaining the trust of everyone in the small hamlet. it partially succeeded but it was cut short when they couldn't quite kill the tavern keeper who then set ablaze to his roost to trap them all inside. unsuccessfully though that led to the whole town catching fire and many perished. dip succeeded in escaping alongside several others. dip lost his twin sister in the blaze. this leads to dips motivation. dip never believed that the sister had perished. he has visions of many hands guiding and pushing him along but his sisters hands are neither in the lead or offering assistance so this leads dip to believe she must still exist on this realm to some degree!

dip wandered for many years eventually falling in line with some spiritual folks who helped him understand the realities that converge on this plane of existence. During his time at the enclave Dip learned the ways of the sage and how powerful meditation can be when communing with others over the multiple planes that exist. during certain festivals when the powers of worship pull people together dip can feel out the weaknesses in reality and is more in tune with the ethereal.

The enclave helped Dip find knowledge in many avenues but only a few really drew dip in. The ones that Dip has been most inspired with have been the literatures of gods and goddesses and their interventions in our lives. the places of holy worship that bring many together has been a point of interest for Dips Pilgrimages as they try to understand the complex relationship of the divine and the mortal.
