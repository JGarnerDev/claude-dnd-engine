Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

Sah was the real and present divine force of **[[Threnai]]**, the god who upheld its function as a keeper of meaning and continuity. His followers, the Sahists, held that meaning is cumulative and lived, preservation a duty, and death a painful but necessary boundary that protects reality from something worse.

When **[[The Correctors]]** enacted the ritual to break the plane's spine, Sah's earlier attempt to correct the first Returned — a resurrected Corrector who resisted the plane's own rules — failed publicly, a turning point in the war. At the collapse, Sah forcibly displaced **[[Iggy]]** to the Material Plane to save him: *"Go now, there are other worlds than these."*

> **Reliability:** Player-backstory canon for Iggy. Sourced to Iggy's debut session; predates the campaign as origin lore. Whether Sah survived intact, was diminished, or persists fractured is deliberately open for DM interpretation.

## Current State

Diminished, intermittent, not fully coherent. Communicates with Iggy through partial impressions — emotional resonance, tonal fragments, brief clarity — rather than sustained dialogue. His coherence strengthens as Iggy grows: a reciprocal relationship driven by lived experience, not doctrine.

- **Passive:** an emotional undercurrent — tired, mournful, quietly hopeful; at peace with his diminished state.
- **Active:** symbolic impressions in the archetype of an old martial master — calm, stern, instructive; his old certainty tempered by awareness that it was flawed. Sees Iggy as both student and conduit for his own growth.
- **Critical:** rare fragments of clarity — recovered songs, lessons, and memories of Threnai — that unlock new understanding, reinforce resolve, or seed the emergent **New Sahism**.

Rather than rebuild the old Sahist system, Sah and Iggy develop an adaptive philosophy: meaning as lived and participated in; balance and continuity without rigid preservation; acceptance of change without surrendering coherence. Followers may gather organically around Iggy's actions over time.

**Direction of the reformation (player intent, 2026-10-13):** the old Sah kept meaning in one place, the Library, and treated an ending as a wall around it, which held until the place fell. The new path is **remembrance**: meaning lasts because people carry it in songs, retellings and companions who remember each other. A library can burn, but a song that has been passed on can't be burned. Every story must end, but the telling doesn't. Iggy's vow: *"What is done, I will remember. What must end, I will let end. What I remember, I will carry."*

> **DM-only:** At the collapse, Sah spent part of his remaining power keeping the power invested in Iggy's unfinished final song alive after Threnai was gone. The player's wish is that the reformation completes when Iggy breaks his vow for a dying friend by finishing the song: choosing which story ends, not refusing every ending. Full scenario in [[Iggy]] (Oath & Convictions).
