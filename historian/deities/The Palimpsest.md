## Overview

The scribes of [[The Citadel]] call it **The Palimpsest**. It governs the plane of the gnome Navigators and nothing past it: ambient cosmic weather, a switchboard-god that now and then answers in words meaning two things at once.

It is the reason the transcribers' hearth-channel crosses planes at all. Burn a page to send; read the reply in the embers. The medium is living and dangerous, and it runs on soul-resonance.

## Nothing Is Erased

A palimpsest never truly erases. Older, "erased" messages sometimes ghost up beneath a fresh one, and nothing sent through its plane is ever wholly gone. It is the archive that quietly keeps every word ever passed through it.

## Boundaries

- It is *not* [[Astra]].
- It has nothing to do with why copies exist.
- The name is dramatic irony: [[Odibli]] is himself a palimpsest (DM-only).
