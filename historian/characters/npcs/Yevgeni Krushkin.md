Location: [[Blue Water Inn]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A grim wolf-hunter who works the land around [[Vallaki]] with his partner [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] — the two are the "fur boys." They kill wolves and sell them for a living.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

Together with [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]], greeted the party at the [[Blue Water Inn]], praised the performance, and offered guiding and information — 5 gp for a day, bits of information for drinks.
