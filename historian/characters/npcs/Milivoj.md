## Background

Milivoj tends the grounds and digs the graves at the [[Church of St. Andril]] in [[Vallaki]], working under the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]].

## First Encounter (Session 17)

Under the party's questioning Milivoj grew nervous and confessed: he had stolen the church's sacred saint's bones and sold them to [[Henrik]] the coffinmaker for a couple of gold pieces. He did not understand the bones' importance — that they granted the church a boon of protection. Henrik keeps a shop near the town square.

> **Reliability:** The exact sum Milivoj received is recorded loosely (the notes cite both "10 gp" for the bones' sale and "a couple of pieces of gold"). Confirm with DM.
