Location: [[Burgomaster's Mansion]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Daughter of the late Burgomaster [[Kolyan Indirovich]] and sister of [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]. [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] has taken a dangerous fixation on her — Ismark described it as an "ancient danger" taking a liking to her.

## First Encounter (Session 11)

Party met Ireena at the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]] after Ismark led them there. She explained that wolves and creatures had attacked the mansion and her father died from the trauma of the assault. Her immediate ask: help bury him.

[[Odibli]] was moved and eager to help. The party accepted.

## Known Threats

[[Strahd Von Zarovich]] has fixated on Ireena. The nature of this fixation is not yet fully understood by the party, but Ismark considers it mortal danger.

## Lineage Revelation (Session 13)

During [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s burial at the [[Church of the Morning Lord]], [[Father Donavich]] revealed that Ireena is **not a blood relative** of the Kolyanovich family. Ireena was visibly shocked. Donavich spoke of "trials that await her" without elaborating.

## Her True Origin (Session 19)

The open question from Session 13 — if not a Kolyanovich, then who? — was answered in [[Session 19 The Burning of the Coffin Shop]]. The party noticed Ireena and the Vallaki guard captain **[[Izek Strazni]]** share the same face, and before the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]] the truth surfaced: **Ireena and Izek are blood siblings.** She was born a **Strazni** and adopted into the Kolyanovich family. Izek, born without a soul, had forgotten he had a sister at all.

> **Reliability:** This is consistent with — and resolves — the Session 13 revelation that Ireena is not a blood Kolyanovich. The full history of the Strazni siblings, and how Ireena came to be adopted, is not yet established. Confirm with DM.

## Departure

Following the burial, Ireena agreed to travel to [[Vallaki]] with the party. Her brother [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] joined as a companion. Both departed [[Barovia Village]] after Session 13.

## Destination

[[Vallaki]] — a walled town 2–3 days west of [[Barovia Village]]. Ismark believes she will be safer there.
