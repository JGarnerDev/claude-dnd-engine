Location: [[The Golden Lioness]]

Race: tiefling | Class: [[Warlock]]

[[Sable Wren]] runs [[The Golden Lioness]]. She was hired to exorcise something from the
building, made friends with it instead, and received the building as payment. What she found
was **[[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]]** — a singular ancient deep-water being in the
aquifer the tavern's cellar spring drops into, whom she calls **Asher**. She talked to it
instead of banishing it, was not disgusted by it, and has been bringing it the world's small
news and good drink ever since. He nudged her toward this building early on, in a way she
noticed but did not question. She has never been certain how much of the dream was hers. She
decided it does not matter.

What she gets out of it: the cleanest water in a town whose river is going bad, and the
ability to bless and charge deep-water for brewing. What he gets is a clean well used for
life, someone opposing the fouling, and company.

She came to [[Ashford]] about a week before the raid and runs the place alone for now. She means
to take on [[Lok Thuliaga]] and [[Bree Tealeaf]].

## Canon — Session 29

- **Raided and taken.** Raiders working for [[Nella]] stripped the half-built new wing of the
  Lioness, blew a hole in its side, and bound and gagged Sable. A hedge-mage's *hold person*
  scroll was spent on her before the party arrived (per the plan). The raiders' leader, "the
  big one", was riding off with her on his horse when [[Halsby]]'s magic missiles killed him.
  The horse spooked and she fell clear.
- **Where she stands at session end:** on the ground in the yard, **still bound and gagged**.
  She has not yet spoken to the party, and none of them has spoken to her.
- **Asher sent help.** [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]], through a possessed fox, sent Halsby
  to save her: "Sable is the woman's name, and she will need your help in the future." The fox
  described her eyes as light gray. It said she rarely laughs, and that when she does it is
  "like the really cool kid at school that laughs at you when you drop your food."

> **Reliability:** the eye colour and the laugh come from Asher's fox, describing her secondhand
> to Halsby. Treat them as Asher's view of her, not an established description. Confirm with
> DM (Jeff) before using them as her canonical look.

## Canon — Session 30

- **Freed and practical.** Her first move after [[Luci]] and [[Dip]] cut her loose was to put
  out the fire, one small pail at a time. Then she invited her rescuers to dinner: soup and
  hardtack in the one room still standing.
- **A tiefling, three or four generations removed.** She has seen Aasimar before, just none as
  "extreme" as Luci, and finds the party exotic rather than hostile. She had never heard of
  Barovia.
- **New in town.** She arrived about a week before the raid to open Ashford's first tavern. She
  borrowed about 1,000 gp from [[Silas Crubb]] and still owes about 650 gp. The stolen first stock
  was meant to pay it off; without it she likely loses her permit.
- **The offer.** Rooms, free meals, a stay of a month or two, 10% of earnings rising over time,
  and the right to name the new sections, if the party brings back her stock from [[Nella]]'s
  camp. She needs protection, and they need a home: "partial owners, I guess." They agreed to
  fetch it.
- **Odibli exasperates her.** When [[Odibli]] called her a bad business owner, she nearly chugged
  her drink: "I just got my thing stolen from me and you're telling me I have bad financial
  planning?" She rolled her eyes so hard the party wondered about her anatomy.
- **Warm toward Luci,** who told her to her face she was untouched by darkness.
- **The hearth.** When it went still during [[Iggy]]'s song, she glanced at it once and went on
  pouring. She let [[Sergei Rubelgerd]] and [[Bosco]] sleep beside it; she has a soft spot for
  pets.

## Canon — Session 31

- **The deal.** When [[Dip]] told her 10% was low for the work, she said she was open-minded and
  in dire need, and offered to talk about a more substantial share if the party stays. Nothing is
  signed. She asked whether the group had a name; when [[Luci]] shouted "The Lightbearers!", she
  said she'd just call them "you guys" for now.
- **The glyph.** She told [[Odibli]] the raiders hit the front of the tavern with a fireball, then
  held her with Hold Person when she came to look, and she guesses the smother charm went into the
  hearth then. She asked him urgently to deal with it.
- **The fire is hers to explain.** After he saved it she took him by the shoulder, thanked him,
  said she might talk to him later about the fire because it is "a little bit strange", and asked
  him not to bring it up for now. He swore to it.
- **The send-off.** Breakfast was leftover soup and hardtack. "Bring back the casks if you can,
  but come back anyways if you can't." ([[Session 31 The Red Trail]])
