Location: [[Wizard of Wines]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

The first of [[Baba Lysaga's Druids]] the party met face to face, and the one who revealed that the attack on the [[Wizard of Wines]] was not a siege but a **poisoning**.

The party found her in [[Session 26 The Poisoned Casks]] standing on the balcony above the westernmost cask of the fermentation room — hunched, in animal skins and a **goat-horned headdress** — emptying a flask of thick syrup into the wine. Scores of ravens watched from the rafters above her.

She was unbothered by the party entirely. Asked about the blights outside, she said they *"won't come inside… they are only to keep the ground safe. They are security, if you will."* Asked directly what she was pouring into the wine, she would say only that they would **find out** what it did, and that it *"works like a charm every time."*

Then she cast **Entangle** across a twenty-foot square, spoke a word in Druidic, and **twig blights began pouring out of the cask.**

## Death

[[Dip]] broke free of the vines, raged, and killed her in a single greataxe turn. Searching her body the party took **a necklace of human teeth** and **the flask** itself.

[[Odibli]] then read druidic magic still hanging in the air, animating the blights **from a source he could not locate** — the thread that ran into [[Session 27 The Vampire Staff]] and resolved as [[The Vampire Staff]], three druids deeper into the building.

> **Reliability:** She is never named aloud in any session, and is filed under a descriptive title pending a real name. Confirm with DM.
