Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Human]]

A spry fourteen-year-old who tends [[Ashford]]'s horses, mostly [[Silas Crubb]]'s. He saw the cart
leave: *"The woman on the cart seat, bow across her knees, cool as you like, took the southeast
track, and the ruts are deep. She's not hauling nothing."* The table joked about him being a
centaur; the DM confirmed he is human.

> **Reliability:** the surname Pike comes from the session plan, not the table. Confirm with DM.

## Session 31

- Came with [[Silas Crubb]] and the horses at dawn. He saw the light the party fell in, and asked
  whether they were angels. [[Iggy]] said they came from somewhere strange, by strange magic. Jory:
  "Oh, you got mages? You don't see many mages around here." He asked the party's name, and
  [[Luci]] told him "The Lightbearers". ([[Session 31 The Red Trail]])
