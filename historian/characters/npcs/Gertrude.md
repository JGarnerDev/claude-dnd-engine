## Background

Daughter of [[Mad Mary]]. Her mother was keeping her hidden from [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s attention — for reasons not yet stated. Gertrude went west despite her mother's warnings and has not returned.

[[Mad Mary]] believes she is dead.

## The Doll

Gertrude left behind (or it was kept by Mary) a doll tagged **"Is No Fun, Is No Blinsky!"** — a reference to a toymaker, possibly in [[Vallaki]] or nearby.

## Status

Whereabouts unknown. May be west of Barovia on the road toward [[Vallaki]], which is also the party's planned route.
