## What Is Known

Named by [[Davian Martikov]] in Session 28, in his first words to the party after the exterior fight: *"Adrian and Elvir told me that you encountered all the blights outside and fought your way in, but they had to escape back here because they were overwhelmed by the blights outside. They weren't sure what else had happened."*

That places him among the **winery folk who agreed to fight alongside the party** against the druids back in Session 25. He was in the fight at the winery's edge, was driven off it, and made it back to Davian — but could not say whether the party inside was alive.

He has never appeared on screen. Per [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]], he is a wereraven like every other Martikov, which raises the unasked question of whether he was one of the ravens that came off the winery's upper floor during the Session 28 ambush.

> **Reliability:** Heard on the transcript as **"Adrian"**, with no surname given. The surname **Martikov** is inferred from Davian naming him familiarly alongside Elvir among the winery's own people, and from Davian's statement that *everyone* in the Martikov line is a wereraven — but it is an inference, not something said at the table. He may be household staff rather than family. Confirm with DM.
