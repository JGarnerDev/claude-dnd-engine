Location: [[Blue Water Inn]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A Vallakian townsman, drinking companion of [[Nikolai]]. Both are quiet dissenters against the [[The Burgomaster of Vallaki|Burgomaster]].

> **Reliability:** Karl's account is that of a drunk, self-described dissenter — one-sided on the Burgomaster. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

With [[Nikolai]], beckoned the party over at the [[Blue Water Inn]], praised specific parts of their performances, and spoke of wanting to relieve the town of the "Burgomaster's madness" — the mandatory festivals, and dissenters imprisoned or worse. When [[Iggy]] asked about Vallaki's desire to be free of the Burgomaster, the two only offered the eerie, detached mantra: "all will be well in Vallaki."

## Sessions 21-23 -A Wachter

Karl and [[Nikolai]] **disappeared** from the crowd at the Blazing Sun festival, moments before the cry of "Tiger!" went up. The party tracked where the two had fled and arrived at [[Wachter House]] — where **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** received them, apologized for **her sons**, and shrugged off the tiger business, noting the boys had **lost their father**.

The party never confronted Karl directly. Two sessions later they killed his mother and her devil-worshiping circle in the cellar of his own house.

> **Reliability:** The notes name the festival runaways "Nikolai and Karl," the same names as the [[Blue Water Inn]] dissenters. This record treats them as the same pair. If they are a name collision, this entire Wachter connection belongs on two different NPCs. Confirm with DM. Whether Karl knows his mother is dead is unaddressed.
