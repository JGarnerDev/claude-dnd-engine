Location: [[Blue Water Inn]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A Vallakian townsman, drinking companion of [[Nikolai]]. Both are quiet dissenters against the [[The Burgomaster of Vallaki|Burgomaster]].

> **Reliability:** Karl's account is that of a drunk, self-described dissenter — one-sided on the Burgomaster. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

With [[Nikolai]], beckoned the party over at the [[Blue Water Inn]], praised specific parts of their performances, and spoke of wanting to relieve the town of the "Burgomaster's madness" — the mandatory festivals, and dissenters imprisoned or worse. When [[Iggy]] asked about Vallaki's desire to be free of the Burgomaster, the two only offered the eerie, detached mantra: "all will be well in Vallaki."
