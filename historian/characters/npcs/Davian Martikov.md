## Background

Davian Martikov stepped out of the vineyard trees to beckon the party in, with **nine other folk** emerging behind him, and introduced himself as the winery's **proprietor**.

He told the party that **druids** had been preventing his people from delivering to [[Vallaki]] — the missing shipments [[Danika]] sent the party to find. Asked by [[Dip]] what the winery had done to provoke them, he said they were **jealous**, and that he really did not know. [[Iggy]]'s Insight read **more to the story**.

He named **[[Baba Lysaga]]** as the leader of the druid forces, and reassured the party that she herself was not present.

After [[Luci]] healed his people, **several of them agreed to fight** alongside the party.

> **Reliability:** No family connection has been established in play for the Martikov name. His race, class, and the truth behind the druids' grievance are all unconfirmed. Confirm with DM.
