## Background

Davian Martikov stepped out of the vineyard trees to beckon the party in, with **nine other folk** emerging behind him, and introduced himself as the winery's **proprietor**.

He told the party that **druids** had been preventing his people from delivering to [[Vallaki]] — the missing shipments [[Danika]] sent the party to find. Asked by [[Dip]] what the winery had done to provoke them, he said they were **jealous**, and that he really did not know. [[Iggy]]'s Insight read **more to the story**.

He named **[[Baba Lysaga]]** as the leader of the druid forces, and reassured the party that she herself was not present.

After [[Luci]] healed his people, **several of them agreed to fight** alongside the party.

> **Reliability:** His race and class remain unconfirmed. Session 28 resolved the grievance question and the family question; what is still open is whether he is telling the whole truth even now — see below. Confirm with DM.

## The Confession (Session 28)

When the party came back out of the cleared winery, fought off the blights waiting outside, and told him what they had found, Davian decided the debt had grown past the point of managing it. *"We owe you a debt for all you've done already. I should tell you more about the real history and what has befell this place."*

First he dismantled an assumption the party had carried for three sessions — he is **not** the Wizard of Wines:

> "Oh no, no, no, alas, I'm not even a wizard. Don't take me for wrong — I could cast a spell or two, but I'm no wizard."

The title belongs to [[The Vintner Mage]], the vineyard's long-dead founder, who made [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] — three magic gems that let the vineyard grow through [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s curse — and one further relic.

Then the family secret, which no outsider had ever been told: **every Martikov in the line is a wereraven**. The ravens that had just saved the party's lives were his relatives. See [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]].

Then the decline, in order: the first seed stolen **ten years ago** on his son [[Urwin]]'s watch, taking [[Champagne du Stomp]] with it; [[Baba Lysaga]]'s scarecrow constructs harrying the winery since; and **three weeks ago** Lysaga taking a second seed herself before setting the druids of [[Yester Hill]] on the third. See [[The Theft of the Three Seeds]].

And her motive: *"Baba Lysaga, she believes that she is Strahd's mother, and so she has set out to oppose any that oppose him."* She found out the Martikovs work against Strahd, and came for them on that basis.

## What He Did For Them

- **Cast poison and disease off [[Odibli]]**, clearing both the tainted-cask poisoning from Session 26 and the brown-mold spores from Session 27 in one gesture — despite not being a wizard.
- **Did the same for [[Dip]]** on request, without hesitating.
- **Marked a map** with [[Baba Lysaga]]'s location and the druids' at [[Yester Hill]].
- **Promised** all the wine the party can carry, cleaned, plus *"anything else we can do to repay you."*
- **Gave them [[The Wanderer's Boon]]** — the founder's other relic, held by the family for generations on his instruction that travelers would one day come to protect the vineyard. It carried the party out of Barovia. See [[The Party Steps Through the Portal]].

> **Reliability:** Davian has withheld from this party before. In Session 25 he said the druids were merely jealous and that he did not know why, and [[Iggy]]'s Insight read more to the story — which Session 28 proved correct. Treat the confession as his fullest account rather than a verified one. His parentage claim about [[Baba Lysaga]] also **contradicts his own Session 25 account**, where he called her the midwife to Strahd's mother rather than claiming she thinks she is the mother; both stand as his reports and the DM's ruling is that the gap may be her madness rather than his error. Confirm with DM.
