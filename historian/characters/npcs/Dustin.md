## Overview

Dustin is a raven of the murder that has been perched in the rafters of the [[Wizard of Wines]] since before the party arrived. He is the first of them to speak to the party, and he does so only because [[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals** and opened with a joke about the view.

He is the source of three facts the party had no other way to learn:

- The murder was **dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]]**
- They came because they **"heard the call of [[Davian Martikov]]"** and answered it
- The one the party actually needed was **the druid in the cellar**, who carried **a staff** and was somehow controlling the blights

That last piece is what pointed the party at [[The Vampire Staff]] and, eventually, at destroying it.

He also confirmed that the murder had been watching the winery **for a couple of days** and had seen everything that happened — including the fight in the fermentation room in [[Session 26 The Poisoned Casks]], on which his verdict was that the party *"had pretty good moves down there."* Asked whether the party could speak to Davian through him, he declined: that has to be done in person.

Asked why Davian can command ravens at all, he shifted on his feet and said they had better talk to Davian about that one.

> **Reliability:** Dustin is almost certainly a member of the [[Keepers of the Feather]] — the murder was dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]], which the Keepers run, and answered a Martikov's call. But he never names the faction and never uses the word "Keeper," and the party has not been told the Martikovs are wereravens. Filed as an affiliation, not a stated fact. Confirm with DM.
