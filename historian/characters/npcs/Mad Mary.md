Location: [[House of Mad Mary]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## First Encounter (Session 11)

Party heard crying from the [[House of Mad Mary]] as [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] led them toward the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]]. They entered. Mad Mary was clutching a doll, inconsolable.

Her words:
> *"My daughter. Gertrude. I kept her hidden from Strahd. She went out, I'm sure she's dead. I told her, but she never listened. She went west."*

The doll she clutched bore a tag: **"Is No Fun, Is No Blinsky!"**

## Connection to Gertrude

Mad Mary had been hiding [[Gertrude]] from [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s attention. Gertrude defied her, went west, and has not returned. Mary believes she is dead.
