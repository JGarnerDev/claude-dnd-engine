Location: [[Church of the Morning Lord]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Priest of the Morning Lord — the primary deity acknowledged in Barovia. Stationed at the [[Church of the Morning Lord]] in [[Barovia Village]]. The church has been badly damaged by repeated undead attacks: claw marks, fire, structural decay. Donavich maintains it alone.

## The Tragedy of Doru

More than a year ago, [[Doru]] — Donavich's son — joined a group of young Barovians who attempted to storm Castle Ravenloft and destroy [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]. The attempt failed. Doru returned as a vampire spawn: bound to Strahd, reduced to lesser vampiric abilities, incapable of creating new spawn.

Donavich has kept Doru caged beneath the church floor since his return. He has been praying without rest, hoping for some divine intervention that hasn't come.

## First Encounter (Session 13)

The party found Donavich pacing in the rubble of the main hall, muttering and visibly destabilized. Doru cried out from below the floor: *"Father, I'm starving!"*

[[Dip]] asked whether Donavich wanted his son dead or restored. Donavich answered with visible fury — he wants Doru back.

The party recalled from books that restoring a vampire spawn requires the death of their vampire lord. [[Luci]] and [[Odibli]] confirmed this together. Donavich identified that lord as [[Strahd Von Zarovich]].

## Ireena's Lineage

During [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s burial, Donavich revealed that [[Ireena Kolyana]] is not a blood relative of the Kolyanovich family. He spoke of "trials that await her" without elaborating further. Ireena was visibly shocked.

## Instruction to the Party

Once Kolyan was buried, Donavich told the party to take Ireena away from Castle Ravenloft immediately. He proposed the Abbey of Saint Markovia in Krezk, or failing that, Vallaki. The party chose Vallaki.
