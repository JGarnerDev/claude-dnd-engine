Location: [[Bildrath's Mercantile]] (works) / [[Parriwimple's Domicile]] (lives) | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

Works for [[Bildrath Cantemir]] at [[Bildrath's Mercantile]]. Patrols the village at night and looks out for residents.

## First Encounter (Session 11)

During [[Dip]]'s raccoon prank, [[Bildrath Cantemir]] hollered "Parriwimple!" from inside the shop. A large, imposing figure emerged. The party stealthed away before any direct confrontation.

## Session 12 — The March of the Dead

Parriwimple came upon the party at the graveyard, alerted by [[Luci]]'s Energy Storm. He had fresh scratch marks on him from a raccoon that had been thrown into his domicile earlier (Dip's prank from Session 11 — consequence delayed).

He warned the party not to be outside at night and explained the ghost procession:
> "These aren't the spirits of the people buried here. They're previous adventurers who died trying to destroy Strahd. Every night they march from the graveyard to the castle. Every night they fail."

When zombies attacked and killed a villager, Parriwimple fought alongside the party. He overperformed — spearing multiple zombies with exceptional force. Afterward, he invited the party to stay at his home for the night. They accepted and took a long rest there.

## Combat Profile

Demonstrated significant combat capability in Session 12. Lethal with a spear. More dangerous than his role as a shopkeeper's assistant would suggest.
