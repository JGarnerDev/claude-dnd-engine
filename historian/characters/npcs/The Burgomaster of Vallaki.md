## Background

The ruler of [[Vallaki]]. The party has not met him; everything known so far comes secondhand from the dissenters [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] at the [[Blue Water Inn]]. He holds themed, thematically unpredictable festivals (music, food, dancing) to keep the town happy, and is described as unhealthily obsessed with everyone's happiness — partying and cheer under duress, on pain of treason or harsh punishment. Dissenters are imprisoned or worse.

The town has an eerie, detached mantra: "all will be well in Vallaki."
