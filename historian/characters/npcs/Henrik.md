Location: [[Vallaki]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Henrik is a coffinmaker in [[Vallaki]] whose shop stands near the town square. He bought the [[Church of St. Andril]]'s stolen saint's bones from the gravedigger [[Milivoj]] for a few gold.

> **Reliability:** The notes spell the coffinmaker's name both "Henrik" and "Henric"; surname not given. Canonized here as "Henrik" (alias "Henric") pending confirmation. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 17)

The party deceived their way into Henrik's shop, and he spilled what he knew:

- [[Strahd]], under the alias **"Vasili van Holtz,"** had visited him.
- **Six vampire spawn** are hiding upstairs in the shop.
- The stolen saint's bones specifically grant a **boon of protection** to the [[Church of St. Andril]] — the reason they were taken matters.

> **Reliability:** "Vasili van Holtz" is Henrik's report of Strahd's alias (module-adjacent spelling; likely Strahd von Zarovich in disguise). The count of six vampire spawn is Henrik's claim — the party has not verified it by sight. Confirm with DM.
