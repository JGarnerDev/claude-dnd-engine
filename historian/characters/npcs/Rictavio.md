Race: Half-elf | Class: [[Bard]]

## Background

An extravagantly dressed half-elf bard, friend to [[Danika]] and a resident at the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]]. Danika trusts his judgment on performers.

> **Reliability:** Rictavio is a known Curse of Strahd figure whose public identity is a deliberate disguise — his self-told history should be treated as a cover, not fact. In-world, the party knows only what he claimed and how he dodged. Do not record his "true" identity here until it is revealed in play. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

[[Danika]] asked him to gauge whether the party were "good performers." He led with a boast about slaying a dragon and selling the great treasure. [[Odibli]] pressed for details; Rictavio evaded, and Odibli came away mistrusting him. During the party's set, Rictavio gestured for [[Dip]] to stop his disastrous (natural-1) interpretive dance.

## Session 24 -Rudolph van Richten

At the meeting place northeast of [[Vallaki]], **vampires ambushed the party and downed all four PCs.** Rictavio and his **[[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger|saber-toothed tiger]]** drove the attackers off and **stabilized** the party afterward.

He then told them three things:

- The nobleman calling himself **Vasili van Holtz**, met at the gates minutes earlier, was **[[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**.
- His own real name is **Rudolph van Richten**, a famous **vampire hunter**. He **lost his child and his wife to vampires** a long time ago.
- He is in Barovia to wreck Strahd's plans in [[Vallaki]] — specifically those involving the **Vistani vampires**.

He told the party to seek out the **[[Keepers of the Feather]]** back in Vallaki: also set against Strahd, and possible allies.

The party had earlier given Rictavio's name to [[Izek Strazni]] as the man responsible for the loosed tiger — with a false direction, to keep this meeting. Izek is hunting him without much interest in investigation or trial.

> **Reliability:** Canon recorded Rictavio as a **half-elf** from party observation in [[Session 16 The Blue Water Inn]]; Van Richten is not. Whether the disguise extends to his race, or the earlier read was wrong, is unresolved. His identity is his own claim, made under the pressure of a rescue. Confirm with DM.
