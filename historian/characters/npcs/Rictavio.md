## Background

An extravagantly dressed half-elf bard, friend to [[Danika]] and a resident at the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]]. Danika trusts his judgment on performers.

> **Reliability:** Rictavio is a known Curse of Strahd figure whose public identity is a deliberate disguise — his self-told history should be treated as a cover, not fact. In-world, the party knows only what he claimed and how he dodged. Do not record his "true" identity here until it is revealed in play. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

[[Danika]] asked him to gauge whether the party were "good performers." He led with a boast about slaying a dragon and selling the great treasure. [[Odibli]] pressed for details; Rictavio evaded, and Odibli came away mistrusting him. During the party's set, Rictavio gestured for [[Dip]] to stop his disastrous (natural-1) interpretive dance.
