Location: [[Blue Water Inn]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A Vallakian townsman, drinking companion of [[Karl]]. Both are quiet dissenters against the [[The Burgomaster of Vallaki|Burgomaster]].

> **Reliability:** Nikolai's account is that of a drunk, self-described dissenter — one-sided on the Burgomaster. Confirm with DM.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

With [[Karl]], beckoned the party over at the [[Blue Water Inn]], praised their performances, and described the [[The Burgomaster of Vallaki|Burgomaster]]'s madness: themed, unpredictable festivals of forced happiness, with dissenters imprisoned or worse. Answered [[Iggy]]'s question about freedom only with the mantra "all will be well in Vallaki."

## Sessions 21-23 -A Wachter

Nikolai and [[Karl]] **disappeared** from the crowd at the Blazing Sun festival, moments before the cry of "Tiger!" went up. Following where the two had fled brought the party to [[Wachter House]] and to **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]**, who apologized for **her sons** and shrugged off the tiger, noting the boys had **lost their father**.

The party never confronted Nikolai directly. Two sessions later they killed his mother and her devil-worshiping circle beneath his own house.

> **Reliability:** The notes name the festival runaways "Nikolai and Karl," the same names as the [[Blue Water Inn]] dissenters. This record treats them as the same pair. If they are a name collision, the Wachter connection belongs elsewhere. Confirm with DM. Whether Nikolai knows his mother is dead is unaddressed.
