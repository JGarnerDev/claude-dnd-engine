Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Human]]

Mayor of **[[Ashford]]** and its general-goods merchant — one man doing both jobs, which is
normal for a town this size and explains most of how he governs.

He keeps Ashford and himself carefully neutral in the Cael–Marlow feud. This is a survival
strategy and not cowardice: a ford-town sitting between **[[House Cael]]**'s authority and
**[[House Marlow]]**'s raiders stays alive by being useful to both and committed to neither.
He trades with whoever pays, has never been seen taking a side, and hedges every bet he makes.

He plays as a drawling, affable raconteur — shrewd practicality wrapped in goofy charm and
meandering tangents that always land back on something useful: a price, a shipment, who owes
whom and since when. Unflappable, self-deprecating, and genuinely good at both moving goods
and defusing problems. Slightly slippery, and not at all ashamed of it.

## At the table

His disposition is **neutral** on purpose. He weighs the party as customers *and* as liability
until the ledger tips — newcomers who fight raiders are good for the town and also exactly the
kind of thing that makes raiders come back.

He is the first re-supply contact, a reliable (if purchasable) read on the feud, and the
practical ground-truth of Ashford: what is in stock, what the raids have actually cost, and
which households are quietly in debt and to whom.

## Session 30

- Introduced himself as acting mayor and market holder: one general-goods shop, and organizing
  [[Ashford]]'s construction until it has a town hall.
- Lent [[Sable Wren]] about 1,000 gp to set up [[The Golden Lioness]]; about 650 gp is still owed.
  Not predatory, but it is serious money for a town this size.
- Kicked her ruined brewing copper and named the only two smiths who could remake one,
  [[George Ringo]] and [[Zach]].
- Had never heard of Strahd von Zarovich.
- [[Odibli]] pitched him on rebundling the town's debt, and [[Halsby]] pitched him a 250 gp buy-in
  on "shade on command". He followed some of it, decided the party was "alien enough not to be
  applicable to this town's finances", said they could talk later, and left after his second drink.

## Session 31

- Came at dawn with [[Jory Pike]] and lent the party **five horses and a cart**: "These are loans,
  not gifts. I want them back with all four shoes on each horse. I'll count them." He reminded them
  how much [[Sable Wren]] owes him, but said the Lioness could mean a healthier future for
  [[Ashford]], which needs the trade after the raids.
- Slapped [[Luci]] on the back with a joke about charging her for a missing horseshoe, caught her
  look, and stepped back. Jovial, maybe always slightly drunk; not creepy.
- Thanked [[Odibli]] and [[Halsby]] for their business advice, said they'd talk more once things
  settle, and shook each one's hand. ([[Session 31 The Red Trail]])
