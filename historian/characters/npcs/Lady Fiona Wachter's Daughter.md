## Background

Mentioned once, by her mother. **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** told the party her daughter had been **betrothed to [[Viktor Vallakovich]]** — but Viktor was, and is, deeply unwell, and **messed her daughter up psychologically**. The betrothal is off. Fiona **would not speak about her daughter's current state.**

[[Odibli]] sent a familiar to spy on [[Wachter House]] partly to locate her; it found **nothing remarkable**. She was not among the people the party found in the cellar, and she did not appear during the fight that killed her mother and her mother's circle.

Her brothers [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] are alive and, as far as the party knows, unaware that their mother is dead.

> **Reliability:** She has no name in the notes and has never appeared on screen. Whether she is in the house, elsewhere in [[Vallaki]], or something worse is entirely open. `state: missing` reflects the party's knowledge, not a confirmed disappearance. Confirm with DM.
