## Background

Yeska is the altar boy at the [[Church of St. Andril]] in [[Vallaki]], serving under the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]].

## First Encounter (Session 17)

When the party learned the church's protective saint's bones had been stolen, suspicion fell on Yeska and they questioned him hard — the boy was scared. [[Lucien Petrovic]] insisted on his innocence. The party later learned the actual thief was the gravedigger [[Milivoj]], clearing Yeska.
