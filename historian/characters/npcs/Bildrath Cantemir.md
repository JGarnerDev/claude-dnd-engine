Location: [[Bildrath's Mercantile]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

Employs [[Parriwimple]] as assistant/enforcer.

## First Encounter (Session 11)

Party visited [[Bildrath's Mercantile]] to buy equipment. [[Iggy]] purchased a shield. Bildrath charged exorbitant prices; [[Dip]] cursed him in response.

Later that night, [[Dip]] pranked Bildrath by luring a raccoon into his shop with a biscuit. A ruckus was heard inside; Bildrath hollered for [[Parriwimple]], who emerged from the shop. The party stealthed away successfully.

## Relationship with Party

Bildrath dislikes the party and is specifically hostile toward [[Dip]]. The feeling is mutual.
