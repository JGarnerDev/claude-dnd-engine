Location: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Son of the late Burgomaster [[Kolyan Indirovich]]. Frequents the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]]. Known as one of the kinder, more open Barovians — Insight checks confirmed his disposition is genuine, not a mask.

## First Encounter (Session 11)

Met the party at the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]]. Greeted them, explained [[Arik]]'s coldness as typical of Barovia, bought a second pitcher of wine. Expressed genuine impression that the party survived [[Death House]].

**Quest offered:** escort his sister [[Ireena Kolyana]] to [[Vallaki]], a walled town 2–3 days west. [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] has taken a dangerous liking to her.

Led the party to the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]] after the tavern meeting.

## Joined the Party (Session 13)

After [[Kolyan Indirovich]] was buried at the [[Church of the Morning Lord]], Ismark elected to travel with the party as a companion for the journey to [[Vallaki]]. He is now an active party member.

## Family

- Father [[Kolyan Indirovich]] — buried Session 13 at the Church of the Morning Lord.
- Sister [[Ireena Kolyana]] — primary concern; Strahd has fixated on her. Session 13 revealed she is not a blood relative of the family.
