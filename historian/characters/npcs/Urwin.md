## Background

Co-owner of the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]] and husband of [[Danika]]. Works the kitchen, where their sons [[Brom]] and [[Bray]] also work.

> **Reliability:** The party's notes first recorded his name as "Irwin"; Miguel's corrections give it as **Urwin** — used here. The surname **Martikov** and his relation to [[Davian Martikov]] come from a canonization ruling on Session 28, where Davian named his son as the guard on the night of the theft and the transcript heard it as "Erwin." Same person, ruled at canonization, not stated at the table. Confirm with DM.

## He Is a Martikov (Session 28)

[[Davian Martikov]]'s confession at the [[Wizard of Wines]] establishes two things about Urwin that the party had no way to guess from three stays at his inn.

**He is Davian's son, and a wereraven.** Davian said every Martikov in the line is one — see [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]]. The murder of ravens that had been watching the winery for days was **dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]]**, which means it was dispatched from Urwin's own house. It also all but confirms the [[Keepers of the Feather]] connection [[Rictavio]] gave the party a name for and nothing else: the party has been sleeping in a Keepers safehouse since Session 16.

**He was on guard the night the first seed was stolen**, ten years ago:

> "Ten years ago a gem was stolen, and the best vintage we produce, Champagne du Stomp, stopped being produced. And to this day we have not been able to produce it. My son Urwin was supposed to be on guard, and I don't know what happened. It doesn't matter anymore."

Davian closed the subject rather than pursue it. [[Champagne du Stomp]] has not been made since, and one of [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] is still unaccounted for — the only one of the three with no lead at all. Whatever happened that night, Urwin has never explained it to his father, and he runs an inn in [[Vallaki]] rather than working the family vineyard.

## First Encounter (Session 16)

Came out from the kitchen, introduced himself as Danika's husband, thanked the party for the entertainment, and offered to guide them to their rooms later. [[Dip]] followed him back into the kitchen, where [[Brom]] and [[Bray]] were working.
