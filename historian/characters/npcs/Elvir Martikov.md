## What Is Known

Named by [[Davian Martikov]] in Session 28, alongside [[Adrian Martikov]], as the two who *"had to escape back here because they were overwhelmed by the blights outside"* and who could not tell Davian what had become of the party inside.

That places him among the **winery folk who agreed to fight alongside the party** in Session 25 — in the fight at the winery's edge, driven off it, and back to Davian alive.

He has never appeared on screen. Per [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]], he is a wereraven like every other Martikov, which leaves open whether he was among the raven swarms that fought in the Session 28 ambush.

> **Reliability:** Heard on the transcript as **"Elvir"**, with no surname given. The surname **Martikov** is inferred from Davian naming him familiarly among the winery's own people and from Davian's statement that everyone in the Martikov line is a wereraven — an inference, not table canon. He may be household staff rather than family. Confirm with DM.
