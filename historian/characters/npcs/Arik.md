Location: [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

Employed by the tavern owners [[Alenka]], [[Mirabel]], and [[Sorvia]].

## First Encounter (Session 11)

Bartender when the party entered the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]]. Cold and resentful — [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] explained this as typical of most Barovians. The party bought a pitcher of wine from him.

Later in the evening, when [[Iggy]] played music and the party performed, Arik nearly cried — he had remembered the joy of music, something Barovia had long since buried in him.
