Location: none — long dead | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

The founder of the [[Wizard of Wines]], named to the party by [[Davian Martikov]] in Session 28 only as *"a mage whose name is lost in history."* The vineyard is described as very old and he as *"extremely powerful."*

He is the source of two things that still function:

- **[[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]]** — three magic gems that let the vineyard grow healthy grapes through [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s curse, on soil that should have been poisoned and barren. They worked for generations and all three are now gone.
- **[[The Wanderer's Boon]]** — a relic he left with the family under instruction that *"one day travelers would come to protect the vineyard, and they should be given this relic."* The Martikovs held it for generations and never showed it to an outsider until the party earned it.

[[Odibli]] read the relic on an Arcana 19 and described his magic as **lost** — *"more powerful than what you're used to seeing"*, and of a kind nobody in present Barovia can reproduce.

## Why He Matters

He is the campaign's only established example of pre-curse magic operating at a scale that beat Strahd's curse on its own terms, rather than surviving around it. He also predicted the party — not by name, but as a category, and provided for them.

The confusion is worth noting: the party assumed for three sessions that [[Davian Martikov]] *was* the Wizard of Wines. He is not, and said so flatly — *"alas, I'm not even a wizard."* The title belongs to this man.
