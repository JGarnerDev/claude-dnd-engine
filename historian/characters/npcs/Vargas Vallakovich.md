Location: [[Vallaki]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Vargas Vallakovich rules [[Vallaki]]. He holds themed, unpredictable festivals (music, food, dancing) to keep the town happy, and is described — by the dissenters [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] at the [[Blue Water Inn]] — as unhealthily obsessed with everyone's happiness, cheer under duress on pain of treason. The town repeats an eerie mantra: "all will be well in Vallaki."

> **Reliability:** Prior to Session 19 the Burgomaster's name was unestablished; the file used a descriptive placeholder ("The Burgomaster of Vallaki," now an alias). Name canonized as **Vargas Vallakovich** from Session 19–20 play. Confirm spelling with DM.

## Referenced (Session 16)

Surfaced secondhand through [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]]'s complaints — a mad ruler enforcing mandatory happiness, imprisoning dissenters.

## First Encounter (Session 19–20)

After the party burned down [[Henrik]]'s coffin shop to recover the church's stolen bones, [[Izek Strazni]] hauled them before the Burgomaster. A **nurse** brought them into his office. In [[Session 20 Viktor's Attic]] he set them **two debt-quests** to repay the burned house:

- Gather **12 bushels of sticks** from the forest.
- Find his **missing lady-in-waiting**.

Exploring the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]], the party discovered the Burgomaster's son **[[Viktor Vallakovich]]** in the attic — the one behind the disappearances, experimenting on the missing townsfolk. After the party subdued Viktor, Vargas believed their account and **waived the 600 gp penalty** for the coffin shop in exchange for their **silence** about Viktor. The party kept Viktor's spells and books.

> **Reliability:** Whether Vargas already knew of Viktor's experiments, or was genuinely blindsided, is not established — only that he chose to bury it. Confirm with DM.
