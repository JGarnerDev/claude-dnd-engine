Location: [[Church of the Morning Lord]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Doru was a young Barovian, son of [[Father Donavich]]. More than a year ago he joined a group of Barovians who attempted to storm Castle Ravenloft and end [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s rule. The attempt failed catastrophically. Doru returned as a vampire spawn.

Vampire spawn are lesser vampires: bound to their lord, partially vampiric in ability, incapable of creating new spawn. [[Luci]] and [[Odibli]] confirmed from recalled books that restoring a vampire spawn to humanity is not easy and involves the death of their vampire lord.

[[Father Donavich]] has kept Doru caged in a cellar beneath the [[Church of the Morning Lord]] since his return, refusing to destroy him. He has been praying ceaselessly for restoration that has not come.

## First Encounter (Session 13)

The party heard Doru cry out from below the church floor: *"Father, I'm starving!"* They did not see him. Communication through the trap door was deemed ineffective and they did not descend.

## Status

Doru is alive, caged, and starving beneath the church. His father refuses to give up on him. The party is aware of his existence and his connection to Strahd.

**Restoring Doru** would require killing [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] — the party's eventual goal anyway.
