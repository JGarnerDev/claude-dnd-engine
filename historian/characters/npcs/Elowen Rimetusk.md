Location: [[Ashford]]

Race: [[Half Orc]] | Class: [[Wizard]]

The old mage of **[[Ashford]]** — a half-orc woman, and the only real magic the town has. She
drifts between lucidity and absence. On her good days she is courteous, sharp, and
unmistakably trained; on the others she is somewhere else, kindly, and no help at all.

On the wizard pyramid she belongs to the working tier — guild-trained, the grade that does
construction and military engineering and harvests. What she is doing in a border ford-town
instead of a guild hall is not a question she can reliably answer any more, and it is a real
one: a working wizard is not what a town this size should have.

Her power has outlasted the mind that held it. She is formidable when present, and she stands
beside **[[Mara Tull]]** in the town's defense whenever she is — which is often enough to
matter and never often enough to count on.

Her name is a soft elven given name carried over an orcish battle-name. Half the time she
cannot recall which war the second one is from, and she says so without distress.

## The translocation (DM-only)

In the war her company was cut off and surrounded. She moved all of them at once in a single
translocation, far past what a working-tier wizard should be able to cast. The soldiers she
moved gave her the battle-name. She cannot remember what it means, because the part of her that
would know never arrived.

Not all of her came through. Part of her mind is still in transit, and that gap is the
senility: her lucid hours are when more of her has caught up, her absent ones are when she has
slid back toward wherever she was going. After that she was no use to a guild, and a ford-town
asked no questions.

Not all of the company came through either. **[[Mara Tull]]**'s son and daughter were in it,
and they never arrived. Mara believes they died in the war. Elowen knows exactly where they were
last, and has never told her. Standing beside Mara is a repayment Mara does not know she is
receiving.

She has worn the same guild casting brace on her casting arm ever since: grey leather over a
steel splint, the translocation's working tooled down its length in faded guild script. The tail
end of the spell — the order to *move them* — is cut into it and never finished running. Below
it, her fingers move half a second behind her.

## Odibli, and the brace

In [[Session 35 The Reeve's Ledger]], on Day 3, she found [[Odibli]] shopping near
[[Mara Tull]]'s office and saw at once that there were two of him: *"There's two of you. One's at
a desk a long way from here and he hasn't looked up for years."*

She told him her story as far as she knows it: her company was cut off, she moved all 61 of them
from a circle in the mud, not all of them arrived, and *"not all of me left there too."* She warned
him: *"Every spell you cast, somebody's trying to find you."* Odibli told her about ink and
darkness, and someone reaching through.

Then she unbuckled the brace and gave it to him, [[The Unfinished Binding]]: *"These are your
flock. Count them before and after every time, bring them all back."* Giving it away changed
nothing about her.

She did not mention Mara's children. The planned favor (a sealed letter for Mara, *find the
missing*, *find the rest of me*) was not played and stays with the DM to use or drop.

Whether Mara's children are still somewhere in between is deliberately unanswered.
