## Appearance

Male, 50, 2 ft tall, 85 lb. Tan skin, blue eyes, bald.

Short even for a halfling, with the squarest jaw you've ever seen, a little stubble, and far too much muscle for his size. No monk robes: a blazer, like a small suit, with no shirt under it. Not showy. He doesn't make an entrance; he gets stuck in.

## Personality

Tyler's one-line pitch on the sheet: "he's like if the song Firestarter was a hobbit".

- **Ideal:** Family, family, family.
- **Bonds:** Family, tenacity, honour.
- **Flaws:** Confrontational, stubborn, a little on the short side.

- **A monk on the sheet only.** No inner peace, no monastery. You find him in a bar, out in the field doing a job, or not at all. Plays like a soldier or an agent: Tyler's touchstone is Jason Statham.
- **Hot-headed.** Quick to fight, and he does not take jokes about his height.
- **A job's a job, up to a line.** Chaotic good: he takes the decent jobs when he can and the shady ones when money is short. The line is children and families.
- **A straight shooter.** When he leaves, he leaves in plain sight.
- Quirks emerge in play (Tyler would rather find them at the table than write them ahead).

## Backstory

From the 2026-11-23 meeting with Tyler.

He grew up in a quiet halfling country, a Shire-like place (unnamed). He was too confrontational for that life and didn't care for sitting still, so he left young to find work. Soldiering taught him to fight, and he did all of it: covert cloak-and-dagger jobs and front-line vanguard fighting.

Later he joined [[Harrow Bane]]'s band as muscle and rose to the middle of its ranks. Over time the work turned seedier. Jobs that came with no briefing led him into raids on small, vulnerable villages, and the collateral damage kept growing: upset kids, broken-up families. Just before he left, the middle and upper ranks offered him a promotion: coordinate the raids and lead a crew. He said he'd think about it.

What crossed the line was an order to kidnap someone's daughter for ransom. He'd go in to rescue a kidnapped girl, but he would never take one. He packed his things and walked out in plain sight. When the crew asked where he was going, he said he had something else to do. He went to [[Ashford]], a town he had helped harass for a long time, laid his weapons on the ground in front of [[Mara Tull]]'s office and surrendered. Then he stopped talking: he reasons that saying nothing keeps more people from getting hurt.

He stays silent until he has read the party. Then he speaks up, action-movie style: the quiet man in the corner who suddenly asks "What do you know about it?".

He came in carrying a letter, and it is what sends the party north to Ben's run. Tyler and Ben are writing its text and its reward together.

> **Reliability:** The letter is tentative (Jeff, 2026-11-23). The leading idea from the meeting: Shaw has a long-lost half-brother (different fathers), Chocola, the old PC of Tyson, a past player. The letter is from Chocola, saying he's in prison and needs Shaw to break him out, and Tyson returns as a holiday special. Neither Tyson nor Chocola has a file yet, and Chocola's race is unremembered. Confirm with Tyler and Ben before writing it as fact.

> **Reliability:** Bane's file says the band does "no cruelty for its own sake". Shaw's account (raids on vulnerable villages, a child kidnapped for ransom) pushes past that. A ransom fits the band's business-first style; the village raids need a reason, such as Marlow's orders escalating. Confirm with DM.
