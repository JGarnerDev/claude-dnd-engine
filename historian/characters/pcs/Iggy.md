## Origin — Threnai (Player Canon Backstory)

Iggy is a refugee of **[[Threnai]]**, a destroyed plane that functioned as a semantic anchor for adjacent realities — it preserved not just knowledge but the meaning, context, and emotional weight behind it, centered on a vast Great Library. Its culture blended feudal-Japan and medieval-Europe aesthetics: orderly, artistic, tradition-bound, but lively and celebratory, trading in stories, histories, and legend rather than material goods.

He was one inhabitant among many, no singular status at birth. He cultivated three disciplines in parallel — music (viol), record-keeping (writing), and the sword — and later served in an urban guard. In the plane's final civil war he worked as a **courier between fronts**, documenting events and encoding fallen comrades into song. Preservation under pressure, human-scale, not institutional.

The war was ideological: a faction of Library insiders (**[[The Correctors]]**, led by the **[[The First Corrector]]**) came to believe death was an imposed falsehood — a consequence of living beings being recorded and finalized into fixed narrative. To "free" existence from finality they enacted a ritual to **break the plane's spine**, which instead collapsed Threnai catastrophically. Opposing them were the followers of **[[Sah]]**, the plane's real and present god of meaning and continuity, who held that preservation is a responsibility and finality a necessary, protective boundary.

### Inciting Incident

When the ritual completed, Iggy was in an outlying town near a major battle, the central city and Great Library still visible on the horizon. He was composing a final song meant to encode the whole of what was being lost — the battle, his fallen comrades, the plane itself, the origins of the war. The composition was interrupted mid-act as reality disintegrated around him. Sah forcibly displaced him to the Material Plane to save him, with a directive: *"Go now, there are other worlds than these."* The viol survived, carrying the unfinished, unstable song.

### Memory Reveal (3 Tiers)

His memory loss is selective, not impairing — he functions normally but knows his past only in broad strokes. Intended pacing of reveals:

- **Tier 1 (early):** broad facts — war, destruction, his courier role — plus emotional fragments without clarity.
- **Tier 2 (mid):** the Corrector's ideology; awareness of the Returned and their corruption.
- **Tier 3 (late):** the Failed Intervention; personal memories of a returned comrade, his final moments, and Sah's rescue.

### Oath & Convictions

At the outset Iggy has no formed ideology about the fall — memories too fragmented to assign blame. Present motivation is simple: be a "good" person, seek adventure, earn recognition as a hero alongside trusted companions; protective instinct present but not yet doctrine. As memory returns and his bond with Sah strengthens, his convictions crystallize (Paladin oath) around preventing systemic collapse and opposing those who manipulate power toward destructive ends — the emergent **New Sahism**:

- Primary doctrine: *"Meaning is lived, not escaped — and every story must end."*
- Confrontational form (against the Correctors): *"Nothing that refuses to end remains true."*

## Sah — Celestial Guide

**[[Sah]]** is Iggy's Aasimar guide: the diminished god of Threnai, now fragmented and recovering. Communication comes as partial impressions — emotional resonance, tonal fragments, brief clarity — not sustained dialogue. As Iggy grows, Sah's coherence strengthens in parallel (reciprocal). Passive presence: tired, mournful, quietly hopeful. Active: an old martial-master archetype — calm, stern, instructive, sometimes rigid but aware his old certainty was flawed. Rare critical moments recover songs/lessons of Threnai that unlock new understanding or resolve.

## The Viol

Iggy's viol survived the collapse and holds fragments of the unfinished final song — battle, personal loss, cultural memory, unresolved truth, overlapping and unstable. Tones or sequences sometimes surface that Iggy doesn't recall composing. It functions as both instrument and residual archive of the destroyed plane. (Carried item — no separate entity file.)

## The Returned

A corruption Iggy witnessed once in the war and now recognizes with dread. The Correctors' early "successful" returns of the dead degraded through stages: **Convincing Return** (intact but delayed, flat affect) → **Pale Echo** (dulled presence, incomplete embodiment) → **Intrusion** (fragments of a hostile presence beyond the plane) → **Degradation** (identity fragmentation, loss of self). A returned high-ranking Corrector even resisted Sah's direct intervention — the war's turning point. Iggy briefly encountered a returned comrade; the memory survives only as obscured unease. (Phenomenon documented here; not yet an encounter-ready entity — say the word if you want it built as a monster.)
