## Backstory

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Dip's player backstory (Ben), homebrew-world origin, pre-campaign. Player-authoritative for Dip's history. Their [[Sage]] background is narrative — the sage training, planar communion, and divine pilgrimage below flavor a mechanically [[Barbarian]] Orc. Named entities below are placeholders pending a naming pass.

Dip comes from the woods now, but not always. They remember an earlier life with parents, shunned by society for what they were. From childhood they read the nuances of society more sharply than those around them, and learned fast that a vocal orc was a liability — silence was safer.

Their sharpest childhood memory is their home hamlet's end. The hamlet was **Emberhollow** ([[Dip's Home Hamlet]]). Strangers arrived friendly, earned the town's trust, then turned — sent to slaughter everyone. Rather than swords-swinging, they tried a stealth attack after gaining the town's trust. It partly worked, but they couldn't finish the tavern keeper, **Old Brannoc Hale**, who set his own roost ablaze to trap them inside. The fire caught most of the raiders and the whole town with them; many perished. A few raiders got out. Dip escaped alongside several others — but lost their twin sister, **Vessa** ([[Dip's Twin Sister]]), in the blaze. Both of Dip's parents, orcs, were cut down early in the attack. Dip doesn't remember their faces, only their voices telling the twins to run. See [[The Hamlet Raiders]].

> **Reliability:** Names (Emberhollow, Brannoc Hale, Vessa) and the parents' fate chosen by Jeff (2026-10-14) to fill gaps Ben left "for another campfire." Earlier record said the trap failed to catch the raiders; reconciled with Ben's handout (the villains burned alive) as: most burned, a few fled.

> **DM-only:** Emberhollow is in [[Aethryn]]. Dip has no memory of the world they were born in, only the days leading up to the fire, and doesn't know Aethryn may be home. They could walk into it without recognizing it.

This is their engine. Dip never accepted that their sister died. In their visions many hands guide and push them along — but their sister's hands are neither leading nor helping, which they take as proof she still exists on this realm in some form.

Dip wandered years, then the rumours pulled them in — a murmur of a past wrong undone, a feeling that no longer pulls at the threads of your sanity, a place of calm in a cacophony of sensations. That was [[The Sage Enclave]]. There they learned how the many planes converge on this one, the ways of the sage, and the power of meditation for communing across planes. During festivals, when the powers of worship pull people together, they can feel the weak points in reality and grow more attuned to the ethereal.

To stay, Dip gave up their time, their anger and their sorrow. The Enclave channelled those into talents: a battle-hardened edge for danger, a watchful eye for suspicion and detail, and the ability to seek out empathy (which Dip admits is still hard for this orc on the best of days). It also taught them to see reality as one face among many on the multiversal threads; Dip can feel spirits and souls ebb and flow as they weave in and out of this plane. Dip has never left the Enclave. They are away on personal matters and will return once they have completed their chapters.

### The Night of the Fire (Handout)

Dip doesn't tell this story; it's "better left for another campfire-lit night." When they landed beside the burning [[The Golden Lioness]] (Session 29), it came back as if they were reliving it, and the memories drove them into the blind rage:

- people they had come to know as friends turning into monsters that wanted them eradicated, the hate hidden behind smiles and laughter over many moons of shared meals
- an evil inside those creatures that could only be the vilest betrayal, fuelled by **bloodlust**
- the screams of families being cut down, then the shrieks of the villains burning alive in a cleansing inferno that seemed to grow from the need to choke the very air they breathed
- the strength of sacrifice: Brannoc's fire brought a whole ideology to a standstill and gave birth to a new movement, one of survival against any odds

### The Hands

At first the visions had only a few touches, a few hands. Over time they have grown in number and variety. Dip believes they are like-minded souls pushing and pulling them toward the next destination. Ancestors or gods, they exist outside the mortal coil for now. The more the numbers swell, the surer Dip is of the path. The only thing that could shake this belief is if the hands Dip knows so well, Vessa's, join the mass. Even then, Dip would know she was pushing them toward a greater cause.

The Enclave opened many avenues, but few held them. What drew them most were the literatures of gods and goddesses and their interventions in mortal lives, and the holy sites that gather the faithful. These are the focus of their pilgrimages, as they try to understand the tangled relationship of the divine and the mortal.

The holiest sites are not as common as they used to be, but Dip finds the ones that matter: souls gathered around a warm campfire, a tide that brings in good hope, the sunrise that warms the land and makes the flowers bloom. These answer their questions every day: keep going, keep pushing.
