## Backstory

> **Reliability:** Sourced from Dip's player backstory (Ben), homebrew-world origin, pre-campaign. Player-authoritative for Dip's history. His [[Sage]] background is narrative — the sage training, planar communion, and divine pilgrimage below flavor a mechanically [[Barbarian]] [[Half Orc]]. Named entities below are placeholders pending a naming pass.

Dip comes from the woods now, but not always. He remembers an earlier life with parents, shunned by society for what he was. From childhood he read the nuances of society more sharply than those around him, and learned fast that a vocal orc was a liability — silence was safer.

His sharpest childhood memory is his home hamlet's end. Strangers arrived friendly, earned the town's trust, then turned — sent to slaughter everyone. Rather than swords-swinging, they tried a stealth attack after gaining the town's trust. It partly worked, but they couldn't finish the tavern keeper, who set his own roost ablaze to trap them inside. It failed to trap them and instead caught the whole town; many perished. Dip escaped alongside several others — but lost his twin sister ([[Dip's Twin Sister]]) in the blaze. See [[Dip's Home Hamlet]] and [[The Hamlet Raiders]].

This is his engine. Dip never accepted that his sister died. In his visions many hands guide and push him along — but his sister's hands are neither leading nor helping, which he takes as proof she still exists on this realm in some form.

Dip wandered years, then fell in with spiritual folk who taught him how the many planes converge on this one — [[The Sage Enclave]]. There he learned the ways of the sage and the power of meditation for communing across planes. During festivals, when the powers of worship pull people together, he can feel the weak points in reality and grows more attuned to the ethereal.

The Enclave opened many avenues, but few held him. What drew him most were the literatures of gods and goddesses and their interventions in mortal lives, and the holy sites that gather the faithful. These are the focus of his pilgrimages, as he tries to understand the tangled relationship of the divine and the mortal.

## Death Experience (Session 7, Player-Written)

> *"I was surrounded by shrieks of madness, torment, and despair. The sounds transformed into a cacophony of sound, that when it transitioned into the fury of a maelstrom... winds whipped my face and soon the storm turned into ice as it melted my flesh... I felt something latch onto my legs and pull me down... it was hands, claws, tentacles, wings, a slew of different things, disembodied but at once a part of something much larger... The embrace transferred from a feeling of dread and demise into one of hope, lost memories finding a ledge to grasp onto... A shrill piercing sound and a burst of light, that felt tainted exploded through the limbs and wrenched me from the depths of this ocean back into a place filled with quickly becoming familiar faces."*
>
> -Dip, written account

> **Note:** The death experience description is notably different from Miguel's. Where Miguel's was clean and quick, Dip's involved "something much larger" pulling him down -multiple hands, claws, tentacles, wings. This may be narratively significant.
