# The Party

Members: [[Iggy]], [[Luci]], [[Odibli]], [[Dip]]
Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Formation

Met at the Black Sheep Inn (Session 01) and were drawn together into Death House by the mists. United by circumstance, not by choice.

## Reputation

Unknown. They have not been in Barovia long enough to establish one.

## Notable Accomplishments

- Cleared [[Death House]] — survived all ten sessions within its walls and escaped. Luci died on the altar and was retrieved from death by Strahd.
- Rose and Thorn (the ghost children) were left behind when the mists expelled the party.

## Active Afflictions

These are party-level consequences that persist:

- **Iggy** — shadow does not match his form and moves in the wrong direction.
- **Dip** — hears whispers when it is quiet.
- **Luci** — Sacred Flame permanently sacrificed; cannot be recovered.

Both shadow and whisper afflictions stem from Odibli absorbing shadow essence to revive dead party members in Session 10.

## Current Goals

Survive Barovia. Understand what Strahd wants from them. Find a way to defeat him or escape the mists.
