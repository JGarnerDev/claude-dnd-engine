Location: [[Vallaki]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

Izek Strazni is the captain of the town guard in [[Vallaki]], serving under [[Vargas Vallakovich|the Burgomaster]]. When the party sought help against the vampire spawn, the town guards referred them to the Burgomaster and to Izek, who was found in the town square overseeing his guardsmen as they replaced the festival signage.

## First Encounter (Session 18)

The party found Izek in the town square amid preparations for the next mandatory festival — the **Blazing Sun** party. He was creepy and cold in manner. One of his arms is grotesquely **unnatural**: elongated nails, barbs, and heavy deformity that read as **dark magic**. Beneath his menace he carried a **hollowness and a longing air**.

He was wholly uncaring about the party's problem with the vampire spawn and reluctant even to consider a combined battle if lured to him. He left with a plain threat: he would deal harshly with the party if they broke the law.

Suspecting him, [[Luci]] contrived to spill diluted **holy water** on him (with [[Iggy]] aiding the deception by faking a trip). Izek was **unaffected** — the holy water did nothing to him.

> **Reliability:** Izek's race, class, and the source of his deformed arm and longing are not established in the notes — only observed impressions (dark magic, hollowness). The holy-water test confirms only that he is not affected by it, not what he is. Confirm with DM.

## The Sibling Reveal (Session 19)

After the party burned down [[Henrik]]'s coffin shop, Izek bound them and paraded them to the Burgomaster — angry, but he would not kill them. At the [[Burgomaster's Mansion|mansion]] the party learned that **[[Lake Zarovich]]** is where Izek **lost his arm**, and noticed that Izek and **[[Ireena Kolyana]] share the same face**.

Before the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]], the truth came out: **Izek and Ireena are siblings.** Izek was **born without a soul** — the reason he had forgotten her — which pays off the hollow, longing air he carried since Session 18. He does not know what magic made his deformed arm, and is at peace with it.

> **Reliability:** How Izek lost his arm at Lake Zarovich, what replaced it, and how his soullessness relates to Strahd are all unestablished. Confirm with DM.

## Sessions 21-23 -Marked, and Misdirected

Izek led the guard response to the tiger loosed at the Blazing Sun festival. [[Luci]] deceived him about the direction and **split half his search party** away from [[Rictavio]]'s position.

Later the party gave him **Rictavio's name** as the man responsible — with another false direction. Izek was **determined to destroy Rictavio** without much interest in investigation or trial. The party said nothing about [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]].

He does not know that **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]] asked the party to kill him**, that they accepted the job with no intention of doing it, or that she and [[The Wachter Cult]] died in her cellar days later. [[Luci]] told **[[Ireena Kolyana]]** about the plot and advised that Izek **lay low**; whether that warning ever reached him is unrecorded.

> **Reliability:** Fiona never gave a reason for wanting Izek dead — the party accepted her offer specifically to find out, and she died before they asked. Whether Izek was an obstacle to her coup, a personal grudge, or something tied to his arm is entirely open. Confirm with DM.
