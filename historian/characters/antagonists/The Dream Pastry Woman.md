Location: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A night hag operating openly in [[Barovia Village]], disguised as an elderly woman selling dream pastries from a cart. Her pastries induce a powerful dissociative stupor. She called on Barovian homes and accepted children as payment from desperate families who could not pay otherwise.

*[Inferred from CoS canon: this character may be Morgantha, a night hag known to operate in this region. Name unconfirmed in play — use "The Dream Pastry Woman" until DM confirms.]*

## First Encounter (Session 13)

The party bought dream pastries from her cart without incident. She then knocked on a door; the parents inside begged *"Please don't take him, I'll find another way!"* — she took the child anyway.

**[[Iggy]]** kicked the door in. The parents were already stupored on the pastries, inconsolable.

The party attacked. **[[Dip]]** raged and axed. **[[Odibli]]** cast Magic Missile. **[[Luci]]** activated Radiant Consumption and Energy Storm Psychokinesis. **[[Iggy]]** climbed to a rooftop, activated Radiant Soul for the first time, and sniped her with a heavy crossbow.

She revealed her true night hag form and **shifted to another plane before the party could finish her.** She took the stolen child with her.

## Status

At large. Last seen shifting planes from [[Barovia Village]]. The stolen child's fate is unknown. **[[Parriwimple]]** is now aware of her and watching for any return.

## Threat Assessment

Survived a full-party assault, escaped at will. Her operation in Barovia suggests she has been active for some time. The children she collects — and what she does with them — is unknown.
