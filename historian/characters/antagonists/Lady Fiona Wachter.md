## Background

Lady Fiona Wachter kept a fine house in [[Vallaki]] — well decorated, the finery slightly tarnished, the walls hung with painted portraits. She was the mother of [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]], who fled to her house after loosing [[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger]] during the Blazing Sun festival, and of [[Lady Fiona Wachter's Daughter]], once betrothed to [[Viktor Vallakovich]] until his madness left her psychologically wrecked. She refused to discuss her daughter's condition. The party first met her through a window in [[Session 21 The Festival of the Blazing Sun]].

## The offer

In her parlor she drew [[Dip]] out about [[Vargas Vallakovich|the Burgomaster]], agreed with every complaint, and steered the conversation toward a **coup** — hinting she wanted the regency, and asking the party to **kill [[Izek Strazni]]**. She offered money, protection from the coffin-shop arson charge, and access to her library, but nothing concrete when [[Iggy]] pressed her on backing. The party accepted with no intention of following through.

## The cellar

Beneath [[Wachter House]], past eight animated skeletons and a secret door, she kept a candlelit chamber with a black pentagram cut into the floor, where [[The Wachter Cult]] met and practiced devil worship. Her imp [[Majesto]] served her there.

When [[Iggy]] talked his way out of the room, she called for the party to be seized. In the fight that followed, **[[Dip]] killed her and Majesto.** She dropped a set of keys and a gold ring.

> **Reliability:** Her race is inferred from the Vallakian noble context, not stated. Her class and any spellcasting are unrecorded — an imp familiar implies a warlock or similar, but that is inference. What her keys open, what the ring is, and what the cult was actually building toward all died with her. Confirm with DM.
