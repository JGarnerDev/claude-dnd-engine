Location: [[Barovia]] | Faction: [[Domain of Barovia]] (rules) | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Appearance

Handsome, pale face. Sharp features. Dark eyes that reflect firelight but contain no warmth. When he spoke through the fireplace, the fire did not flicker.

## Voice

Low, measured. Like greeting guests at a dinner party.

## Appearances in Play

### Session 3 -The Fireplace

Appeared as a face formed in the flames of fireplace room 6 in [[Death House]]. Addressed the party: *"You've come to my land uninvited, yet here you are, exploring one of its... oldest wounds. Curious. Most who enter this house do not leave. But you -you interest me. Do carry on."*

Did not threaten. Did not explain himself. When asked his name, smiled slightly and said nothing. When the fireplace was attacked, the fire simply went out and the room went cold.

### Session 8 -The Statue

The statue in the cult quarters bore his face. [[Odibli]]'s Arcana check revealed powerful magic connecting the statue to another entity -the same face as from the fire.

### Session 9 -The Shadow Corridor

Strahd froze time just before shadows would have killed [[Dip]]. Spoke directly to the party: *"The shadows cannot exist in the light. Be wary of that which you cannot see -it is deadly."* Then left. This act saved Dip's life.

### Session 10 -Outside Death House

Greeted the party upon their exit from Death House. Thanked and congratulated them. Retrieved [[Luci]] from death after she sacrificed herself on the altar.

> **Reliability:** All appearances confirmed from session notes. The dialogue from Session 3 and Session 9 was written by the DM in the notes, so it is high reliability. Session 10 congrats and Luci retrieval confirmed.

### Session 24 -Vasili van Holtz, Confirmed

Outside the gates of [[Vallaki]], a **noble-appearing man** approached the party, congratulated them on defeating [[Lady Fiona Wachter]] and her planned uprising, and remarked how poorly [[Vargas Vallakovich|the Burgomaster]] was rewarding them. He gave his name as **Vasili van Holtz**.

Minutes later, vampires ambushed the party at their meeting place and **downed all four PCs**. [[Rictavio]] and his [[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger|saber-toothed tiger]] drove them off, stabilized the party, and told them plainly: **Vasili van Holtz was Strahd.**

This closes the [[Strahd Walks Vallaki as Vasili van Holtz]] thread opened in Session 17 — the alias [[Henrik]] reported is confirmed, and Strahd has now used it to speak with the party face to face.

Strahd also knows their number. [[The Raven River Wolf]], posted at the [[Raven River]] crossing, told [[Odibli]] it served Strahd and had been ordered to stop **a group of four adventurers** from going further west.

> **Reliability:** That Vasili was Strahd is [[Rictavio]]'s assertion, given as Rudolph van Richten immediately after saving the party. The party did not independently verify it. Whether the vampire ambush was Strahd's own doing or the work of the Vistani vampires Van Richten named is not established. Confirm with DM.

### Session 25 -Baba Lysaga

[[Davian Martikov]] named **[[Baba Lysaga]]** — "Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night" — as **midwife to Strahd's mother**, the one who **brought him into this world and directed his life**. Her druids besiege the [[Wizard of Wines]].

> **Reliability:** Secondhand from Davian, whose account [[Iggy]] read as incomplete. Confirm with DM.
