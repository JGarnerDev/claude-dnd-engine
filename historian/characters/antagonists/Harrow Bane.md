Location: [[Duskwater]]

Race: [[Human]]

Harrow Bane leads the land-bandit band that **[[Lady Sorcha Marlow]]** uses, unofficially,
to raid **[[Ashford]]** — the band behind the Session-1 raid. He is based out of
**[[Duskwater]]** and operates overland, never on the river.

## The professional

He was a soldier, and he still runs like one. The band has a command structure, staged crews,
assigned objectives, and a withdrawal plan. He does not permit cruelty for its own sake,
because cruelty for its own sake costs him people and attracts attention, and both are bad
for business. He avoids fights that are not worth winning.

The **matched boots** are his doing — the band is kitted consistently, because a kitted band
moves and fights better than a rabble. It is also the town's first real clue: independent
bandits do not have a quartermaster. Anyone in Ashford who looks at the bodies instead of
the chaos can see that something is paying for this.

His name carries more weight than his face. A few deliberately brutal public examples early
on bought him a reputation he has not needed to top up since — people go quiet when he is
named, and the quiet does the work that violence would otherwise have to.

## The leash he keeps slack

Marlow's coin reaches him through an intermediary. He takes no direct orders from her, holds
nothing in her hand, and has arranged it that way on purpose — it protects her, which is what
she is paying for, but it also protects *him*. He is a contractor, not a retainer, and he
intends to stay one.

Pulling that intermediary is the optional traitor-reveal of the act. Harrow Bane is the thread
the party can follow; the intermediary is the knot.

## The crack

There is someone back in Duskwater — a sibling or a child — he quietly provides for and
protects. Nobody in the band discusses it. It is why the paydays matter past a certain point,
and it is the lever the party will eventually find if they go looking for one, because the
rest of him does not offer any.

## Session 1

He does not appear. He is **the name only** — spoken by townsfolk, attached to nothing the
party has seen, and already more frightening for it. His lieutenants **[[Jorn]]**,
**[[Wick]]** and **[[Nella]]** carry the early raids; he arrives when the party has earned
him.

## Session 30 — What the party heard

Not met, not described. Someone in the Golden Lioness said the name, and the room went quiet,
almost superstitious. Someone gave [[Agnes Brand]] a look to keep her from carrying it further.
