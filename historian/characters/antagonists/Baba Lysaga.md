## Background

Named to the party by [[Davian Martikov]] at the [[Wizard of Wines]] as the leader of the **druid forces** blockading his winery and choking off [[Vallaki]]'s wine supply. He called her a **dark sorceress**, and gave three names for her: **"Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night."**

Her established significance is her tie to **[[Strahd Von Zarovich]]**: she was **midwife to Strahd's mother**, brought him into this world, and **directed his life**.

Davian told the party she is **not present** at the winery — only her druids are.

## The Mother Claim (Session 28)

In his full confession, [[Davian Martikov]] gave the party a different version of her tie to Strahd than he had in Session 25:

> "Baba Lysaga, she believes that she is Strahd's mother, and so she has set out to oppose any that oppose him."

**The two accounts are deliberately left unreconciled.** Session 25 has her as the *midwife* to Strahd's mother — the woman who caught him and then steered his life. Session 28 has her *believing she is* the mother. The DM's ruling at canonization is that the gap is not an error to be resolved but a fact about her: **she may simply be insane**, a woman present at the beginning of Strahd who collapsed the distinction across centuries.

Both stand as *reports*. Neither is established parentage.

This also supplies her motive, which had been blank. She is not fighting over wine and never was. She learned the Martikovs oppose Strahd, and she hunts anyone who does.

## What She Has Done to the Winery (Session 28)

Davian's account of her campaign, in order:

- Harried the winery for some time with her **scarecrow constructs** — see the [[Scarecrow]] stat block.
- **Three weeks before Session 28**, attacked in force herself, **found the second of [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] and took it**. She still holds it.
- Then **convinced the evil druids of [[Yester Hill]]** to attack for the third seed, which they took to their hideout.
- When the Martikovs counter-attacked and failed, the druids returned with the blight horde the party has since destroyed.

See [[The Theft of the Three Seeds]]. Davian marked her location on a map for the party.

> **Reliability:** Everything about her is still [[Davian Martikov]]'s account, not party observation — she has never appeared on screen. His Session 25 framing was already suspect ([[Iggy]]'s Insight read more to the story, correctly), and his two versions of her parentage conflict. "Our mother," from Session 25, remains unexplained and now reads differently. Session 25 also has her reported **not present** at the winery, which sits awkwardly against Session 28's claim that she attacked it in person three weeks earlier. Her race, class, whereabouts, and current holdings beyond the one seed are unknown. Confirm with DM.
