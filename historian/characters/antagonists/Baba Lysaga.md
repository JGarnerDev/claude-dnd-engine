## Background

Named to the party by [[Davian Martikov]] at the [[Wizard of Wines]] as the leader of the **druid forces** blockading his winery and choking off [[Vallaki]]'s wine supply. He called her a **dark sorceress**, and gave three names for her: **"Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night."**

Her established significance is her tie to **[[Strahd Von Zarovich]]**: she was **midwife to Strahd's mother**, brought him into this world, and **directed his life**.

Davian told the party she is **not present** at the winery — only her druids are.

> **Reliability:** Everything here is Davian Martikov's account, not party observation. [[Iggy]]'s Insight said there is more to the winery's quarrel with the druids than Davian admitted, so his framing of her is suspect. "Our mother" is unexplained — a druid title, a Barovian epithet, or something more literal. Her race, class, whereabouts, and actual goal are all unknown. Confirm with DM.
