## Background

Employed as nursemaid in the Durst household. Had an affair with [[Gustav Durst]] and bore his illegitimate son, Walter. [[Walter Durst]] was born stillborn (or sacrificed immediately at birth -notes ambiguous). [[Elisabeth Durst]] discovered the affair, was consumed by jealousy, and ordered the nursemaid killed by the [[Durst Cult]].

The nursemaid now haunts Death House as a specter.

## Appearance (from Ben's Session 5 Description)

Tallish, medium build. Slim. Fast moving but also mysteriously slow. Hair all over -or just blurry. Hard to make out clearly.

## Session History

- **Session 4:** Her history was glimpsed through the nursery mirror: nursemaid and master of the house kissing; stillborn child; nursemaid killed horrifically by a cult in the same room.
- **Session 5:** Specter entered the room and attacked the party. Initiative order: Ben, Miguel, Specter, Paul, Jeff. Downed Miguel and Ben; killed Ben outright. Paul sacrificed Sacred Flame to revive both.
