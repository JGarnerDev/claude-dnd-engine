## The Winery Druid

A **hunched, wild-looking woman** in a **gown of animal skins** and a **headdress of goat horns**, hair long and unkempt. She wore a **necklace of human teeth**, which [[Dip]] took from her body.

The party found her on the balcony of the [[Wizard of Wines]] fermentation room, above the westernmost cask, **pouring a flask of thick syrup into the wine**.

> **Reliability:** She is never named aloud in the session. "The Winery Druid" is a placeholder chosen at canonization so she can be referenced; if she had a real name at the table, it did not survive the recording. Confirm with DM.

### What she said

She was unbothered by the party's arrival and unbothered by their questions. Of the blights besieging the winery:

> "They won't come inside. They are only to keep the ground safe. They are security, if you will."

Of the flask, when [[Dip]] asked directly what she was pouring into the wine, she would say only that they would find out — that it **"works like a charm every time."**

### How she fought

She opened by casting **Entangle** across a twenty-foot square, catching [[Dip]] and [[Odibli]], then **spoke a word in Druidic** that brought [[Twig Blight|twig blights]] pouring out of the nearest cask. She fought in melee with **Shillelagh** and used **Thunderwave** once.

She was killed by [[Dip]] in a single greataxe turn — raging, 25 damage, hacked apart limb by limb and cleaved in half.

### What she leaves behind

- The **flask** and the **necklace of human teeth** are in the party's hands.
- **Every cask in the winery is poisoned.** [[Odibli]] drank from one and is poisoned himself.
- The **druidic magic animating the blights outlived her.** Odibli read it still hanging in the air afterward: the blights are not natural and do not exist of their own accord, meaning **at least one more druid** is still unaccounted for in the building.

> **Reliability:** Her connection to [[Baba Lysaga]] is inferred from [[Davian Martikov]]'s account in [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]] that Baba Lysaga leads the druids besieging the winery. The druid herself never named an allegiance. Confirm with DM.
