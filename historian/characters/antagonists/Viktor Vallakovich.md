## Background

Viktor Vallakovich is the son of Burgomaster [[Vargas Vallakovich]], hiding in the attic of the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]]. He is the source of Vallaki's disappearances — the missing townsfolk were **his experimental subjects**. He is building a **teleporter**, which does not yet work reliably.

## First Encounter (Session 20)

The party, exploring the mansion, found Viktor in the attic. They convinced him that [[Odibli]] and company could help repair his broken **teleporter** — and did, to a degree — but Viktor insisted the party **stand on the teleporter** to prove it was safe.

Refusing, the party cast **Hideous Laughter** on Viktor, cutting off his spellcasting. While most of the party held him and fought off his **skeleton cats**, [[Luci]] ran to fetch the Burgomaster and [[Odibli]] gathered evidence to clear the party of blame. The Burgomaster believed them, waived the 600 gp coffin-shop penalty for their silence, and the party walked away with **Viktor's spells and books**.

> **Reliability:** Viktor's fate after the confrontation, and his class/level, are not established in the notes — only that he was subdued and exposed. Whether he remains free, confined, or under his father's control is open. Confirm with DM.
