Location: [[Duskwater]]

Race: [[Human]] | Class: [[Ranger]]

One of three lieutenants under **[[Harrow Bane]]** in the land band **[[House Marlow]]** uses
deniably against **[[Ashford]]**, alongside **[[Jorn]]** and **[[Wick]]**. A female human
ranger — the band's woodsman, tracker, and marksman, who works from range and does not stay
anywhere she can be reached.

On the ranger pyramid she is the working tier: the guide-and-bounty-hunter grade whose trade is
reading ground early and being where nobody looked. She came up through the bottom of it,
village hunting and the kind of trapping the local lord calls poaching, and it shows in how
little she wastes.

## Debt-collared

Bane holds a debt over her — a blood-price or a bought contract, the sort of thing that does
not get written down where she can see it. She raids to buy herself out, and she keeps count
of it meticulously: a notched antler tally, coin sorted into counted stacks, every payment
accounted for.

She is not loyal and believes in none of it. She is working off a number, which makes her the
cleanest resolution lever in the band — buy the debt out, or show her that Bane never had any
intention of releasing her, and she can walk away from the crew instead of to a rope.

## The crystals

Every raider in her crew wears an **Earshot Crystal**, one per head, all cut from one stone. The
crew never uses them. When the party loots theirs in Session 1, Nella keeps her own, and so do a
few of the crew who got away. Any message the party sends to "all" within 300 ft of her, she
hears, and she can answer. She sits on that through many fights before it comes out.

## The three meetings

- **Session 1.** She runs the raid on **[[The Golden Lioness]]**'s new wing, leaves with half
  her mooks and the stolen ale before the party can close, and gets away clean. She is the face
  the party has, and the reason there is something to chase.
- **Session 2.** She escapes the pursuit a second time, on her own skill, through a fallback she
  rigged in advance and the party can see her use. Earned, not handed to her.
- **The third.** The rule of three pays off. The natural slot is as support marksman on the
  convergence night, where the party finally has her — and where the debt is on the table
  instead of her life.

## Canon — Session 29

- **First sighting.** During the raid on [[The Golden Lioness]], [[Odibli]] saw a long-haired
  woman with a bow sitting on a cart of the Lioness's barrels, 200–300 ft down the road and
  heading south. Her cape was blowing, and she looked back at him before vanishing under tree
  cover.
- **Her name was spoken aloud.** The raiders' leader shouted "we need to get packing up and back
  to Nella," and the party heard it. They have not tied the name to the woman on the cart on
  screen.
- **Her crew left behind at the Lioness died to a man:** Arden, Willem, Gib, the hedge-mage
  Tallis and the big one. Her cart and the barrels already loaded got away. The party stopped
  the rest of the yard's stock from leaving.

## Canon — Session 30

- **Named and placed.** [[Sable Wren]] told the party that Nella, "one of the raider
  commanders", took the stock to a nearby camp. The party has now tied the name to the
  long-haired archer on the cart.
- [[Jory Pike]] saw her leave: "bow across her knees, cool as you like", southeast, the ruts deep.
- The party took the job of bringing the stock back. Luci favours ambushing her crew as they
  leave camp over a head-on assault.
- The [[Earshot Crystals]] from her dead crew are now with the party, one each.

## Canon — Session 32

- **Her camp** is [[The Waystation]], an old drover's relay southeast of [[Ashford]], with the
  stolen cart still loaded in the yard.
- **Her rear guard** (an archer, a crossbowman and a wolf) held a fork on the road. The crossbowman
  died to [[Dip]]; the archer and the wolf fled toward the camp.
- **She keeps a griffon,** hooded and staked behind the ridge past the camp's bridge. [[Halsby]],
  scouting invisible, found her saddling it.
- **She wears an [[Earshot Crystals|earshot crystal]].** When Halsby reported to the party by
  speaking to every holder at once, she heard him, turned toward the party's trail, whistled and
  called **"Get moving, they're coming,"** and launched the griffon into the air. Halsby saw the
  crystal at her neck, so the party now knows she can hear an all-holder broadcast.
- The session ended with her airborne over a mobilising camp, at about 10 a.m. on Day 2.
