# Sabathiel at the Ford

[[Luci]] drank the draught and woke in the ford. [[Sabathiel]] was there: "You have run so far that
you've forgotten what you are… I called for you for 113 years." And: "Nothing will stain them.
When you strike, I will strike with you." She came out of the water wearing [[The Stillwater Wraps]]. [[Old Tobin Reed]] watched it all from his nets.
