# The Griffon Comes Home to Ashford

[[Iggy]] played the fiddle on the road home. At the stables [[Silas Crubb]] checked every hoof:
"All four shoes on each one… we're pretty square. Next time you need horses you come to me
first." [[Ashford]]'s square was full at sundown when the wagon came in with [[Tithe]] on it.
[[Hob Fenner]] told the crowd how the raider in the cell had "put his sword down… like he was
returning a borrowed thing." [[Mara Tull]] said a reeve was riding from Orrend. [[Sable Wren]]
called the casks home: "That's twice now that you saved this place… Ten percent might be too
little now." The party slept at [[The Golden Lioness]].
