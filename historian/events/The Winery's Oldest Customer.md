Almost every delivery went to one of four standing customers: the Blood of the Vine Tavern in the village of Barovia, the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki, Krezk, and the Vistani. Flipping back through pages and pages of records, the oldest entries named a fifth — 'S', Strahd himself — and those shipments stopped decades, if not hundreds of years, ago. Luci read it as proof the Martikovs are in league with Strahd, doubling down when he recalled the Vistani are Strahd-aligned too. Kellan declined to confirm the inference, noting only that selling wine to him once is not allegiance, and that the Wizard of Wines is a very old winery."
