## What Happened

The party had cleared the winery's interior and confirmed with the DM that nothing remained inside. [[Iggy]] took point out of the building, peering ahead for blights; the grounds read calm and the rest followed his lead. Then skittering broke out from every direction and the blights came at them from the treeline and around the corner of the building.

These were the blights that **survived**. When [[Odibli]] destroyed [[The Vampire Staff]] in the cellar the previous session, its scream killed every blight in the building — and Kellan had wondered aloud whether the sound carried past the thick stone. It did not. Miguel had it right in character before the fight started: *"the staff that was broken, it screams and kills all the blights, but they need to actually hear it to die."*

**[[Odibli]]** opened with Shatter across roughly seven blights for 9–20 damage each, then took a needle for 9 piercing and hit 0 HP — his fourth drop in two sessions, and by now the table's running joke.

**[[Dip]]** switched off a rage they had already spent, bellowed across the grounds for [[Davian Martikov]] — *"we have vanquished all the druids, but we need help with these blights"* — and cleaved blights down two at a time.

**Raven swarms** began arriving off the winery's upper floor in answer to that shout, taking a blight at a time and accounting for roughly half the kills.

**[[Iggy]]** pulled the spine out of Odibli, stabilized him with Lay on Hands for exactly 1 HP, then burned a second-level Healing Word for 10 more — *"I pretty much scream: get the fuck up, we need to do stuff."* This was his session: he was the one who kept both downed PCs alive.

**[[Luci]]** tried to springboard off Iggy into a wall-run, **critically failed** the acrobatics check, fell onto a blight for 1 damage and took 10 back off its spike, then set up an energy storm that struck anything moving in or out of its radius. A crit for 11 piercing dropped them to 0, and Iggy spent his last second-level slot to bring them back with 10 HP.

Odibli's second Shatter was widened to catch Luci's own square with Paul's spoken consent — Luci was down, auto-failed, and took a death-save failure for it. Luci came up, cast Sanctuary on Odibli, and dashed eighty feet clear of the fight to go find Davian, calling the raven swarms *"air superiority."* Odibli finished one blight with Firebolt and Dip cleaved the last for 16.

Kellan's verdict: *"you guys didn't even need the staff."*
