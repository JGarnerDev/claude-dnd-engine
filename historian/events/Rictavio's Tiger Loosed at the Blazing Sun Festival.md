Held in [[Vallaki]] on the day of the mandatory **Festival of the Blazing Sun**.

## The festival

[[Vargas Vallakovich]] tried and failed to light a **wicker sphere**. A citizen laughed and was **arrested on the spot**; [[Iggy]] claimed the laugh for one of his own jokes, told it to the crowd, and got the man released. Vargas forced a chant of **"all will be well,"** got flustered, and declared the festival a huge success while the effigy still would not burn. [[Luci]] and [[Odibli]] then lit it with **oil and a cantrip**.

## The tiger

[[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] slipped away moments before the cry of **"Tiger! Tiger!"** went up. The party found tracks and a busted cage-cart — the animal had been **well fed and cared for**. They found **[[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger]]** with **[[Rictavio]]**, who was calming it with meat.

Odibli cast **Speak with Animals**; the beast was happy to be scaring people. A summoned frog familiar drew it back into the cage.

[[Izek Strazni]] intercepted the party with guards; Luci deceived them about the direction and **split half his team** away from Rictavio.

Following where Karl and Nikolai had fled brought the party to **[[Wachter House]]**.
