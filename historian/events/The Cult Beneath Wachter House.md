Occurred beneath [[Wachter House]] in [[Vallaki]].

## The door

After killing eight animated skeletons in the cellar, the party found **footprints leading to a wall and disappearing into it**. [[Dip]] detected a **secret door**; [[Odibli]]'s **Detect Magic** read only thin arcana. The door let through **neither air nor light** and carried **no traps**.

## The chamber

> *"Flickering candles in iron holders fill this room with light and shadows. This room has a ten-foot-high ceiling and a large black pentagram inscribed on the stone floor. At each point of the pentagram rests a wooden chair. Seated in four of the five chairs are men and women in black robes with hoods: a young man who has the face of an angel; a balding hulk of a man; a squat, middle-aged woman; and a taller, younger woman with an unsettling glare. They rise to confront you."*

Dip claimed **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]**'s explicit permission and [[Iggy]] sold the lie. The cultists relaxed, called themselves a **book club**, mentioned the **devil worship** they practice, and said the lady would be down soon. **[[Majesto]]**, her imp, was kept there.

Then Fiona arrived, took their presence as agreement, and declared that **[[Izek Strazni]] must die**.

> **Reliability:** None of the four hooded members were ever named, and what devil or patron **[[The Wachter Cult]]** served is unestablished. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM. Recorded in [[Session 23 The Cult in the Cellar]] as part of the [[Curse of Strahd]] campaign.

Witnessed by [[The Party]].
