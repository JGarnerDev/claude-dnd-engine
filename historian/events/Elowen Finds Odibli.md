# Elowen Finds Odibli

[[Elowen Rimetusk]] found [[Odibli]] that afternoon. "There's two of you. One's at a desk a long way
from here and he hasn't looked up for years." She gave him [[The Unfinished Binding]]: "These are
your flock… count them before and after every time, bring them all back." Every spell he casts,
she said, helps somebody find him.
