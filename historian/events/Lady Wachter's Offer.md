Occurred in the parlor of [[Wachter House]] in [[Vallaki]].

## The conversation

**[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** apologized for her sons and asked why the party was curious about them. [[Luci]] spoke first, but Fiona turned to **[[Dip]]**, who vented about the village and the insane mayor. She agreed with all of it, **shrugged off her sons** (they had lost their father), and refused to discuss **[[Lady Fiona Wachter's Daughter]]**, once betrothed to [[Viktor Vallakovich]] until his madness wrecked her.

## The offer

She steered to their shared dislike of **[[Vargas Vallakovich]]**, hinted at a **coup**, and asked the party to **kill [[Izek Strazni]]**. [[Iggy]] pressed her for anything concrete behind the proposal and got nothing but secrecy.

Outside, the party agreed to **accept and betray it** — reach Izek first, find out why she wanted him dead. Fiona offered **money**, **protection from the coffin-shop arson charge**, and **access to her library**. [[Odibli]] left a familiar watching the house.

They then fed Izek a false direction on [[Rictavio]], and [[Luci]] told **[[Ireena Kolyana]]** the whole plot — who defended the Burgomaster as a good man with bad execution and urged research before anything rash.

> **Reliability:** Fiona never gave a reason for wanting Izek dead, and she died before the party asked. What her library, her money, or her promised "protection" were actually worth is untested.
