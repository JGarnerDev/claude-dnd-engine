Occurred at the **[[Raven River]]** crossing, on the road south from [[Vallaki]].

## The warning

A large **dire wolf** came out of the trees — **[[The Raven River Wolf]]**. [[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals** and asked whether it was friendly.

It told the party to **walk away**. They were **not welcome**. It **serves [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, and had been told to prevent **a group of four adventurers** from entering further **west**.

The party's road ran **south** to the [[Wizard of Wines]], not west across the bridge, so they went on unopposed.

## Why it matters

The order is the tell. Strahd knew there were **four** of them, knew they were coming, and posted a sentry on a specific crossing to turn them back from the **west** — not to kill them. Something over there is being kept from this party in particular.

Earlier the same day, at the [[Luna River]], a skeletal horse and rider had passed them without a word.

> **Reliability:** Nothing is established about what lies west, or why the order specifies a number rather than names. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM. Recorded in [[Session 24 The Vampire Hunter]] as part of the [[Curse of Strahd]] campaign.

Witnessed by [[The Party]].
