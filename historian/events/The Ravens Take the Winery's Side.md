## The Ravens Take the Winery's Side

When the party barricaded themselves into the fermentation room of the [[Wizard of Wines]], they found **scores of ravens quietly gathered along the rafters**, watching them **with great interest**.

It was the second sighting, not the first. During the retreat inside, [[Iggy]] — cornered by needle blights — glimpsed **something swoop down and snatch two of them**, killing one, and talked himself out of believing it.

Through the fight with [[The Winery Druid]] and her blights, **the ravens came down repeatedly**, tearing twig blights apart and thinning the numbers the party could not out-kill on their own.

Nobody asked them to. Nobody asked them why afterward.

> **Reliability:** No allegiance is established here. The party has been told the [[Keepers of the Feather]] exist and are anti-Strahd, but nothing in this session connects them to these birds — the link is DM-side inference and is deliberately not canonized. Confirm with DM.
