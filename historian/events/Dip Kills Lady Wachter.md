Occurred beneath [[Wachter House]] in [[Vallaki]].

## The break

**[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** had just declared that **[[Izek Strazni]] must die** and that "these heroes" would make it happen. [[Iggy]] said he needed to step out to take a piss. Told to do it there, he objected that it would **ruin their magic circle** — and walked out.

Fiona shouted **"get him!"** Combat began.

## The kill

The party won. **[[Dip]] killed Fiona and her imp [[Majesto]]** — the session's MVP. **[[The Wachter Cult]]** died with its leader.

Fiona dropped a set of **keys** and a **gold ring**.

The party then went to **[[Vargas Vallakovich]]** and told him they had **saved the day**.

> **Reliability:** What the keys open and what the ring is remain unknown; they are assumed carried by [[Odibli]] pending confirmation. How much the party actually disclosed to the Burgomaster is unrecorded, as is the fate of the household — the servants, the bodies, and [[Lady Fiona Wachter's Daughter]]. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM. Recorded in [[Session 23 The Cult in the Cellar]] as part of the [[Curse of Strahd]] campaign.

Witnessed by [[The Party]].
