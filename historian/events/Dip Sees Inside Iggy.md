# Dip Sees Inside Iggy

DM-only. During [[Iggy]]'s song at [[The Golden Lioness]] the hearth went still for about ten
seconds. [[Dip]]'s private roll (Arcana 15) opened the moment like a nesting doll: real, then
hollow, then real again, each layer smaller than the last. The innermost layer was not in the fire;
it was inside Iggy. Rolled and delivered at Session 31, for a moment that happened in Session 30.
Dip has shared none of it.

> **Reliability:** the 15–19 message from the plan's `Private (Dip)` block was sent; Sah is never
> named to Dip. Confirm with Ben what Dip took from it.
