# Nella Escapes Downriver

Over the crystals [[Nella]] said goodbye to [[Tithe]] ("19 flights and he never once dropped
me… fuck you all"), hung her hood on a bush and ran. [[Luci]] went after her alone. She ran
through the Spike Growth Nella had laid behind her, sprang off a tree to land at the ranger's
side, and the cinders of her own Energy Storm set the tree alight. Nella reached the river, caught
a rope she had rigged across it and was gone downstream. Luci carried the hood back and laid it on
the griffon.
