# Luci Names the Moon

Rising from the rubble mid-fight, Luci looked up at Aethryn's moon. It is vast and broken, shards drifting away from it, and its light is far stronger than any light in Barovia. She felt closer to the plane she came from. Without thinking, she named it Fida in the Dead Sun tongue, and her guiding voice answered one word, clearer than ever: 'Closer.' She leveled to 5.
