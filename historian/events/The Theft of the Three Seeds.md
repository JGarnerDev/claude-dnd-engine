Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Location: [[Wizard of Wines]]

## What Happened

In the same monologue where he revealed [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]], [[Davian Martikov]] explained what is actually wrong with his vineyard.

[[The Vintner Mage]] who founded the place made **three magic gems, also called seeds** — [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] — *"that allowed for healthy, great wines, even through Strahd's curse, which normally would have poisoned the ground and prevented the growth."* Every bottle the winery has ever shipped depended on them.

### Ten years ago — the first seed

> "Ten years ago a gem was stolen, and the best vintage we produce, Champagne du Stomp, stopped being produced. And to this day we have not been able to produce it. My son Urwin was supposed to be on guard, and I don't know what happened. It doesn't matter anymore."

[[Champagne du Stomp]] has not been made since. Davian closed the subject rather than dwell on it. The thief is unnamed and the seed unaccounted for.

### Three weeks ago — the second and third

Davian's account of the recent attacks runs in sequence:

1. The winery had been fending off **[[Baba Lysaga]]'s scarecrow constructs** for some time.
2. Three weeks before, Lysaga attacked in force herself, **found the second seed and took it for herself**.
3. She then **convinced the evil druids of [[Yester Hill]]** to attack, and they **took the third and carried it to their hideout**.
4. The Martikovs **counter-attacked and failed** — *"it was too hard to fight and we were overwhelmed."*
5. The druids came back with **a horde of blights** — the ones the party has now destroyed across Sessions 26, 27 and 28.

### Why Lysaga

> "Baba Lysaga, she believes that she is Strahd's mother, and so she has set out to oppose any that oppose him."

She learned the Martikovs work against [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] and came for them on that basis. The wine was never the point.

## The Bargain

Davian marked both Lysaga's location and the druids' on a map and asked for help, promising all the wine the party can carry and *"anything else we can do to repay you."* Each seed recovered restores one more of the winery's historic wines; all three restore the full range. Without them the winery is finished and no inn in Barovia carries Martikov wine again.

[[Odibli]] asked whether gifted wine would be poisoned too, was promised it cleaned, and immediately proposed using good wine as leverage in [[Vallaki]]'s power shift — *"we can convince people through the power of wine."* [[Iggy]] agreed the town was overdue a decent party. The party accepted.

> **Reliability:** Entirely one NPC's testimony, recovered from a machine transcript, and Davian has withheld from this party before — in Session 25 he claimed the druids were merely jealous and [[Iggy]]'s Insight read more to the story. Treat the account as what Davian says, not as settled fact. The "three weeks ago" figure and the ten-year gap are his. [[Baba Lysaga]]'s parentage claim **conflicts with Session 25 canon**, where the same man called her the midwife to Strahd's mother; both stand as reports, and the DM's ruling is that the gap may be her madness rather than an error. Whether she was physically present at the winery three weeks ago is also unclear — Session 25 has her reported *not* present. The vintage name and the hill name are both transcription corrections.
