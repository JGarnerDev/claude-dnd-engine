In the master bedroom of Death House, the fire in room 6 took the shape of a watching face — Strahd von Zarovich, regarding the intruders. Animated armor lurched to life and felled both Odibli and Iggy before the party put it down. The party did not yet know the name behind the face, but the land's master had marked them.
