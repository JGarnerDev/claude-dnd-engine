At the [[Blue Water Inn]], [[Odibli]] began a story about a dog, cast speak with animals, and mused on how many animals carry hidden stories in a language we don't share. He introduced [[Sergei Rubelgerd]], who barked and borfed his tale while Odibli translated: a Tibetan Mastiff from the **Zeatchean Steppes**, adventuring for years alongside his best friend, a humanoid named **Tidak**. They had seen dragons and giant serpents. One night a thick fog came over them and took Tidak away; Sergei waited at the edge of the fog for many days before finding the courage to follow — and was lost and captured in [[Barovia]].

The room wept. They loved Sergei.
