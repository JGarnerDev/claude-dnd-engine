# The Fox Fuses the Shadowless Spear

At a bridge just outside [[The Golden Lioness]], the fox that speaks for
[[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]] waited for [[Halsby]] with green, smoke-leaking eyes. "You did
what I asked. People rarely do." Halsby tested it first: it was earnest, possessed by something he
could not name, and it tracked him through his own Darkness. It turned down his offer of shadow
gifts in return for its name. He set down his spear and his shard, and it fused them into
[[The Shadowless Spear]]. "The debt is repaid." It ran into the woods, and Halsby reached level 5.

Seen by [[The Party]].
