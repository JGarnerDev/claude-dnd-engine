# Tithe Dies at the Waystation

Late on the morning of Day 2, [[Nella]] landed [[Tithe]] on the ridge above [[The Waystation]],
dismounted and sent it hunting: "Alright, Tithe. Go hunt." It dropped into the yard and clawed
[[Halsby]] through his Shield. A round later it snatched [[Odibli]] about 10 ft into the air; he
wriggled free and landed on his feet.

[[Iggy]] struck it with a smite that left a line of light, then opened wings of light and flew at
it. Halsby's Fire Bolt burned its left wing ash-grey. [[Dip]] leapt 10 ft up, hit it, and brought
it crashing down. On the ground it pecked Odibli to 0 HP and clawed Iggy. Halsby's second Fire
Bolt caught under the burnt wing and spread through the feathers. The griffon screamed at him,
leaned on the building and collapsed on top of Odibli.
