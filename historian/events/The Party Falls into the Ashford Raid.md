# The Party Falls into the Ashford Raid

The light of the Wanderer's Boon set the party down, rested, over a moonlit brewery yard in Ashford as raiders stripped it of barrels. Dip read the scene, relived the burning tavern of their childhood, raged, leveled to 5, and landed feet-first on a cask-hauler. Odibli landed hidden outside the wall and watched a cart of barrels escape south with a long-haired archer aboard, whom the raiders' leader called Nella. Luci and Iggy landed prone in the yard.
