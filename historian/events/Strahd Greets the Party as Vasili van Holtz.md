Occurred outside the gates of [[Vallaki]].

## The greeting

On their way to meet [[Rictavio]], the party was approached by a **noble-appearing man**. He congratulated them on defeating **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** and her planned uprising, and remarked how sad it was that **[[Vargas Vallakovich|the Burgomaster]]** was not rewarding them much.

He gave his name as **Vasili van Holtz**.

## The confirmation

Minutes later, vampires ambushed the party and downed all four PCs. After driving them off and stabilizing everyone, Rictavio told the party plainly: **Vasili van Holtz was [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]].**

This confirms firsthand the alias [[Henrik]] reported in [[Strahd Walks Vallaki as Vasili van Holtz]] — and shows Strahd had current, detailed knowledge of a killing the party had committed in secret the day before.

> **Reliability:** The identification is [[Rictavio]]'s assertion, given as Rudolph van Richten immediately after saving the party's lives. The party did not verify it independently. Whether the ambush minutes later was Strahd's doing is not established. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM. Recorded in [[Session 24 The Vampire Hunter]] as part of the [[Curse of Strahd]] campaign.

Witnessed by [[The Party]] — [[Iggy]], [[Luci]], [[Odibli]], and [[Dip]] all stood in front of him and did not know it.
