## The Winery Druid Falls

The party fought clear of the needle blights, barricaded the fermentation room, and found [[The Winery Druid]] on the balcony above the westernmost cask, **pouring a flask of thick syrup into the wine**.

She was unbothered. The blights outside were **"security, if you will."** Of the flask she would say only that they would find out — that it **"works like a charm every time."**

Then she cast **Entangle**, spoke **a word in Druidic**, and [[Twig Blight|twig blights]] came pouring out of the cask.

The fight was long and attritional — every blight that fell was replaced by another climbing out of the barrel — until [[Iggy]] read the room and understood that **they were not infinite**. Overhead, the winery's ravens came down again and again to help (see [[The Ravens Take the Winery's Side]]).

[[Dip]] broke the Entangle with a Strength save, raged, closed, and **killed her in a single greataxe turn** — 25 damage, hacked apart limb by limb and cleaved in half.

### What it did not resolve

Killing her **did not clean the wine** and **did not stop the blights**. [[Odibli]]'s arcana read afterward found druidic magic still hanging in the air, animating them from a source he could not locate — **at least one more druid**, still unaccounted for.

> **Reliability:** Her service to [[Baba Lysaga]] is inferred from [[Davian Martikov]]'s account in [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]], not from anything she said. Confirm with DM.
