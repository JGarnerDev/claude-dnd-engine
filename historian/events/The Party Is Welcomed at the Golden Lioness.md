# The Party Is Welcomed at the Golden Lioness

The night after the raid, [[Sable Wren]] fed her rescuers soup and hardtack in the one surviving
room of [[The Golden Lioness]]. [[Ashford]]'s townsfolk came by torchlight to thank them: Sheriff
[[Mara Tull]], her hurt deputy [[Dunny Marsh]], whom [[Iggy]] and [[Luci]] healed, and the mayor
[[Silas Crubb]], to whom Sable owes about 650 gp. Sable offered [[The Party]] rooms, meals and 10% of
the bar if they bring her stolen stock back from [[Nella]]'s camp, and they agreed
([[The Stolen Casks]]).

Iggy played and Luci danced, pulling old [[Agnes Brand]] into it. [[Sergei Rubelgerd]] and
[[Bosco]] found the party and joined in, and now sleep by the hearth.
