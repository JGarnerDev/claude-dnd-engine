Occurred at the **[[Wizard of Wines]]**, south of the [[Raven River]].

## The proprietor

**[[Davian Martikov]]** stepped out of the vineyard trees with **nine other folk** and introduced himself as the winery's proprietor. He told the party that **druids** had been preventing his people from delivering to [[Vallaki]] — the missing shipments [[Danika]] had sent them to find.

[[Dip]] asked what the winery had done to provoke them. Davian said they were just **jealous**, and that he really did not know. [[Iggy]]'s Insight read **more to the story**.

## The name

Davian named the leader of the druid forces: **[[Baba Lysaga]]**, a dark sorceress — **"Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night."** She was **midwife to [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]'s mother**, brought him into this world, and **directed his life**.

He reassured the party that she herself is **not present** — only her druids are.

## The assault

[[Luci]] healed the winery folk, and **several agreed to fight**. The party counted **three main doors and a loading area**. [[Odibli]]'s **familiar bird** scouted ahead, sensed animals, and was **taken out by something strange**. Luci's **Augury** on the loading dock returned **"wheel."**

**Five needle blights** attacked as the party closed in.

> **Reliability:** Everything about Baba Lysaga here is Davian's account, not party observation — and Iggy read his framing as incomplete. "Our mother" is unexplained. "Wheel" is not a standard Augury result. What killed the familiar is unidentified. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM.
