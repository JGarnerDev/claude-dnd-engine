# The Party Storms the Waystation

At mid-morning on Day 2, [[The Party]] drove [[Silas Crubb]]'s cart down the hill into [[Nella]]'s
camp at [[The Waystation]]. [[Dip]] cut the five horses loose about 100 ft out and tried to steer
by hand. The cart turned too little, glanced off the long shed, and knocked down the big
cask-hand.

[[Halsby]], who had seen the cart coming, sent a readied Lightning Bolt from the tip of
[[The Shadowless Spear]] through four of the crew. It killed the hedge priest. In the first round
[[Luci]] killed the big cask-hand, and Dip took the drover's head and his whip and split the wolf
in two. Nella shot down from her griffon under the low cloud. The fight was still going when the
table stopped.
