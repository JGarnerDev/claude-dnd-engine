Occurred at the meeting place northeast of [[Vallaki]].

## The ambush

A group of **vampires** ambushed the party and **downed all four PCs** — [[Iggy]], [[Luci]], [[Odibli]], and [[Dip]].

**[[Rictavio]]** and his **[[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger]]** drove the attackers off, and he **stabilized** the party afterward.

## The name

He then told them three things:

- The nobleman calling himself **Vasili van Holtz**, met at the gates minutes earlier, was **[[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**.
- His own real name is **Rudolph van Richten**, a famous **vampire hunter**. He lost his **child and his wife** to vampires long ago.
- He is here to wreck Strahd's plans in Vallaki — specifically those involving the **Vistani vampires**.

He told the party to seek out the **[[Keepers of the Feather]]** back in Vallaki: also set against Strahd, and possible allies.

The party took a **long rest** afterward.

The bitter irony: they had already given Rictavio's name to **[[Izek Strazni]]** as the man who loosed the tiger.

> **Reliability:** Van Richten's identity is his own claim, given in the minutes after a rescue. Canon recorded Rictavio as a **half-elf** from party observation in [[Session 16 The Blue Water Inn]]; Van Richten is not, and whether the disguise extends to his race is unresolved. The vampires were not connected in play to any named Vistani. The in-world date is estimated. Confirm with DM. Recorded in [[Session 24 The Vampire Hunter]] as part of the [[Curse of Strahd]] campaign.
