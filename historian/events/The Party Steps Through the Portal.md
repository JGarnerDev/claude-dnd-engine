## What Happened

The party accepted [[Davian Martikov]]'s quest to recover [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]]. He answered it with one more secret — something the Martikovs had never shown an outsider:

> "The mage who founded this vineyard, he created one other relic that has survived here until this time. We don't know exactly what magic resides in it — it's a powerful and ancient magic that none can reproduce anymore. But he told us that one day travellers would come to protect the vineyard, and they should be given this relic. So here it is. All we know is it will bring you somewhere where you can gather strength, and then return strengthened and ready to protect and save the vineyard."

[[Odibli]] rolled **Arcana 19** on [[The Wanderer's Boon]] and read considerably more than the family knew: a portal, and an extraordinarily powerful one — *"a lost magic that is more powerful than what you're used to seeing"*, unmistakably the same hand that made the seeds. He could tell that accepting the gift was the whole activation. He could not tell where it led.

He asked whether they needed to hold hands, got no answer, and decided for himself — *"I'm gonna hold everyone's hands."* Then he grasped the stone.

> "You kind of are surrounded by a light all of a sudden, and the light is almost like mist but still luminous. And just as it starts to hit the point of being blinding, you realise that the light is fading — yet you are no longer where you are. You are no longer at the winery. You've never been to this place before."

Kellan cut the session there, offering Jeff the choice of playing the arrival now or picking it up cold next time. Jeff took the segue — *"let's close it exactly here."*

## Why It Is the Seam

This is the campaign season-swap, executed in fiction. The engine's design has the two campaigns alternating like television seasons, with the party persisting across both and moving between worlds by a shallow in-narrative device. That device is this relic.

Kellan made the handoff explicit at the table:

> "My position is, I'll just say, it's up to you, Jeff, in your domain. The party has been sent here to gain strength, whatever that ends up meaning."

[[Luci]]'s read, immediately: *"the party's been sent to make Strahd more dangerous."*

Session 27 had closed on an ambiguity — Kellan saying *"prepare to take over"* without specifying whether he meant the homebrew or just the next stretch of Barovia. This settles it as a **full season-swap**.

## What Is Left Behind

Barovia is paused, not finished:

- Three seeds owed to [[Davian Martikov]] — one held by [[Baba Lysaga]], one at [[Yester Hill]], one missing for ten years with no lead.
- Every other open Strahd thread the campaign was carrying, from [[Ireena Kolyana]]'s safety to the Tarokka reading.
- [[Ireena Kolyana]], [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], [[Bosco]] and [[Sergei Rubelgerd]] — whether the party's companions came through the portal with them was never asked.
