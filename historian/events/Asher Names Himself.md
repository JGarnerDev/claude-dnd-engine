# Asher Names Himself

[[Sable Wren]] walked the party to the cave by the river. Over [[Tithe]]'s body [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak|Asher]]
said, "There is something under him that remembers being killed. My water… has gone sour," and
turned it to gold. He gave his full name with a bow. To [[Halsby]]: "You did what I asked. And I
did what I said. We are square." [[Dip]] told the story of his hands and took on Asher's ask, the
soul under the Four, for nothing ("I think I love you, Asher"). Asher sensed Dip's sister, let
[[Iggy]]'s guest stay, gave Iggy [[Oathcaller]], and gave Dip's cart-whip back as [[Tithe-Taker]].
"The debt has been paid."
