# Mara Frees Shaw

In the last cell [[Shaw Proudfoot]] spoke for the first time: "Tell them to get in line," and "I'm
not a threat, but I could be for the right price." He told his story and showed the letter he
carries north to the [[Deorum Ostium]]. [[Luci]] lit the cell. [[Iggy]] knelt, named him a hero
and asked for him. [[Mara Tull]] said of the courts, "They haven't done jack shit until there's
something to be hanged," and unlocked the door: "I don't forgive you for what you did. But you come
back here with a different name in about a month, I'll look the other way."
