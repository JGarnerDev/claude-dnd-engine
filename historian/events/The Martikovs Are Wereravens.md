Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Location: [[Wizard of Wines]]

## What Happened

The blight ambush outside the [[Wizard of Wines]] was won as much by raven swarms coming off the winery's upper floor as by the party. When [[Davian Martikov]] met them afterwards and heard the whole account — four druids dead, the staff destroyed, every cask poisoned — he said the party had earned a debt and that he should tell them *"more about the real history and what has befell this place."*

He opened by dismantling an assumption the party had held for three sessions: he is **not** the Wizard of Wines, and not a wizard. *"Alas, I'm not even a wizard. Don't take me for wrong, I could cast a spell or two, but I'm no wizard."* The title belongs to [[The Vintner Mage]], the vineyard's long-dead founder.

Then the family secret:

> "Us Martikovs have run the winery for a very long time. Something else you should know — those ravens that saved you. Well, our whole family, a long time ago, we became wereravens. It's lost to history and to our family history exactly how that happened, but somewhere along the line we became infected, and to this day everyone in our line is a wereraven."

The table's reaction was immediate and delighted — *"that's so cool"* from Ben, who then asked to become a whale.

## What It Answers

Session 27 ended with an open thread: **why can Davian command ravens?** [[Odibli]] asked [[Dustin]] directly under Speak with Animals and Dustin shifted on his feet and said to ask Davian. The answer is that there is no commanding involved. The murder dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]] to watch the winery was family, answering family.

That also drags the [[Keepers of the Feather]] into focus. [[Rictavio]] named them in Session 24 as an anti-Strahd group in [[Vallaki]] and possible allies, and the party has had nothing but the name since. The ravens watching the winery came from the Blue Water Inn; the Blue Water Inn is run by [[Urwin]] and [[Danika]]; Urwin is a Martikov. The party has been sleeping in a Keepers safehouse.

## What It Costs Lysaga

[[Baba Lysaga]]'s siege stops looking arbitrary. Davian's account is that she *"found us and found out that we oppose Strahd and that we are working against him"* — the Martikovs are not collateral in a wine dispute, they are an anti-Strahd network and she came for them as one.

> **Reliability:** Recovered from a machine transcript. The family name was heard as "Mardukovs" throughout and is written **Martikov** from prior canon. Davian said *everyone* in the line is a wereraven, which makes [[Urwin]], [[Brom]] and [[Bray]] wereravens by blood — but [[Danika]] married in, and whether the condition passes by marriage, by bite, or only by birth was never asked. The [[Keepers of the Feather]] link is now strongly implied from two directions but **still never stated at the table**. Confirm with DM.
