# The Camp Horses Give Consent

In the yard of [[The Waystation]], [[Odibli]] cast Speak with Animals and put it to the five horses
the crew had left behind: "Horses, I would like your consent." [[The Camp Horses]] gave it, all
five. Old Tom went further: "The promised land has come… They will free us. Free us all."
