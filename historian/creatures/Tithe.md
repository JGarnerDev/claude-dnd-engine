# Tithe

A griffon: AC 12, 95 HP as the waystation boss, fly 80 ft, beak and claws. While Nella rode it, it
only dashed, disengaged or dodged. Loose, it dove on whoever was in the open, and could snatch a
Medium target and carry it up.

## Session 34

Nella landed it on the ridge, dismounted, slapped its neck and said over the crystals, "Alright,
Tithe. Go hunt." It climbed back into the cloud, then came down into the yard. It clawed Halsby
through his Shield, snatched Odibli about 10 ft into the air (he wriggled free and landed on his
feet), and buffeted Halsby and Odibli with its wings. Iggy smote it, Halsby's Fire Bolt burned its
left wing ash-grey, and Dip leapt onto it 10 ft up and brought it down. On the ground it pecked
Odibli to 0 HP and clawed Iggy. Halsby's second Fire Bolt caught under the burnt wing and spread
through the feathers. It screamed at Halsby, leaned on the building and collapsed on top of
Odibli's body.

The DM called it an animal "trained for war, amongst other things".
