## Background

Majesto was **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]**'s imp, kept in the hidden pentagram chamber beneath [[Wachter House]] where [[The Wachter Cult]] met. His presence is the clearest evidence the circle's talk of **devil worship** was more than posturing.

He died in the fight that followed [[Iggy]]'s escape from the chamber. **[[Dip]] killed both Majesto and Fiona** — the session's MVP.

> **Reliability:** The notes name him only as "Majesto her imp." Whether he was a bound familiar, a pact servant, or something the circle summoned is unestablished, as is his part in the fight. Stat block assumed to be the standard [[Imp]]. Confirm with DM.
