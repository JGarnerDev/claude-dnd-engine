## Background

Bosco's documented history begins in Session 12, when [[Dip]] threw him into [[Parriwimple]]'s domicile as a prank. Bosco left scratch marks on Parriwimple — his first recorded act of property damage, and technically not his fault. Parriwimple mentioned the scratch marks the next morning, unaware of the culprit.

In Session 13, [[Dip]] decided to find him again. The full party helped.

## The Name

Named by [[Iggy]] (Jeff). Bosco Trashborn, the Pillager — acknowledging his origins, his nature, and his documented history of breaking and entering.

Iggy witnessed it first — a raccoon entering a shop, stealing bread, fighting the owners, departing heroically. He composed the ballad over breakfast. Found Bosco by afternoon.

## Appearance

Grey-black fur gone slightly ragged at the edges — but thicker and better-kept than anything else surviving in Barovia has any right to be. The mask is darker than it should be, almost deliberate. A thin scar cuts across the left side of his muzzle, origin unknown, predating the party. His hands move before he decides to move them. His eyes are always already working.

## Bonding (Session 13)

- **[[Odibli]]** cast Minor Illusion to create a cotton candy supply. Bosco attacked him for 1 claw damage.
- **[[Iggy]]** threw a ration nearby. Bosco ate it. Companion bond established.
- Odibli ate a ration in front of Bosco immediately after. Cold war began. Status: ongoing.

## The Cold War

[[Odibli]] cast Minor Illusion to produce cotton candy — fake food, presented as real. Bosco attacked him for 1 claw damage. Moments later, Iggy produced an actual ration and Bosco ate it, establishing the companion bond.

Odibli then ate a ration directly in front of Bosco. Deliberately, or obliviously — the intent is unclear. Bosco registered it either way.

The grievances are layered: deception first, then provocation. Bosco does not distinguish between the two. Both are on the ledger. The cold war persists into the road to Vallaki.
