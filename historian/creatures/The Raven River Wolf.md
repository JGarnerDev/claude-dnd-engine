Location: the [[Raven River]] crossing | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Background

A large **dire wolf** that came out at the [[Raven River]] crossing as the party rode south for the missing wine.

[[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals** and asked whether it was friendly. It told the party to **walk away** — they were **not welcome**. It said it **serves [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, and that it had been told to prevent **a group of four adventurers** from entering further **west**.

The party's road ran **south** to the [[Wizard of Wines]], not west across the bridge, so they went on unopposed.

## Why it matters

The order is the tell: Strahd knew there were **four** of them, knew they were coming, and posted a sentry on a specific crossing to stop them going **west** — not to kill them. Something west of the Raven River is being kept from this party in particular.

> **Reliability:** The wolf's own name, whether it is a dire wolf or something wearing that shape, and what lies west that Strahd is guarding are all unknown. Stat block assumed [[Dire Wolf]] from the description "big dire wolf." Confirm with DM.
