Location: [[Ashford]]

Five horses from the raiders' camp at [[The Waystation]], kept by the party after the fight in
[[Session 35 The Reeve's Ledger]]: **Clover**, **Old Tom**, **Biscuit**, **Nettle** and **Parson**.

[[Odibli]] cast Speak with Animals in the yard and put it to them plainly: *"Horses, I would like
your consent."* They gave it. Old Tom, the eldest, took the party for something out of prophecy:
*"The promised land has come. They will free us. Free us all."* They like Odibli, and he can talk to
them.

They came home to [[Ashford]] with the party's own five horses, the casks and the griffon.

> **Reliability:** the names were said aloud at the table and match the plan's names (?). Stat
> block assumed. Who keeps and stables them, and where, is not recorded. Confirm with DM.
