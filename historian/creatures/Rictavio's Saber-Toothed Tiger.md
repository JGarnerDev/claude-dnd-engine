## Background

The beast that turned the Blazing Sun festival into a manhunt. Townspeople fled shouting **"Tiger! Tiger!"**, and the party found **tracks and a busted cage-cart** — the cart made it clear the animal had been **well fed and cared for**, not wild.

They found it with **[[Rictavio]]**, who was calming it with pieces of meat. [[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals**; the tiger was **happy that it was scaring people**. The party lured it back toward the cage with treats, and Odibli's **summoned frog familiar** drew it the rest of the way in.

[[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] are the party's suspects for loosing it — the thread that led them to [[Lady Fiona Wachter]]'s door.

## The rescue

In [[Session 24 The Vampire Hunter]], when vampires ambushed the party at the meeting place and downed all four PCs, **Rictavio and the saber-toothed tiger** drove the attackers off. Rictavio then stabilized the party and revealed his name as **Rudolph van Richten**.

> **Reliability:** Session 21 calls the animal a "tiger"; Session 24 specifies **saber-toothed**. This record treats them as the same creature. One note also calls it a "lion" — read here as the same animal. Whether it is a trained companion, an ordinary circus beast, or something stranger a vampire hunter keeps on hand is unconfirmed. Confirm with DM.
