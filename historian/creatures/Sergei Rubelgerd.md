## Acquisition (Session 15)

Bought at the [[Vallaki]] market, with the whole party pitching in. [[Odibli]] passed an animal handling check to ride him, then used speak with animals to offer the name **Sergei Rubelgerd**. Sergei accepted it, declared he would be a good boy, and asked for snacks.

## Backstory (revealed Session 16)

At the [[Blue Water Inn]], [[Odibli]] cast speak with animals and let Sergei tell his own tale to the room. Sergei is a Tibetan Mastiff, native to the **Zeatchean Steppes**, who spent his life adventuring alongside his best friend — a humanoid named **Tidak**. The two had seen dragons and giant serpents and traveled far and wide.

One evening a thick fog came over them and took Tidak away. Sergei waited at the edge of the fog for many days until he found the courage to go after him — but was lost, and captured, in [[Barovia]]. That is how the party came to find him in a shop in [[Vallaki]]. The room wept at the tale.

> **Reliability:** Tidak, the Zeatchean Steppes, and Sergei's capture are from Miguel's Session 16 notes, told in-fiction through Sergei. Tidak has no entity file yet — recorded here as lore. Confirm with DM whether Tidak is a thread to develop.
