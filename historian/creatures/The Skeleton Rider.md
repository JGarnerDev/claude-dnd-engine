## Background

As the party crossed the **[[Luna River]]** on the road south, a **skeletal horse and rider** passed them. It went by **benignly**, ignoring [[Odibli]]'s attempts to get its attention, and rode **south into the forest**.

No fight, no words, no explanation. It is recorded here because it was undead, purposeful, and travelling the same direction the party was — and because nothing in Barovia moves without a reason.

> **Reliability:** The party never learned what it was, who rode it, or where it was going. Whether the rider was itself skeletal or merely mounted on a skeletal horse is ambiguous in the notes. `state: unknown` and CR unknown reflect that it was never engaged. Confirm with DM.
