## Overview

The central tavern of [[Barovia Village]]. Owned by [[Alenka]], [[Mirabel]], and [[Sorvia]]. [[Arik]] serves as bartender. Named for the wine of the region.

Villagers whisper of it in the streets — it's one of the few gathering places in an otherwise hushed and frightened town.

## First Visit (Session 11)

Party followed villagers' whispers to find the tavern upon arriving in Barovia. Purchased pitchers of wine. Met [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], who bought a second pitcher and offered the escort quest for [[Ireena Kolyana]].

Evening: the party returned after their other errands, got mildly drunk, and performed. [[Iggy]] played music; [[Dip]] recounted tales; [[Luci]] danced. [[Arik]] nearly cried. The bar owners all enjoyed it. [[Alenka]] recommended visiting [[Madame Eva]] before the party left.
