## First Visit (Session 11)

Party visited to buy equipment. [[Iggy]] purchased a shield. [[Bildrath Cantemir]] charged exorbitant prices; [[Dip]] cursed him. The interaction ended poorly.

Later: [[Dip]] returned to prank Bildrath — lured a raccoon into the shop with a biscuit. Ruckus inside. Bildrath hollered for [[Parriwimple]], who emerged. Party stealthed away.

## Party Relationship

[[Dip]] is specifically on hostile terms with Bildrath. The rest of the party is unwelcome but not targeted individually. Bildrath likely remembers their faces.
