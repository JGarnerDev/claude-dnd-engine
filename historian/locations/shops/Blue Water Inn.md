## Overview

A lodging house and tavern inside the walls of [[Vallaki]], recommended to the party by a gate guard. Run by [[Urwin]] and his wife [[Danika]], who tends the bar; their sons [[Brom]] and [[Bray]] work the kitchen. The extravagantly dressed bard [[Rictavio]] is a resident.

The crowd is rough. Wine is normally a draw, but shipments are overdue — the "wizard of wine" who ships it is late (see [[Find the Wine Wizard]]).

> **Reliability:** The Blue Water Inn is a real, canonical Curse of Strahd location, but its layout and full cast beyond those met in Session 16 are not yet established in play. Confirm details with DM as they surface.

## First Visit (Session 16)

The party entered to a hostile crowd and performed to win them over — disastrously for [[Dip]], triumphantly for [[Bosco]] and [[Sergei Rubelgerd]]. [[Danika]] rewarded them with a free night's lodging and stew, and asked them to find her missing wine shipment. [[Urwin]] offered to lead them to their rooms; the party took the night as a long rest. The party also met the dissenters [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] and the wolf-hunters [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] and [[Yevgeni Krushkin]] here.
