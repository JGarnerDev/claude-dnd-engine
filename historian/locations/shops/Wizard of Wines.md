Region: south of the [[Raven River]] | Proprietor: [[Davian Martikov]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## The estate

A **vineyard with a house** and a **winery**. The party approached through the trees and were met by **[[Davian Martikov]]** and **nine other folk** who emerged behind him.

Scouting the assault, the party counted **three main doors and a loading area.**

## The siege

**Druids** have been preventing the winery from delivering to [[Vallaki]] — the cause of the overdue shipments [[Danika]] asked the party to chase. Davian named **[[Baba Lysaga]]** as the leader of the druid forces, and said she herself is **not present**.

[[Luci]] healed the winery folk, and **several agreed to fight** alongside the party.

[[Odibli]]'s **familiar bird** scouted ahead, sensed animals, and was then **taken out by something strange** — still inside. Luci's **Augury** on the loading-dock approach returned **"wheel."**

**Five [[Needle Blight|needle blights]]** attacked as the party closed in.

> **Reliability:** [[Iggy]]'s Insight said there is more to the winery's quarrel with the druids than Davian admitted, so the siege's cause is not settled. What killed Odibli's familiar in one stroke is unidentified. `state: closed` reflects the halted deliveries, not a confirmed shutdown. Confirm with DM.
