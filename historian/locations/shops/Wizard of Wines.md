## The estate

A **vineyard with a house** and a **winery**. The party approached through the trees and were met by **[[Davian Martikov]]** and **nine other folk** who emerged behind him.

Scouting the assault, the party counted **three main doors and a loading area.**

## The siege

**Druids** have been preventing the winery from delivering to [[Vallaki]] — the cause of the overdue shipments [[Danika]] asked the party to chase. Davian named **[[Baba Lysaga]]** as the leader of the druid forces, and said she herself is **not present**.

[[Luci]] healed the winery folk, and **several agreed to fight** alongside the party.

[[Odibli]]'s **familiar bird** scouted ahead, sensed animals, and was then **taken out by something strange** — still inside. Luci's **Augury** on the loading-dock approach returned **"wheel."**

**Five [[Needle Blight|needle blights]]** attacked as the party closed in.

> **Reliability:** [[Iggy]]'s Insight said there is more to the winery's quarrel with the druids than Davian admitted, so the siege's cause is not settled. What killed Odibli's familiar in one stroke is unidentified. `state: closed` reflects the halted deliveries, not a confirmed shutdown. Confirm with DM.

## Inside the winery (Session 26)

The party fought through the blights and barricaded themselves in the **fermentation room** — a two-story chamber holding **four casks**, each eight feet wide and twelve feet tall, under rafters where **scores of ravens** roost.

[[The Winery Druid]] was on the balcony above the westernmost cask, **emptying a flask of thick syrup into the wine**. She was killed there by [[Dip]]. See [[The Winery Druid Falls]].

**Every cask in this room is poisoned.** The druidic magic animating the blights **outlived her**, meaning at least one more druid is still somewhere in the building. The ravens in the rafters fought on the party's side throughout — see [[The Ravens Take the Winery's Side]].

## What the Place Actually Is (Session 28)

[[Davian Martikov]] is **not** the Wizard of Wines. The title belongs to [[The Vintner Mage]], the mage who founded the vineyard — *"a mage whose name is lost in history"* — and the place is described as very old.

He made **[[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]]**: three magic gems, also called seeds, *"that allowed for healthy, great wines, even through Strahd's curse, which normally would have poisoned the ground and prevented the growth."* They are the entire reason anything grows here. All three are now gone — one stolen ten years ago on [[Urwin]]'s watch, one taken by [[Baba Lysaga]], one carried to [[Yester Hill]] by her druids. See [[The Theft of the Three Seeds]].

The **Martikovs** have run the place for generations, and **every one of them is a wereraven** — which is what the ravens in the rafters have been all along. See [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]].

The founder also left **[[The Wanderer's Boon]]** here, held by the family for generations against the day travelers would come to defend the vineyard. It is now gone with the party. See [[The Party Steps Through the Portal]].

## The Exterior (Session 28)

The party left the cleared building and were ambushed on the grounds by the blights that had been waiting outside — untouched by [[The Vampire Staff]]'s death scream, which **did not carry past the building's walls**, settling the question Session 27 left open. Raven swarms came off the **upper floor** in answer to [[Dip]]'s shout and killed roughly half of them. See [[The Blight Ambush at the Winery]].

The exterior is now clear. **[[Adrian Martikov]]** and **[[Elvir Martikov]]**, two of the household who had gone out against the blights earlier, had been driven back to Davian and could not say whether the party survived.
