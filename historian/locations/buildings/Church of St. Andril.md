City: [[Vallaki]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

The Church of St. Andril stands in [[Vallaki]], dedicated to [[Lathander]] and named for its namesake saint, St. Andril. Stained-glass saints line its windows. The bones of the saint, kept here, granted the church a **boon of protection** — until they were stolen.

Its clergy and staff: the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]], the altar boy [[Yeska]], and the groundskeeper-gravedigger [[Milivoj]].

> **Reliability:** The saint's name is recorded from the notes as "St Andril" (also rendered "St Andral"); the church is canonized here as the "Church of St. Andril" pending confirmation. Confirm with DM.

## Session 17 — The Stolen Bones

The party came to the church seeking shelter from [[Strahd]] for [[Ireena Kolyana]]. There they learned the protective saint's bones had been stolen. Their investigation traced the theft to the gravedigger [[Milivoj]], who had sold the bones to [[Henrik]] the coffinmaker — clearing the suspected altar boy [[Yeska]]. The bones grant a boon of protection specifically to this church; with them gone, the church is exposed.
