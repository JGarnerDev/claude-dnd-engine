City: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Description

The Church of the Morning Lord is a battered stone building in [[Barovia Village]], dedicated to the god known as the Morning Lord — the primary deity acknowledged in Barovia. The exterior is claw-marked from repeated undead attacks. Fire has scarred sections of the structure. Inside: unlit, four doors, rubble in the main hall, and the persistent smell of mildew.

It is not abandoned — [[Father Donavich]] lives here — but it has the feeling of a building that survived something terrible and was never repaired.

## Below the Floor

A trap door in the main hall leads to a cellar where [[Doru]], Donavich's son turned vampire spawn, is caged. The party heard him cry out *"Father, I'm starving!"* Communication through the trap door was attempted and abandoned.

## Churchyard

[[Kolyan Indirovich]], late Burgomaster of Barovia Village, was buried in the churchyard during Session 13. Each party member took a turn at the shovel; [[Dip]] used his hands.

## First Visit (Session 13)

The party arrived carrying Kolyan's coffin. They found Donavich in the main hall — pacing, muttering, clearly destabilized by a year of sleepless prayer. During the burial proceedings, Donavich revealed that [[Ireena Kolyana]] is not a blood relative of the Kolyanovich family.
