City: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

Residents: [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], [[Ireena Kolyana]]. [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s body is inside, awaiting burial.

## Description

Exterior: visibly damaged — evidence of wolf and creature attacks. Interior: intact, mansion-like. A faint odor of decay was noted by the party (from [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s body in an adjacent room).

## First Visit (Session 11)

[[Ismark Kolyanovich]] led the party here after their meeting at the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]]. [[Ireena Kolyana]] explained that wolves and creatures had attacked the mansion, and her father [[Kolyan Indirovich]] had died from the trauma. His body remains inside.

Ireena's request: help bury her father. [[Odibli]] and [[Iggy]] were eager to help. [[Odibli]] spoke of "guiding them through the veil."

## Status

[[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s body is inside, awaiting burial. Active pending action for the party.
