Location: [[Barovia]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

A dilapidated windmill the party spotted from a hilltop on the road to [[Vallaki]] in Session 15, while scouting for a food source. They never went inside.

## What the Party Knows

- A squawking, agitated raven guarded the door. Via Odibli's speak with animals, it warned them to turn away and run — enter and you will die — then flew off.
- [[Bosco]] confirmed danger by scent: meat pies, the same smell tied to the [[The Dream Pastry Woman|dream-pastry hag]] from Session 13.
- The party concluded a night hag lives here and chose to equip themselves in [[Vallaki]] before any confrontation.

> **Reliability:** The hag's presence is the party's inference (raven warning + Bosco's scent association), unconfirmed by entry. "Old Bonegrinder" is used as the windmill's name per the DM's tagging; it is not stated in the session notes. Confirm with DM.
