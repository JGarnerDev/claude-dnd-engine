Region: past the [[Raven River]], near the [[Wizard of Wines]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## What is known

When the party returned to the tavern after the vampire ambush, **[[Danika]]** placed the missing **wine shipment** as **past Ravenriver, and nearby Van Richten's tower.**

The party has not visited it.

## The name

The direction was given in the same session in which **[[Rictavio]]** revealed his true name as **Rudolph van Richten**, the vampire hunter. Neither Danika nor the party remarked on the coincidence at the table.

> **Reliability:** The tower is a landmark in someone else's directions — never seen, never entered, its condition and contents unknown. `state: abandoned` is an assumption from the fact that Van Richten himself is living under an alias in [[Vallaki]]. Whether the tower is his, whether Danika knows it is his, and whether the wine's disappearance near it is coincidence are all open. Confirm with DM.
