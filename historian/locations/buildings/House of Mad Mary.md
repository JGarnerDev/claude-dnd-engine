City: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

A private residence in [[Barovia Village]], known informally as the House of Mad Mary. [[Mad Mary]] lives here, consumed by grief for her missing daughter [[Gertrude]].

## First Visit (Session 11)

Party heard crying from the house as [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] led them toward the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]]. They entered and found [[Mad Mary]] clutching a doll.

The doll bore a tag: **"Is No Fun, Is No Blinsky!"**

Mary explained that [[Gertrude]] had gone west despite her warnings, and she believed her daughter to be dead.
