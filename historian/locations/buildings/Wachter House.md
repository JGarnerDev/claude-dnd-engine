City: [[Vallaki]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Above ground

A **fine house** in [[Vallaki]], staffed with at least one servant who turned [[Luci]] away at the door. The **parlor** is well decorated, the finery **slightly tarnished**, the walls hung with **many nice painted portraits**. [[Iggy]] first saw the interior — and its mistress — through a window.

[[Odibli]] scouted the house with a summoned bird and found **nothing remarkable**.

## The cellar

**Eight animated hostile skeletons** occupied the basement. Past them, **footprints led to a wall and disappeared into it**. [[Dip]] detected a **secret door**; [[Odibli]]'s **Detect Magic** read only thin arcana in the air. The door let through **neither air nor light**, and carried **no traps**.

## The pentagram chamber

Behind the door: a **ten-foot-high** room, **flickering candles in iron holders**, and a **large black pentagram inscribed on the stone floor** with a **wooden chair at each of its five points**. This was the meeting place of [[The Wachter Cult]], and where [[Majesto]] was kept.

**[[Lady Fiona Wachter]] and her circle died here**, killed by the party in [[Session 23 The Cult in the Cellar]].

> **Reliability:** The house's formal name is inferred from the family name; the notes call it only "the house." What happened to the household after the killings — the servants, the sons, the bodies, who now holds the property — is entirely unaddressed. Confirm with DM.
