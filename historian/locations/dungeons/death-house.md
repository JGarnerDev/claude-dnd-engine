## Overview

A four-story haunted manor with a dungeon basement, just outside the village of Barovia. Built and operated by the Durst family, who ran a death cult within. Rose and Thorn's ghosts lured travelers inside; the house itself functioned as a trap.

## Floor Layout (as explored)

- **Floor 1:** Entry, Black Sheep Inn access not applicable (this is Death House proper). Animated armor in main area.
- **Floor 2:** Library with hidden room; Strahd's letter to the Dursts found here. Hand crossbow, bolts, books, iron key to area 20.
- **Floor 3:** Nursery with cursed mirror (visions of nursemaid and Walter). Balcony looking easterly. Master bedroom with jewelry.
- **Attic:** Rose and Thorn's room. Dollhouse with trapped door to basement. Animated broom.
- **Dungeon/Basement:** Walter's empty crypt. Cultist chests. Well with key. Ghoul chambers. Cult leader quarters. Ghast chambers. Cult ritual room with statue (Strahd's face). Altar. Portcullis to flooded southern tunnel.

## Key Discoveries

- **Strahd's letter** (Session 2): Hidden room in library. Strahd's letter to the Dursts dismissing their cult as nothing to do with him.
- **Nursery mirror** (Session 4): Visions of nursemaid/Gustav affair, Walter's birth, and nursemaid's murder.
- **Walter Durst's crypt** (Session 6): Empty. Thorn revealed the full backstory.
- **Cult leader quarters** (Session 8): Cloak of protection, 4 healing potions, chain shirt, mess kit, alchemist's fire, bullseye lantern, thieves' tools, yellow leather spellbook (spells listed in Session 8 record).
- **Cult relics** (Session 10): 13 ritual items in niches — see Session 10 record.
- **Orb on statue** (Session 10): Smashed by Odibli. Contained shadow essence that he absorbed to revive the party.

Session 10: Luci sacrificed herself on the altar. The mists carried the party out. Rose and Thorn were stranded when Ben and Miguel left their scroll pages behind. Strahd greeted the party outside.
