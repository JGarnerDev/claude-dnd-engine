## Overview

Barovia is a valley domain sealed from the outside world by supernatural mists. Those who enter cannot leave. The land itself seems to answer to [[Strahd Von Zarovich]], who has ruled here since before living memory. Villages cling to existence; the roads are dangerous after dark.

## Known Locations

- [[Death House]] — haunted manor just outside [[Barovia Village]], cleared by the party in Session 10
- [[Barovia Village]] — the main settlement; party arrived here after exiting Death House
- Vallaki — a walled town further into the valley; not yet visited

## The Party's Arrival

The party was drawn into Barovia through the mists after Session 1. They spent Sessions 1–10 inside [[Death House]] before emerging onto the outskirts of [[Barovia Village]], where [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] greeted them.

> **Reliability:** The region's existence and general character are campaign-established facts. Specific settlement details (Vallaki, the castle's interior) are canonical Curse of Strahd lore — not yet confirmed at table.
