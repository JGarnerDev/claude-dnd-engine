Campaign: [[Homebrew Campaign]]

Threnai was a structured, living civilization built around the preservation and exchange of **meaning** — not just knowledge, but the context, narrative continuity, and emotional weight behind it. At its center stood a grand city and the **Great Library**, ringed by towns and villages in a fractal spread. Culturally it was mercantile in a non-material sense: its people traded in stories, histories, artifacts, and fragments of legend gathered across planes, valuing narrative significance and interpretive depth over rarity. It was lively and celebratory — festivals, performance, and storytelling made preservation something lived, not static.

Functionally, Threnai served as a **semantic anchor** for adjacent realities. Its god **[[Sah]]** was a real and present force, and its dominant culture, the Sahists, treated preservation as a sacred responsibility.

## Destruction

A decades-long ideological schism escalated into civil war. **[[The Correctors]]** — Library insiders who believed death was an imposed falsehood — accumulated influence and enacted a ritual to **break the plane's spine**, meaning to sever the process that "finalized" beings into fixed narrative and thereby defeat death. Instead it triggered catastrophic structural collapse: the framework holding the fractal settlements and central city fractured, cascaded outward, and severed the systems that upheld meaning. Threnai's function as an anchor collapsed with it.

> **Reliability:** Player-backstory canon for [[Iggy]], who survived only through Sah's intervention. Sourced to his debut session; origin lore predating the campaign. The plane's fate is fixed (destroyed); finer detail is open for DM use.
