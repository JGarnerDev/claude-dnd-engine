The celestial body responsible for the creation of [[Whatwill]]. 6.7433 millennia ago, the Astra collided with the dwarf planetoid — gouging the massive Caldera into the Struck Side, dislodging the Arc Side's islands, and casting [[The Slices]] into orbit. Smaller Islifts continue to orbit at varying altitudes.

On impact, the Astra fused briefly with the planet, then separated in a pirouette — spinning off trillions of glass shards into its orbital path. These shards are the source material of all Whatwill.

The Astra and the planet now travel separate elliptical trajectories, reconverging less impactfully every thousand years.

## The Orbit

Astra's path is a long, narrow ellipse. For most of a thousand years it sits far out in the dark, faint and nearly still against the Blanket. In the last stretch it brightens, speeds up, threads between the two suns, and is flung back out; the pass between the suns lasts weeks. The Glistenings fall on round thousands from the impact (year 0). In 6743 Astra is far out and gives no sign of its return.

## Glistenings

Each **Glistening** is anticipated and tracked by [[The Vitricants]] through their Vitrian calendar. [[The Lifted]] regard the Astra as the source of propulsion for their Islifts and interpret its actions toward them in near-religious terms.

The Astra's surface and interior are entirely unknown. No expedition is recorded as having reached it.
