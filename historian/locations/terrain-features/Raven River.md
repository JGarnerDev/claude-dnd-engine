## The crossing

The party reached the Raven River crossing while riding south for the missing wine. A large **dire wolf** came out of the trees — **[[The Raven River Wolf]]**.

[[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals**. The wolf told them to **walk away**: they were not welcome, it **serves [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, and it had been posted to prevent **a group of four adventurers** from going further **west**.

The party's road ran **south** to the [[Wizard of Wines]], not west across the bridge, so they passed on without a fight.

## What lies beyond

[[Danika]] placed the missing wine shipment **past Ravenriver, near [[Van Richten's Tower]]**.

> **Reliability:** The notes spell it both "Raven River" and "Ravenriver"; both are recorded as this feature. `state: contested` and `controlled_by` reflect Strahd's posted sentry, not a formal territorial claim. What is west of the crossing — and why the wolf's orders name four adventurers by number — is unknown. Confirm with DM.
