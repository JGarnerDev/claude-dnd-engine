## The crossing

The first river on the road south from [[Vallaki]] toward the wine. The party crossed it without difficulty.

As they crossed, a **skeletal horse and rider** — [[The Skeleton Rider]] — passed them. It went by **benignly**, ignored [[Odibli]]'s attempts to hail it, and rode **south into the forest**.

Further along the same road lies the [[Raven River]] crossing.

> **Reliability:** The river is named only in passing in the notes. Its size, its bridge or ford, and its relation to the [[Raven River]] are unestablished beyond the party crossing one before the other. Confirm with DM.
