Global Location: [[Barovia]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

> **Reliability:** Named "Tser Pool Encampment" per Curse of Strahd module canon (the Vistani camp west of Barovia Village where Madame Eva resides). The Vistani did not name it in play. Confirm with DM if a different in-world name is preferred.

## Known Information

- A lake several hundred feet across, ringed with tents and improvised encampments.
- The Vistani here are loyalists to [[Strahd]] — protection, stability. Traders, farmers, bards.
- Their ancestors helped Strahd recover from a grievous wound long ago.
- [[Madame Eva]] keeps her tent here and reads the tarokka.
- Nearby terrain: a waterfall beside a chasm, and the River Ivlis below the falls.

## Party Knowledge

The party reached the camp on the road to [[Vallaki]] in Session 14, traded performances with the Vistani, received [[Madame Eva]]'s tarokka reading, and stayed the night before continuing west. They were supplied with torches and basic provisions on departure.
