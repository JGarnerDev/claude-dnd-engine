## Overview

The outskirts are the muddy, mist-shrouded open ground that rings [[Barovia Village]] — the last stretch of open land before the village proper begins. [[Death House]] sits on this perimeter.

The party emerged here at the end of Session 10 after the mists carried them out of [[Death House]]. [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] was waiting.

## Known Features

- [[Death House]] — haunted manor; the party's point of exit

## Session 10 Events

[[Strahd Von Zarovich]] greeted the party on these grounds. The mists surrounded everything. [[Rose]] and [[Thorn]] remain stranded inside [[Death House]].
