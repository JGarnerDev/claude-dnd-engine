Region: [[Barovia]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## What Is Known

Named once, by [[Davian Martikov]], in Session 28: the evil druids that [[Baba Lysaga]] convinced to attack the [[Wizard of Wines]] came from Yester Hill, and it is where they *"carried it to their hideout"* — the third and final seed, taken after the Martikovs' counter-attack failed.

Davian marked its location on a map for the party alongside Lysaga's own. Nothing else about the place is established: not its size, its approach, what stands on it, or how many druids remain there after the four killed in the winery across Sessions 26 and 27.

## Why It Matters

One of the three [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines|seeds]] is here, and recovering all three is the only thing that will let the winery produce wine again. It is one of the two destinations Davian gave the party — the other being [[Baba Lysaga]] herself, who holds a second seed. The first seed, stolen ten years ago on [[Urwin]]'s watch, is not accounted for by either.
