Global Location: [[Barovia Village]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

The graveyard of [[Barovia Village]]. A likely resting place for [[Kolyan Indirovich]], whose burial [[Ireena Kolyana]] has requested.

## Ghost Procession (Session 11)

Near session's end, a ghostly procession emerged from the graveyard and passed through the village:

- Eerie green spectral figures
- Doughty women with greatswords
- Woodwise men with slender bows
- Dwarves with glittering axes
- Mages with pointed hats

The nature and origin of these spirits is unknown to the party. After witnessing the procession, the party (mildly drunk) decided to investigate the graveyard.

## Status

Party was en route to investigate at session end. Unexplored.
