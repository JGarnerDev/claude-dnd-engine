Location: [[Calenmere Basin]], north shore of [[Calenmere]] | Nation: Blue (unnamed)

The biggest city in the basin, where the Bayward River meets salt water and the bay runs green with river silt. Largest harbor, largest fleet, most money. Everything the plains grow and the mountains dig comes through here on its way somewhere else, and Tassad takes its share of all of it.

## Arriving by water

The first thing anyone sees, a day out on the bay, is the towers of [[The Templar Triumvirate]]: marble, onyx and malachite, each half a kilometre across and nearly a kilometre tall, leaning together under a suspended saucer some two and a half kilometres wide. The city sprawls in their shadow. Closer in, the water turns green, and the docks smell of silt, tar, and [[Coffee]] roasting in the harbor quarter.

## Coin is the faith

Tassad is devout to [[Waukeen]] and cynical about everything else. Commerce *is* its religion, and the coin-goddess's temple is the real civic church. Foreign shrines are tolerated as quarters good for trade, and taxed. Fervor is distrusted, and any priesthood that could stir the dockside underclass meets quiet hostility.

## The towers and the toll

The Triumvirate is one of the basin's three wizard guilds, and the one that underwrites the strait toll: toll silver buys its standing contract with the strait nation (Cyan), and sinking the gatekeeper means guild punishment. So every ship Tassad sends to sea pays a toll its own resident guild protects. The merchant houses resent the towers they live under, and they can do nothing about it.

The Templar registry of recognised Speakers of the Word is enforced inside the walls and across Blue. Beyond Blue, the other two guilds refuse it, and that refusal is how the rivals fight. An unregistered caster in Tassad is a problem the Templars will find.

## Hunger

Tassad cannot feed itself. [[Grain]] comes down the Bayward from the plains (Purple), and a blockade would starve the city within a season. The hunger drives Blue's politics: it leans on the plains as buyer, patron, or something harder.

## The docks

[[Fargrsh]], "the Havenot," grows only on well-walked ground, and no ground in the region is walked more than Tassad's. A dockside underclass uses it to death, while the merchant floors use it as a party drug. [[The Deorful Domas]] hide easily in a port this big and divided.

## DM-only

- **[[House Bleth]]** keeps a trading office on the merchant floors, run by a factor who has never met a Bleth heir. To [[Halsby]] it is one more foreign house among dozens; the name means nothing to him yet.
- **[[The Correctors]]** have a faint trace here and nothing more. A funeral fashion is spreading among the bereaved: grave-markers carved with a birth date and no death date. No one in the city thinks it means anything.
- **[[Halsby]]** was on his way here when the woods stopped him (Session 29). [[The Nightveil]] sent him to find out what, or who, is behind a rumor the order dislikes. Still open: what the rumor is.
