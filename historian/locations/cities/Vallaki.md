Global Location: [[Barovia]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Known Information

A walled town located west of [[Barovia Village]]. Journey time: approximately 2–3 days on foot.

[[Ismark Kolyanovich]] believes [[Ireena Kolyana]] will be safer in Vallaki than in [[Barovia Village]]. The walled nature of the settlement implies better fortification against Strahd's creatures.

## Party Knowledge

Party has not visited Vallaki as of Session 11. Known only through [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]'s description. It is the destination for the escort mission.

[[Gertrude]] (missing daughter of [[Mad Mary]]) went west — she may have been headed toward Vallaki.

[[Madame Eva]] lives west of Barovia Village, likely between the village and Vallaki on the road.
