## Overview

**Emberhollow**, the small hamlet where [[Dip]] was born and raised — and shunned, for being an orc. Their sharpest childhood memory is its end.

## Destruction

Strangers arrived friendly and earned the town's trust, then turned — [[The Hamlet Raiders]], sent to slaughter everyone. Rather than storming in, they attempted a stealth attack after gaining the town's trust. It partly succeeded, but they couldn't finish the tavern keeper, **Old Brannoc Hale**, who set his own roost ablaze to trap them inside. The fire caught most of the raiders, burning them alive, and spread to the whole hamlet; many townsfolk perished too, among them Dip's orc parents, cut down early in the attack. A few raiders fled. [[Dip]] escaped alongside several others, but their twin sister, Vessa ([[Dip's Twin Sister]]), was lost in the blaze.

## The Survivors

Brannoc's sacrifice brought the raiders' ideology to a standstill and gave birth to a new movement among the survivors, one of survival against any odds. They scattered afterward; Dip wants to reunite them. (No entity file for Brannoc or the movement until they come up in play.)

> **DM-only:** Emberhollow is in Aethryn, but Dip has no memory of their birth world and doesn't know this is it.
