## Overview

The small hamlet where [[Dip]] was born and raised — and shunned, for being an orc. Their sharpest childhood memory is its end.

## Destruction

Strangers arrived friendly and earned the town's trust, then turned — [[The Hamlet Raiders]], sent to slaughter everyone. Rather than storming in, they attempted a stealth attack after gaining the town's trust. It partly succeeded, but they couldn't finish the tavern keeper, who set his own roost ablaze to trap them inside. The trap failed and the fire spread to the whole hamlet; many perished. [[Dip]] escaped alongside several others, but his twin sister ([[Dip's Twin Sister]]) was lost in the blaze.
