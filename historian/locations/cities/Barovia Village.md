## Overview

Barovia Village is the principal settlement of the valley. It sits under a permanent pall — shuttered windows, muddy streets, residents who avoid eye contact and speak in hushed voices. The village has always answered to Strahd, but the recent death of the Burgomaster has left it without stable local leadership.

The party emerged from [[Death House]] onto the village outskirts after Session 10, where [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] appeared to greet them.

> [!warning] Reliability — General
> The village's existence and general character are campaign-established. Specific NPC names, the tavern, and leadership details are canonical Curse of Strahd lore that have not been confirmed at table. Treat as probable but not authoritative until played.
