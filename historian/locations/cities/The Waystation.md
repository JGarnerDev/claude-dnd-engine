# The Waystation

An old drover's relay on the cattle road southeast of [[Ashford]], toward [[Duskwater]]. The party
reached it in [[Session 32 The Red Trail]] chasing [[Nella]] and the stolen ale of [[The Golden Lioness]].

## The ground

Seen from the hill to the south and by [[Halsby]], who circled it invisible along the tree line:

- **A long shed** with a sagging roof, about 50 ft on a side, with doors on the south and east. A
  crossbowman keeps watch from its roof.
- **A corral** of horses, kept ready to move fast.
- **A well** with a bucket on a chain, and a watch-fire in the yard.
- **The stolen cart**, still loaded with about 12 casks under a lashed tarp.
- **A banner** on a pole above the shed, too fine for the place.
- **A rocky ledge** climbing to a plateau about 20 ft up, with a trail, a river and a bridge.
  Beyond the bridge, a hooded griffon on a stake. Nella was saddling it when Halsby found her.

Halsby read it as a busy, well-run relay with plenty of coming and going. He found no traps.

## Who was there (Session 32)

None of them were named at the table.

- A hedge priest at the fire, with a cord of mismatched holy charms
- A drover in studded leather with a cart-whip, throwing daggers and a scuffed bracer, barking orders
  like a soldier
- Two cask-hands at the cart, one huge and one long-armed (asleep against the wheel)
- A clerk with a ledger, ink on his cuffs, checking the carts
- A crossbowman on the shed roof
- [[Nella]] and the griffon, beyond the bridge

## History

- **[[Session 32 The Red Trail]]:** at about 10 a.m. on Day 2, Halsby scouted the camp invisible and
  reported back through his crystal, speaking to every holder at once. Nella wore a crystal too. She
  heard him, called "Get moving, they're coming," and took off on the griffon. The camp was
  mobilising when the session ended.
