Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Producer: [[Wizard of Wines]]

## What It Is

The [[Wizard of Wines]]' best wine, named by [[Davian Martikov]] in Session 28: *"ten years ago a gem was stolen, and the best vintage we produce, Champagne du Stomp, stopped being produced. And to this day we have not been able to produce it."*

It is the one wine the winery cannot make and, by Davian's own account of how [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] work, the last one that would come back — each seed recovered restores a better wine, and all three restore the full historic range.

## Why It Matters

It dates the decline. The blights, the druids and [[Baba Lysaga]] are all recent — three weeks before Session 28 — but the winery has been diminished for **ten years**, since a theft on [[Urwin]]'s watch that Davian still cannot explain. This vintage is the evidence of that older wound, and the reason the first seed matters as much as the two the party has leads on.

It sits alongside [[The Wizard of Wines Shipping Manifests]] as a record of what the winery used to be.
