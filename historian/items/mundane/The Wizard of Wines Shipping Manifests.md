## Overview

Found on the plain desk beneath the window of the [[Wizard of Wines]]' master bedroom, in the same room as the raven-carved bed, the church tapestry, and the rocking cradle.

The manifests record the winery's wine shipments **across roughly the past century**. A cursory reading gives four standing customers:

- **BV** — the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]] in the village of Barovia
- **BW** — the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]]
- **K** — Krezk
- **The Vistani**

Flipped further back, the oldest records name a fifth:

- **S** — [[Strahd von Zarovich]]

Those shipments **stopped**. Kellan put it as *"decades, if not hundreds of years — a very long time,"* and deliberately left the exact date unfixable.

## What the party made of it

[[Luci]] read it as evidence the Martikovs are **in league with Strahd**, and pushed further when he remembered they also supply the Vistani, who are Strahd-aligned. Kellan declined to confirm the inference, noting only that they *sold wine* to Strahd at one point, that this is not the same as allegiance, and that the Wizard of Wines is a **very old winery** — an invitation to weigh the century against the conclusion.

The question is live and unresolved. It sits directly alongside the grievance [[Davian Martikov]] deflected about in [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]]: what the winery actually did to earn [[Baba Lysaga's Druids]]' hatred.

> **Reliability:** The letter codes (BV, BW, K, S) are as Kellan read them aloud; "K" was given as Krezk and the Vistani entry was spoken rather than coded. Neither Krezk nor the Vistani has an entity file yet. [[Odibli]] said he was taking the manifests and asked about copying them; whether he took the originals, copies, or only read them is not pinned down. Confirm with DM.
