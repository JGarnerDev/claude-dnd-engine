[[Halsby]]'s own spear, with his black shard set into the head as if it grew there. The fox that
speaks for [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]] made it at a bridge outside [[The Golden Lioness]],
the morning after Halsby saved [[Sable Wren]], and said the debt was repaid.

Halsby tested the fox first. It was earnest and possessed by something he could not name, and it
turned down his offer of shadow gifts in exchange for its name. Then he set both relics on the
ground. The fox carried the shard to the spear in its teeth and closed its eyes. Green light wound
round them both, the haft put out new wood that bound the shard on as its point, and the light
faded.

## What it does

- **+1 weapon.** +1 to attack and damage rolls made with it.
- **Spellcasting focus.** It replaces the bare shard as his sorcerer focus, and the +1 carries
  to his spell attack rolls and spell save DC when he casts through it (DM ruling, 2026-10-28).
- **Slows the fade.** While he carries it, each dark-moon mourning rite holds the fade off twice
  as long (12 days instead of 6, or 16 instead of 8 after a good night). Lose it and the old span
  returns.
- **Shadow reach.** In dim light or darkness, its reach is 15 feet.
