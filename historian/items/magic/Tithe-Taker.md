[[Dip]]'s whip, twice made. It began as Varrick's twelve-foot drover's whip, plaited ox-hide
over a lead-shot core, which Dip took off his body in [[Session 33 The Red Trail]]. In
[[Session 35 The Reeve's Ledger]], in his cave on the morning of Day 3, [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]]
asked for it. Dip handed it over reluctantly. One of [[Tithe]]'s wing bones became a moon-pale
single-edged blade, four of the griffon's talons sank into the lash, and the whip came back to
Dip. *"The debt has been paid."*

The griffon snatched people out of the yard; now its talons do the snatching.

## What it does

- **+1 whip.** Finesse, reach 10 ft (table ruling, Session 35), 1d6 slashing. Dip's whip mastery
  (Slow) applies.
- **Bring it down.** Twice per short rest, when Dip hits a Large or smaller creature with it, Dip
  can drag it up to 15 ft in a straight line toward them, and it is grappled until the end of
  Dip's next turn (escape DC 13). A flying creature grappled this way has a speed of 0, so it falls
  unless it can hover.
- **Drover's crack.** While holding it, Dip has advantage on Animal Handling checks to drive,
  steer or calm draft animals. The crack carries about 300 ft.
