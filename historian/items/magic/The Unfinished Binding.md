A battle-caster's brace of grey-dyed leather over a thin steel splint, laced shut with three
buckles, the guild's script tooled down its length. [[Elowen Rimetusk]] wore it on her casting arm
from the day she moved her company until the afternoon she gave it to [[Odibli]].

She found him shopping near [[Mara Tull]]'s office on Day 3 and saw at once that there were two of
him: *"One's at a desk a long way from here and he hasn't looked up for years."* She told him how she
had moved 61 soldiers out of a valley from a circle in the mud, that not all of them arrived, and
that not all of her left there either. *"Every spell you cast, somebody's trying to find you."* Then
she gave him the brace. *"These are your flock. Count them before and after every time, bring them
all back."*

## What it does

- **Unfinished crossing.** Once per short rest, when Odibli rolls initiative or is surprised, he
  can teleport up to 60 ft to an unoccupied space he can see. No action; works while surprised;
  happens before the first turn of combat.
