[[Iggy]]'s sword, given by [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]] in his cave on the morning of Day 3,
in the same sitting where the griffon became gold and [[Dip]]'s whip came back as
[[Tithe-Taker]].

A long, single-edged blade with a slight curve, and a grip long enough for a second hand that it
never needs. It is balanced like a much lighter sword.

## What it does

- **+1 weapon.** Rapier statistics (1d8, finesse, one-handed), slashing instead of piercing.
- **The call.** Once per short rest, as a bonus action, Iggy raises the blade and calls out one
  creature within 30 ft that can hear him. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against his spell save
  DC. On a failure, until the start of his next turn it has disadvantage on attack rolls against
  anyone but him, and if it attacks someone else anyway he can use his reaction to make one melee
  weapon attack against it.

> **Reliability:** the gift was never named on the recording; the DM confirmed Asher gave it in
> the Day 3 gifts scene (DM, 2026-11-25). The exact moment and words are not recorded. Confirm
> with DM.
