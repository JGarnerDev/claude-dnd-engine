Item type: staff | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## Overview

The staff was the engine of the entire winery infestation. [[Dustin]] flagged it before the party ever saw it — the druid in the cellar *"seems like they have a staff and somehow are controlling the blights."*

Its bearer was the third druid the party met in [[Session 27 The Vampire Staff]]: a man with wild hair, rotted teeth, and skin painted red with blood, who bolted rather than fight and was run down on the barrel ramp. Before the fight [[Odibli]] caught what he was shouting in Druidic:

> "Nature bows to my every whim, for I have the vampire staff."

The name the party uses for it comes from that line, and from nothing else. Whether "vampire staff" is its name, a boast, or a claim about [[Strahd von Zarovich]] is unresolved.

## What it did

Reading it after the druid's death, Odibli established two things:

- **Destroying it would kill every blight in the winery** — at the cost of the staff
- **Blight Bane** — while attuned, blights and evil plant creatures do not regard the bearer as hostile. This protects the bearer *only*; the rest of the party would still be attacked

He also read a risk of **short-term madness** if the bearer attacked with it. The party agreed to let him carry it a while and clear the building under its protection, but the plan was overtaken by the cellar fight.

> **Reliability:** The party's reading of the staff's properties is Odibli's in-fiction Arcana, relayed to the table in his own words — rarity, attunement time, and the exact wording of Blight Bane were never stated as rules text. Odibli never completed attunement (the party noted attunement needs a rest, and argued about whether the earlier short rest counted). Confirm the item's actual statblock and whether attunement ever took effect with DM.

## Destruction

In the cellar, with a fourth druid and a group of needle blights engaged, Odibli oiled the staff, threw it clear of the party, and lit it with a firebolt. Fire alone proved too slow — the staff blackened and burned but would not break — so he finished it with **Magic Missile**.

> The staff broke, still burning. A horrendous scream came out of it, and every blight in the cellar withered and died.

Kellan noted aloud that the scream **carried through the cellar** and wondered whether it reached beyond the thick stone walls — deliberately left open. Whether blights elsewhere in or around the winery died with it is unknown.

## Its Reach (Session 28)

Session 27 closed on an open question: when the staff broke, its scream killed every blight in the cellar and reverberated through the room, and Kellan wondered aloud whether the sound carried past the thick stone walls.

It did not. The party walked out of the cleared building and were ambushed on the grounds by the blights that had been waiting outside, entirely unharmed — see [[The Blight Ambush at the Winery]]. [[Odibli]] had called it correctly in character before the fight began: the staff screams and kills the blights, but they have to *hear* it to die.

The staff's reach was the building. Whatever blights [[Baba Lysaga's Druids]] fielded beyond it, including any at [[Yester Hill]], are still standing.
