## What They Are

Three magic gems, which [[Davian Martikov]] also calls **seeds**, made by [[The Vintner Mage]] who founded the [[Wizard of Wines]]. Their function is agricultural and singular: they let the vineyard grow healthy grapes through [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s curse, on land that should by every other measure be poisoned and sterile.

Davian's account of how they work is tiered rather than binary — *"each gem will let us produce a better wine until we can finally produce all three wines that we've historically produced."* With all three gone, the winery is finished as a producer.

## Where They Went

- **The first** was stolen **ten years ago**, on a night [[Urwin]] was supposed to be standing guard. Davian does not know what happened and said it no longer matters. [[Champagne du Stomp]], the winery's best vintage, has not been made since. **Its whereabouts are unknown.**
- **The second** was taken by **[[Baba Lysaga]]** herself, three weeks before Session 28, when she attacked the winery in force and found it. She kept it.
- **The third** was taken by the druids Lysaga recruited, who **carried it to their hideout at [[Yester Hill]]**. The Martikovs counter-attacked and failed, and the druids came back with the blight horde the party has now destroyed across Sessions 26–28.

## Why They Matter

They are the reason the [[Wizard of Wines]] exists as anything other than dead ground, the reason [[Vallaki]]'s wine supply stopped, and the object of the quest the party accepted in Session 28. Davian marked Lysaga's location and the druids' on a map for them; the first seed has no lead at all.

They also establish that [[The Vintner Mage]] was working at a scale nothing in present Barovia can reproduce — the same hand made [[The Wanderer's Boon]], which [[Odibli]] read as lost magic more powerful than anything he had seen.

> **Reliability:** Everything here comes from a single lore monologue by [[Davian Martikov]] in Session 28, recovered from a machine transcript. The gems' appearance, size, and mechanical properties beyond the vineyard effect were never described. Whether "gem" and "seed" are two words for one object or a distinction Davian was drawing is not clear from the source. The tiered restoration effect is his claim, not tested. The name of this entity is an engine construction — the table only ever said "the gems" or "the seeds." Confirm with DM.
