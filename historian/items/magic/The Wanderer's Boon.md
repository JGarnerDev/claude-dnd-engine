Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Holder: [[Odibli]]

## What It Is

The second thing [[The Vintner Mage]] made that has survived — the first being [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]]. [[Davian Martikov]] produced it the moment the party accepted the quest to recover the seeds, prefacing it with *"we've never shared this with anyone outside before."*

The family's knowledge of it is thin and entirely secondhand. They do not know what magic is in it. What they have is the founder's instruction: that one day travelers would come to protect the vineyard, and that when they did, this should be given to them. All Davian could say about its function was that it *"will bring you somewhere where you can gather strength, and then return strengthened and ready to protect and save the vineyard."*

[[Odibli]] rolled Arcana 19 on it and read considerably more: a portal, and an extraordinarily powerful one — *"a lost magic that is more powerful than what you're used to seeing"*, unmistakably the same hand that made the seeds. He could tell from holding it that accepting the gift was the whole of the activation. He could not tell where it led.

## What It Did

[[Odibli]] had the entire party hold hands before he grasped it — his own improvisation, not an instruction. Light rose around them like luminous mist, climbed to the edge of blinding, and faded, and the party was no longer at the winery, in a place none of them had ever seen. See [[The Party Steps Through the Portal]].

## Open Questions

- **Is it spent?** Nothing establishes whether this was a single use, and the object's post-activation state was never described.
- **How do they get back?** The founder's promise includes a return, but nothing says whether the return is automatic, triggered by the bearer, or gated on the strength actually being gained.
- **Was the hand-holding load-bearing?** It worked, so nobody tested the alternative.
- **Did the mage know where it goes?** He built it, described its purpose to the family in terms of outcome rather than destination, and the destination turned out to be entirely outside Barovia.

> **Reliability:** The name **The Wanderer's Boon** is an engine construction. At the table the object was only ever called "the relic", "the gift", "the gem" and "the stone" — Kellan used more than one of those words for it, so even its physical form is not settled. Everything in `effect` past the observed transit is inference from Davian's secondhand account and Odibli's single Arcana roll. Rename it freely. Confirm with DM.
