Plain grey hand wraps, cool to the touch, that never take a stain. [[Luci]] came out of her vision
in the ford at [[Ashford]] wearing them.

On the morning of Day 3 she drank [[Aashal-Groon Mellenegon Borak]]'s draught, milky spring water
with a crumbled rib in it. In the vision, Ashford tore open into gold walls, it went dark at noon
with both suns up, and the stars made [[Sabathiel]]'s face. Sabathiel told her she had run so far
she had forgotten what she was, that the serpent had scattered her into the dark, and that she had
called for her for 113 years. *"Stay with them, though it may pain you."* Then grey cloth was on her
hands: *"Nothing will stain them. When you strike, I will strike with you."*

[[Old Tobin Reed]], mending nets on the bank, watched her stand in the ford for a quarter of an
hour before she sent him off.

## What it does

- **+1 unarmed strikes** while she wears them.
- **Stillwater.** If she has not moved this turn, her unarmed strikes deal an extra 1d4 radiant
  damage.

> **Reliability:** the net-mender was heard as "Old Tovin Reed"; taken to be Old Tobin Reed.
> Sabathiel's name for Luci was heard as "Lucitania" (?). Confirm with DM.
