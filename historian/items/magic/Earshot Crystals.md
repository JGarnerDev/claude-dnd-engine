Taken from the raiders [[Nella]]'s crew left dead at [[The Golden Lioness]]. Each is a rough
crystal crudely wrapped in metal on a leather cord, and all of them are identical.

[[Halsby]] read them with [[Luci]]'s guidance: they let holders speak across distance without
speaking aloud. He pulled them off the bodies, handed one to Luci, kept one, and dumped the rest on
the table: *"Grab one."* [[Iggy]], [[Odibli]] and [[Dip]] each have one.
