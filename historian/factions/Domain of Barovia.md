Domain: [[Barovia]] | Ruler: [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]

## Overview

Barovia is not merely a nation — it is a domain of dread, sealed by the mists and ruled by [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] since long before living memory. No army has marched against him successfully. No one has escaped the mists. The political structure exists only because Strahd permits it.

## Structure

Below Strahd: village Boyars (minor hereditary nobility), the Burgomaster of [[Barovia]] village, and the Burgomaster of Vallaki. Each governs their territory but ultimately answers to the will of the castle.

## Relationship to the Party

[[Strahd Von Zarovich]] has expressed direct interest in the party. The nature of that interest is unclear. He has saved party members from death (Session 10) and spoken to them directly.

> **Reliability:** Strahd's rule and the domain's existence are campaign-established facts. Structural details (Boyars, Burgomasters) are canonical Curse of Strahd lore — not yet confirmed at table.
