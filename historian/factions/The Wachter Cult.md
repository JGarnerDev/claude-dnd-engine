## The circle

Behind a secret door in the cellar of [[Wachter House]] — a door that let through neither air nor light — the party found a candlelit chamber with a **ten-foot ceiling** and a **large black pentagram inscribed on the stone floor**. A **wooden chair rested at each of the five points**; four were occupied:

- a young man with the face of an angel
- a balding hulk of a man
- a squat, middle-aged woman
- a taller, younger woman with an unsettling glare

All wore **black robes with hoods**. **The fifth chair was empty** — [[Lady Fiona Wachter]]'s, who arrived shortly after.

## The book club

Caught mid-meeting, they interrogated [[Dip]], who claimed the lady's explicit permission; [[Iggy]] sold the lie. The cultists relaxed and said they were here for a **book club** — then mentioned the **devil worship** they practice. The imp [[Majesto]] served the circle.

Fiona arrived, read the party's presence as agreement, and declared that **[[Izek Strazni]] must die** and "these heroes" would make it happen. When Iggy excused himself and walked out, she called for him to be seized.

The party won the fight. The circle died with its leader.

> **Reliability:** None of the four hooded members were named, and none are confirmed dead individually — only that "the party wins battle." Which devil or patron they served, how long the circle had operated, and whether it had members elsewhere in [[Vallaki]] are all unknown. `state: dissolved` assumes no surviving cell.
