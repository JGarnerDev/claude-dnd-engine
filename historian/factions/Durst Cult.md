## Overview

The Durst Cult grew out of Gustav and Elisabeth Durst's descent into dark worship. What began as a private obsession became a small but active cult operating in the hidden basement levels of [[Death House]]. They sacrificed victims to a dark presence — a thing in the walls that whispered promises neither Gustav nor Elisabeth could resist.

## Known Members

- [[Gustav Durst]] — founder, high priest; consumed by devotion
- [[Elisabeth Durst]] — co-founder; drove much of the cult's darker impulses
- [[Walter Durst]] — the Durst infant; circumstances of death unconfirmed, possibly sacrificed or offered
- [[The Nursemaid]] — associated with the household; exact role unconfirmed

## Dissolution

The party cleared [[Death House]] across Sessions 1–10. The cult's physical space was destroyed, its remaining manifestations defeated. The dark presence in the walls may not be fully gone.

> **Reliability:** Cult existence and members are confirmed from Death House lore and session notes. Walter's exact fate and the Nursemaid's role are unconfirmed — see individual files.
