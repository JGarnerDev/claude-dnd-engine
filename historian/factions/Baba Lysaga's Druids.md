## Overview

The druids who took the [[Wizard of Wines]]. [[Davian Martikov]] named their leader to the party in [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]] as [[Baba Lysaga]] — *"Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night"* — the sorceress who was midwife to [[Strahd von Zarovich]]'s mother and steered his life afterward.

Their campaign against the winery had two parts: a **siege** of blights outside that stopped the wine reaching [[Vallaki]], and inside, the **poisoning of the stock itself**. The second is what made it more than a blockade — the winery druid of [[Session 26 The Poisoned Casks]] was pouring a flask of thick syrup directly into the vats, and told the party only that they would find out what it did, and that it *"works like a charm every time."*

## Known members

All four encountered inside the winery are dead. None was ever named aloud.

- **[[The Winery Druid]]** — the fermentation-room druid in animal skins and a goat-horned headdress, poisoning the vats from the balcony. Killed by [[Dip]] in [[Session 26 The Poisoned Casks]]
- **The label-room druid** — a woman caked in mud, moss over her face, twigs in her hair, rifling [[Davian Martikov]]'s cabinet with two vine blights behind her. Killed by [[Dip]] in [[Session 27 The Vampire Staff]]
- **The staff-bearer** — a man with wild hair, rotted teeth, and skin painted red with blood, carrying [[The Vampire Staff]]. Fled rather than fight, was run down on the barrel ramp, and killed by [[Dip]]
- **The cellar druid** — waited behind the eastern wine rack with roughly half a dozen humanoid figures, one of them wearing a full rack of antlers. Opened with Thunder Wave and dropped [[Odibli]]. Killed by [[Luci]]

> **Reliability:** None of the four is named in any session. They are described here by where they were found. The label-room and cellar druids were both read as female at the table but never explicitly stated as such. The antlered figure among the cellar group is described once and never separately fought or identified — whether it was a distinct creature or flavour on the blight group is unresolved. Confirm with DM.

## Tactics

Consistent across all four encounters: **plant magic and blights as the front line, the druid behind it.**

- **Blights** — needle blights outside as the siege line, twig blights pouring from a cracked cask, vine blights as bodyguards, needle blights again in the cellar. All of them were sustained by [[The Vampire Staff]] and died the moment it broke
- **Entangle / Entangling Plants** — used in both Session 26 and 27 to lock the party in place
- **Thunder Wave** — used twice in Session 27, both times to devastating effect on a bunched-up party

## Current state

Four dead, the staff destroyed, and the winery's interior cleared. Whether that ends them is open: the party has never seen [[Baba Lysaga]], the winery's **exterior is untouched**, and the grievance [[Davian Martikov]] deflected about is still unexplained.
