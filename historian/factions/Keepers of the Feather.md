## What is known

After the vampire ambush, **[[Rictavio]]** — having just given his real name as **Rudolph van Richten** — told the party to seek out the **Keepers of the Feather** back in [[Vallaki]]. They are, in his account, also set against **[[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, and could be **good allies**.

That is the whole of it. The party has a name and a town, and nothing else.

> **Reliability:** Entirely secondhand from Van Richten, given in the minutes after he saved the party's lives. Membership, leadership, base of operations, and how one contacts them are all unknown. The name suggests a connection worth watching for — birds, feathers, ravens — but the party has drawn no such link in play. Confirm with DM.

## The Martikov Connection (Session 28)

The party still has no confirmed roster, base, or contact for the Keepers. What Session 28 supplies is a set of facts that fit nothing else:

- The **Martikovs are a family of wereravens** — see [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]], disclosed by [[Davian Martikov]] at the [[Wizard of Wines]].
- The murder that watched and then defended the winery was **dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]]**, which [[Urwin]] — Davian's son — runs.
- One of those ravens, [[Dustin]], deflected the question of how Davian commands them rather than answering it.
- [[Baba Lysaga]] attacked the family specifically because she *"found out that we oppose Strahd and that we are working against him."*

An anti-Strahd network in Vallaki, built on ravens, run out of an inn. It has never been said aloud at the table that the Martikovs *are* the Keepers, and it should not be recorded as canon until it is — but the party has been lodging with them since Session 16.
