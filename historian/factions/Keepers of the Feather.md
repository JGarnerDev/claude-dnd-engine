## What is known

After the vampire ambush, **[[Rictavio]]** — having just given his real name as **Rudolph van Richten** — told the party to seek out the **Keepers of the Feather** back in [[Vallaki]]. They are, in his account, also set against **[[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, and could be **good allies**.

That is the whole of it. The party has a name and a town, and nothing else.

> **Reliability:** Entirely secondhand from Van Richten, given in the minutes after he saved the party's lives. Membership, leadership, base of operations, and how one contacts them are all unknown. The name suggests a connection worth watching for — birds, feathers, ravens — but the party has drawn no such link in play. Confirm with DM.
