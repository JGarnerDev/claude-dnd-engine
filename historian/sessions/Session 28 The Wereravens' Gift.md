## What Happened

### The blights outside

The session opened on the party still spent from Session 27 — no long rest since Session 24, [[Odibli]] with zero spell slots and asking for sleep, [[Dip]]'s rage already expended. Kellan confirmed the winery's interior held nothing further to find. [[Iggy]] took point out of the building, peering ahead for blights and finding the grounds calm; the rest followed him deliberately, Miguel saying so out loud at the table — *"Jeff is a leader, Jeff pops off."*

The calm was a setup. Skittering broke out from every direction at once and blights came at the party from the treeline and around the corner of the building. The staff's scream had killed everything inside the winery and nothing outside it — [[The Vampire Staff]]'s reach, left as an open question at the end of Session 27, is now settled as the building's walls.

[[Odibli]] opened with Shatter across roughly seven blights for 9–20 damage each and immediately took a needle for 9 piercing that put him at 0 HP. [[Dip]] switched off rage, bellowed across the grounds for [[Davian Martikov]] — *"we have vanquished all the druids, but we need help with these blights"* — and cleaved two blights down. Swarms of ravens began arriving off the winery's upper floor in answer, killing roughly as many blights as the party did across the fight.

[[Iggy]] pulled the spine out of [[Odibli]], stabilized him with Lay on Hands for exactly 1 HP, then burned a second-level Healing Word for another 10 — *"I pretty much scream: get the fuck up, we need to do stuff."* [[Luci]] tried to springboard off Iggy into a wall-run, critically failed the acrobatics check, fell onto a blight for 1 damage and took 10 back off its spike, then set up an energy storm that let them strike anything moving in or out of its radius while leaving them lightly obscured. Kellan ruled the obscurement as disadvantage on ranged attacks only, melee unaffected. A crit for 11 piercing then dropped Luci to 0, and Iggy spent his last second-level slot on Healing Word to bring them back up with 10.

[[Odibli]] cast Shatter a second time, deliberately widened to include [[Luci]]'s square with Paul's spoken consent — Luci was down and auto-failed, taking one death-save failure for it. Luci came up, cast Sanctuary on Odibli, and dashed eighty feet clear of the fight to go find Davian, calling the raven swarms *"air superiority."* Odibli finished one blight with Firebolt and [[Dip]] cleaved the last for 16. Kellan's summary: *"you guys didn't even need the staff."*

### Davian's confession

[[Davian Martikov]] came out to meet them; [[Adrian Martikov]] and [[Elvir Martikov]] had already fled back to him overwhelmed by the exterior blights and could not say what had happened inside. The party reported the druids dead, the staff destroyed, the ravens fighting on their side, and every cask of wine poisoned. Davian took the wine badly — *"that'll mean that we can't ship anyone out"* — but said they would look for a way to save the batch.

He then cast poison and disease off [[Odibli]], lifting both the tainted-cask poisoning from Session 26 and the brown-mold spores from Session 27 in one gesture. [[Dip]] asked for the same on the grounds that their own mushroom-spore infection was *"a serious health hazard"*, and got it, with a parting shot: *"shouldn't have that many boars in your basement."*

Then Davian said the party had earned the real history, and gave it:

- He is **not** the Wizard of Wines, and not a wizard — *"alas, I'm not even a wizard. Don't take me for wrong, I could cast a spell or two, but I'm no wizard."* The vineyard was founded by [[The Vintner Mage]], a mage whose name is lost, and it is very old.
- That mage made **three magic gems, also called seeds** — [[The Seeds of the Wizard of Wines]] — which let the vineyard grow healthy grapes through [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s curse, on ground that would otherwise be poisoned and barren.
- The **Martikovs** have run the winery for generations, and the ravens that saved the party are that family. **Every Martikov in the line is a wereraven** — see [[The Martikovs Are Wereravens]]. This answers the question [[Dustin]] refused in Session 27 and firms the [[Keepers of the Feather]] connection the party had only a name for.
- **Ten years ago one seed was stolen** and [[Champagne du Stomp]], the winery's best vintage, has not been produced since. His son [[Urwin]] was supposed to be on guard that night, and Davian does not know what happened.
- Lately the winery has been fending off **[[Baba Lysaga]]'s scarecrow constructs**.
- **Three weeks ago** Lysaga attacked in force, found a second seed and took it for herself, then talked the druids of [[Yester Hill]] into attacking for the third. The Martikovs' counter-attack failed and the druids returned with the blight horde. See [[The Theft of the Three Seeds]].
- Without the seeds the winery **will never produce wine again** and no inn in Barovia will carry its product. Each seed recovered restores one wine; all three restores the full historic range.
- **Baba Lysaga believes she is [[Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s mother**, and so sets herself against anyone who opposes him. She learned the Martikovs work against Strahd, and came for them.

### The quest and the relic

Davian marked both Lysaga's location and the druids' on a map and asked for help recovering the seeds, promising all the wine the party can carry and anything else in the family's power. [[Odibli]] asked whether gifted wine would be poisoned too; Davian promised it cleaned, and that restored production would put wine back into every inn in Barovia. Odibli floated using good wine as leverage in [[Vallaki]]'s power shift — *"we can convince people through the power of wine"* — and [[Iggy]] agreed the town had not had a decent party in a very long time, blaming [[Vargas Vallakovich]] for about half of it. The party accepted.

On acceptance Davian produced [[The Wanderer's Boon]] — one more relic made by the founding mage, held on his promise that travelers would one day come to defend the vineyard and should be given it. All the family knows is that it *"will bring you somewhere where you can gather strength, and then return strengthened."* [[Odibli]] rolled Arcana 19 and read it as an extraordinarily powerful portal of lost magic, unmistakably the same hand that made the seeds; accepting the gift is all that triggers it, and the destination is unknowable from the object.

Odibli had the entire party hold hands, then grasped the stone. Light rose around them like luminous mist, climbed to the edge of blinding, and faded — and they were no longer at the winery, in a place none of them had ever seen. Kellan cut there. See [[The Party Steps Through the Portal]].

## Cliffhanger

The party stepped through [[The Wanderer's Boon]] hand-in-hand and arrived somewhere unrecognizable — unrested since Session 24, out of spell slots, and carrying an open promise to recover three seeds, two from [[Baba Lysaga]] and one from the druids of [[Yester Hill]].

The portal is the season-swap device. Kellan handed off on the arrival — *"it's up to you, Jeff, in your domain. The party has been sent here to gain strength, whatever that ends up meaning"* — answering the Session 27 open thread about what his *"prepare to take over"* meant. It is a **campaign season-swap**, not a stretch of Strahd under a different DM. Barovia is paused mid-quest; the Martikovs are owed a return.
