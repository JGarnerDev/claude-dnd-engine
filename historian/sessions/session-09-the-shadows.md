## Session Notes

### Jeff Killed by Shadows
Jeff attempted to move through a room. Two shadows hit him with opportunity attacks, reducing his Strength to 0. He was killed.

### Strahd Intervenes
Froze time just before the shadows would have caught Ben. Addressed the party directly:

*"The shadows cannot exist in the light. Be wary of that which you cannot see — it is deadly."*

Then left. This was an act of intervention — Strahd saved Ben's life.

### Ben Killed Twice
1. After Strahd left, Ben ran from the shadows and was killed by necrotic damage.
2. Miguel threw a healing potion to revive Ben.
3. Ben tried to hit a shadow — missed. Shadow counterattacked and reduced Ben's Strength to 0. Ben killed again.

### Luci Kills All Shadows
Luci cleaned up the fight — dodging, reliable damage, MVP of the session.
