## Session Notes

### Morning at Parriwimple's — The Song

Party woke hungover from the long rest. **[[Iggy]]** offered a song in exchange for the hospitality. **[[Parriwimple]]** invited **[[Bildrath Cantemir]]** to listen.

Iggy performed a ballad: a raccoon enters a shop, steals bread, fights the owners, and departs heroically. Both Parriwimple and Bildrath loved it.

Iggy asked Bildrath about his high store prices. Bildrath's response went unrecorded.

### Carrying the Coffin

Party carried [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s coffin from the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]] through the streets of [[Barovia Village]] to the church. Streets empty, town grim.

### Church of the Morning Lord

The [[Church of the Morning Lord]] was battle-scarred — claw marks, fire damage, worn-down. Unlit interior, four doors, smell of mildew.

- **[[Dip]]** did a nature check; spotted a severed zombie arm nearby
- **[[Ireena Kolyana]]** identified the priest as Donavich; said zombie attacks are frequent at night
- Party heard soft prayer and an inhuman scream from below the floorboards

### Father Donavich

**[[Father Donavich]]** was pacing and unstable in the rubble of the main hall, hearing things, muttering.

From below: *"Father, I'm starving!"*

- **[[Obible]]** asked if he'd been praying for the creature below
- Donavich explained: his son **[[Doru]]** and several other Barovians tried to storm Castle Ravenloft. Doru came back a vampire spawn — bound to his vampire lord, lesser abilities, cannot create new spawn. It's been a little more than a year.
- **[[Dip]]** asked: does he want Doru back, or gone? Donavich answered with anger: *back.*
- **[[Luci]]** and **[[Odibli]]** combined minds — recalled that vampire spawn are attached to their lords; restoring one to humanity likely requires the death of the vampire lord. Donavich confirmed: the vampire is Strahd.
- **[[Dip]]** asked to speak with Doru. Donavich agreed tentatively. Dip used religious framing to get access to the trap door. Party decided communication through the trap door was ineffective and didn't proceed further.

### The Burial — The Revelation

Donavich revealed during burial proceedings: **Ireena is not a blood relative of the Kolyanovich family.** Ireena was shocked.

He spoke of "trials that await her" without elaboration.

Each party member took a turn shoveling. **[[Dip]]** used his hands.

### Donavich's Instruction

Once [[Kolyan Indirovich]] was in the ground, Donavich told the party to take Ireena away from Castle Ravenloft as fast as possible. He proposed:

- The **Abbey of Saint Markovia** in Krezk (Chapter 8)
- The **fortified town of Vallaki** (Chapter 5)

Party agreed to take her to Vallaki, per her wishes. Ismark and Ireena went home to prepare. **[[Ismark Kolyanovich]]** would also travel as a companion.

### The Raccoon

**[[Dip]]** said goodbye to **[[Parriwimple]]** — Parriwimple wished them well and invited them back for drinks.

Party tried to find the raccoon from earlier. **[[Odibli]]** cast Minor Illusion (cotton candy supply). Raccoon struck ODB for 1 claw damage. **[[Iggy]]** threw a ration; raccoon ate it. **Iggy gets raccoon companion.** ODB ate a ration in front of the raccoon, triggering a brief cold war between them.

*Named by Iggy: [[Bosco]].*

### The Dream Pastry Woman

Wooden wheels rolling down the street. An old woman with a cart, selling "dream pastries." The party each bought one.

She knocked on a door. A parent answered: *"Please don't take him, I'll find another way!"* The woman took a child anyway.

- **[[Iggy]]** tried to talk to the parents. Door shut. Iggy kicked it down. Parents were inconsolable — already eating pastries and slipping into a stupor.
- Party decided: she's evil, attack her.
- **[[Iggy]]** ran to fetch **[[Parriwimple]]**, told him there were zombies in the street to get him moving. Iggy ran back, equipped heavy crossbow, climbed a roof, activated **Radiant Soul** (Aasimar Protector — first use). Sniped her from the rooftop with the heavy crossbow.
- **[[Dip]]** raged and axed. **[[Odibli]]** Magic Missile + Diesel Cougars. **[[Luci]]** Radiant Consumption + Energy Storm Psychokinesis.
- **The hag revealed herself and shifted to another plane, cackling.** She took the child with her.
- **[[The Dream Pastry Woman]]** escaped. Child taken.

Iggy reconciled with Parriwimple, apologized for the zombie lie. Parriwimple forgave — fist bump. Parriwimple is now on alert for hags or suspicious pastry-sellers.
