Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: recover the wine, break the siege

## Recap

### Davian Martikov

The party approached the [[Wizard of Wines]]. **Nine other folk** emerged from the trees. **[[Davian Martikov]]** introduced himself as the proprietor. The party announced they were there at the behest of the town of [[Vallaki]].

Davian said that **druids** had been preventing them from delivering to the town.

[[Dip]] asked what the winery had **done** to the druids. Davian said they were just **jealous**, but that he really did not know. [[Iggy]] read him with Insight and sensed only that **there is more to the story.**

### Baba Lysaga

Davian named the leader of the druid forces: **[[Baba Lysaga]]**, a dark sorceress — **"Ravenbane," "our mother," "servant of the night."** She has a close connection to [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]: she was **Strahd's mother's midwife**, brought him into this world, and **directed his life**.

Iggy said this sounded dangerous. Davian reassured them that **Baba Lysaga is not present herself** — only the druids.

> **Reliability:** Every detail about Baba Lysaga here is Davian's account, not the party's observation. "Our mother" in particular is ambiguous in the notes — whether it is a title the druids use, a Barovian epithet, or something more literal is unresolved. Confirm with DM.

### The plan

[[Luci]] did some **healing**, and several of the winery folk were willing to help against the druids.

Scouting the approach, the party found **three main doors and a loading area**.

[[Odibli]] cast a **familiar bird** to scout ahead. It sensed animals — and was then **taken out by something strange**.

Luci cast **Augury** about approaching from the **loading dock**, and received **"wheel"** in response.

> **Reliability:** "Wheel" is not a standard Augury result (weal / woe / weal and woe / nothing). Whether this was "weal," a mixed reading, or a deliberate table variant is unresolved. Confirm with DM.

### The blights

As the party closed on the winery, **five [[Needle Blight|needle blights]]** appeared and attacked.
