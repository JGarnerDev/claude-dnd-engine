Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: survive the festival, find who loosed the tiger

## Recap

### The festival

After the ordeal with [[Viktor Vallakovich]] and the Burgomaster, the party returned to the inn in [[Vallaki]]. It was full of people discussing the **festival**, held that same day. The townspeople were openly eye-rolling about it; [[Vargas Vallakovich]] was thrilled.

Vargas tried to light a **wicker sphere** on fire, and failed. A citizen laughed, and was arrested on the spot.

[[Iggy]] intervened, claiming the man had been laughing at one of *his* jokes and not at the Burgomaster. Vargas told Iggy to "share with the town." Iggy told the joke in front of the crowd, it landed, and the man was released. The festival resumed — *"All will be well."*

Vargas still could not light the wicker ball. He forced the crowd into a chant of **"all will be well,"** got flustered, and declared the festival a huge success regardless.

[[Luci]] and [[Odibli]] then used **oil and a cantrip** to ignite the sphere. The Burgomaster and the townspeople were delighted.

> **Reliability:** The festival's formal name is taken from the campaign state's standing "Blazing Sun" thread; the notes call it only "the festival." Confirm with DM.

### The tiger

[[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]] disappeared from the crowd.

Townspeople began shouting **"Tiger! Tiger!"** as [[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger]] broke loose, and fleeing from one direction. Odibli cast **Speak with Animals** in preparation. [[Izek Strazni]] and several guards ran toward the noise, and the party followed.

They found tracks and a **busted cage-cart**. Examining it, they determined the animal had been **well fed and cared for** — not a wild beast. They followed the tracks.

Odibli asked [[Sergei Rubelgerd]] to find Karl and Nikolai; Sergei was distracted by a food smell and was little help. He did eventually pick up the tiger's scent.

They found the tiger — and [[Rictavio]], soothing it with pieces of meat.

Odibli spoke with the beast, which seemed **happy that it was scaring people**. The party lured it back toward the cage-cart with treats, and Odibli summoned a **frog familiar** to draw it the last of the way in. It worked. Rictavio thanked them and said they needed to talk **outside the village, to the northeast**.

### The misdirection

Izek intercepted the party with his guards, hunting the tiger and the cart. Luci partially deceived them about the direction, **splitting half of Izek's team away** from Rictavio's position.

### The house

With the Rictavio meeting set for evening, the party went to the house where they had seen Karl and Nikolai flee. Luci knocked and argued their business with a servant, and was ultimately refused.

Iggy peered through a window of [[Wachter House]]: the interior was **elegant**, and an **elegant lady** inside — [[Lady Fiona Wachter]] — was looking straight back at him. The party began talking through the glass about her sons and their suspicion that the two had loosed the tiger. She reacted **poorly to the news** — and invited them in.

> **Reliability:** The notes name the two runaways "Nikolai and Karl," the same names as the dissenting drunks met at the [[Blue Water Inn]]. This record treats them as the same pair, now revealed as the lady's sons. Confirm with DM that they are one and the same and not a name collision.
