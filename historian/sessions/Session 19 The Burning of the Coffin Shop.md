Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: destroy the spawn, recover the bones, survive the fallout

## Recap

Picking up from the blown ambush at [[Henrik]]'s coffin shop, the party regrouped and went back in.

### Hired guns

They hired the wolf-hunters [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] and [[Yevgeni Krushkin]] for the day — **10 gp**.

> **Reliability:** Earlier notes put the hunters' rate at 5 gp/day; this session logs 10 gp. Confirm with DM.

### The bones, and the fire

Taking the **correct upstairs door** this time, the party's three most dexterous members snuck in to search for the bones. They found them — and [[Iggy]] confirmed by an **Arcana** check that these were the saint's bones they had been hunting.

One of the wolf-hunters made **too much noise**. The party **torched the shop** and ran. As it burned, they noticed **more bodies** inside the house.

### Captured

The villagers were furious, but the hunters worked the crowd, arguing the fire wasn't the party's fault. Then **[[Izek Strazni]]** arrived. The party was **bound with no chance to escape** and paraded through town toward the dungeon and the Burgomaster. The coffin shop was left a **smoking heap of rubble**.

### The priest's intervention

**[[Lucien Petrovic]]** ("Father Lucien") saved them, declaring they were free to go — that they had **saved the town** by finding the bones. Izek was angry but would not kill them.

Lucien took the bones for himself. But **perception checks** read something more: Lucien has his own **curiosity**, an ulterior motive behind his haste to return the bones to the church immediately. A thread to pull later.

> **Reliability:** Lucien's ulterior motive is inferred from the party's read, not stated. What he actually wants with the bones is unknown. Confirm with DM.

### At the Burgomaster's

A **nurse** brought the party into the office of Burgomaster **[[Vargas Vallakovich]]**. Here they learned that **[[Lake Zarovich]]** is where [[Izek Strazni]] lost his arm — and noticed that **Izek and [[Ireena Kolyana]] share the same face**.

### The sibling reveal

The party went with Izek to the priest. There the truth surfaced: **Izek and Ireena are siblings.** Izek was **born without a soul** — which is why he had forgotten she was his sister. He does not know what magic made his deformed arm, but he is at peace with it.

> **Reliability:** This resolves, rather than contradicts, the Session 13 revelation that Ireena is not a blood Kolyanovich — she was born a Strazni and adopted. Confirm the exact framing with DM.

### Level up

The party **leveled up** (to level 4).
