Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: shelter [[Ireena Kolyana]] at the [[Church of St. Andril]]; recover the stolen bones

## Recap

The party came to the [[Church of St. Andril]] in [[Vallaki]] — a church dedicated to [[Lathander]], named for the saint St. Andril — to ask the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]] for shelter from [[Strahd]] for [[Ireena Kolyana]]. This was folded together with the party's ongoing errand into the missing wine shipment (see [[Find the Wine Wizard]]) and a detour tied to [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] and Ireena.

Lucien revealed a problem of his own: the church's sacred **saint's bones**, which grant the building a **boon of protection**, had been **stolen**.

### The altar-boy inquisition

Suspicion first fell on the altar boy [[Yeska]], and the party questioned him hard. The boy was scared, and Lucien insisted firmly on his innocence.

The investigation — driven largely by [[Iggy]] and [[Luci]] working the church's people for information — turned to the groundskeeper and gravedigger [[Milivoj]]. Nervous under questioning, Milivoj confessed: he had told and sold the bones to [[Henrik]] the coffinmaker for a couple of gold pieces, not knowing their importance. Henrik's shop sits near the town square.

> **Reliability:** The sale price is loosely recorded (the notes cite both "10 gp" and "a couple of pieces of gold"). Confirm with DM.

### Can the bones fight the hag?

[[Odibli]] pressed on whether the saint's bones could be jury-rigged into something usable against the [[Old Bonegrinder|hag coven]]. The priest knew of the coven but said the bones would be useless against a hag; Odibli's own attempt to improvise a use came up with a "no-can-do." The bones protect the church — nothing more.

### Deceiving Henrik

The party deceived their way into Henrik's shop, and the cornered coffinmaker spilled everything:

- [[Strahd]], under the alias **"Vasili van Holtz,"** had visited him.
- **Six vampire spawn** are hiding upstairs.
- The bones specifically grant the boon of protection to the [[Church of St. Andril]].

> **Reliability:** "Vasili van Holtz" is Henrik's report of Strahd's alias (spelling per the notes). The six-spawn count is Henrik's word — unverified by sight. Confirm with DM.

### Old wives' tales

Pressed for aid, Lucien knew only old wives' tales. One concerned the windmill hag on the hill outside town:

> Old mother comes when the sun goes down, / With pies and sweets from the hill outside town. / One for a copper, two for a dream, / Three for the children who don't get to scream. / Don't eat the pastry, don't take her hand, / Don't follow the flour road up through the land. / The windmill grinds slow, the windmill grinds fine, / What goes in as children comes out tasting divine.

This points back at [[Old Bonegrinder]] / [[The Dream Pastry Woman]]. The party also confirmed the [[Wizard of Wines]] winery lies **west, through the woods**. In-world, it is **Tuesday**; the mandatory festival falls on **Friday**.

> **Reliability:** [[Rictavio]] was named in play as "the only gallant hero in the village" — a party impression, not established fact. Confirm with DM.

### Table lore

A running table bit established **Kranken Hammershaft** as a "canon dwarf bard," alongside a joke that Metallica's "Nothing Else Matters" exists in-universe. Held for DM decision on whether to file Kranken as an entity.
