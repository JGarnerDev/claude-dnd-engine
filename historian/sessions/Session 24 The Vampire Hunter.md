Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: meet Rictavio, then find the missing wine

## Recap

### The nobleman at the gate

After reporting the Fiona Wachter fight to the Burgomaster, the party set out to find [[Rictavio]].

Outside the city doors a **noble-appearing man** approached, congratulating them on defeating Fiona and her planned uprising — and remarking how sad it was that the Burgomaster was not rewarding them much. He gave his name as **Vasili van Holtz**.

### The ambush

At the meeting place, a group of **vampires ambushed the party and downed all four of them.**

Rictavio and his **[[Rictavio's Saber-Toothed Tiger|saber-toothed tiger]]** saved them, and **stabilized** them afterward. He told them that **Vasili van Holtz was [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**.

### The vampire hunter

Rictavio then gave his real name: **Rudolph van Richten**, a famous vampire hunter. He lost his **child and his wife** to vampires long ago. He is in Vallaki to wreck Strahd's plans — specifically those involving the **Vistani vampires**.

He told the party to seek out the **[[Keepers of the Feather]]** back in [[Vallaki]]. They are also set against Strahd, and could make good allies.

The party took a **long rest**.

> **Reliability:** Canon has recorded [[Rictavio]] as a half-elf from party observation; his identity as Van Richten is his own claim, given under duress after a rescue. Whether the disguise extends to his race is unconfirmed. Confirm with DM.

### The road south

Back at the tavern, [[Danika]] gave more on the missing **wine shipment**: past **Ravenriver**, near **[[Van Richten's Tower]]**.

> **Reliability:** "Ravenriver" in the notes is read here as the [[Raven River]]. The tower's association with Van Richten's newly revealed name is not commented on in the notes. Confirm with DM.

Crossing the **[[Luna River]]**, the party was passed by a **skeletal horse and rider** — [[The Skeleton Rider]]. It went by benignly, ignoring [[Odibli]]'s attempts to get its attention, and rode **south into the forest**.

At the **[[Raven River]] crossing**, a large **dire wolf** came out — [[The Raven River Wolf]]. Odibli cast **Speak with Animals** and asked whether it was friendly. The wolf told them to **walk away** — they were not welcome. It **serves Strahd**, and had been told to prevent **a group of four adventurers** from going further **west**.

The party was heading **south** for the wine mission, not across the Raven River bridge, so they went on.

They reached the **[[Wizard of Wines]]**: a house, and a man standing by the trees, beckoning them.
