Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: hear out the noblewoman, decide who to trust

## Recap

### The parlor

The party joined the lady in the parlor of [[Wachter House]], in [[Vallaki]]. The room was **well decorated**, the finery **slightly tarnished**, the walls hung with many fine painted portraits.

She named herself **[[Lady Fiona Wachter]]** and apologized for her sons [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]], then asked why the party was curious about them. [[Luci]] spoke first, but Fiona turned to question **[[Dip]]** personally. Dip vented — about the village, the insane mayor, and her kids letting a tiger loose. She liked the part about the mayor (she agreed), **shrugged about her sons** (they had lost their father), and pushed for more about the Burgomaster, suggesting the conversation might be constructive for both parties.

Dip vented further about [[Vargas Vallakovich]]. She dug it. She mentioned that her **daughter** — [[Lady Fiona Wachter's Daughter]] — had been betrothed to **[[Viktor Vallakovich]]** — but Viktor was, and is, deeply unwell, and had **messed her daughter up psychologically**. The betrothal is off. Fiona would not speak about her daughter's current state.

### The offer

Fiona steered to their common dislike of the Burgomaster, and subtly hinted that a **coup** was desirable — and that she would be interested in the party's help, by **killing [[Izek Strazni]]**.

[[Iggy]] asked probing questions, given that she was proposing usurpation without offering any concrete material, personal, or other support. She stayed steadfastly secretive; the party stayed noncommittal.

Outside, the party agreed on a plan: **accept the quest, but don't do it.** They would lie to Fiona and speak to Izek first, to learn why she wanted him dead. They all disliked the Burgomaster — but this lady was shifty.

Fiona agreed and offered **money**, **protection from the charge of burning down the coffin maker's house**, and **access to her library**.

On the way out, [[Odibli]] used a summoned familiar to **spy on the house**, hoping to locate Fiona's daughter or anything else shifty.

### Izek

Back in town, the party found Izek still hunting the cause of the freed beast. They gave him **[[Rictavio]]'s name** — and a **false direction**, so they could still make the pre-arranged meeting. Izek was plainly determined to destroy Rictavio without much interest in investigation or trial. The party said nothing about Fiona's sons.

> **Reliability:** The notes call the escaped animal a "lion" in this session and a tiger elsewhere; this record treats it as the tiger throughout. Confirm with DM.

### The church

At the [[Church of St. Andril]], [[Ireena Kolyana]] thanked the party, and Luci opened up. She moved from the party's concern for Ireena and the town's safety to the substance: **Fiona wants Izek killed**, the party is not inclined to do it, and **Fiona wants the regency of Vallaki** from the Burgomaster. Luci advised that Izek **lay low** for now, and shared her own past — that history shows the **worship of light does not bode well**, and has led to **mass ritual suicide** before. The Burgomaster, she argued, is heading that way.

Ireena was more sympathetic to Vargas and was not convinced. They discussed Fiona's ambitions. Ireena warned that **many actors in this village may have bad intentions**, and that the party should do their research before committing to anything rash. She stressed that the Burgomaster is a **good man** and **against [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]**, despite being a little crazy — good motives, bad execution.

### The cellar

Odibli's bird finished scouting Wachter House and found **nothing remarkable**.

Dip and Odibli then went covertly into the house's **basement** while Iggy and Luci kept watch outside. The basement was occupied by **eight animated, hostile skeletons.** Battle began.
