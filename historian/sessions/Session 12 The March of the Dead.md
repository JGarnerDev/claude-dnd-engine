## Session Notes

### The Graveyard — Ghost Procession

Party arrived at [[Barovia Village Graveyard]] while mildly drunk (Session 11 cliffhanger). The procession began: eerie green light, growing ranks of ghostly adventurers — women with greatswords, woodwise men with bows, dwarves with axes, mages in pointed hats.

- **[[Luci]]** — depressed; spirits unguided by light; discussed her time in the astral plane; feels sad for spirits with nowhere to go
- **[[Iggy]]** — uncomfortable with necromancy; ghosts are marginally OK; pure necromancy not his thing; summoned a spiritual weapon (greatsword) and called to the spirits; [[Luci]] amplified with light cantrip; no effect
- **[[Dip]]** — seen these kinds of processions in dreams; never ended well

### Dip's Dream (Recounted)

Dip as a young orc child. A rainfall that turned into spirits. Spirits processed out of the forest. Two days later, bandits burned the forest. Sludge rained on everything. Their orc community was driven out to survive. Beginning of their lifelong association of spirit processions with catastrophe.

Dip recounted this to the spirits. No response.

### Luci's Energy Storm

[[Luci]] cast Energy Storm at the front of the procession — attempting to communicate or interrupt. No impediment, no acknowledgement. The spirits didn't notice.

### Parriwimple Appears

A large figure approached from the village — **[[Parriwimple]]**, alerted by Luci's spell. He was muttering and had fresh scratch marks on his body (from a raccoon someone threw into his home earlier). He told the party it was too dangerous to be outside and to follow him.

**[[Iggy]]** drunkenly confronted him: *what the fuck is going on, why follow you.*

Parriwimple explained:
> "These aren't the spirits of the people buried here. They're previous adventurers who died trying to destroy Strahd. Every night, they march from the graveyard to the castle. Every night they fail."

Party agreed to follow him back.

### Zombie Attack

Before they could move: a villager nearby began screaming. A zombie chased him, caught him, and tore his arm off. Dead. Three zombies turned on the party.

**Initiative:** Luci 21, Iggy 21, Dip 14, Red Zombie 12, Parriwimple 12, Blue Zombie 11, Green Zombie 6

- [[Luci]] threw a dagger and severed the red zombie's leg — the leg continued to advance independently
- Blue and green zombies touched by faerie fire
- **[[Luci]]** used Radiant Consumption for the first time (first use ever)
- **[[Parriwimple]]** overperformed — speared multiple zombies with exceptional force
- Party won

### Long Rest

Parriwimple invited the party to spend the night at his domicile. Party accepted. Long rest completed.
