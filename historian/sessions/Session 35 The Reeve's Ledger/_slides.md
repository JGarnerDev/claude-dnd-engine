## The ridge

Nella hung her hood on a bush and said goodbye over the crystals. Luci went after her alone:
thorns across the ground, a leap off a tree, and a storm that set it on fire. Nella got away by
the river. Luci laid the hood on the griffon.

## Asher's cave

The party carried Tithe to Asher whole. Asher gave his true name, and Sable turned out to be his
warlock. Dip told the story of his hands. Asher turned the griffon to gold.

## The ford

Luci drank Asher's draught and stood at the ford as it was 113 years ago, where Sabathiel once
crossed. She came back with the Stillwater Wraps. Old Tobin Reed watched from his nets.

## The cell

The prisoner was Shaw Proudfoot, carrying a letter north to the Deorum Ostium. He told his story.
Iggy asked Mara to let him go, and Mara called off the hanging. Shaw is a month from home.

## What the party chose

- Dip took Asher's job looking into the Four, and wouldn't take pay for it.
- Dip handed over his cart-whip; it came back as Tithe-Taker.
- Odibli believed Elowen and took what she offered.
- The party kept the five camp horses, with their consent.

## What changed

The casks are home. They came back on the raiders' own wagon at sundown, with Iggy fiddling on
the driver's bench. Silas checked every hoof and called the loan square. Sable called the casks
home and the party's share ten percent too little. Nothing is signed yet.

## The party

- Iggy: Oathcaller, from Asher.

- Luci: the Stillwater Wraps.
- Odibli: the Unfinished Binding, from Elowen, and the camp's tap key and bung mallet.
- Shaw: free.
- The party: five horses, five crystals, Bane's ledger, a tally stick, and 150 gp, 500 sp, 5 pp.
