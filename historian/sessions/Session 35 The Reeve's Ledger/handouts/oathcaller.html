Oathcaller

Weapon (long single-edged blade), uncommon · no attunement · Iggy

A long blade of pale griffon bone with a slight curve, veined grey where river stone grew into it. The grip is long enough for a second hand, though it never needs one, and it's balanced like a much lighter sword. It came out of the water cold, and it stays cool.

  • The call

    Once per short rest, as a bonus action, you raise the blade and call out one creature within 30 feet of you that can hear you. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, until the start of your next turn it has disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you. If it attacks someone other than you anyway, you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack against it.

Asher worked it from Tithe's wing bone in his cave by the river, and floated it to you across the water.
