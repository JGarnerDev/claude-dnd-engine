The Unfinished Binding

Wondrous item (arm brace), uncommon · no attunement · Odibli

A battle-caster's brace of grey-dyed leather over a thin steel splint, laced shut with three buckles, the guild's script tooled down its length. Sized for a half-orc's forearm, it covers you from wrist to past the elbow. The script ends on an order: move them.

Rarity
Uncommon
Attunement
None
Uses
1 per short rest

  • Out of the way

    When you roll initiative or are surprised, you can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. This takes no action, works while you're surprised, and happens before the first turn of combat. Once you use it, you can't again until you finish a short or long rest.

Elowen Rimetusk buckled it onto your arm herself. "Count them. Before and after. Every time."
