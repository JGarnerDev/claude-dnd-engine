Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: find out what Lady Wachter is hiding

## Recap

### The secret door

The skeletons in the cellar of [[Wachter House]], in [[Vallaki]], were killed.

In the cellar the party found **footprints**, some leading to a wall and **disappearing into it**. [[Dip]] detected a **secret door**; [[Odibli]] cast **Detect Magic** and sensed only thin arcana in the air — the door let through **neither air nor light**, but carried no traps.

Dip forced it open onto a room full of **cultists** — [[The Wachter Cult]].

> *"Flickering candles in iron holders fill this room with light and shadows. This room has a ten-foot-high ceiling and a large black pentagram inscribed on the stone floor. At each point of the pentagram rests a wooden chair. Seated in four of the five chairs are men and women in black robes with hoods: a young man who has the face of an angel; a balding hulk of a man; a squat, middle-aged woman; and a taller, younger woman with an unsettling glare. They rise to confront you."*

### The book club

The **balding hulk** interrogated Dip. Dip asserted that they had **explicit permission from the lady**, and [[Iggy]] helped sell the deception. The cultists bought it, explained that they were here for a **book club**, and said the lady would be down soon. They mentioned the **devil worship** they practice.

[[Lady Fiona Wachter]] entered and said she assumed their presence meant they were **on board with her plans**. Dip spoke to ease the tension. Fiona followed up: **[[Izek Strazni]] must die**, and "these heroes" would make it happen.

### The fight

Iggy said he needed to step out briefly to take a piss. Told to do it there, he objected that it would **ruin their magic circle** — and walked out. Fiona shouted **"get him!"**, and combat began.

The party won the battle. **Dip killed Fiona and her imp, [[Majesto]].**

Fiona dropped a set of **keys** and a **gold ring**.

> **Reliability:** What the keys open and what the ring is are not established in the notes. Assumed carried by [[Odibli]] pending DM confirmation.

### The report

The party told the Burgomaster that they had **saved the day**.

> **Reliability:** Exactly what the party told [[Vargas Vallakovich]] — and how much of the cellar, the cult, or Fiona's coup they disclosed — is not recorded. Confirm with DM.
