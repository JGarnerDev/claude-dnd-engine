## The ravens

Picking up in the fermentation room from [[Session 26 The Poisoned Casks]], the party searched the room properly for the first time: three vats all poisoned, the blight-source cask cracked and empty, the north sliding doors **chained shut from the inside**, and the ravens still watching from the rafters.

[[Odibli]] cast **Speak with Animals** and opened a conversation with one of them, who gave his name as [[Dustin]]. What he told them reshaped the session:

- The murder was **dispatched from the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]]**
- They came because they *"heard the call of [[Davian Martikov]]"* and answered it
- They had been watching the winery **for a couple of days** and had seen everything
- The one the party wanted was **the druid in the cellar**, who carried **a staff** and was somehow controlling the blights

Asked whether they could reach Davian through him, Dustin declined — that has to be done in person. Asked why Davian can command ravens at all, he shifted on his feet and said they had better ask Davian.

[[Luci]] then performed an elaborate **corvid dance** in the murder's honour, with [[Iggy]] accompanying on violin. The ravens loved it, hopping back and forth and chattering along — and Odibli, still under the spell, reported they were singing, and decently in tune.

The party took a **short rest** (Iggy's Song of Rest), the ravens warning it was not safe enough here for a long one. [[Dip]] floated a theory that the druids might be aligned with [[Strahd von Zarovich]], since Vallaki is the one place with real protection against vampires; Iggy recalled that [[Baba Lysaga]] was Strahd's mother's midwife — which the party had in fact been told in [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]].

## The ground floor and the label room

A sweep of the ground floor turned up a room of **thirteen empty barrels** with a spiral staircase, and a storage room where Davian's family had taken their rain cloaks when they fled but left behind the **wooden sandals they wear crushing grapes**. Luci barred the outer door with a five-foot beam, then went along the big casks **kicking the bungholes off** to drain the poisoned wine.

Upstairs they walked into a chamber with a desk, a cabinet, and a large contraption, where a **second druid** — a woman caked in mud, moss over her face, twigs in her hair — was rifling the cabinet and throwing its contents on the floor, with two **vine blights** behind her.

Dip, raging, opened for 17 radiant and closed the fight by killing her on a **cleave**; the blights fell to further cleaves inside a round or two. There was a rules discussion mid-fight about whether cleave triggers off an opportunity attack, settled by reading the text as written.

She carried nothing. The cabinet held **a key on a loop of twine**, and the contraption proved to be the **printing press [[Davian Martikov]] uses to make wine labels**, its ink made from wine. Odibli took a supply of the ink — a callback to his own backstory that Ben prompted him toward.

## The manifests

The upper floor held bedrooms and a kitchen. The master bedroom was the find: a four-poster bed with a **giant raven carved into the headboard**, a **tapestry of a church**, and a **handsomely carved rocking cradle**.

On the desk were the [[The Wizard of Wines Shipping Manifests|shipping manifests]] — a **century** of the winery's deliveries. Almost all of them went to four places: the [[Blood of the Vine Tavern]] in the village of Barovia, the [[Blue Water Inn]] in Vallaki, Krezk, and the Vistani. Flipped back far enough, the oldest records named a fifth customer: **"S" — Strahd**. Those shipments stopped *"decades, if not hundreds of years"* ago.

Luci read it as proof the Martikovs were in league with Strahd, and doubled down on the Vistani entry. Kellan declined to confirm it — they *sold wine* to him once, which is not allegiance, and the Wizard of Wines is a very old winery.

A second key, found **on the bed**, opened the locked chest: **50 gp, 130 ep** stamped with Strahd von Zarovich's profiled visage, and **350 sp**.

## The staff-bearer

Beyond was a room with a **ten-foot square hole cut in the floor** and a wooden winch over it. Perched at the top was a man with wild hair, rotted teeth, and **skin painted red with blood**, gripping a gnarled black staff and babbling. He took one look at Dip and **bolted**.

Dip's grapple failed against a natural 20 and the party gave chase down the turret's barrel ramp. In the fight that followed:

- The druid cast **Thunder Wave**. Iggy alone made the save (23 against DC 12)
- Luci was thrown backward into Dip and Odibli, bowling all three into a pile — Dip and Odibli each taking 1d4 from the fall on top of the thunder damage
- Odibli, already poisoned, came out of it **deafened**
- Odibli relayed what the druid had been shouting in Druidic: **"Nature bows to my every whim, for I have the vampire staff"**
- Odibli landed **Hold Person**, Luci tripped and grappled him, and Dip killed him for 17

Reading [[The Vampire Staff]] off his body, Odibli established that **destroying it would kill every blight in the winery** — at the cost of the staff, whose **Blight Bane** property meant that while attuned, blights and evil plant creatures would not treat the bearer as hostile. He also read a risk of **short-term madness** for attacking with it. The party agreed he could carry it a while and clear the building under its protection.

> **Reliability:** Two mechanical points are unresolved. On **Hold Person**, Kellan called a 20 on the druid's wisdom save, but Paul then read out the failed-save result and the druid was played as paralyzed — the record contradicts itself. On **attunement**, the party noted it requires a rest and argued about whether the earlier short rest counted; Odibli never clearly completed it. Confirm both with DM.

## The cellar

The cellar was **ice-cold**, split by a five-foot brick wall, mist across the floor, with two eight-foot wine racks — the western empty, the eastern half-full of bottles. Behind the eastern rack were **roughly half a dozen humanoid figures, one wearing a full rack of antlers**, and a **fourth druid** whose gravelly voice was already speaking a spell.

She opened with another **Thunder Wave** that smashed Odibli into the wall behind him, dropping him to 0 and sending him to death saves **at disadvantage** because of the poison. It also blew out half the eastern rack, spraying wine everywhere — no longer poisoned wine, as Kellan noted.

Iggy administered a healing potion **from Odibli's own inventory** to bring him back up, then spent his last Bardic Inspiration on Dip. Odibli **oiled the staff, threw it clear of the party, and lit it with a firebolt**. Fire alone was too slow — it blackened and burned without breaking — so he finished it with **Magic Missile**.

> The staff broke, still burning. A horrendous scream came out of it, and every blight in the cellar withered and died.

Kellan noted the scream carried through the cellar and wondered aloud whether it reached past the thick stone walls — left deliberately open. Luci killed the last druid by punching a **wine bottle through the rack into her face**, with Kellan allowing bonus glass damage for it.

## The mold

Exploring after, the party found the cellar grows markedly colder toward the north wall, where three barrels sit labelled **"Purple Grape Mash No. 3"** — a label also on bottles, which Dip immediately clocked as an anagram of Strahd.

Dip's investigation found a **hidden door** in the wall. Behind it: a fifteen-foot tunnel into a shallow cave whose walls, floor and ceiling are furred with **ten patches of brown mold**, which is what keeps the wine cellar cold.

Odibli tried to take a sample. The spores burst. He and Dip both failed their saves for **4d10** — dropping Odibli for the third time that session and **poisoning him again**, on top of the Session 26 wine poisoning. Luci stabilized him with **Spare the Dying** and Iggy brought him back to 1 HP. Luci managed the actual sample collection by **mage hand**, keeping it out of Odibli's fingers.

Kellan confirmed the winery's **interior is now cleared**, and that the remaining wine — including bottles of **Red Dragon Crush** — is recent enough to be safe to drink. The party grabbed a few bottles, asked for a long rest, and were refused.

> **Reliability:** Odibli's condition needs a ruling. He entered the session poisoned from the Session 26 wine and failed a second save against the mold spores. Whether these stack, extend one another, or are separate conditions was never settled, and his **deafened** condition from Thunder Wave was mentioned once and never tracked afterward. Recorded here as stated, not as resolved mechanics. Confirm with DM.

> **Reliability:** No level-up is recorded. Miguel asked directly whether the fight earned one and Kellan's answer was a joke rather than a ruling; nothing in the rest of the session shows new abilities or hit points. All four PCs are stamped at level 4 as of this session. Confirm with DM.
