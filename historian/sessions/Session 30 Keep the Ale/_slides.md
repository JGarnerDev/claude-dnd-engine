## Session 30 — Keep the Ale

Played 2026-10-20. The first night in Ashford.

## Previously on…

Raiders stripped a brewery yard by moonlight. The party fell into the fight out of falling light,
and a stranger walked out of the shadows to finish it. A woman lay bound beside a spooked horse,
and a cart of barrels was gone south.

## The yard

- The stranger, Halsby, said a fox had sent him. Luci tested him and found no lie
- Freed, Sable went straight for the fire. Luci's windstorm spread it; Odibli and Halsby
  smothered most of it
- Every dead raider wore the same crude crystal on a cord. Halsby worked out they carry silent
  speech, and dealt one to everyone

## What the raid left

- Ashford arrived by torchlight: Sheriff Mara, a hurt deputy named Dunny, and Silas, the mayor
- Every street was hit at once, by raiders more organized than before, camped a day across the
  river, all in boots from one cobbler
- Iggy and Luci mended Dunny's bones
- Sable owes Silas about 650 gold, and the stolen barrels were meant to pay it

## Names in the dark

- Old Tobin the ferryman described Wick, an elf raider with a neat brand on his hand
- Someone said "Harrow Bane", and the room went quiet
- Jory the stable boy saw the archer take the cart southeast, wheels sunk deep

## The Mistake

- Sable poured her bestselling ale, deep red and colder than the mug
- She offered rooms, meals and a share of the Golden Lioness for her barrels back
- Odibli told her she ran a bad business. Halsby tried to sell the mayor some shade

## The song

- Iggy played and Luci danced, and old Agnes danced too
- Once, the tune wandered somewhere strange, and the fire in the hearth stopped moving for ten
  seconds
- Then a familiar bark: Sergei, muddy, with Bosco clinging to his back

## What the party chose

- Told the town the truth: they fell from the sky
- Took Sable's job: bring the barrels home from Nella's camp
- Halsby shared the crystals with everyone
- Odibli read the hearth, even though he didn't trust it

## The party

- **Iggy**: played the fire still, and reached level 5
- **Luci**: found someone untouched by darkness
- **Odibli**: found the hearth tied to something absent, and got his dog back
- **Halsby**: handed out the crystals and pitched awnings to a mayor
- **Dip**: helped free Sable, then pocketed the raiders' coin

## Next time…

The barrels are somewhere southeast with a woman named Nella. The hearth that feeds itself has
gone quiet again. Dawn is coming, and so is the road.
