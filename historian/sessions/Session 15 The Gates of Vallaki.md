Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: Escort [[Ireena Kolyana]] to [[Vallaki]]

## Recap

Trying to shortcut the winding road and gain height to read the terrain, the party hauled their cart up the nearby hills — but the foliage mired the wheels and the cart stuck fast. Dip tried shaving the grass down with his axe; it went poorly, and he lost a small toe. [[Ireena Kolyana]] told them these hills were once a field of battle, with many souls buried beneath them.

Remembering Madame Eva's reading, Iggy played mournful music to the mist in respect for the dead, telling [[Bosco]] to sit in the cart. Ireena and [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] thanked him for honoring the buried. Odibli made an arcane check on the fog: not a curse, not quite a spell — more a law of this plane. He let it pass over him, and the fog covered the whole party.

When it lifted, they found themselves back at the earlier bridge. They took the same route again, this time keeping to the roads rather than the hills. Odibli tried to recover Dip's missing toe with mage hand and failed; Dip appreciated the effort regardless.

Cresting a hill, they saw a dilapidated windmill in the distance. With food running slightly low, Iggy eyed it as a possible source. A squawking raven at the door was agitated; Odibli cast speak with animals, and the raven warned them to turn away and run — go inside and you will die. Odibli asked Bosco, who caught a familiar, dangerous smell and backed the raven's warning: meat pies, the smell from the [[The Dream Pastry Woman|hag]] encounter. The party concluded the night hag lived there, and chose to equip themselves in town before any confrontation rather than fight her tonight.

> **Reliability:** The windmill is identified here as the night hag's ([[The Dream Pastry Woman]]'s) lair on circumstantial evidence — the raven's warning plus Bosco's meat-pie scent association. The party has not entered or confirmed it. Confirm with DM whether the hag is actually inside.

They continued to Vallaki. The city limits were grim — many spiked wolf heads. They introduced themselves at the gates; the guards recognized Ireena and Ismark and admitted everyone, though wary of the party. The guards mentioned a festival coming in a few days, and that fun is mandatory. The party professed interest and agreed to have fun; Odibli asked for a 0–10 rating of how much fun they'd have, was largely ignored, and recorded it as a 5. The guards directed them to the inn and the merchant stalls.

At the market Odibli bought a dog, the party pitching in. He passed an animal handling check to ride it, and — via speak with animals — named it [[Sergei Rubelgerd]]. Sergei accepted the name, declared he would be a good boy, and asked for snacks.

> **Reliability:** Dip's lost toe is treated as fiction with no assumed mechanical effect; the session name and cliffhanger framing are synthesized, not from the notes. Confirm with DM.
