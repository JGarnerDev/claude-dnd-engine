### Nursery Mirror Visions ([[Iggy|Iggy]])
Three scenes in sequence:
1. Nursemaid and master of the house (Gustav Durst) kissing
2. A stillborn child born (in an adjoining room — per [[Iggy|Iggy]]'s relay to [[Odibli]]: "baby born in maybe an adjoining room")
3. The nursemaid killed horrifically by a cult in the same nursery room

[[Iggy|Iggy]] relayed to [[Odibli]]: "Couple kissed on bed. Baby born in maybe an adjoining room. Killed by the fireplace."

> **Reliability:** [[Iggy|Iggy]]'s relay to [[Odibli]] was abbreviated and possibly imprecise. Treat as paraphrase, not exact account.

### Cursed Bed
[[Iggy|Iggy]] checks and then kicks the bed. Becomes ensnared (restrained). [[Luci]] uses sun spell (unidentified) to free him. While getting out, [[Iggy|Iggy]] experiences a flashback: he is a writer, musician, poet, good with a sword, a protector.

> **Reliability — Flashback:** Unclear if this is [[Iggy|Iggy]]'s own backstory bubbling up, or a curse/imprint from the bed. DM to clarify.

### Luci's Backstory (Session Disclosure)
[[Luci]] is the embodiment of light. She was ripped from her home light dimension and thrown to the material plane. Without the infinite temple structure, she is in a permanent near-seasonal-disorder. A scourge aasimar who has made pacts with unnamed beings. Party does not know the details.

The specter was not triggered this session. Party approaching the attic and dungeon.
