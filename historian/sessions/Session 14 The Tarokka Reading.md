## Session Notes

### The Lakeside Encampment

On the road to [[Vallaki]] with [[Ireena Kolyana]] and [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], the party followed a twisted, muddy path into a clearing: a lake several hundred feet across, ringed with tents and improvised encampments, figures gathered around a campfire.

- **[[Iggy]]** greeted them and offered friendship; the Vistani returned it.
- Iggy offered music; **[[Odibli]]** offered illusory magic — but his illusions imaging the vampire lord caused serious disquiet among the people.
- **[[Dip]]** moved the vibes along, regaling them with a story and using oil to ignite performative fire — a tale of a man who dropped a big bag of flour, sparking a fire that killed his family.

### The Tale of the Wizard

Some of the Vistani reciprocated with their own story:

> *"A mighty wizard came to this land over a year ago. He stood exactly where you're standing — a very charismatic man. He thought he could rally the people of Barovia against the devil Strahd. He stirred them with thoughts of revolt and bore them to the castle en masse. When the vampire appeared, the wizard's peasant army fled in terror. A few stood their ground and were never seen again. The wizard and the vampire cast spells at each other — from the courtyards of Ravenloft to a precipice overlooking the falls. Thunder shook the mountainside, and great rocks tumbled down upon the wizard, yet by his magic he survived. Lightning struck him, and again he stood. But when the devil Strahd fell upon him, the wizard's magic couldn't save him. I saw him thrown a thousand feet to his death. I climbed down to the river to search his body — but the River Ivlis had already spirited him away."*

> **Reliability:** The wizard is unnamed in play. In module canon this is Khazan, but the party has not learned a name — left as told. Confirm with DM before attaching a name.

Dip questioned the Vistani about the wizard, Strahd, and themselves. They named themselves **loyalists to Strahd** in soft terms — protection, stability. Traders, farmers, bards. Their ancestors helped Strahd back to health after a grievous wound long ago. They have not interacted with Strahd directly, though some have seen him from afar. When Dip pressed about aiding the party aggressively, they directed him to **[[Madame Eva]]**, who had been waiting for them.

### Madame Eva's Reading

The party entered her tent — flame and a big show. The crone spoke: *"At last you have arrived."* She addressed each in turn:

- To **[[Dip]]** — asked about the shadows they felt when they passed away.
- To **[[Luci]]** — *"Someone has fallen silent, and therefore you have lost something of import. But perhaps you have also gained from this silence."*
- To **[[Iggy]]** — who said music is his greatest power.
- To **[[Odibli]]** — *"I know what you once found in the fireplaces."*

She drew five cards immediately. The party felt a little high from the incense, then asked about the reading.

#### The Tarokka — Five Fortunes

1. **Dictator** — *"This card tells of history. Knowledge of the ancient will help you better understand your enemy. I see a throne fit for a king."*
2. **Mercenary** — *"This card tells of a powerful force for good and protection — a holy symbol of great hope. The thing you seek lies with the dead, under mountains of gold coins."*
3. **Paladin** — *"A card of power and strength. It tells of a weapon of vengeance: a sword of sunlight. I see a sleeping prince, a servant of light and the brother of darkness. The treasure lies with him."*
4. **Marionette** — *"This card sheds light on one who will help you greatly in the battle against darkness. Look for a man of music, a man with two heads. He lives in a place of great hunger and sorrow."*
5. **Broken One** — *"Your enemy is a creature of darkness, whose powers are beyond mortality. This card will lead you to him! He haunts the tomb of the man he envied above all."*

#### Eva's Riddles

- **Lucy** asked about the sword of sunlight — Eva: she has said enough; you will know more when you need.
- **Iggy** asked about the importance of music — Eva: *try the mists.*
- **Dip** asked about the shadows and death — Eva, intensely: *a part of Dip remains in the land of the dead.*

> **Reliability:** Dip's "part remains in the land of the dead" is left open for Kellan to define — whether it deepens Dip's existing whisper affliction or opens a new thread. Confirm with DM.

- **Iggy** asked *"who are you?"* — Eva: *"I'm a seer, that's all you need to know."*
- **Iggy** offered a song — Eva: save it for when you need it the most.

### Smoothing the Mood

Eva told the party to leave the camp — the vibes were off — but the Vistani, being decent, still supplied them: torches, basic provisions.

To ease things, **[[Iggy]]** fed **[[Bosco]]** in front of the Vistani and played an ode to him. **[[Luci]]** failed to dance charmingly and fell past the fire; Bosco got the zoomies and ran over her. The crowd was charmed, and the party was invited to stay the night.

- **[[Luci]]** investigated the camp by stealth, suspecting hidden evil. Nothing notable found.
- **[[Iggy]]** began training Bosco — "Sit, stay, come" — over long rests. **Only "Sit" learned.**

### The Road On — Strahd's Carriage

The next morning the party continued down the road to a waterfall beside a chasm and a river. They could cross a nearby bridge or take the long way around by another path — they chose the shorter bridge path.

A large carriage waited ahead. **[[Ireena Kolyana]]** and **[[Ismark Kolyanovich]]** warned the party not to interact with it — Ireena said it was sent by Strahd. **[[Odibli]]** suggested stealing the carriage; Ireena refused flatly.
