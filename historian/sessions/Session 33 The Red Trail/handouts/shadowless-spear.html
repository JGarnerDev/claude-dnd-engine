The Shadowless Spear

Weapon (spear), uncommon · no attunement · Halsby

Your own plain spear, with your black shard grown onto it as the point. The haft throws a shadow. The head does not. It still hums when you cast through it, the way the bare shard did.

Damage
1d6 + 1 piercing
Two-handed
1d8 + 1
Thrown
20/60 ft

  • +1 weapon

    +1 to attack and damage rolls made with it.

  • Your focus

    It replaces the bare shard as your sorcerer focus. When you cast through it, the +1 also applies to your spell attack rolls and your spell save DC.

  • Slows the fade

    While you carry it, each dark-moon mourning rite holds the fade off twice as long: 12 days instead of 6, or 16 instead of 8 after a good night. If you lose the spear, the old span returns.

  • Shadow reach

    In dim light or darkness, its reach is 15 feet.

The fox made it at the bridge outside the Golden Lioness, the morning after you saved Sable Wren. It said the debt was repaid.
