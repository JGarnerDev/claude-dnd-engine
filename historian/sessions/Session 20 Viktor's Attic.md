Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: work off the coffin-shop debt

## Recap

### The audience

Deliberating their next move, the party remembered they had been granted an audience and returned to the office of Burgomaster [[Vargas Vallakovich]].

Vargas set them **two quests** to repay the debt for burning down [[Henrik]]'s coffin shop:

- Gather **12 bushels of sticks** from the forest.
- Find his **missing lady-in-waiting**.

### The attic

Exploring the rooms of the [[Burgomaster's Mansion]], the party found an **attic** — and in it **[[Viktor Vallakovich]]**, the Burgomaster's son. Viktor was the one **experimenting on the missing people** the Burgomaster had asked them to find.

### The teleporter

The party convinced Viktor that [[Odibli]] and company could help fix his broken **teleporter**, and managed to repair it to a degree — but Viktor insisted they **stand on the teleporter** to prove it would work properly.

Instead, the party cast **Hideous Laughter** on him, stopping him from casting any spell to force the issue. While most of the party stayed to watch Viktor and fight back his **skeleton cats**, [[Luci]] ran down to fetch the Burgomaster and [[Odibli]] searched for **evidence** to prove the party was not at fault.

### The bargain

Vargas believed the party's case and agreed to **waive the 600 gp penalty** for the burned house — in exchange for **telling no one about Viktor**. The party also walked away with **Viktor's spells and books**.

> **Reliability:** Viktor's fate afterward — free, confined, or under his father's control — is not established. The two original debt-quests (sticks, lady-in-waiting) were superseded by this bargain but not formally cancelled in the notes. Confirm with DM.

### The rest

The party took a **long rest** afterward — but the **hag denied [[Iggy]] its benefit**, reaching into his sleep.

> **Reliability:** Whether this is the dream-pastry night hag ([[The Dream Pastry Woman]]) specifically, or Iggy's existing shadow affliction, is not spelled out. Confirm with DM.
