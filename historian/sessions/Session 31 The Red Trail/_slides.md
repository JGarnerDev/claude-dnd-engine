## Session 31 — The Red Trail

Played 2026-10-27. The rest of the night, and dawn.

## Previously on…

The party's first night in Ashford ran late. Sable's barrels had gone southeast with an archer
named Nella, and the party had agreed to bring them home. While Iggy played, the fire in the
hearth stopped moving for ten seconds.

## The stillness

- Dip saw the fire go still too, and felt something in it. What, Dip has kept to themselves
- Dip took a share of the raiders' coin, and a crystal of their own
- Odibli turned the old stone over: dead now. Halsby felt the shape where its magic used to be,
  waiting to fill again

## A charm in the fire

- Odibli found a page pressed into the hearth: a charm to smother the fire slowly, left there
  during the raid
- His pen slipped, and the charm woke up with ten seconds to go
- He rode Sergei out, folded it into a paper airplane, and threw. The yard blew up instead of the
  tavern

## Around the bar

- Dip told Sable 10% was low for the work. She offered to talk about a bigger share when they're
  back
- Halsby told how a door somewhere else opened onto a forest here, and a talking fox sent him
- Luci shouted "The Lightbearers!" Sable said she'd call them "you guys" for now

## Dawn

- Soup and hardtack, after a long night's sleep
- Silas lent five horses and a cart: loans, not gifts, and he'll count every shoe
- Jory asked if they were angels. Iggy said only strange magic

## The fox at the bridge

- Green eyes leaking smoke: "You did what I asked. People rarely do."
- Halsby tested it with darkness and an offer. It bowed, declined, and gave no name
- It wove his spear and his shard into one. "The debt is repaid." Then it was gone into the woods

## What the party chose

- Dip pushed Sable for a bigger share
- Odibli rewrote the charm instead of tearing it out, and promised Sable to keep quiet about the
  fire
- Halsby rode with the party, and took the fox's gift without its name
- Iggy kept his wings hidden from Jory
- Dip kept what they felt to themselves
- Luci named the party. Nobody took it up

## The party

- **Iggy**: played the night through, and kept his wings to himself
- **Luci**: steadied everyone's hands, and named a party nobody joined
- **Odibli**: saved the hearth with a paper airplane, and reached level 5
- **Halsby**: turned an old debt into a new spear, and reached level 5
- **Dip**: coin, a crystal, and something unsaid

## Next time…

Five borrowed horses, a cart, and deep ruts running southeast. Somewhere down that road are Nella
and the barrels.
