Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: enlist help and supplies against the vampire spawn; recover the stolen saint's bones

## Recap

Fresh off tracing the stolen saint's bones to [[Henrik]] the coffinmaker — and learning six vampire spawn nest above his shop — the party set out to gather help and supplies before striking.

### Back to the church

They returned to the [[Church of St. Andril]] and the priest [[Lucien Petrovic]]. Lucien believed the vampire spawn holding the bones pointed to a **deeper plot**, and stressed that it was imperative both to **kill the spawn** and to **recover the bones**. He gave each party member a **vial of holy water**.

### The Blue Water Inn

At the [[Blue Water Inn]], [[Odibli]] flattered [[Rictavio]] for his autograph before asking for help getting the bones back. Rictavio bristled at any talk of the **disappearing bones**, told Odibli to shut up, and warned him to be cautious about spreading such dangerous things. [[Iggy]] tried a gentler, more careful appeal for aid from Rictavio or anyone he knew — but Rictavio seemed out of ideas or unwilling to help.

### Izek in the town square

The party turned to the town guards, who referred them up to [[Vargas Vallakovich|the Burgomaster]] and to the guard captain **[[Izek Strazni]]**, said to be around the town square.

There they found preparations and proclamations for the upcoming festival — the next one being the **Blazing Sun** party — with Izek and his guards replacing the party signs. Izek looked deeply unsettling: one of his arms was **unnatural**, marked by dark magic — elongated nails, barbs, heavy deformity — and he carried a **hollowness and a longing air**. He was uncaring about the party and their problem. When they floated luring the vampires to him for a combined battle if he didn't believe them, he remained reluctant even to consider it, and left with a flat threat to deal harshly with them if they broke the law.

Suspicious of Izek, [[Luci]] moved to **test holy water** on him — diluting a vial with water and contriving to spill it on him "by accident." She landed one roll and failed another, nearly getting caught; Iggy aided the deception by faking a trip and shoving her into place. The cover worked — but the holy water had **no effect** on Izek.

> **Reliability:** What Izek is — and why holy water does nothing to him, and what his deformed arm and longing signify — is unestablished. Only the test result (no effect) and the party's impressions are canon. Confirm with DM.

### The ambush plan

The party went back to Henrik's coffinmaker's house to build a **holy-water smoke bomb** — igniting oil and holy water together in a cauldron. Dip fetched a small cauldron and prepared it.

The plan: two teams — an **oil team** and a **bone-stealing team**. All go upstairs with their gear and heat the oil. Quietly crack the two doors to see what lies behind each (the vampire spawn behind one, the bones behind the other). On the signal "go," the bone-stealers grab the bones and run; the oil team throws holy water into the hot oil to release **holy steam**, then flees — ideally escaping with the bones while harming the spawn.

### The wrong door

They opened the **wrong door** first — and their investigations **woke the vampires**.

> **Reliability:** The count of six vampire spawn remains Henrik's word, unverified by sight going into the fight. Confirm with DM.
