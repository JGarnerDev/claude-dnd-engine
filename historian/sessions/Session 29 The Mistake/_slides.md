## Session 29 — The Mistake

Played 2026-10-06. The first night in a new world.

## Previously on…

Davian's relic swallowed the party in pure light. They fell out of it, rested and whole, over a
moonlit brewery yard that raiders were stripping bare.

## The green eyes

On a night road, a fox with smoky green eyes stopped Halsby. It asked him to save a woman from
bandits across the river, and promised to make it worth his while. He said yes.

## Falling into the fight

Dip spotted the raiders on the way down and came down feet-first on one of them. Odibli dropped
quietly outside the wall, out of sight. Luci crashed down by the gap in the wall. Iggy hit the
ground flat on his back.

## The moon

Luci looked up at a vast broken moon shedding shards. She named it **Fida**, in the Dead Sun tongue.
Something answered: *"Closer."*

## The raid on the new wing

A short, bloody fight left six raiders dead. Dip cut down three, their mage among them. Halsby
walked out of the shadows and finished the rest, including the biggest of them, riding off with a
bound woman across his saddle.

## What the party chose

- Halsby agreed to the fox's deal without asking for more
- Dip and Odibli watched the yard as they fell; Luci tried to hold onto the light
- The captive or the casks? The party went after both: she fell clear, and the raiders took
  nothing more

## The party

- **Dip**: flew into a rage, reached level 5
- **Luci**: named the moon, reached level 5
- **Odibli**: saw a cart of barrels escape south, a long-haired archer riding it
- **Halsby**: stepped out of the shadows mid-fight, still waiting on the fox's promise
- **Iggy**: fell out of the light flat on his back, into the middle of a raid

## Next time…

The yard is quiet. The woman lies bound and gagged beside a spooked horse and hasn't said a word.
A cart full of barrels is gone south under the trees. And four strangers just fell out of the sky.
