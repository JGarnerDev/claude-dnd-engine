## Session 29 — The Mistake

Played 2026-10-06. The first night in a new world.

## Previously on…

Davian's relic swallowed the party in pure light. They fell out of it, rested and whole, over a
moonlit brewery yard that raiders were stripping bare.

## The green eyes

On a night road, a fox with smoky green eyes stopped Halsby. Save a woman from bandits across the
river, it said, and it would fuse his spear and focus into a gift. He took the job.

## Falling into the fight

Dip read the yard on the way down and landed feet-first on a raider. Odibli landed hidden outside
the wall. Luci hit hard by the breach. Iggy landed flat on his back.

## The moon

Luci looked up at a vast broken moon shedding shards. She named it **Fida**, in the Dead Sun tongue.
Something answered: *"Closer."*

## The raid on the new wing

Two rounds, six raiders dead. Dip cut down three, their mage among them. Halsby walked out of the
shadows and finished the rest, including the biggest of them, riding off with a bound woman across
his saddle.

## What you chose

- Halsby took the fox's job without haggling
- Dip and Odibli watched the yard as they fell; Luci tried to hold onto the light
- The captive or the casks? You went after both: she fell clear, and the raiders took nothing more

## The party

- **Dip**: raged and reached level 5
- **Luci**: named the moon, reached level 5
- **Odibli**: saw a cart of barrels escape south, a long-haired archer riding it
- **Halsby**: stepped out of the shadows mid-fight, still owed the fox's gift
- **Iggy**: fell out of the light flat on his back, into the middle of a raid

## Next time…

The yard is quiet. The woman lies bound and gagged beside a spooked horse and hasn't said a word.
The archer's cart is gone south under the trees. And four strangers just fell out of the sky.
