# Session 29 — what's established

Not an entity. The table-safe facts from `_world-questions.md` and the files it touched, sorted by
scale so the DM can answer at a glance. The session runner shows each `##` section in its side
panel. Anything marked DM-only is true but not for the table.

## Town — Ashford

- A ford-town of a few hundred on a creek that runs down to the Moorthistle. Named for the ford,
  which is how goods come in and how the raiders come in too
- [[Silas Crubb]] is mayor and general-goods merchant. Neutral to a fault: trades with whoever
  pays, favours neither house out loud
- [[Mara Tull]] is sheriff, and most of the actual defence. Too few deputies, no garrison
- [[Elowen Rimetusk]], the old mage, helps "when she's having a good day"
- [[Richmond Ellesine]] is the smith. Raiders wrecked his stock. He was there for the old war and
  won't like being asked
- The raids have run two or three years. Several families have left; the town is worn down and
  cynical. Tonight is the first strike to hit the whole town at once
- The raiders call themselves independent. Matched boots: *"Third crew this season. Same
  cobbler."* Someone is paying them. "Bane's lot" ([[Harrow Bane]]) stops the talk cold
- The stolen delivery was worth 1,000 gp. [[Sable Wren]] had paid a third and owes Silas
  about 650 gp
- Healing: a roadside shrine to Lathander and a herb-wife who sets bones. No priest who can cast.
  For worse cases, "the witch" at the edge of town, mentioned reluctantly
- Healing potions at Silas's, marked up. No scrolls, no magic shop
- [[The Golden Lioness]]: the safest building in town, nobody can say why. Sable's crew hasn't
  arrived. Free beds tonight. Brews: Lioness's Pride, The Standing, Pale Crossing, Embertide,
  Ironhold, The Mistake. None of it is magic
- Locals see the party as strangers who fell out of the sky, not as freaks. The town is used to
  odd blood because of Sable
- A few townsfolk saw the pillar of light. By Scene 4 the rumour is "they came down out of the
  sky"
- DM-only: the witch is a vampire and [[Wick]]'s target. The hearth, the name "The Mistake", and
  what's under the town are held forever

## Region — Stonemarch and the Moorthistle

- [[Stonemarch]] is [[Lord Corren Cael]]'s fief ([[House Cael]]) on Velmora's southern frontier.
  Western-frontier country: hard travel, homesteads that defend themselves
- Lord Cael is honest, means well, and doesn't come. His hall is a modest fortified seat 3–4
  days' ride north. Mara: *"Lord Cael writes a very good letter. I've a drawer full."*
- The Moorthistle is the border-river. Across it is Riolgatha, the highland nation
- [[Duskwater]] lies southeast along the river: greener country, another house's land
- Tassad is the big port on the bay: rich, crowded, hungry. Three weeks by road, or ten days by
  barge down the Moorthistle and a ship from the bay
- Maps: Silas's road sketch is the base layer. Elowen, [[Halsby]], Mara and Richmond add layers
  later
- DM-only: [[House Marlow]] runs the raids out of Duskwater through cutouts. Deflect: *"That's a
  long story and it isn't mine. Ask the smith."*

## World — Velmora and Aethryn

- The kingdom is Velmora: a weak crown over powerful landed families. It doesn't send help
- Humans and most of Aethryn's people speak Common, on both sides of the Moorthistle. Another
  language only where a people's file says so
- The world is Aethryn. A frontier sheriff would never say it; Halsby might
- It is early autumn. Locals say "after harvest", never a date
- The sky has a broken moon: one huge moon with a bite out of it and a faint ring of debris.
  Locals don't look up
- Long ago a body called Astra struck the world. Broken islands still hang in the sky. Glass falls
  in showers somewhere most weeks after a whistling Kerning, and rich men fight over it. None of
  it has touched Ashford
- Coin is plain gp, sp and cp. Whatwill pearls are heard of, never held
- Magic is rare and reads as awe. Wizard guilds run like cartels; nobody in Ashford enforces
  anything, Tassad is different
- Gods: real devotion goes to powers in the land (the Grey Mothers on the plains). Distant "old
  gods" sit over the top: Waukeen, Kelemvor, Lathander, Umberlee. Divine magic works
- Nobody has heard of Barovia or Strahd. Nothing followed the party. Barovian gear works; only
  the curse-bound things (afflictions, the Boon) go quiet
- DM-only: Sah, the fallen flying city, Bleth and Rivalen, and the Whatwill politics are held
