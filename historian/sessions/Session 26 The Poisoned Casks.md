> **Reliability:** This session was canonized from a machine transcript of the table audio rather than from DM notes. Names spoken aloud are the least reliable part of that source. Individual flags below mark the specific unresolved points. Confirm with DM.

## Recap

### The retreat inside

Picking up mid-combat from [[Session 25 The Wizard of Wines]], the party broke off the needle blight fight and made for the winery doors.

[[Dip]] pushed inside and called back for [[Odibli]] to follow. [[Luci]] — cut off from the others — spent a focus point on **Step of the Wind**, hurled a **bullseye lantern** through a window to break it, vaulted through, landed on barrels stacked on a cart, and rejoined Dip.

[[Iggy]], cornered by roughly **nine needle blights**, disengaged and ran. Mid-flight he glimpsed **something swooping down out of the air** to snatch two of the blights, one of which died — then convinced himself his eyes were playing tricks on him.

Odibli turned and threw a **firebolt** at the pursuers, rolled a **natural 20**, and incinerated one for 16 damage, describing the fire spreading through its insides until it collapsed as "a self-effigy of its own death." He then shut the door behind him.

> **Reliability:** One of the winery folk who joined the assault in Session 25 was clawed by a needle blight during the retreat. The transcript renders their name three different ways within two minutes, so no name is recorded here. Confirm with DM.

### The fermentation room

The party barred the door with barrels and took in the chamber: the smell of fermenting wine, a two-story space dominated by **four enormous casks**, each eight feet wide and twelve feet tall; a central staircase to a ten-foot balcony; old empty barrels branded *Wizard of Wines* stacked beneath it.

Along the rafters overhead, **scores of ravens had quietly gathered**, watching the party with great interest.

### The druid

A creak drew their eyes to the balcony above the westernmost cask: a **hunched, wild-looking woman** in a gown of animal skins and a **headdress of goat horns**, hair long and unkempt, **pouring a flask of thick syrup into the wine**. A small creature of twigs skittered out from under the stairs and vanished beneath the easternmost cask.

Dip called up to her. She was entirely unbothered — the blights outside **"won't come inside… they are only to keep the ground safe. They are security, if you will"** — and asked what brought travellers all this way. Asked directly what she was pouring into the wine, she said only that they would **find out**, and that it **"works like a charm every time."**

> **Reliability:** The party told her they had been sent by "the White Wizard." This is recorded as Iggy meaning the [[Wizard of Wines]] or [[Davian Martikov]], but the phrase is unclear in the source. Confirm with DM.
>
> **Reliability:** The druid is never named aloud in the session. She is filed as [[The Winery Druid]] pending a real name. Confirm with DM.

### The fight

The druid cast **Entangle** across a twenty-foot square, catching Dip and Odibli while Iggy and Luci broke free, then spoke **a word in Druidic**. [[Twig Blight|Twig blights]] — a different creature from the needle blights outside — **poured out of the cask** nearest the party.

The fight was long and attritional: **as fast as one blight fell, another crawled out to take its place.** Iggy opened with Bardic Inspiration to Dip and a **Faerie Fire** over the cluster. Odibli, restrained by vines but with his hands free, cast **Chromatic Orb** for 13 fire damage and leapt it to a second target for 16, killing two at once. Luci lit up her **radiant aura**, burning everything within ten feet each round — allies included — then later **ran up a wall and landed on top of a cask** to fight from above.

The druid fought with **Shillelagh**, multi-attacking Dip for 10 bludgeoning, and later dropped a **Thunderwave** on Dip and Luci, both of whom saved. Odibli, cornered and down to roughly 10 HP, dropped an **Acid Splash** into the cluster that caught Dip in the blast and killed three blights.

**Dip broke the Entangle with a Strength save, raged, and killed the druid in a single greataxe turn** — 25 damage from Divine Fury and Iggy's inspiration die — hacking her apart "limb by limb, like cutting down a tree," and finishing by cleaving her in half. They yelled as they did it: *"Maybe you should have thought before picking on someone. This isn't fair to us."*

### The ravens

Through the whole fight, **the ravens in the rafters came down**. Swarms dropped repeatedly to tear twig blights apart, **fighting on the party's side**, and nothing in the session explained why.

> **Reliability:** The ravens' allegiance is recorded exactly as the party observed it — unexplained. No connection to the [[Keepers of the Feather]] is canonized here, though the party has been told that faction exists. Confirm with DM.

The blights kept coming after the druid fell. Iggy asked to read the situation and perceived that **they were not infinite** — the herd had been thinned enough that what stood in front of them were likely the last. The party ground the remainder down, ravens still helping, until the cask finally ran dry of them.

### What the druid was carrying

Searching the body, the party found the animal-skin gown, the horned headdress, and a **necklace of human teeth**, which **Dip took**. They recovered **the flask**.

Odibli read the room on a 27 Arcana and found **magic still hanging in the air**: the blights are **not natural** and "do not exist of their own accord" — something is controlling them. He could not locate the source, but the flavour was **druidic**, meaning **at least one more druid**, powerful enough to animate and command all of them, is still unaccounted for.

[[Luci]] bottled **about a quarter cask** of the tainted wine for later inspection. **Odibli drank from a cask directly and was poisoned** — confirming what the flask had done. The party floated finding **a priest** to cleanse it and save the townsfolk, and stopped there.

> **Reliability:** Dip's Arcana check on the flask returned that it is a magic potion, with a suggestion it might turn the drinker into a frog. Whether that was a genuine result or table banter is unresolved; the party's working conclusion — that it is the wine poison — is what is recorded above. Confirm with DM.
>
> **Reliability:** Luci spent what the source calls a warlock spell slot on Hellish Rebuke, but [[Luci]] is recorded as a level-4 Monk with no warlock levels. Her radiant aura is accounted for by Scourge Aasimar Radiant Consumption; the spell slot is not. Confirm with Paul.
