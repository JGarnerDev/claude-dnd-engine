### Rose and Thorn Found
- Searched the dollhouse, discovered trapped door to basement
- Ben and Miguel wrote glyphs on blank scrolls to bind the ghosts
- **Rose** (11) bound to Ben's scroll — said thank you, happy to be taken along, will help
- **Thorn** (8) grabbed by Miguel on his sheet
- Paul danced with a mannequin and was inspired

### Level Up (to Level 2)
- Paul chose **Augury** as new spell ability

### Walter Durst's Crypt
Found in the dungeon — empty. Thorn explained:
- Walter was the illegitimate child of Gustav Durst and the nursemaid
- Elisabeth Durst discovered the affair and was consumed by jealousy
- The Durst-led cult sacrificed baby Walter on the basement altar
- Elisabeth had the nursemaid killed — the nursemaid is the specter in area 15
- Thorn says his parents would not allow him in the dungeon

### Key and Cultist Chests
- Jeff spotted a key at the bottom of the well
- Miguel Mage Handed the key — unlocked all the cultist chests

### Grick Fight
Initiative: Jeff ? Grick ? Ben ? Paul ? Miguel. Quick work.
