## Session Notes

### Strahd Fireplace Appearance (Room 6)
Miguel checked the fireplace (his habit). A face formed in the flames — handsome, pale, sharp features, dark eyes that reflected but contained no warmth. Fire did not flicker when he spoke. Voice came from everywhere in the room, low and measured.

Strahd's words: *"You've come to my land uninvited, yet here you are, exploring one of its... oldest wounds. Curious. Most who enter this house do not leave. But you — you interest me. Do carry on."*

Did not threaten. Did not explain himself. When asked who he was, smiled slightly and said nothing. When the fireplace was attacked or a spell cast, the fire simply went out and the room went cold. He was gone.

Odibli: Arcana check — sensed ancient and powerful magic.

### Animated Armor Fight
Suit of armor attacked when Paul inspected it. Killed Miguel and Jeff (downed). Party defeated it.
