Shazzam is a spell for Jeff. Triggers the instant he says the word, whether intended or not. Heals someone randomly for 1 HP.

- Paul's connection to Sabathiel (divine contact) goes silent

### Miguel's Minor Illusion

**Initiative:** Ben ? Miguel ? Specter ? Paul ? Jeff

- **Price:** Paul permanently sacrifices Sacred Flame. Gone for the rest of the campaign.

Tallish, medium build. Slim. Fast moving but mysteriously slow. Hair all over — or just blurry. Hard to see clearly.
