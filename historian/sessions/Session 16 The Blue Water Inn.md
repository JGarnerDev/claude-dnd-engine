Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]] | Mission: Settle into [[Vallaki]] at the [[Blue Water Inn]]

## Recap

A gate guard had recommended the [[Blue Water Inn]] for lodging, so the party made their way there. Inside, it looked a tough crowd. [[Dip]] made a jovial entrance and introduction — while audibly remarking that these people were "less than stellar company."

The barkeep, [[Danika]], summoned them to the bar, sounding irritated. She asked her nearby friend [[Rictavio]] — an extravagantly dressed half-elf bard — to judge whether the party were "good performers." Rictavio opened with a story about slaying a dragon and selling the great treasure. [[Odibli]] sharply asked for details; Rictavio evaded. Odibli came away mistrusting him. [[Luci]] told Rictavio of her background on an amazing celestial plane of light, and Danika quipped that Luci might be a better storyteller than Rictavio.

> **Reliability:** Rictavio's dragon-slaying boast is his own claim, unverified — and his evasion under Odibli's questioning reads as a cover story. Treat his background as suspect. Confirm with DM.

### The performance

The party performed for the bar:

- [[Dip]] did an interpretive dance about the winds and trees of their homeland, narrating it aloud — and rolled a natural 1. They crashed into tables; the crowd hated it; Rictavio gestured for them to stop.
- [[Bosco]] and [[Luci]] danced together. Luci fared poorly; Bosco kind of killed it. Food was pelted, but Bosco was exalted. When the dust settled, [[Iggy]] took Bosco's hand and raised it with a "for Bosco!" cheer. The bar loved Bosco.
- [[Iggy]] told a story from his far-off plane about a gold dragon that once turned into a raccoon to steal apples, turning it into a viol jig.
- [[Odibli]] began a story about a dog, cast speak with animals, and mused on how many animals carry hidden stories we know little of, for their hidden language. He introduced [[Sergei Rubelgerd]], who barked and borfed a tale that Odibli translated: a survivor's search for his best humanoid friend, **Tidak**. Sergei and Tidak had seen dragons and giant serpents and traveled far, until one night a thick fog came over them and Tidak was taken away. The room cried. They loved Sergei.

### Karl and Nikolai — the Burgomaster's madness

Two drunk men, [[Karl]] and [[Nikolai]], beckoned the party over and praised specific parts of their performances. They said they wanted to relieve the town of the "[[The Burgomaster of Vallaki|Burgomaster]]'s madness." Asked to explain, they spoke of the festivals as a bad thing — dissenters imprisoned or worse. The Burgomaster holds themed, thematically unpredictable festivals (music, food, dancing) to keep people happy, and is unhealthily obsessed with everyone being happy: partying and happiness under duress, on pain of treason or harsh punishment.

When Iggy asked about Vallaki's desire to be free of the Burgomaster, Karl and Nikolai only said "all will be well in Vallaki" — a detached, eerie mantra.

> **Reliability:** Karl and Nikolai are drunk and self-described dissenters; their account of the Burgomaster is one-sided. The Burgomaster's name is not yet known to the party. Confirm with DM.

### Szoldar and Yevgeni — the fur boys

Two other grim men, [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] and [[Yevgeni Krushkin]], greeted the party from another table. They kill wolves and sell them for a living, and liked the performance. They offered a service: guides and information about the land — 5 gp for a day, bits of information for drinks.

### Danika's gift and the missing wine

[[Ismark Kolyanovich]] and [[Ireena Kolyana]] mentioned they'd like to visit a church in the morning.

[[Danika]] called the party over, thanked them for the entertainment, and offered them a free night at the inn, with stew. Wine was available, but shipments were overdue — a "wizard of wine" who usually ships it was late. **Mission offered:** find the wine wizard. (See [[Find the Wine Wizard]].)

A man came out from the kitchen and introduced himself as [[Urwin]], Danika's husband. He thanked them and offered to guide them to their rooms later. He walked back into the kitchen; [[Dip]] followed him. His and Danika's sons, [[Brom]] and [[Bray]], were working in the kitchen.

The party took the free night as a long rest.

> **Reliability:** Iggy's market trade — a platinum necklace for thieves' tools and hide armor — is recorded from the notes; the party member and item details should be confirmed against the character sheets. Confirm with DM.
