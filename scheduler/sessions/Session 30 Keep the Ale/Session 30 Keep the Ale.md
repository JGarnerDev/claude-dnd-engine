Acolyte, Mastiff and Wolf, reskinned, plus Nella's custom block (from
`workshop/first-homebrew-act/notes/session-2-pursuit-statblock.md`, tuned here). Mixed in for
variety (Jeff, 2026-10-07): a sneak, a healer, dogs and a wolf, so the fights aren't all melee bodies.

### Difficulty check (v0.18.0)

Session 29's raid ended in two rounds, so both fights were re-run against the party's offense.

- **Party thresholds** (all level 5 once the night's two level-ups and the dawn one land): Easy
  1,250 · Medium 2,500 · Hard 3,750 · Deadly 5,500.
- **Party damage, after the dawn short rest:** ~70 a round. Dip ~25–30 (raging, Reckless, Extra
  Attack, Frenzy, Cleave), Luci ~14, Odibli ~12, with Shatter or now **Fireball** on clusters,
  Halsby ~12 with 3rd-level slots, and Iggy ~9 plus Bless or Faerie Fire (more with Smite if he
  takes Paladin 2). Whatever slots went into the hearth (`S2_HEARTH`) are gone for the camp; the
  short rest doesn't return them, except through Arcane Recovery.
- **The fork:** 175 XP, ×2 = 350 adjusted, **under Easy**. 38 HP, RTC under 1. Left small on
  purpose: the stake is the rider and the horses, not HP.
- **The camp:** 1,675 XP, 9 creatures ×2.5 = **4,188 adjusted, Hard** (Deadly starts at 5,500).
  235 HP; RTC ~3.4 overall, ~2.7 on the floor. Varrick survives two hits from anyone; the Thugs
  survive two from anyone but Dip, and one Fireball. Sennet, Hesper, Clatter and the dogs are
  one-hit bodies, but each asks the party a different question: find the sneak, drop the healer,
  get the roof, deal with the dogs.
- **RTD, crew on its stations:** a front-liner takes ~35 a round (Varrick ~10, the Thugs ~12
  between them with Pack Tactics, Sennet's Sneak Attack ~5 averaged over misses, Nella's share
  ~8), so RTD ~2 for a raging Dip and ~1.2 for Luci if she dives the cart. The dogs add prone,
  not much damage. The back line takes Nella's spread, Clatter's crossbow and Hesper's Sacred
  Flame, ~RTD 3. Standard lethality holds **only** while the crew stays on its
  stations and Nella keeps to wound and pin.
- **Why Hard:** the day carries ~11 of standard's 18–24 rounds, so the camp is the day's one real
  fight. The missing long rest is the squeeze; the party is otherwise near full.

### Running it

- **The crew stays on its stations:** Haddo and Brask at the cart, Varrick between cart and fire,
  Hesper at the fire, Clatter on the shed roof, Brick and Mortar chained at the corral, and Sennet
  working between the shed's side door and the cart. All eight converging on one PC is ~50 a
  round, RTD under 1, and quietly turns this into a deadly fight. Same rule as the Session 29 raid,
  and it matters more now the camp is sized Hard. Sennet is the one who moves; he goes where the
  Thugs already are, for Sneak Attack, not after the back line.
- **Alert states** (set by the chase, `S2_APPROACH`):
  - **Unaware** (4 stages won): Clatter and Hesper dicing by the fire, Brask asleep against the
    cart wheel, Sennet in the shed over the ledger, the dogs dozing (Stealth still has to beat
    their passive 18 near the corral). The party picks its position and gets surprise if it wants
    it. Nella is in the shed (O6), so her **first two rounds** are the run to her perch: out the
    side door and up the slope is ~80 ft of movement, and her best is 70 (Dash via Skirmisher's
    Step). She shoots from the slope on round 2 if anyone is in the open.
  - **Dusk** (2–3 won): Clatter on the roof and Hesper at the fire on watch, Nella on her perch at
    the dusk rotation.
  - **Night alert** (0–1 won): perimeter staked (difficult ground, or DEX 12 to cross at speed),
    double watch, fires banked. Nella pre-positioned, Prepared Ground live from round 1.
- **Round budget:** ~3 on the clock from the raid, ~1–2 at the fork, ~3–4 at the camp ≈ 8–9 of
  standard's 18–24. The squeeze is the missing long rest, not the round count.

## Scenes

Everything below is **guidance, not script**. Lines and read-alouds are there to improvise from,

### Scene 1 — The Yard

- **Trigger:** session open. Pick up in the yard exactly where Session 29 stopped.
- **Location:** [[The Golden Lioness]], the yard and the blown-open new wing. Scorched timber,
  dropped tools, one cask on its side, the horse still dancing.
- **NPCs present:** [[Sable Wren]], bound and gagged.
- **Time:** ~10 minutes. Quick.

> *"The yard goes quiet the way places do after a fight — not silent, just done. A cask rolls a
> slow half-turn and stops. The horse has backed itself into the corner of the wall and is
> breathing like a bellows. Beside it, a woman with horns and her wrists tied behind her is
> looking up at you over a gag, and her expression isn't fear. It's assessment."*

#### S2_SABLE_FREE — Cut Sable loose. Her first words are practical, not grateful

- Whoever frees her gets her first words. They are not thanks.
- **Sample lines** (pick one):
  - *"Horse first. He'll break a leg on that wall."*
  - *"Is the fire out in the wing? Then I'm fine."*
  - Once standing, to all of them, level and unhurried: *"Right. Who are you, and are you staying
    for supper?"*
- If someone asks if she's hurt: *"Ask me tomorrow. Tonight I'm counting what they took."*
- She does not ask about the light. She will, later, and only once.

#### S2_LOOT — The bodies: coin, and a crystal on a cord around every neck

- **Coin:** ~120–180 gp across the bodies and the big one's purse. About 25–35 gp a head.
- **The Earshot Crystals**, one per PC (confirmed, `meta/loot-options/Party/misc/Earshot
  Crystals.md`). A cloudy crystal on a cord around every raider's neck, identical.
- **First touch:** cold, then suddenly not. Let them find what it does by accident. Wait for
  someone to mutter while holding one, and tell the nearest other holder they heard it *without
  hearing it* — the word arrived in their head, not their ears.
- **What they do:** touch your crystal to speak telepathically, words only and past any language,
  to one holder you can see or to "all" holders within 300 ft. Listeners only need to carry theirs.
- **History, at most three sentences, said only if asked:** *Every raider wore one, identical and
  cloudy, and not one of them was seen to use it.* Nothing about who made them or why.
- **DM only — the long fuse:** [[Nella]] kept hers, and so did a few of her crew. Any "all"
  message within 300 ft of one of them, they hear. **This matters tonight.** If the party uses
  "all" near the waystation, Nella hears it. She does not act on it in any way the party can
  notice. Her escape must read as her own skill.
- **Transition:** voices up the road. The town is coming.

### Scene 2 — What the Raid Left

- **Trigger:** the yard is cleared.
- **Location:** [[Ashford]], the road up to the Lioness. A ford-town that was hit everywhere at once.
- **NPCs present:**
  - **[[Mara Tull]]** — sheriff. *Wants:* to know what five armed strangers are, and not to have
    to arrest them. *Voice:* quiet authority, gives an order once.
  - **[[Silas Crubb]]** — mayor and general goods. *Wants:* the ledger to balance. *Voice:* drawling
    tangents that always land on a number.
- **Time:** ~10 minutes. Quick. Narrate around the lines.

#### S2_TOWN — The whole town was hit at once; they couldn't reach the Lioness

- Every street fought its own raid. That's why nobody came for Sable. The town arrives late,
  bloodied, from its own fights.

> *"They come up the road in ones and twos, not running. Nobody runs who's already done their
> running tonight. A woman with a sheriff's star has a deputy's arm over her shoulder and his blood
> down her side. Behind her, a big soft man in a shop apron with a burn on one sleeve, looking at
> the yard the way a merchant looks at a spilled order."*

- **Also coming up the road:**
  - **Dunny Marsh:** young deputy, arm in a sling, insists he's fine.
  - **Wenna Moss:** herb-wife who sets bones. Smells of yarrow and smoke.
  - **The Hollises:** four kids under one blanket, mother counting heads.
  - **Agnes Brand:** washerwoman, burned hands wrapped. Hums when nervous.
- **Mara's lines** (each said once):
  - *"You five. You can keep your weapons. Just keep them pointed at the ground while we talk."*
  - *"Half the town says you fell out of the sky in a flash of light. I'd like to hear what happened
    from you."* — the party chooses how to explain. She listens all the way through and doesn't
    argue.
  - *"Every street was fighting. I couldn't get to the Lioness, and you held it for me. Thank you.
    Tomorrow I'll want to know who you are."*
  - To Dunny, not looking at him: *"Sit down before you fall down. That's an order."*
- **Silas's tangent:** he spots the new wing's brewing copper, the big kettle beer is boiled in,
  half out of its crate with a spear-hole punched through the side. He's genuinely offended.
  *"Do you know what that is? That's a brewing copper. One sheet, hammered round by hand, and
  there's maybe three smiths on this side of the river who can do it without splitting the seam.
  You can't patch it. Patch it and the beer tastes like a penny. And they put a hole in it. Didn't
  take it, mind. Took the casks, took the cart, and stabbed the one thing worth stealing. Idiots.
  …That was on the delivery I sold her, too. A thousand gold all told, and she'd paid me a
  third."* Sable owes him about 650 gp. She would never say so.
- **The Barovian coin (optional, one beat):** only if someone spends or shows coin. Silas turns
  Iggy's electrum over: *"Never seen this face before. Where's this coin from?"* Then quietly: a
  coin nobody recognizes looks forged, and he'd rather they didn't spend more of it in his town.
  Seed only.

#### S2_NAMES — Three names and the boots, one line each, then stop

- **[[Wick]]**, secondhand from **Old Tobin Reed** (ferryman, lost his boat tonight, sees
  everyone who crosses): *"Elf. Had a mark on his hand like a brand, but neat,
  like someone did it with paperwork. Looked at the houses like he was counting the shutters."*

#### S2_HEARTH — The hearth is dying. Odibli finds the charm and writes over it

> *"The last stroke goes into the coals and the glyph stops being what it was. For a heartbeat the

#### S2_CHASE_RULES — Four stages, one check each, scored by stages won

## Contingencies

- **They skip the pursuit and stay to build.** Fine. The cart reaches a fence in Duskwater. The
  casks aren't gone, but recovering them becomes a costlier beat later (buy-back or a riskier lift).
  Sable eats the loss and the Stake ladder starts one deed behind. The waystation Common is still
  there if they go later.
- **The fork rider gets away and the camp is on night alert.** Still winnable. Lean on the heist
  (the camp is watching the road, not the gully side) or a parley. The casks are always there.
- **They corner Nella.** Run the escape ladder honestly down to the river plunge. If even that can't
  stick, the table wins — capture her, and re-plan her arc around the tally and the debt. Never
  cheat visibly. Captured-Nella sample, cold: *"Well. That'll cost me."*
- **They want to chase Nella past the gully.** She's mounted and gone. Let them find the cached
  horse's tracks running toward the river and stop at the bank. *"She knows this water. You don't."*
- **They go straight at the feud with the ledger or the banner.** Give the next rung only:
  *someone* is paying Harrow Bane. Stop there. Silas recognises the banner back home and won't say
  more in the street.

## Closing

Land it on the cell door, not on the casks.

> *"The cart's back, the ale's back, and nobody's looking at either. They're looking at a barred
> window, and a man behind it who walked in on his own and hasn't said a word. Mara's at the door
> with her arms folded. Somewhere behind you, Sable is rolling casks into a wing that finally has
> something in it. And someone in the crowd says 'week's end,' and someone else agrees."*

**Leave unresolved:** who the prisoner is and why he surrendered · Bane's face · whose banner it is
· Nella's tally, which she will want back · whether the Boon ever opens again.
