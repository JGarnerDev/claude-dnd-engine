While You're Away

What Sable can build at the Golden Lioness while you're gone

Leave Sable gold and a list. When you come back, some of it is built.

At a time
Two projects
Paid with
Gold you leave
Stake
Still by deeds

  • Hire hands

    Lok Thuliaga on the door, Bree Tealeaf behind the bar, Oswin on the stage. 50 gold each covers a season's wages.

  • A room in the new wing

    250 to 500 gold each, and about a month of work.

  • A better kitchen

    150 gold and two weeks. Better food, and more of it.

  • Clear the back plot

    50 gold and a month of digging out weeds and bad trees. It opens ground for stables, a garden or a yard.

  • Stables

    300 gold, once the plot is clear.

  • A new copper

    Only George Ringo or Zach can remake it, and someone has to find one of them first. Until then the Lioness pours, but it can't brew.

A busier Lioness gets noticed. By everyone.
