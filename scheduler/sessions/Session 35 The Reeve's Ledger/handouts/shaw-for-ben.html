Shaw Proudfoot

Halfling, 50, bald, two feet tall and far too much muscle for it. Monk 5 (Warrior of the Open Hand), Soldier background, Chaotic Good. He wears a small blazer with no shirt under it, not robes. Tyler's pitch: "he's like if the song Firestarter was a hobbit." His touchstone is Jason Statham.

  • A monk on the sheet only. No monastery, no inner peace. You find him in a bar or out on a job.
  • Hot-headed and stubborn. Do not joke about his height.
  • He takes shady jobs when money is short, up to one line: children and families.
  • Ideal: family, family, family. A straight shooter. When he leaves, he leaves in plain sight.
AC13
HP43
Speed40 ft
Focus5

Extra Attack, Stunning Strike, Deflect Attacks, Open Hand Technique, and the Grappler and Savage Attacker feats. Save DC 12. His D&D Beyond sheet is the source of truth.

His story

He left a quiet halfling homeland young, too confrontational to sit still, and learned to fight as a soldier: cloak-and-dagger work and front-line vanguard alike. He joined Harrow Bane's band as muscle and rose to its middle ranks.

The jobs grew seedier. Missions with no briefing led to raids on small villages, with upset kids and broken-up families. The band offered him a promotion to lead a crew. Then came an order to kidnap someone's daughter for ransom. He would rescue a kidnapped girl, but he would never take one. He packed up, told the crew he had "something else to do", walked into Ashford, the town he'd helped raid, laid his weapons down in front of the sheriff's office and surrendered. He hasn't spoken since, because he thinks silence gets fewer people hurt.

How he enters

The last scene of Jeff's season finale, Session 35. The town has set his hanging for noon. The party walks into the cell, and after sizing them up the silent man in the corner finally speaks: "What do you know about it?" Then he holds out a letter.

The party opens the letter at the table, and it is what sends them north to Cinto and your run. Tyler gets about half an hour's notice on Discord before his entrance, so he isn't waiting around all night. Bring him in near the end of a session the same way if you need to again.

Yours and Tyler's to write

The letter's text, and the reward that makes the party go north. Tyler said he'd message you about it. Jeff's run only needs the letter to exist and be handed over.

The leading idea: Shaw has a long-lost half-brother (different fathers): Chocola, Tyson's old character. The letter is from Chocola, saying he's in a prison up north and needs Shaw to break him out, and Tyson comes back as the holiday special.

Tentative. Nobody remembers Chocola's race, and neither Tyson nor Chocola has a file yet. Tyler also floated, as a joke, that "Santa needs security."
