The Lioness Deed

Terms of the party's share in the Golden Lioness

Sable's square hand. The ten is crossed out, and the new number is written next to it.

Share
20%
Earned by
Two deeds

  • What the share gives

    A room each at the Lioness, free meals, and the right to name one thing in the new wing.

  • How it grows

    Only by deeds. Coin never buys stake.

  • The new wing

    Each new room costs 250 to 500 gold to build. Gold for building is always welcome.

Signed at the bar of the Golden Lioness, Ashford.
