On the House

Sable's thanks for the casks: two upgrades to the Golden Lioness, free

Pick two. Sable builds them in the next two weeks, at her cost. Whatever you don't pick goes on the list with a price.

Pick
Two of six
Cost
Nothing
Ready
In two weeks

  • Hire hands

    Lok Thuliaga on the door, Bree Tealeaf behind the bar, Oswin on the stage, their wages paid for a season. The Lioness opens every night whether you're home or not, and Lok remembers every face that comes through the door.

  • A proper kitchen

    A bread oven and a smoke hood over the hearth. Better food, and more of it. When you finish a long rest at the Lioness, each of you regains one extra Hit Die.

  • Clear the back plot

    The weeds and bad trees come out, and the ground opens for stables, a garden or a yard. The trees you fell give 3 loads of timber.

  • A room in the new wing

    One finished room with a bed, a lock and a window: the first the Lioness can let. Travelers pay to sleep in it, and the coin goes in Sable's ledger.

  • Palisade and lamp watch

    A fence round the yard, a gate, and a lantern on a post with someone under it all night. Nobody reaches the Lioness unseen, and you can't be surprised there.

  • A better dock

    A proper landing on the river, with pilings, a crane arm and room for two boats. Stone and iron come in by water instead of by cart, so they no longer arrive a week late. It's also the one place a boat of your own could tie up.

After these two, Sable builds for gold and materials.
