The Stillwater Wraps

Weapon (hand wraps), uncommon · no attunement · Luci

Plain grey hand wraps, wound from wrist to knuckle, cool to the touch. They never take a stain. Nobody in Ashford wove the cloth.

Rarity
Uncommon
Attunement
None
Bonus
+1 unarmed

  • Bound

    While you wear the wraps, your unarmed strikes gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

  • Stand still

    If you haven't moved this turn, your unarmed strikes also deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage.

You woke standing in the ford at Ashford with these on your hands, and the water around you holding still.
