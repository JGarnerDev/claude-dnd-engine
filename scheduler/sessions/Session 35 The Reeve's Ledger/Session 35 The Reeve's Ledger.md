#### S35_01_IGGY_BEAT — Iggy's wings, the smite, and the voice in every crystal

- **Tell the table:** this is a recap. Each of you gets the fight back from your own side,
  including the moments you lived and didn't hear. Iggy first.
- **Say:**

> *"Go back to the yard. Iggy, you're at seven, an arrow in you, and the griffon is a shadow in
> the cloud. Something in you stands up. Your eyes go white-gold, all the way across, and two
> wings unfold out of your back, wide and luminous, the light off them strong enough to throw
> every shadow in the yard the other way. Your copper hair lifts as if there's a wind only you
> are standing in."*
>
> *"And every crystal within three hundred feet speaks at once: yours, the crew's, the one at
> Nella's throat. An old voice, level and unhurried, like a sergeant who has said it a hundred
> times on a hundred drill yards. 'Up, Wallaed.'"*
>
> *"You go up. The cloud lights from underneath, and the griffon is a dark shape on white. When
> your blade finds it, the smite goes off like a bell in a library: warm, steadying, the feeling
> of a hand on your shoulder. Under the warmth there's something else, a wrong note, a chord
> held a beat too long. And under your breath, without meaning to, you say two names. Caelion.
> Liora."*

- **Say (to Odibli):** *"From under the cart, you see the light go across the yard, and for a
  heartbeat there's writing in it, margin notes in a hand you almost know, there and gone."*
- **Say (to Halsby):** *"The light takes your shadow and pins it hard and short under your feet,
  darker than it should be, and grey at the edges. Then it lets go."*
- **Private (Dip):** what Dip sees
  - **The message:** Your eye is trained to see the world as one face of many. When the wings
    open, it all goes thin. Behind Iggy you see an old soldier, tall and very tired, one hand on
    Iggy's shoulder, the other at his side, ink to the wrist. Behind him, shelves. Shelves that
    go up out of sight, half of them empty, the books on the rest scorched at the spines. A
    sealed letter turns slowly in the air between them, a wax seal stamped with an open book.
    And under it all, a song, not finished, the last line still waiting. It's the same thing you
    saw at the hearth, the innermost doll. It lives in Iggy. Then the threads come loose and it's
    a yard again, on fire. For a moment you felt your own hands too, the many that push you, all
    of them reaching for him. Hers still aren't among them.
- **What stays hidden:** what Sah is, why the crystals carried him, anything about the Lioness
  hearth beyond Dip's private. Nobody says the name Sah but Iggy. If someone asks Iggy who that
  was, it's his answer.
- **Iggy's first spell last session** already came out in a language nobody knew. That's the
  first cue landed; don't replay it.

#### S35_02_COLD — Odibli's fight, and the Citadel's cold

- **When:** straight after Iggy, still in the recap.
- **Say:**

> *"Odibli. The relief rides in, four of them, and you do what every story says not to: you
> dive. Belly-first under a cart, a camera's view through the spokes, sand in your beard,
> 'Gnomaha'. And from there, for one shining round, you are exactly where an evoker belongs.
> Three darts of light off your fingers and into the captain."*
>
> *"Then you lean out and ask Luci a question no wizard should ever have to ask. 'Can I
> Fireball you?' 'Sounds like fun, dude.' The yard goes orange. Three riders don't get up. Luci
> walks out of it. A second cart catches."*
>
> *"Then the griffon comes for you. Talons, the ground falling away, the yard getting small. You
> twist free and drop, and land on your feet, because of course you do. And it finds you
> again. A beak like an axe. And the yard goes dark."*

- **Private (Odibli):** the Citadel's cold
  - **The message:** Go back to your first Magic Missile, from under the cart. As the darts
    leave your hand, the cold comes. Not a chill: a cold with edges, like ink drying on your
    skin. Far away, in a quiet room you've never seen, a pen stops scratching. Something turns a
    page and reads you. Your own narration, the voice that's always running under your breath,
    stops mid-word. Then the darts hit, the yard comes back loud, and the cold goes as if
    someone closed a book. It's the same quiet that came after the orb, all those months ago,
    only this time it felt like being looked up.
- **Nothing else at the table.** If Miguel asks, it happened once and hasn't happened since.

#### S35_07_CHASE — One roll for Luci

- **When:** the goodbye ends. Nella runs for the water.
- **What Nella does (RAW):** as she runs, she casts **Spike Growth** on the bank behind her (20 ft
  radius, hidden thorns, 2d4 piercing per 5 ft). Then a staked line she rigged days ago, under
  the bank, takes her out into the current. With Roving she swims as fast as she runs.
- **Say:**

> *"She's fast. You're faster. You close the gap, forty feet, thirty, and as she passes the last
> willow she flicks two fingers back at the ground without looking. The grass behind her
> stands up. Every blade of it is a thorn now, a carpet of them between you and her, and she's
> past it, at the water's edge, a hand already on a rope you didn't see."*

- **Ask Paul for one check, DC 18:** Acrobatics or Athletics, his pick. He says how Luci does it.
  This is the whole chase; make it count.
  - **Advantage** if she spends a focus point on **Step of the Wind** (her jump distance doubles,
    so she clears the thorns), or if Iggy flew and lands on the bank. Not both; advantage
    doesn't stack.
  - **If she runs through the thorns** without leaping, she takes the damage (2d4 per 5 ft,
    about 15 ft of it) and still rolls.
- **Success:** Luci lands on her at the water's edge, a hand on the rope, a knee on the bow.
  - **Say (Nella):** she stops. She unstrings the bow, sits down in the mud, and looks at the
    river for a long moment. *"Well. That'll cost me."*
  - **Choice (Luci):** take her, or let her go. Taken, she's the party's to question, and her
    tally is theirs to bargain with. Let go, she owes Luci, and she counts it.
- **Fail:** the thorns, a heartbeat too long, and Nella is gone. `S35_08_ESCAPE`.
- **If the table forces a fight anyway:** her block is in Combatants. Roll initiative only for her
  and whoever is there; she has advantage (Prepared Ground). Wound and pin, never kill. Grappled
  or with the river cut off, she sits down as above.
