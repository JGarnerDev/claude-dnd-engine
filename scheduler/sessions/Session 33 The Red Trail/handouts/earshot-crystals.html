Earshot Crystals

Wondrous item, common · no attunement · one each

A rough crystal, crudely wrapped in metal, on a leather cord. Every one is the same, cut from one stone. You took them off the raiders left dead at the Golden Lioness.

Range
300 ft
Uses
Unlimited
Carries
Words only

  • Speaking takes touch

    While touching your crystal, you speak telepathically to other holders within 300 feet. Pick one: a single holder you can see, and only they hear it, or all holders.

  • Listening doesn't

    You hear messages as long as the crystal is on your body. To answer, touch your own.

  • Words only

    No images, feelings or senses. The listener hears your own voice.

  • Any language

    The words are understood whatever language either of you speaks.

"All" means all

A message to all reaches every crystal from this stone within 300 feet, including ones you don't know about. You get no sense of who heard it.

Halsby worked out what they do and dealt one to each of you: "Grab one."
