A Notched Antler and a Purse

Found at the river, where the griffon banked

A stick of antler worn smooth where a thumb rests, covered in hundreds of small notches cut in groups. Some groups are scored through. Beside it, a leather purse tied off in three places, each section holding a different coin.

Gold
150 gp
Silver
500 sp
Platinum
5 pp
Every section is counted and sorted, one kind of coin to a tie.
