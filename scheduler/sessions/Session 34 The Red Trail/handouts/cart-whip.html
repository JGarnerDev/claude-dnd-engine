Varrick's Cart-Whip

Weapon (whip), common · no attunement · Dip

Twelve feet of plaited ox-hide around a core of lead shot. It's heavier than it looks. The brass cap on the handle is worn flat where one man held it for years, and the knot at the tip has been replaced so often it's gone black.

Damage
1d6 slashing
Properties
Finesse, reach
Mastery
Slow

  • Haul

    Once per short rest, when you hit a Large or smaller creature with it, you can drag that creature up to 10 feet in a straight line toward you.

  • Drover's crack

    While you hold it, you have advantage on Animal Handling checks to drive, steer or calm draft animals. Its crack carries about 300 feet.

You took it off Varrick the drover's body at the waystation.
