## Overview

A holiday pilgrimage to the north pole, where the veil between worlds is thin. It ends with the party freeing a misunderstood groundhog who has been stuck at the heart of reality for a thousand years.

- **System.** D&D 5e, 2024 rules.
- **Party.** 5–6 players, level 5 (Choco La joins in the Buckle).
- **Tone.** A little silly, with moments of wonder.
- **Mini-boss.** [[The Yule Log]], CR 7.
- **Final boss.** [[Groundhog Claus]], CR 9 (a surprise).
- **Stat blocks.** Holiday Boss Stat Blocks (.docx and .xlsx) in Desktop\DnD Bosses.

**Route:** [[Cinto]] → the [[Deorum Ostium|Deorum Sanctum]] → [[The Rimefolk Maze|the Maze]] → [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] at [[The Ice Bridge|the Ice Bridge]] → [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] → [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]].

**The hook:** the party is here because of [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]. His long-lost half-brother Choco La, a monk on pilgrimage, wrote to him from [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], deep in the [[Deorum Ostium|Deorum Sanctum]]. The Post (a [[The Blackmarket Guildsects|Blackmarket guildsect]]) was carrying the letter to [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] when he and Harrow Bane's band robbed the mail, and [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] found his own name on it. It asks [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] to come and find him if he hasn't written again by Midwinter. Nothing else has come. It's Midwinter. [[Dip]] is on their own version of a pilgrimage and absent for the whole campaign. In [[Cinto]] the party also falls into [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]]'s troubles: a small task from an NPC that snowballs, and every hook points the same way Choco La went.

## Tesla Bombeck

An optional NPC who can turn up in any Part. Shaw arrives with the party, so Tesla is just for fun, with a special moment for Shaw (below).

**Who they are:** the pilgrim from the d8 table who arrived yesterday but already knows the party's names. They slipped through a thin spot in reality and have lived bits of this adventure before, out of order. They remember small things they shouldn't (which way to turn, that the log is rude, where the good cocoa is), but never the big things.

**What Tesla is like:** a changeling bard with a pocket watch that runs backward. Tesla never fights: at the first sign of danger, they're suddenly somewhere else.

**Where they can drop in** (pick whichever fits the session):

- **[[Cinto]]:** at [[The Last Fire|the Last Fire]], already sitting at the party's table, with each of their favorite drinks ordered.
- **The maze:** walking the other way down a corridor, muttering "Oh, not this bit again," and pointing right.
- **[[The Buckle]]:** in [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s best cabin, which they somehow paid for last week.
- **[[The Hollow Mountain|The Mountain]]:** on the curved horizon above the party, upside down, waving.

#### What they bring

- **"I've seen this before":** once in the session, after anyone rolls, Tesla can say they remember how this goes, and that roll is rerolled.
- **Half-remembered hints:** they can offer one answer to a puzzle or riddle, but they always get one detail slightly wrong (for example, the right chains in the wrong order). The party has to fix it.
- **A parting gift, the [[Pebble from Tomorrow]]:** once, when a creature fails a saving throw, it can succeed instead. Then the pebble crumbles to sand.

**How they leave:** at the end of the session, they step around a corner and are simply gone, and the snow where they stood falls upward. If the party asks [[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]] about them later: "Ask me who they were, and I'll ask you who they'll be."

**Shaw's moment:** Tesla knows everyone's name, but Shaw is the one they light up for: "Oh, it's you. You're the one I came back for." They won't say why. Before leaving, Tesla presses the [[Pebble from Tomorrow]] into Shaw's hand and tells him one small thing about his future. You decide what it is, and it should come true before the campaign ends. If Shaw mentions his brother, Tesla adds: "Your brother's fine. Quiet, but fine. He's saving his words for you."

## Shaw, Choco La and Hobbes

**[[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]** (Tyler): a halfling monk, and the reason the party goes north. His long-lost half-brother Choco La went north on a pilgrimage and wrote to him from [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. The Post was carrying the letter to [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] when [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]], riding with Harrow Bane's band, robbed the mail and found his own name on it, as he told the party in Session 35. Robbing [[The Post|the Post]] may get him blacklisted (see The Post and the [[The Blackmarket Guildsects|Blackmarket Guildsects]], below). Everything else about [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] is Tyler's to shape.

**Choco La** (a new friend's monk; name of the player and race to come): [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]'s half-brother, on pilgrimage to the [[Deorum Ostium|Deorum Sanctum]]. The party finds him in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] in Part 5, and that's where his player joins. Until then, he's the person they're looking for.

**Why he stopped writing:** in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], Choco La met [[Hobbes]], a halfling scholar who heard the scratching and went down to say hello. Choco La promised to wait for him, and took a vow of silence with the cupola monks "until [[Hobbes]] is back." Then [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] closed [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]], so even his last letter never got out. When the party reaches the cupola, he's one of the silent monks on the cushions.

**[[Choco La's Letter|Choco La's letter]]** (handout: taken from a robbed Post shipment, still sealed with [[The Post|the Post]]'s small closed envelope pressed into the wax; give it to Tyler before the first session, or have [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] read it aloud in [[Cinto]]):

> [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]],
>
> It's been a long time, and you probably don't remember much of me beyond a name. It's your brother. Half of one, anyway: Choco La. I've been looking for you for years, and the postmaster here swears his couriers can find anybody, so here's hoping.
>
> I'm writing from [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], which is a whole town built round an iron ship frozen in the ice, deep under the [[Deorum Ostium|Deorum Sanctum]]. That's what the pilgrims call it. The monks here call it the [[Deorum Ostium]] and won't say why.
>
> The monks in the cupola say that if you're very still, you can hear the oldest place, the one underneath everything. They have a word for it: [[The Zahareena|Zahareena]]. I sat with them for an hour and heard something else. Scratching. Something is digging, far down.
>
> I've made a friend. [[Hobbes]] is a halfling scholar in a red knitted hat who talks more than anyone I've ever met, and he wants to go down and say hello to whatever is digging. Someone has to keep him out of trouble, so I'm staying until he's back. He says he'll be back after breakfast. He says that a lot.
>
> Something is happening up here, [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]. If you don't hear from me again by Midwinter, come and find me. Bring the good cocoa.
>
> Your brother,
>
> Choco La
>
> P.S. A sailor in the bar says the bonfires on [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]] have started lighting themselves. Probably nothing.

**Choco La's trail:** he came through [[Cinto]] and talked about his little half-brother the whole way. See [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]: Choco La got here first (Part 1) and Choco La and [[Hobbes]] in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] (Part 5).

**Finding him (Part 5):** he's in the cupola's listening hall, among the silent monks. [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] spots him at once; anyone else who knows what he looks like needs WIS (Perception) DC 12. He can't speak, because of the vow. He can hug, though, and write.

- **The vow:** Choco La's player decides whether to break it to greet [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]]. If he does, the monks don't mind: "Some words are worth more than silence." If he keeps it until [[Hobbes]] is found, [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]] is moved, and blesses him: once before [[Hobbes]] is found, he has advantage on a WIS saving throw.
- **[[Hobbes]]'s pack:** [[The Matron|the Matron]] is holding it, and she'll give it only to Choco La, the friend [[Hobbes]] named. If he's keeping his vow, he has to ask without words: a mime is CHA (Performance) DC 10, or he can write it down, which she accepts with a sigh. Inside: [[Hobbes]]'s notes on the first four riddles (crossed-out guesses, no answers); his map of the grown tunnels, which gives advantage on the Survival check to find the hollow mountain; a tin of the good cocoa; and a drawing of Choco La and [[Hobbes]], labelled "for luck."

**Who [[Hobbes]] is:** a halfling scholar: soft-spoken, endlessly curious and hopeless at keeping track of time. He came north looking for the oldest place in the world, and he thinks he found the door. He wears the red knitted hat his mum made and never takes it off. He always says he'll be "back after breakfast," and he never is.

**[[Hobbes]]'s marks at the gates** (Choco La's moment, the whole way down through [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]]): beside each riddle gate, at halfling height, [[Hobbes]] scratched a note in chalk, signed with a little acorn. He showed Choco La the acorn over cocoa in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. Anyone finds the marks with INT (Investigation) DC 12; Choco La spots them automatically. Each one is half a hint:

1. **Outer shell:** "Set down my pack. My boots. My dignity. Then I set down the thing I carried all the way north, and the giant laughed at me. Kept it anyway. —H" (hope)
2. **Drowned halls:** "Shouted. Sank. Sulked. Sank slower. Sat down and had a biscuit, and the water held me up. —H" (calm)
3. **Glass gardens:** "Told the flowers about my friend the monk who won't stop meditating. They lit up like a festival. Sorry, Choco. —H" (a story)
4. **Shrine ring:** "Said Mum's name. Said Choco La's. Something very old woke up and said thank you. —H" (a name)
5. **The core gate:** the same line, over and over, dozens of times, climbing the wall: "Haven't got this one. Back after breakfast. —H" The first ones are neat. Under possibility 1 they just get more tired. Under possibility 2 they get rougher, then scratchier, and the last few look like they were done with claws.

### Where Hobbes is now: pick one (DM-only)

Pick before Part 6. The marks work for both, so you can decide late.

**Possibility 1: [[Hobbes]] is stuck in the loop at the last gate.** [[Hobbes]] never solved the core riddle. He camped in front of the last gate, close enough to the core that [[Groundhog Claus]]'s Same Day Again caught him too. Every morning he wakes up, makes the same breakfast, tries the riddle, chalks "Back after breakfast" on the wall and goes to sleep. He's been doing it for weeks and thinks it's his first day.

- **The reunion:** at stop 5 there's a little tent, a kettle on, and [[Hobbes]] in his red hat. "Choco! You came down? I only left this morning!"
- **What he knows:** every day he hears a voice through the gate ranting about shadows and spring. He can recite the whole speech word for word without knowing who says it. (It's [[Groundhog Claus]]'s monologue, so the surprise stays a surprise.)
- **At the core:** [[Hobbes]] hides behind a mound of letters. Once a round he shouts what's coming ("He does the presents next!"), because he's heard it all before. The first creature to act on it gets advantage on its next save against that.
- **When [[Groundhog Claus]] is freed:** the loop breaks, and [[Hobbes]] catches up on all those weeks at once. His beard grows a month in a second, his stomach growls like a bear, and he asks, "How long was I gone?"

**Possibility 2: [[Hobbes]] is [[Groundhog Claus]].** [[Hobbes]] solved the first four doors, but not the fifth. He found a way round it, sat down by the fissure at the core to think, and the light leaking from the crack in reality soaked into him while the loop held him still. One day, lived a thousand times over, became a thousand years. He isn't [[Hobbes]] anymore: he's a very large groundhog in a very worn red coat and a red knitted hat, and he has forgotten everything except winter, his shadow and the letters.

- **How it fits the rest:** outside the mountain only weeks have passed; inside the loop, a thousand years. Thin reality folds time both ways, which is why [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]] has heard the scratching "for years" and why letters to the Claus were piling up before [[Hobbes]] ever arrived. [[Tesla Bombeck]] is proof that time comes unstuck here.
- **The red hat:** [[Choco La's Letter|Choco La's letter]] mentions [[Hobbes]]'s red knitted hat. [[Groundhog Claus]] is wearing it. Let that land.
- **Clues on the way:** the claw-scratched chalk lines at the core gate; the acorn scratched into the stone at the burrow mouth; [[Hobbes]]'s pack smells faintly of fresh earth, though he never took it down; and [[Groundhog Claus]]'s monologue slips into [[Hobbes]]'s phrases ("back after breakfast").
- **Recognising him:** the first time [[Groundhog Claus]] speaks, Choco La makes a WIS (Insight) check, DC 10. On a success, something in the way he talks is his friend.
- **Talking him down:** calling him "[[Hobbes]]," or showing him the drawing from his pack, is an automatic success, like the other props. Choco La saying "back after breakfast" makes him hesitate, and he loses his next reaction.
- **Fighting:** reaching 0 HP breaks the loop instead of killing him, as always, so nobody can kill [[Hobbes]]. Beating him by force without trying to understand him is still a crack.
- **When he's freed:** the hour of calm comes as usual. Then the groundhog peels off him like a coat of shadow and darts into the fissure without him. Left behind in the letters is a skinny halfling with a beard down to his knees. "Choco? I was only gone a minute." [[Groundhog Claus]] is free out there somewhere, still a little bit [[Hobbes]]. Whether the red hat stays with [[Hobbes]] or goes with the groundhog is your call.

## Choices tracked across the campaign

> **What happens here:** everything the party did pays off. That means the gifts they earned, the five messages pulled through the fissure and the final choice about who should know about the core. If they made too many mistakes, [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] falls.

**Read aloud, when he's freed:** *And then, everywhere at once, the world goes still. Not silent: calm. In [[Cinto]], the bargaining stops mid-sentence. Out at sea, the waves lie down. Every pilgrim in every tunnel feels it, the way you feel the sun come out. For one hour, nothing in the whole world is afraid.* If [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] falls, skip this and go to The worst ending.

The ending changes depending on what the party has done. Secretly track these choices across the campaign:

- **Secrets.** Where: [[Cinto]]. Example: Did they sell forbidden knowledge or protect it?
- **[[The Yule Log]].** Where: Ice Bridge. Example: Did they put it out for good, or leave an ember burning?
- **The letters.** Where: Buckle post office. Example: Did they bring the Claus letters with them?
- **Gifts.** Where: [[The Buckle]]. Example: Which gifts did they earn, and did they gather all four food-stall gifts?
- **Relics.** Where: [[The Hollow Mountain|The Mountain]]. Example: Did they take relics, copy them or leave them?
- **The finale.** Where: The core. Example: Did they free [[Groundhog Claus]] by force or by understanding?

The choices could change where the hour of calm falls or how far it reaches, what comes through the fissure, what the monks do next, and whether [[Cinto]] remembers the party as heroes or thieves.

## Calling for rolls

Each Part ends with a table of the rolls it might need. A few rules of thumb so you can stay in character:

- **Only roll when failing is interesting.** Great roleplay can skip the roll, or earn Advantage.
- **DCs from the 2024 rules:** Very easy 5, Easy 10, Medium 15, Hard 20, Very hard 25, Nearly impossible 30.
- **Group checks:** everyone rolls, and the group succeeds if at least half of them do.
- **Helping:** a character who helps gives Advantage.
- **Passive scores:** 10 + the modifier, for noticing things without anyone asking.
- **Contests:** when two creatures are working against each other, both roll and the higher result wins.
