Act: [[Act 2 - Below the Ice]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

> **What happens here:** the party reaches the base camp built around the frozen iron ship. They can rest at [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s inn and visit [[The Buckle Post Office|the post office]], [[The Cupola Temple|the cupola temple]] and the food stalls, earning the NPCs' gifts. This is where the small hook snowballs into the larger quest for the core.

**Read aloud:** *The cavern is vast, and filling it is a ship. Not a ship of wood and sail, but an iron mountain riveted together, its towers bristling with black tubes that point at nothing. Lanterns hang from every rail, smoke curls up from food stalls on its deck, and somewhere a bell rings. In the ice beside it, a little post office carved to look like stone has its lamp lit.*

[[The Buckle]] is the base camp deep in [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]], carved out around a vast iron ship found in the ice. No one knows who built it, but pilgrims see it as a beacon of hope. Rumor says it slipped through from another world during a great cosmic shift.

#### [[The Iron Leviathan]], as pilgrims describe it

- **The hull:** as long as a cathedral, with no sails or oars, made of riveted iron plates so perfectly matched they seem forged by one hand.
- **The Thunder Towers:** iron towers on the deck holding giant black iron tubes. Scholars argue whether they're siege engines, wands or a god's organ pipes. Pilgrims call them the Pointing Fingers.
- **The Castle on its Back:** a tall iron keep lined with round glass windows like the eyes of a giant insect.
- **The Bronze Flowers:** huge petalled bronze shapes frozen under the stern, purpose unknown.
- **The Bell:** a brass bell and plaque in an unknown script. *Comprehend Languages* could hint at the ship's name.

**Theories:** dwarven smiths say it was made without magic, which they find more frightening. Wizards say it's a warship from a world where the gods died. The monks say it proves the veil is thin.

#### Locations

- **Post office.** What it is: Carved into the ice to look like a stone building, run by the brothers Post.
- **The inn.** What it is: The ship's main body, run by [[The Matron|the Matron]], with rooms below deck like "the Gun Deck Suite" and "the Boiler Cabin".
- **The Temple.** What it is: Carved into the cupola of the chamber, run by the lead monk. Reached by precarious stairs or by climbing the mast (DC 13 Athletics), which pilgrims treat as part of the pilgrimage.
- **Deck stalls.** What it is: Food and merchant stalls between the Thunder Towers.

People: see [[The Matron]], [[Mitzi]], [[Pip Post]], [[Barnaby Post]], [[Stamp]], [[Aurelian Sellenor]] and the stall keepers at [[The Deck Stalls]].

## The Buckle has heard too

The stories reached [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] before [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] cut it off, and they've been retold every night since.

- **[[Pip Post]]** has been holding a letter addressed "To the Viol Player, Somewhere North." It's fan mail for [[Iggy]]. Nothing stops the letters from getting through.
- **[[Barnaby Post]]** has a whole sack for [[Halsby]], addressed "To the Lightning One." "Half are fan mail. Half are complaints. One's from a hedge priest's sister. I didn't open that one."
- **[[The Matron]]** has added a line to the house rules in every cabin the party sleeps in: "7. No lightning indoors." [[Mitzi]] has added underneath, in crayon: "8. Dancing is allowed (NOT near the stove)."
- **[[Mitzi]]** wants to meet the horse. She is devastated that there's no horse.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]** says nothing about the stories, but his glow flickers whenever [[Halsby]] speaks. The static is louder near [[Halsby]], and he'd like to know why.
- **[[Prophet Boone]]** has heard about [[Odibli]] and the horse. "A friend to beasts! The Great Burrower will love you!" He gives [[Odibli]] a free Shadow Snack.
- **[[Hari]]** asks [[Iggy]] to play while she tells her story of light over darkness. If they perform together, both [[Iggy]] and the listener get advantage on their next roll that day.
- **[[Sister Kirsty]]** has already got a carol about the party. Most of it is wrong, and it rhymes "[[Luci]]" with "juicy."

#### [[Odibli]]'s fans in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]

- **[[Pip Post]]** has heard about the airmail curse. "An airmail man after my own heart!" He offers [[Odibli]] an honorary post office satchel, and from then on any letter [[Odibli]] folds into a paper airplane is delivered for free.
- **[[Stamp]]** the puffin, who tolerates nobody, sits still for [[Odibli]]'s interview. [[Odibli]] succeeds automatically on Animal Handling with [[Stamp]]. [[Stamp]]'s answers are all one honk, but the honks are noticeably warmer.
- **[[The Matron]]** adds another line to the house rules in the party's cabins: "9. No paper airplanes in the dining hall." [[Mitzi]] folds the rule into a paper airplane and throws it at [[Odibli]].
- **[[Sister Kirsty]]**'s carol about the party has a whole verse for [[Odibli]]. It rhymes "gnome" with "horse that followed him home."
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]** asks to see [[Odibli]]'s journal, holds it for a long moment, and hands it back without a word. Later he says only: "Your ink is very old." (A hint toward [[Odibli]]'s secret in [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]].)

#### [[Halsby]]'s, [[Luci]]'s and [[Iggy]]'s fans in [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]

- **[[Barnaby Post]]**, about the sack of letters for [[Halsby]]: "They're all addressed to 'the Lightning One' because nobody knows your name. If you told me, I could deliver them properly." He keeps asking, every time, with a sigh.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]** asks [[Halsby]] to sit with him in the cupola for a listening meditation. If [[Halsby]] does, the static goes quiet for the first time in years, and [[Halsby]] makes the WIS check with advantage.
- **[[Mitzi]]** wants [[Luci]] to teach her the dance from the Golden Lioness. She falls over a lot. She glows the whole time, and so does [[The Matron|the Matron]], slightly.
- **[[The Matron]]** gives [[Luci]] the cabin furthest from the boiler, without being asked. "I've heard about you and fires."
- **[[Prophet Boone]]** wants [[Luci]] to lead [[The Order of the Burrow|the Order of the Burrow]]'s Groundhog Dance (spinning in circles until you fall down). He's thrilled when she's better at it than anyone.
- **[[Sister Kirsty]]** wants [[Iggy]] to play the viol while she leads the carols around the mast. If [[Iggy]] plays, everyone who joins in earns [[Kirsty's Candle]] more easily: the Performance check needs no roll.
- **[[Pip Post]]** delivers [[Iggy]] a second fan letter, this one from a child in Ashford who wants to learn the viol. It ends: "Please write back. Pip says nothing stops the letters."

#### Finding Choco La

he's in the cupola's listening hall, among the silent monks. Shaw spots him at once; anyone else who knows what he looks like needs WIS (Perception) DC 12. He can't speak, because of the vow. He can hug, though, and write.

- **The vow:** Choco La's player decides whether to break it to greet Shaw. If he does, the monks don't mind: "Some words are worth more than silence." If he keeps it until [[Hobbes]] is found, [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]] is moved, and blesses him: once before [[Hobbes]] is found, he has advantage on a WIS saving throw.
- **[[Hobbes]]'s pack:** [[The Matron|the Matron]] is holding it, and she'll give it only to Choco La, the friend [[Hobbes]] named. If he's keeping his vow, he has to ask without words: a mime is CHA (Performance) DC 10, or he can write it down, which she accepts with a sigh. Inside: [[Hobbes]]'s notes on the first four riddles (crossed-out guesses, no answers); his map of the grown tunnels, which gives advantage on the Survival check to find the hollow mountain; a tin of the good cocoa; and a drawing of Choco La and [[Hobbes]], labelled "for luck."

#### Choco La and [[Hobbes]] in the Buckle

- **[[Pip Post]]** remembers stamping [[Choco La's Letter|Choco La's letter]]. "Monk, very calm, talked about you the whole time. Said he'd been looking for you for years. I gave it to our best courier. It got to you, didn't it? Nothing stops the letters. ...How did it get to you, exactly?" (See [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] and [[The Post|the Post]], in the Overview.) Pip also has Choco La's second letter to [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]], which never got out because [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] closed the bridge: "Still here. [[Hobbes]] went down this morning. Back after breakfast, he says. I've taken a vow until he's back, so this is the last you'll hear from me for a bit. —C" Pip finally gets to deliver it by hand, and cries a little.
- **[[The Matron]]** is holding [[Hobbes]]'s pack for Choco La (see Finding him, in the Overview).
- **[[Mitzi]]** brings Choco La cocoa every day, and he nods his thanks. She's thrilled the party has come: "He hasn't said a word in weeks! I've been doing both halves of the conversation." She also learnt a halfling walking song from [[Hobbes]], and hums it whenever she passes the mast.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]:** "Your brother has kept silence with us since his friend went down. His friend was the only one who ever wanted to go and say hello." If [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] or Choco La does a listening meditation with him and succeeds, they hear, very faintly and far below, someone saying "back after breakfast."
- **[[Prophet Boone]]** remembers [[Hobbes]] buying a Shadow Snack and asking a great many questions about burrowing beasts. "A true seeker!"

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** how they treat the Matron. Honesty and paid debts win her favor; bargaining and rudeness lose it, and losing it is a crack.

## Rolls

- **Climbing the mast to the temple.** Roll: STR (Athletics). DC: 13. What happens: Fail by 5 or more: fall 10 ft., taking 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage. The stairs need no roll, just a lot of complaining.
- **Winning [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s favor.** Roll: No roll. What happens: Honesty, paid debts and respect for the ship win it. Bargaining or rudeness loses it automatically.
- **Lying to [[The Matron|the Matron]].** Roll: CHA (Deception) against her passive Insight. DC: 17. What happens: Fail: she knows, and her favor is lost.
- **Reading [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s mood.** Roll: WIS (Insight), watching [[Mitzi]]. DC: 10. What happens: [[Mitzi]] glows when [[The Matron|the Matron]] softens and flickers when she's hurt.
- **Getting Barnaby to finish his delivery.** Roll: CHA (Persuasion). DC: 13. What happens: Intimidation works too, but Pip disapproves.
- **Petting [[Stamp]].** Roll: WIS (Animal Handling). DC: 15. What happens: Fail: one disapproving honk and a nip for 1 Piercing damage.
- **Listening meditation with [[Aurelian Sellenor|the Archhierophant]].** Roll: WIS (Perception or Insight). DC: 15. What happens: A clue about what lies ahead.
- **Proving what the scratching is.** Roll: WIS (Survival) or INT (Investigation), listening below the cupola. DC: 15. What happens: It has the rhythm of something digging, not worlds grinding.
- **Studying [[The Iron Leviathan|the Iron Leviathan]].** Roll: INT (Arcana). DC: 12. What happens: There's no magic anywhere in it, which is somehow worse.
- **Working out who built it.** Roll: INT (History). DC: 15. What happens: The rivets match no forge in this world.
- **Reading the bell plaque.** Roll: INT (Investigation), or Comprehend Languages. DC: 18. What happens: A fragment of the ship's name.
- **Singing carols with [[Sister Kirsty]].** Roll: CHA (Performance). DC: 10. What happens: Any attempt earns [[Kirsty's Candle]]. A 20 makes her cry.
- **Solving [[Joseph Schwartz|Joseph]] and [[Bella Schwartz]]'s lamp oil.** Roll: INT (Arcana) or INT (Religion). DC: 14 or 12. What happens: The thin veil keeps it burning. Earns [[Oil That Lasts]].
- **Helping finish [[Hari]]'s rangoli.** Roll: DEX, adding proficiency with painter's or calligrapher's tools. DC: 12. What happens: Earns [[Hari's Diya]]. Listening to her story needs no roll.
- **Agreeing with [[Prophet Boone]].** Roll: No roll: say it out loud, in public. What happens: A DC 10 CHA (Performance) check gets the crowd cheering along. Earns the [[Burrow Hood]].
- **Robbing [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s vault ([[Shadwell]]'s hook).** Roll: DEX (Sleight of Hand) with thieves' tools. DC: 20. What happens: Caught: [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s favor is lost for good, which counts as a crack.
