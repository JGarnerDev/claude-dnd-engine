Act: [[Act 1 - The Fire That Eats the Way]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

> **What happens here:** the party enters the glacier temple and travels through the pilgrims' carvings, from fresh nicks on the outer walls to the strange work of the deep ice. It's mostly atmosphere and travel: meet pilgrims and feel the veil getting thinner.

**Read aloud:** *The glacier opens like a cathedral door. Inside, the light turns blue and the noise of [[Cinto]] falls away behind you. Every surface is carved: names, prayers, initials, a lopsided heart. The deeper you go, the older the carvings get, until you can't read them at all, and the silence starts to feel like it's listening.*

The [[Deorum Ostium|Deorum Sanctum]] is the whole north pole: a massive temple carved into the glaciers, where the veil between worlds is thin. A single order of monks oversees it. They believe a site this holy can't belong to one faith, so they act only as stewards, helping others find the answers they seek. Their motto: *"We keep the door, not the key."*

**Pilgrims carve the ice.** Some leave a single nick, some have carved elaborate murals for decades, and some have dug huge tunnels searching for their "opening." The carvings get older the deeper the party goes:

- **Outer walls:** fresh nicks and names.
- **Middle tunnels:** decades-long murals and obsessive tunnels.
- **Deep ice:** carvings in scripts no one alive can read. Rumor says forbidden knowledge is hidden where no light has touched for eons.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** leave a carving of their own, as every pilgrim must, and choose where and what.

## Rolls

- **Crossing the outer glacier.** Roll: WIS (Survival). DC: 12. What happens: Automatic with a Useful route secret. Fail: an extra hour in the cold.
- **Extreme cold.** Roll: CON save, each hour. DC: 10. What happens: Fail: 1 level of Exhaustion. [[Malingo]]'s furs give Advantage.
- **Spotting a hidden crevasse.** Roll: WIS (Perception). DC: 13. What happens: Fail: the lead character makes a DC 12 DEX save or falls 10 ft., taking 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Reading the outer carvings.** Roll: INT (History). DC: 10. What happens: Names and dates, some very old.
- **Reading the middle murals.** Roll: INT (History). DC: 15. What happens: Murals of a great burrowing beast and a sun that never sets.
- **Reading the deep-ice scripts.** Roll: INT (History), or Comprehend Languages. DC: 20. What happens: A Forbidden secret worth 500+ gp in [[Cinto]].
- **Monk etiquette.** Roll: INT (Religion). DC: 12. What happens: They bow correctly, and the monks point the way to [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]].
- **Working out which pilgrim is strange.** Roll: WIS (Insight). DC: 12. What happens: They notice which pilgrim knows more than they should.
