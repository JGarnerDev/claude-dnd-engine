Act: [[Act 2 - Below the Ice]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

Each key NPC can give the party a single-use gift, depending on the party's actions. The ways to earn each gift are suggestions.

- **[[Return-Receipt Parcel]].** From: The brothers Post. What it does: Wrap an item that fits in a backpack and name a place you've been. It arrives there at once. Later, say "Return to sender" and it comes back to you. Works once. How to earn it: Deliver a lost letter found in [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]], or help Barnaby finish a delivery he's been avoiding.
- **[[The Matron's Hearth Tent]].** From: [[The Matron]]. What it does: Takes 1 minute to set up anywhere: a ledge, a storm, mid-dungeon. Creatures inside can finish a Long Rest, and nothing outside can find, harm or enter it. Afterward it folds into a snowflake and melts. Works once. How to earn it: Earn her favor with honesty, paid debts and respect for the ship. Being kind to [[Mitzi]] counts too.
- **[[The Fold of Static]].** From: Archhierophant [[Aurelian Sellenor]]. What it does: As an action, you and up to 8 willing creatures within 10 ft. step through the static to any place you've already been, even on another plane. You arrive exactly where you meant to. Works once. How to earn it: Complete a listening meditation, or bring him proof of what the scratching really is.
- **[[Kirsty's Candle]].** From: [[Sister Kirsty]] (Christian stall). What it does: While lit, it sheds light in a 20-ft. radius for 1 hour. The first time a creature in its light drops to 0 HP, it drops to 1 HP instead. How to earn it: Join the carols around the mast.
- **[[Oil That Lasts]].** From: [[Joseph Schwartz|Joseph]] and [[Bella Schwartz]] (Jewish stall). What it does: One creature regains one expended use of a limited ability, or one spell slot of level 3 or lower. It lasts longer than it should. How to earn it: Help them work out why their lamp oil never runs out.
- **[[Hari's Diya]].** From: [[Hari]] (Hindu stall). What it does: As an action, light it for 1 minute: it sheds bright light in a 30-ft. radius, dispels magical darkness there, and allies in the light have advantage on saves against being Frightened. How to earn it: Help finish her rangoli, or listen to her story of light over darkness.
- **[[Burrow Hood]].** From: [[Prophet Boone]] (Order of the Burrow). What it does: A burlap groundhog hood. While wearing it, you have advantage on Persuasion checks with [[Groundhog Claus]], who sees you as kin. How to earn it: Agree, out loud and in public, that there will be six more weeks.

**All four food-stall gifts together:** [[The Festival of Lights|the Festival of Lights]]. When the candle, oil, diya and hood are brought to the core together, their lights merge. Once, the party can dim or brighten the Inner Sun for 1 minute. Dimming it switches off Sees Its Shadow, and is one way to break [[Groundhog Claus]]'s loop.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** which NPCs to help. All four stall gifts together make [[The Festival of Lights]], one way to break the loop.
