Act: [[Act 3 - The Hour of Calm]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

> **What happens here:** the party descends into the hollow mountain at the deepest part of the ice. They pass through the relic layers, each with a riddle inspired by Dante's Inferno, while thin reality throws strange effects at them.

**Read aloud:** *The tunnels stop looking carved and start looking grown. Then the ice simply ends. You stand at the edge of a cavern so big it has its own sky. A small, sleepy sun hangs in the middle, and the land curves up and away on every side, until forests and lakes hang above your heads. A mountain points down from the ceiling and up from the floor, like a reflection. It's warm. Somewhere, snow is falling upward.*

The deepest part of the ice holds a mountain older than time, with relics of countless ancient species and unknown gods. Reality is thinnest here, and the final boss waits at its heart.

**Name ideas:** the First Stone, the Root of the World, the Hollow Crown, or Mount Before, because monks say it was there before anything else.

**Finding it:** the deepest pilgrim tunnels stop looking carved and start looking grown. Then the ice opens into a vast cavern, with a mountain pointing down from the ceiling and up from the floor, as if reflected.

#### Hollow Earth

- **The Inner Sun:** a small, sleepy glowing orb hangs at the center and never sets.
- **Curved horizon:** the land curves upward in every direction, so forests, lakes and ruins hang overhead.
- **Gravity follows the ground:** walk far enough and you're upside down compared to where you started.
- **Warmth:** it's strangely warm, green and quiet. Near the walls, snow falls upward.

## The rings and the party

Each ring around [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]] echoes one character. These are optional moments: let them happen if that character goes looking, and keep the details loose so the player can shape them.

- **Outer shell (giants).** For: [[Iggy]]. Why it fits: The giants' world ended too, and they kept hope anyway, just like the gate riddle says. What can happen: A war horn the size of a house lies across the giant steps. If [[Iggy]] sounds it (DC 12 CHA (Performance); a fail gets one terrible honk and the giant's face laughs), the walls answer with the giants' song: a tune from a world that ended, like [[Iggy]]'s. It gives [[Iggy]] a few bars for their unfinished song, and the giants' carved faces are veined with copper-red stone, the same shade as [[Iggy]]'s.
- **Drowned halls (fish folk).** For: [[Halsby]]. Why it fits: The murals only move in shadow, and the fish folk gave their names to the deep water so it would let them cross. What can happen: [[Halsby]]'s shadow drifts loose and swims into a mural. The painted fish folk all turn to face it, and one hands it something. When the shadow comes back, [[Halsby]] knows one thing no one else does: a name, a warning, or where their power comes from (work it out with Kellan). A DC 13 CHA save calls the shadow back early. On a fail, it comes back when it's ready, a little colder.
- **Glass gardens (people who became light).** For: [[Luci]]. Why it fits: The people who became light built temples to the sun, perhaps the very one [[Luci]] woke up in. What can happen: The crystal plants show [[Luci]] the memory they've been missing: the last moment before they woke up alone in that temple. For as long as [[Luci]] stands in the garden, they can finally sit still. The light drawn out of them at the end hums the same note as these plants.
- **Shrine ring (forgotten gods).** For: [[Odibli]]. Why it fits: The gods here faded because no one wrote their names down, and [[Odibli]] writes everything down. What can happen: [[Odibli]]'s secret at the nameless altar (above).
- **The core (the Inner Sun).** For: [[Dip]], from afar. Why it fits: [[Dip's Letter|Dip's letter]] is drawn through the fissure here, and [[Dip]]'s twin was lost in a fire. What can happen: Among the thousands of letters to the Claus is one scorched at the edges, unsigned, in handwriting very like [[Dip]]'s. Whether it's from [[Dip]]'s twin is a story for when [[Dip]] comes back. Whoever finds it can keep it for [[Dip]].

**Bonus for [[Halsby]], at the shrine ring:** the riddle's answer is "a name." If [[Halsby]] ever chooses to say their own first name aloud here, the forgotten god that stirs answers to them alone, and grants inspiration to the whole party.

**Bonus for [[Halsby]], at the core:** a shadow sorcerer can do what no one else can, and take [[Groundhog Claus]]'s shadow away. Any shadow or darkness magic [[Halsby]] casts on him switches off Sees Its Shadow until the start of [[Halsby]]'s next turn. Doing it three rounds in a row breaks the loop, as another way to free him.

**Relic layers**, from the outside in:

- **Outer shell.** Who left it: Giant ancestors. What's there: Steps too big to climb, and tools the size of houses.
- **Drowned halls.** Who left it: A fish-like elder race. What's there: Dry halls covered in barnacles, with murals that move when you look away.
- **Glass gardens.** Who left it: A people who became light. What's there: Crystal plants that hum and hold memories.
- **Shrine ring.** Who left it: Forgotten gods. What's there: Hundreds of altars to gods whose names have worn away.
- **The core.** Who left it: No one knows. What's there: The Inner Sun, and [[Groundhog Claus]].

Sketching a mural or copying an inscription gives a Forbidden secret worth 500+ gp back in [[Cinto]].

**Riddles:** each relic layer has a riddle to pass, inspired by Dante's Inferno. Like Dante's Hell, the descent ends in ice, not fire, and gravity flips at the center.

1. **Outer shell.** *Inferno echo:* the gate's warning to abandon hope, and the giants who guard the last well.
   - **Riddle:** "Every pilgrim carries me north. I weigh nothing, yet this gate won't open while you hold me. What must you set down at the door?"
   - **Answer:** hope.
   - **Then:** the gate opens, and a giant's carved face booms with laughter: "Only joking. Keep it, you'll need it."
2. **Drowned halls.** *Inferno echo:* the river Styx, where the wrathful drown in mud.
   - **Riddle:** "Shout at me and you'll sink. Rage at me and you'll drown. Only one thing carries you across. What is it?"
   - **Answer:** calm.
   - **Then:** the dry halls flood with still water, and a stone path rises across it. This foreshadows the ending's hour of calm.
3. **Glass gardens.** *Inferno echo:* Limbo, home of the noble ancients who lived in gentle light with no heaven to wait for.
   - **Riddle:** "We had no heaven, so we became light instead. What grows when you share it, and dies when you hoard it?"
   - **Answer:** knowledge, or a story. A nod to [[Cinto]], where knowledge is currency.
   - **Then:** the crystal plants hum and show each character a memory. This is also where [[Sister Suzanna]]'s flower grows.
4. **Shrine ring.** *Inferno echo:* the circle of heretics, locked in burning tombs for denying the gods.
   - **Riddle:** "Hundreds of us were prayed to, and now no one remembers. What dies when no one says it, and lives again the moment someone does?"
   - **Answer:** a name.
   - **Then:** if the party says a name aloud, even one they make up, a forgotten god stirs and grants one character inspiration.
5. **The core.** *Inferno echo:* the frozen lake at the bottom of Hell, where the worst traitor is trapped in ice, and where Dante climbs down until down becomes up.
   - **Riddle:** "At the bottom of the world, the deepest place is ice. The one trapped here betrayed no one. He only waited. What comes every year to everyone, but never down here?"
   - **Answer:** spring.
   - **Then:** the way to [[Groundhog Claus]] opens. Saying "spring" in front of him later counts as a first step toward freeing him.

**Thin reality:** roll a d6 each hour or at dramatic moments.

- **1.** Effect: **Echo:** everyone hears their own voices from a few seconds ago.
- **2.** Effect: **Other self:** each character briefly sees a version of themselves from another world.
- **3.** Effect: **Gravity tips:** "down" shifts 90°. DC 12 DEX save or fall prone.
- **4.** Effect: **Déjà vu:** the last minute repeats, and one player can redo one roll.
- **5.** Effect: **Spell bleed:** the next spell also triggers a harmless effect, like flowers or bubbles.
- **6.** Effect: **Calm:** nothing happens, which is somehow worse. This foreshadows the ending's hour of calm.

## Odibli's secret (DM-only)

An optional moment just for [[Odibli]], at the nameless altar in the shrine ring.

- **The sign:** as [[Odibli]] gets close, the ink in his fingertips tingles. He can see writing on the altar that no one else can: fresh, wet ink, in his own handwriting. He has never been here.
- **Reading it:** seeing it takes no roll. Understanding all of it takes a DC 15 INT (Arcana) check, or it comes to him in a dream the next time he rests. No one else can read it, even with Comprehend Languages.
- **What it says:** this one is your call, so it fits [[Odibli]]'s story. Some ideas:
  - His ink was [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]]'s first: the Oldest wrote the very first word with it, and a drop of it has come down to him.
  - His true name, written here long before he was born.
  - A message from another [[Odibli]], in another world, who got here first and left him a warning, or a kindness.
- **The catch:** this is the one secret in the campaign that can't be sold. If [[Odibli]] tries to sell it, or writes it down for anyone else, the ink fades from the altar and from his memory.
- **The payoff:** at the end, when the ink is pulled from his fingertips onto the imaginary scroll, it rolls up and is carried off to [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]]. It's his reply.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** copy the murals and inscriptions, or take the relics. Taking them is a crack.
- **Choice (Odibli):** read the nameless altar, and whether to share what it says. It can't be sold.
- **Choice (Halsby):** say their own first name aloud at the shrine ring, or not.

## Rolls

- **Following the grown tunnels.** Roll: WIS (Survival). DC: 14. What happens: Fail: roll on Thin reality.
- **Climbing the giant steps.** Roll: STR (Athletics), or use a rope. DC: 13. What happens: Fail: slide down a step, taking 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Walking the curved horizon.** Roll: CON save. DC: 12. What happens: Fail: Poisoned for 1 minute from dizziness.
- **Catching the drowned murals moving.** Roll: WIS (Perception). DC: 14. What happens: The murals show the party arriving, carved long ago.
- **Copying a mural or inscription (10 minutes).** Roll: INT (History), or DEX with calligrapher's or painter's tools. DC: 12. What happens: Success: a Forbidden secret worth 500+ gp. Fail: it smudges, and is only a Rare secret (100 gp).
- **A nudge on a riddle.** Roll: INT (History, Religion or Investigation). DC: 13. What happens: Riddles are solved by the players, not by rolls. A nudge narrows down the answer. Fail: roll on Thin reality.
- **Picking a [[Glass-Garden Flower|glass-garden flower]].** Roll: INT (Nature) or DEX (Sleight of Hand). DC: 13. What happens: Fail: it shatters. Finding another takes a DC 15 WIS (Perception) check.
- **Remembering a forgotten god's real name.** Roll: INT (Religion). DC: 15. What happens: Saying a real name grants inspiration to two characters instead of one.
- **Gravity tips (Thin reality).** Roll: DEX save. DC: 12. What happens: Fail: Prone.
- **Noticing [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]]'s symbol in every layer.** Roll: INT (History or Arcana). DC: 15. What happens: Every ancient people was chasing the same place: [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]].
- **Looking straight into the Inner Sun.** Roll: CON save. DC: 15. What happens: Fail: Blinded for 1 minute. Either way, a moment of perfect stillness.
- **[[Odibli]] understanding the writing on the nameless altar.** Roll: INT (Arcana). DC: 15. What happens: Fail: it comes to him in a dream at his next rest instead (see [[Odibli]]'s secret).
