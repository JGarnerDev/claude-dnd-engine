Act: [[Act 1 - The Fire That Eats the Way]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

> **What happens here:** the mini-boss fight. At [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]]'s exit, [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] guards [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]] into [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. Beating it, ideally without wrecking the bridge, reopens [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] and resolves every opening hook.

**Read aloud:** *Warm air hits you first, then the smell of woodsmoke, impossible this deep in the ice. The tunnel opens onto a chasm spanned by a single bridge of glassy ice. Beside it, crackling happily, sits a log the size of a wagon, wreathed in flame and wearing what might be a festive ribbon. It pops at you. Somehow, you know it was an insult.*

[[The Yule Log]] lives at [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]]'s exit, burning beside an ice bridge that spans a chasm into [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. It keeps itself going by feeding on carvings and offerings left by lost pilgrims.

- **Fire next to ice:** each time it uses Yule Roll on the bridge, the bridge cracks. After three cracks, a section collapses, so the party must choose between fighting on the bridge or drawing it away.
- **Encounter:** [[The Yule Log|the Log]] and 2 Twig Blights with 4 players (2,950 XP, about Moderate). With 5 players, add 4 Magmins (3,350 XP).
- **Extras:** Marshmallow Moment lets players toast snacks for temporary HP, and Crackling Commentary means everyone understands its rude pops and hisses.

Full stats are in the Holiday Boss Stat Blocks files.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** fight on the bridge or draw the Log away.
- **Choice (party):** hunt down the last ember, or move on.

## Rolls

- **Noticing it feeds on offerings.** Roll: WIS (Perception). DC: 10. What happens: Carvings and offerings can be used as bait.
- **Luring it off the bridge.** Roll: CHA (Deception or Performance), or toss it an offering. DC: 13. What happens: It follows for a round. Insulting it gives Advantage, because it's very rude and very proud.
- **Keeping your feet on a cracked bridge.** Roll: DEX (Acrobatics). DC: 12. What happens: Rolled each time the bridge cracks. Fail: Prone.
- **Jumping clear when a section collapses.** Roll: DEX save. DC: 14. What happens: Fail: fall 20 ft. into the chasm, taking 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage, and climb out with a DC 15 STR (Athletics) check.
- **Dousing Hearth Aura.** Roll: An action, no roll. What happens: A bucket of water, a cauldron-sized snowball, or any Cold damage.
- **Finding the last ember.** Roll: WIS (Perception). DC: 13. What happens: Stamping it out puts [[The Yule Log|the Log]] out for good (see Endings).
- **Toasting a snack.** Roll: No roll. What happens: Marshmallow Moment.
