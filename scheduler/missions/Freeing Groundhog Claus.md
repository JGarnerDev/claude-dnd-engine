Act: [[Act 3 - The Hour of Calm]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

## DM-only

> **What happens here:** the final boss, and the surprise. At the heart of the mountain, the party finds [[Groundhog Claus]] stuck and misunderstood. They can fight, talk or break the loop to free him, or mix all three. Once freed, he gives the world an hour of calm and darts into a fissure in reality.

**Read aloud:** *At the heart of the mountain, under the glare of the little sun, is a burrow piled high with letters, thousands of them, all addressed to "the Claus." Something big shifts among them. A red hat. A white beard. Whiskers. A very large groundhog in a very worn red coat looks up, sees his shadow, and shrieks.* Then comes his monologue, which is on your to-do list.

[[Groundhog Claus]] is misunderstood and stuck, not evil, and the players don't know he's there. He has relived the same day at the core for a thousand years, believing he's guarding the world from spring.

#### How the mountain explains his powers

- **The Inner Sun:** its constant bright light means he always sees his shadow, so winter is always on. Dimming the sun or blocking it with relics weakens him.
- **Same Day Again:** his time loop comes from how thin reality is here.
- **The scratching:** the lead monk has heard him digging for years and mistaken it for the multiverse.
- **The letters:** his chamber is piled with letters addressed to "the Claus" from [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] post office.

**Ways to free him** (mixing them works too):

- **Fight:** reaching 0 HP breaks the loop instead of killing him.
- **Talk:** prove spring is safe, with the letters, a [[Glass-Garden Flower|glass-garden flower]] or the baker's cookies.
- **Break the loop:** dim the Inner Sun so he finally stops seeing his shadow.

**When he's freed,** he gives the whole world an hour of calm, then darts off into a fissure in reality.

**Encounter:** [[Groundhog Claus]] and Mimics disguised as presents, 2 with 5 players or 4 with 6 players (about High). He's CR 9, toughened for the bigger party: AC 17, 150 HP, +9 to hit, Special Delivery DC 16 for 27 (6d8), and Same Day Again brings him back with 60 HP. Full stats are in the Holiday Boss Stat Blocks files.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** fight, talk, or break the loop, or mix them. Beating him by force without ever trying to understand him is a crack.
- **Choice (Halsby):** take his shadow away with shadow magic, three rounds in a row.

## Rolls

- **Realizing he isn't evil.** Roll: WIS (Insight), as an action. DC: 13. What happens: He's scared, not angry. Anyone can try mid-fight.
- **Talking him down.** Roll: CHA (Persuasion): 3 successes before 3 failures. DC: 15. What happens: Each prop (a Claus letter, the [[Glass-Garden Flower|glass-garden flower]], [[Bayta Darell|Bayta]]'s cookies) is an automatic success. Having said "spring" counts as the first. The [[Burrow Hood]] gives Advantage. Intimidation always fails: "SIX MORE WEEKS!"
- **Milk and Cookies.** Roll: His WIS save. DC: 15. What happens: [[Bayta Darell|Bayta]]'s cookies give him Disadvantage on the save.
- **Spotting a Mimic present.** Roll: INT (Investigation). DC: 15. What happens: The Mimic doesn't get surprise, and can be avoided.
- **Finding the child's letter in the pile.** Roll: INT (Investigation). DC: 10. What happens: A ready-made prop for talking him down.
- **Dimming the Inner Sun.** Roll: No roll with [[The Festival of Lights|the Festival of Lights]]; INT (Arcana) with [[Jerichau]]'s [[Shade Candles|shade candles]]. DC: 15. What happens: Switches off Sees Its Shadow. A Darkness spell also works inside its area.

## The burrow at the core

- **The approach:** the deepest ring opens onto a bowl of warm stone under the Inner Sun, so bright that everyone casts a hard, black shadow. A worn path spirals down to the burrow.
- **The letter mounds:** thousands of letters addressed to "the Claus," heaped into hills higher than a person, some yellow with age. They shift and slide, which is Difficult Terrain. *Poke at:* any letter; they're all asking for something, mostly spring. *Hidden:* [[Arkady's Letter|Arkady's letter]] (INT (Investigation) DC 10), and a scorched, unsigned one in handwriting very like [[Dip]]'s.
- **The presents:** wrapped boxes stacked among the letters, with ribbons and tags. Some are Mimics (2 with 5 players, 4 with 6). Spotting one is INT (Investigation) DC 15.
- **The burrow mouth:** a tunnel into the stone, big enough for something Large, with fresh claw marks round the edge and fresh earth heaped outside. The scratching comes from here. This is where [[Groundhog Claus]] bursts out with Special Delivery.
- **Under the Inner Sun:** the open centre of the bowl, with no cover from the light. Anyone fighting here is in Bright Light, so [[Groundhog Claus]] always sees his shadow. Dimming the sun ([[Shade Candles|shade candles]], [[The Festival of Lights|the Festival of Lights]], or [[Halsby]]'s shadow magic) changes the whole fight.
- **The fissure:** a hairline crack in the air at the edge of the bowl that nobody notices until the end. When he's freed, it opens, and he darts through.
