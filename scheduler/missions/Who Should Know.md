## DM-only

> **What happens here:** everything the party did pays off. That means the gifts they earned, the five messages pulled through the fissure and the final choice about who should know about the core. If they made too many mistakes, [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] falls.

**Read aloud, when he's freed:** *And then, everywhere at once, the world goes still. Not silent: calm. In [[Cinto]], the bargaining stops mid-sentence. Out at sea, the waves lie down. Every pilgrim in every tunnel feels it, the way you feel the sun come out. For one hour, nothing in the whole world is afraid.* If [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] falls, skip this and go to The worst ending.

The ending changes depending on what the party has done. Secretly track these choices across the campaign:

- **Secrets.** Where: [[Cinto]]. Example: Did they sell forbidden knowledge or protect it?
- **[[The Yule Log]].** Where: Ice Bridge. Example: Did they put it out for good, or leave an ember burning?
- **The letters.** Where: Buckle post office. Example: Did they bring the Claus letters with them?
- **Gifts.** Where: [[The Buckle]]. Example: Which gifts did they earn, and did they gather all four food-stall gifts?
- **Relics.** Where: [[The Hollow Mountain|The Mountain]]. Example: Did they take relics, copy them or leave them?
- **The finale.** Where: The core. Example: Did they free [[Groundhog Claus]] by force or by understanding?

The choices could change where the hour of calm falls or how far it reaches, what comes through the fissure, what the monks do next, and whether [[Cinto]] remembers the party as heroes or thieves.

**The five messages:** as [[Groundhog Claus]] darts into the fissure, depending on the party's choices, five messages are pulled through the cracks in reality:

- **[[Dip]]:** a paper letter to [[Dip]]'s sibling, held at [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] post office, lifts out of the mailbag and is drawn into the fissure.
- **[[Odibli]]:** ink pulls from his fingertips, scrawls across an imaginary scroll, rolls up and disappears, carried off to [[The Zahareena|the Zahareena]] (see [[Odibli]]'s secret in Part 6).
- **[[Iggy]]:** an unfinished song is pulled from their head and hands.
- **[[Luci]]:** a ray of light is drawn out of them.
- **[[Halsby]]:** their shadow peels away from their feet and slips into the fissure like ink into water. A heartbeat later it's back, facing the other way, and for a moment [[Halsby]] hears a name whispered from the other side. Only [[Halsby]] knows if it was their own (see The rings and the party in Part 6).

## The final decision: who should know about the core

The endings come from the gifts the party earned and one last choice: who should know what lies at the heart of [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]]. In a town where knowledge is currency, this is the most valuable secret in the world.

- **No one.** What they get: Nothing but the memory. What happens: The way to the core seals behind them. The hour of calm becomes a legend no one can explain, and the scratching simply stops.
- **[[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]].** What they get: The monks' lasting trust. What happens: The monks become keepers of the core and open a new, harder inner pilgrimage for the worthy. True to their motto, they keep the door, not the key.
- **[[The Matron]].** What they get: Her favor, for good. What happens: She guards the way below the ice, as she guards everything else. Nobody passes without her say, and [[Mitzi]] glows for a week.
- **Archivist [[Ebling Mis]].** What they get: A fortune: the biggest sale [[Cinto]] has ever seen. What happens: The secret is sold, so it spreads like every other sold secret. Within a season, it's no longer worth anything, and the guild charges admission to the core.
- **All of [[Cinto]].** What they get: Fame, and free drinks at [[The Last Fire|the Last Fire]]. What happens: Pilgrims flood down. [[Cinto]] booms, but so many people at the thinnest place in reality may crack the fissure open again, a hook for a sequel.
- **[[Dip]].** What they get: A letter only [[Dip]] can read. What happens: The brothers Post deliver it, wherever [[Dip]]'s pilgrimage has taken them. What [[Dip]] does with it is a story for later.

**The twist:** selling the secret to [[Ebling Mis|Mis]] and telling all of [[Cinto]] end up in the same place, because sold secrets never stay secret. [[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]] or Cal can hint at this before the party chooses.

## The worst ending: the Buckle falls

If the party messes everything up, [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] is destroyed. Secretly mark a **crack** for each of these:

- They sold forbidden secrets in [[Cinto]].
- They left an ember of [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] burning.
- They earned no gifts from [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]'s NPCs.
- They lost [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s favor.
- They took relics from [[The Hollow Mountain|the Mountain]] instead of copying them.
- They beat [[Groundhog Claus]] by force without ever trying to understand him.

**At 4 or more cracks,** freeing [[Groundhog Claus]] goes wrong. Instead of an hour of calm, he panics, sees his shadow one last time and tears the fissure wide open as he flees.

- **The ice gives way:** the cavern around [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] cracks, and [[The Iron Leviathan|the Iron Leviathan]] groans as it starts to slide back toward the world it came from.
- **The escape:** the party has 10 rounds to get everyone out of [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] and across [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]] before the ship slips through the fissure and the cavern collapses. Each round, a new problem: a trapped NPC, a falling Thunder Tower, a stall on fire.
- **A last mercy:** if the party still has [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s Mercy, she holds back the ice for 3 extra rounds. [[Mitzi]] stays at her side.
- **Aftermath:** [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] is gone, and the ship vanishes back into its own world. Whoever made it out gathers in [[Cinto]], the pilgrimage is changed forever, and the five messages never make it through.

No matter how badly it goes, [[Groundhog Claus]] is still freed. It's the party's mistakes that cost [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], not his.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** who they tell: no one, the Archhierophant, the Matron, [[Ebling Mis]], all of Cinto, or [[Dip]].

## Rolls

- **The final decision.** Roll: No roll. What happens: It's the party's choice alone.
- **Seeing the twist coming.** Roll: WIS (Insight). DC: 13. What happens: They realize that selling the secret and telling everyone end the same way.
- **Escape: freeing a trapped NPC.** Roll: STR (Athletics). DC: 15. What happens: Fail: it takes another round.
- **Escape: a falling Thunder Tower.** Roll: DEX save. DC: 14. What happens: 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage on a failure, or half on a success.
- **Escape: a stall on fire.** Roll: An action with water or snow, or STR (Athletics) to pull someone out. DC: 13. What happens: Fail: 3 (1d6) Fire damage, try again.
- **Escape: herding panicking pilgrims.** Roll: CHA (Persuasion or Intimidation). DC: 14. What happens: Success saves a round.
- **Escape: crossing the cracking ice bridge.** Roll: DEX (Acrobatics). DC: 12. What happens: Fail: Prone, and half Speed that round.
