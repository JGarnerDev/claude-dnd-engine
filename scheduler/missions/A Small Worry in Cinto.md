Act: [[Act 1 - The Fire That Eats the Way]] | Campaign: [[A short quick trip to see some family]]

> **What happens here:** the party arrives in the pilgrim port and learns that knowledge is the local currency. Whoever they talk to first hands them a small worry (the opening hook), and every hook points into [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]]. This is also where they can sell secrets and pick up tools that help free [[Groundhog Claus]] later: the baker's cookies, the candlestick maker's [[Shade Candles|shade candles]] and the gardener's [[Glass-Garden Flower|glass-garden flower]].

**Read aloud:** *The ship's horn sounds three times as [[Cinto]] rises out of the fog: a jumble of rooftops, temple domes and smoking chimneys crammed against a wall of blue ice that climbs higher than the clouds. The docks ring with bells, bargaining and a dozen languages. A banner over the harbor reads WELCOME, PILGRIMS, and underneath, in smaller letters, ALL SECRETS APPRAISED.*

[[Cinto]] is the pilgrim port outside the glaciers, a melting pot of faiths and commerce that never sleeps. Shops, temples, guilds, a port, an infirmary, homes and an inn crowd together, and the town swells during festivals. Even monks under vows of silence struggle to keep quiet in the excitement.

### 2. The reeve from [[Orrend]]

Lord Cael's reeve is riding in from [[Orrend]] for Bane's ledger and the hanging, and arrives the morning after the party leaves, to find the prisoner gone. Jeff's handover asks you to leave Harrow Bane's band and the ledger for Redwater's return, so keep this light:

- **[[Sergei Rubelgerd|Sergei]] and [[Bosco]] aboard:** [[Bosco]] steals from the galley; [[Hober Mallow|Mallow]]'s cook declares war. In [[Cinto]], [[Malingo]] offers fur coats "in dog and raccoon." In [[The Buckle|the Buckle]], [[Stamp]] the puffin and [[Bosco]] hate each other on sight.

## Knowledge is currency

**Knowledge is currency.** It's worth more than gold, though gold still has its place. Players can pay with a secret, and the merchant judges how rare it is:

- **Gossip.** Worth: 1–5 gp. Example: Who's feuding with whom at the inn.
- **Useful.** Worth: 25 gp. Example: A safe route through the outer glacier.
- **Rare.** Worth: 100 gp. Example: Where an old pilgrim tunnel really ends.
- **Forbidden.** Worth: 500+ gp. Example: Something carved where no light reaches.

**The catch:** once a secret is sold, it spreads through [[Cinto]] and soon becomes worthless, so players learn to guard what they know.

- **Festival crowds** push up prices and draw pickpockets who steal notebooks, not purses.
- **Guilds** buy, sell and verify knowledge, like banks for secrets.

## Opening hooks

The adventure starts with whichever [[Cinto]] NPC the party talks to first. Each has a small worry that snowballs. They all have the same cause: [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]] is blocking [[The Ice Bridge|the ice bridge]], so nothing has come out of [[The Buckle|the Buckle]] in weeks. Even the brothers Post, whose motto is that nothing stops the letters, have gone quiet.

- **[[Cal Mooney]] (innkeeper).** Their small worry: "I haven't heard from [[The Matron|the Matron]] in months. She always writes." Where it leads: [[The Buckle]] inn.
- **Archivist [[Ebling Mis]] (guild head).** Their small worry: [[The Buckle]]'s copies of new carvings have stopped arriving. Where it leads: The maze and [[The Buckle|the Buckle]].
- **[[Preem Palver]] (farmer).** Their small worry: His seed order from [[The Buckle Post Office|the post office]] never came. Where it leads: The brothers Post.
- **[[Bayta Darell]] (baker).** Their small worry: Her spices from [[Hari]]'s stall have run out, and no new ones have come. Where it leads: The deck stalls.
- **[[Jerichau]] (candlestick maker).** Their small worry: The monks always order candles before the festival, but nothing has come this year. Where it leads: The cupola temple.
- **[[Sister Suzanna]] (gardener).** Their small worry: [[Aurelian Sellenor|The Archhierophant]]'s yearly blessing for the garden never arrived. Where it leads: The cupola temple.
- **Captain [[Hober Mallow]] (sailor).** Their small worry: Pilgrims she ferried north haven't come back, and she's heard there's a monster blocking the passage. Where it leads: [[The Yule Log]].
- **[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]] (riddle master).** Their small worry: A riddle about "the fire that eats the way." Where it leads: [[The Yule Log]].
- **[[Malingo]] (tailor).** Their small worry: [[Barnaby Post]] owes him for a new coat and hasn't paid. Where it leads: The brothers Post.
- **[[Shadwell]] (thief).** Their small worry: Wants help stealing something from [[The Matron|the Matron]]'s vault, which is a shadier start. Where it leads: [[The Buckle]] inn.

## Word got here first

Pilgrims and sailors carried the party's stories north, and in [[Cinto]] a story is money. Each one has grown in the telling. A story about the party sells as Gossip (1–5 gp); the true version of a famous one is Useful (25 gp). Every time the party corrects one, the next NPC has heard an even wilder version.

#### What [[Cinto]] has heard

- **[[Dip]]:** "Beat a man to death with his own leg." (It was the crossbowman's own leg, and [[Dip]] only beat him with it.) Also: "drove a cart through a bandit camp at full gallop and steered it with their bare hands."
- **[[Halsby]]:** "Turns priests to ash. Lightning out of a clear sky."
- **[[Luci]]:** "Set a brewery on fire by dancing." And the warning that travels with it: don't touch her shoulder.
- **[[Iggy]]:** "Played the viol so well a whole town forgot it was being raided."
- **[[Odibli]]:** "Mails curses. Folded one into a paper airplane." And: "bribed a dead man's horse with oats, and it followed him."

#### How [[Cinto]] reacts

- **[[Nurse Dors]]**, hearing [[Dip]]'s name, puts her knitting down. "[[Dip]]? [[Dip]] T? The one who beat a crossbowman with his own leg?" She starts pulling out extra bandages. "Oh no. We're going to get a rush of incidents with this lot around." Told [[Dip]] isn't with them: "Oh, thank goodness." Then, quieter: "...Is the one with the lightning here?"
- **Doctor [[Susan Calvin]]** has a patient who came through the Waystation and swears he saw a hedge priest turned into an outline of ash. To [[Halsby]], without looking up: "Do you do that on purpose, or is it a condition?"
- **[[Cal Mooney]]** recognises [[Iggy]] at once. "You're the viol player who got Ashford through a bad night! Play us one and the soup's free all week." A Performance at [[The Last Fire|the Last Fire]] earns the party free rooms.
- **Captain [[Hober Mallow]]**'s crew traded stories the whole way north. They step carefully around [[Luci]] and keep her away from the lanterns. [[Hober Mallow|Mallow]]: "I heard what happened to the brewery. My ship is mostly wood."
- **Archivist [[Ebling Mis]]** keeps a slim file with the party's names on it, and it's growing. She'll pay 5 gp for the true version of the cart story: "The one going round has you flying it."
- **[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]]** greets them with a riddle: "What has two legs at the start of a fight and one at the end, and the other is in someone else's hand?" (A crossbowman who met [[Dip]].)
- **[[Shadwell]]** has his eye on one notebook in particular: "The gnome who mails curses? That's a notebook worth reading." He tries for [[Odibli]]'s spellbook first.
- **[[Jerichau]]** won't sell candles to [[Luci]] ("Not to the one who fans fires") until she promises, out loud, to keep them away from wind. Until then, [[Luci]] has disadvantage buying from him.
- **[[Malingo]]** offers [[Luci]] a fireproof cloak "for professional reasons", and measures [[Halsby]] for something lightning won't scorch.
- **[[Bayta Darell]]** has heard about the horse and the oats. She presses a bag of oat cookies on [[Odibli]]: "For the horse. Or for you. No judgement."
- **[[Mendelson Shape]]** is delighted. "Nobody wants gold anymore, but a good story? I'll keep yours in the vault." He opens a deposit box in the party's name and keeps their stories in it, cut from pilgrims' letters.

## Choices

- **Choice (party):** which NPC's worry they take up first. Every hook leads to the Buckle, but each one points at a different NPC there.
- **Choice (party):** sell what they learn, or guard it. Selling a Forbidden secret is a crack toward the worst ending.
- **Choice (party):** hire [[Shadwell]] for the Matron's vault (a shadier start), or turn him down.

## Rolls

- **Noticing an NPC's worry.** Roll: WIS (Insight). DC: 10. What happens: The small worry comes out and the hook opens.
- **Selling a secret for more.** Roll: CHA (Persuasion), or CHA (Deception) to oversell it. DC: 12 Gossip or Useful, 15 Rare, 18 Forbidden. What happens: Success: the merchant pays the next tier up. Fail by 5 or more: they suspect it's already spreading and pay half.
- **Telling if a secret you're buying is real.** Roll: WIS (Insight). DC: 12. What happens: They spot a fake or a secret that's already gone stale.
- **Lying to [[Ebling Mis|Archivist Mis]].** Roll: CHA (Deception) against her passive Insight. DC: 15. What happens: Fail: she marks them as unreliable and pays less from then on.
- **Spotting a pickpocket in the crowd.** Roll: WIS (Perception), or passive Perception. DC: 14. What happens: They catch the thief before the notebook is gone.
- **Catching [[Shadwell]].** Roll: STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) against his DEX (Acrobatics) +6. DC: Contest. What happens: Win: he offers a secret to be let go.
- **Hiring [[Shadwell]].** Roll: CHA (Persuasion). DC: 13. What happens: Or pay him a Useful secret.
- **Buying [[Jerichau]]'s [[Shade Candles|shade candles]].** Roll: CHA (Persuasion), spoken softly. DC: 15. What happens: Or trade a Rare secret. Raising your voice gives Disadvantage.
- **Getting a maze hint from [[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]].** Roll: INT (Investigation) to untangle her answer. DC: 13. What happens: Fail: she still answers, with a harder riddle.
- **Opening an unclaimed deposit box.** Roll: CHA (Persuasion) with [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]], or INT (Investigation) in his ledgers. DC: 13 or 14. What happens: Finds the box with the old map of the first pilgrim tunnels.
- **Picking a deposit box lock.** Roll: DEX (Sleight of Hand) with thieves' tools. DC: 15. What happens: [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]]'s passive Perception is 9, so he won't notice.
- **Helping in [[Cinto Infirmary|the infirmary]].** Roll: WIS (Medicine). DC: 12. What happens: [[Susan Calvin|Doctor Calvin]] gives them a Potion of Healing.
- **Understanding the sleep-talking patients.** Roll: INT (Arcana). DC: 15. What happens: They're speaking scripts from the deep ice, a clue that the veil is thinning.
- **Understanding how fast a blust is.** Roll: INT. DC: 25. What happens: No one ever succeeds. [[Hober Mallow|Captain Mallow]] explains it again, louder.
- **Piecing together the Lay of Sartori.** Roll: INT (History). DC: 15. What happens: Recalls one verse of the old story. Comparing two versions heard in [[Cinto]] needs no roll (see The dam and the wheel).

People and places: see [[Cinto]] and its NPC files. The local units (fongs, sheins, manymeters, blusts) are on [[Cinto]].
