Act: [[Act 1 - The Fire That Eats the Way]] | Campaign: [[It's a cold day to be so lit]]

> **What happens here:** the deep tunnels become a maze. The party works out its rules, follows the brothers Post's stamps, runs the dam and wheel to lower the drawbridge, and deals with random encounters along the way.

**Read aloud:** *The tunnel splits, then splits again. Every passage is the same smooth blue ice, the same low ceiling, the same faint drip somewhere you can't see. Above the first fork, someone carved words long ago: "Only the righteous find the way."*

A tangle of pilgrim tunnels in the deep ice, dug over centuries, stands between [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]] and [[The Buckle|the Buckle]]. It's adapted from the Tomb of the Unknown King maze in Final Fantasy VIII.

#### Rules

- **Turn right:** this always leads to the next room.
- **Turn left:** the passage curves back to the last room the party visited.
- **Go straight:** this only works from the entrance, and leads to the frozen moat.
- **No position markers:** the corridors look the same, so players draw their own map. Chalk marks work.
- **Hint:** "Only the righteous find the way" is carved at the entrance.

**The Post clues:** the brothers Post have marked the route with postal stamps. Right turns carry "FORWARD" stamps, and left turns carry "RETURN TO SENDER." A dropped letter partway through tells players they're on the right track.

#### The route

1. **Entrance:** a safe place to rest.
2. **Fallen Sword:** a dead pilgrim's sword carries the clue for the Dam Room.
3. **Statue Room:** an empty plinth, with scorch marks where something once burned.
4. **Dam Room:** a chain releases frozen meltwater, flooding the corridors with slush.
5. **Wheel Room:** a jammed gear must be cleared to restart the water wheel.
6. **Back to the entrance:** turning right leads outside, which is intended.
7. **The moat:** going straight from the entrance reaches the lowered drawbridge, then [[The Ice Bridge|the Ice Bridge]] and [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]].

The dam and wheel lower the drawbridge, so the straight path only opens after a full lap of [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]].

### The dam and the wheel

The drawbridge over the frozen moat only lowers once meltwater from the dam turns the water wheel. Both machines are far older than [[Deorum Ostium|the Sanctum]], and both are built around the same forgotten story. Each room has a small puzzle, and both can be brute forced.

**[[The Lay of Sartori and the River Daughter]]:** long before the monks came, a people called [[The Rimefolk|the Rimefolk]] dug the first tunnels here. Their master builder, Sartori, wanted to reach the deep ice, so he caught the river Celestine in a dam of ice and stone and used her strength to raise a bridge. Celestine wept, then slept beneath the ice for a hundred years, and at last she sang, and her song cracked the dam. Sartori came down to the water and bowed to her. She forgave him, and turned his wheel ever after. [[The Rimefolk]] are long gone, but the bridge still only lowers for those who remember the story.

#### Where to hear it in [[Cinto]]

- **[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]]** knows the oldest version, and gives it out a line at a time as riddles: "What did the river do before she slept?"
- **[[Ebling Mis|Archivist Mis]]** holds a scrap of the Lay in [[The Knowledge Bank|the knowledge bank]], a Rare secret worth 100 gp. It has the verses in the right order, but the ending has crumbled away.
- **[[Hober Mallow|Captain Mallow]]'s sailors** sing it as a shanty, "The River Daughter," but they sing the verses in the wrong order because it rhymes better. Comparing it with another version sorts it out.
- **[[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]]'s deposit boxes:** one unclaimed box holds a [[The Rimefolk|Rimefolk]] mason's notebook with the ending: "and the builder bowed."

**[[Kissoon|Old Kissoon]]'s riddles:** she tells the Lay one verse at a time, and each verse hides behind a riddle. Her price is a Gossip secret per riddle, or a riddle of their own that she hasn't heard before. She gives names freely ("The river? Celestine, of course, everyone knows that") and hides only what happened. A wrong answer gets a cackle and a second try. A DC 13 INT (Investigation) check earns the hint.

1. **The people.** *"We carved before the carvers and dug before the diggers, and we left nothing behind but the cold. Frost is in our name, and folk is in our blood. Who are we?"*
   - Answer: [[The Rimefolk|the Rimefolk]].
   - Hint: "What do you call frost that clings to a window?"
   - She tells: long before the monks, [[The Rimefolk|the Rimefolk]] dug the first tunnels, and their master builder was Sartori.
2. **What he caught.** *"I run and never walk. I have a mouth and never eat, and a bed I never sleep in, until a builder taught me how. What am I?"*
   - Answer: a river.
   - Hint: "Every bridge is built because of me."
   - She tells: Sartori caught the river Celestine behind a dam of ice and stone, and used her strength to raise his bridge.
3. **The first verse.** *"When the walls closed in, she did what the clouds do and what you do when the onions win. What did she do?"*
   - Answer: she wept. This is the teardrop.
   - Hint: "Salt water, falling."
   - She tells: first, Celestine wept.
4. **The second verse.** *"A hundred years went by like a single night. What do you do every night that she did for a century?"*
   - Answer: she slept. This is the closed eye.
   - Hint: "Close your eyes and you'll have it."
   - She tells: then she slept beneath the ice for a hundred years.
5. **The third verse.** *"What breaks a wall without hands, and lifts a heart without arms?"*
   - Answer: a song. She sang, and this is the open mouth.
   - Hint: "Your bard could tell you, if they'd stop humming."
   - She tells: at last Celestine sang, and her song cracked the dam.
6. **The ending.** *"The builder was proud as a mountain. In the end, he did the one thing a mountain never does. What did he do?"*
   - Answer: he bowed.
   - Hint: "Watch a pilgrim greet a monk."
   - She tells: Sartori came down to the water and bowed to her, and she forgave him and turned his wheel ever after. She only gives this last one if they've answered all the others, and adds: "The bridge remembers. Do you?"

**The sailors' shanty, "The River Daughter":** [[Hober Mallow|Captain Mallow]]'s crew sing it while hauling ropes, and the whole tavern joins in on the chorus. Swapping the order is deliberate: the sailors sing "slept" before "wept" because it sings better, and their version stops before the ending. A DC 13 WIS (Insight) check gets [[Hober Mallow|Mallow]] to admit they swapped two verses "for the tune."

**[[Lemarchand's Notebook|Mendelson's notebook]] (handout):** deposit box 117, unclaimed for longer than [[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]] has worked there. Inside is a water-stained leather notebook wrapped in oilcloth. Most pages are measurements (in fongs, naturally) and sketches of gears, chains and a great wheel. Folded in the back is a hand-drawn map of the first pilgrim tunnels, which is [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]], with the words *"Turn always as the river turns: to the right."* The last written page reads:

> Master Sartori says the bridge must remember what he learned, so we have carved it into the wheel. The builder stands proud, and the river waits. Only one who bows as he bowed can turn it.
>
> I was there. When she sang, the dam broke and we all ran, but he did not run. He came down to the water, and the builder bowed. She forgave him. She turns his wheel still.
>
> I carved her face on the hub myself. I hope I got it right.
>
> *Lemarchand, apprentice mason*

[[Mendelson Shape|Mendelson]], peering over their shoulder: "Oh, how lovely. I always wondered what was in that one. Do keep it, nobody's coming back for it."

**In [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]]:** the Fallen Sword's blade carries three worn [[The Rimefolk|Rimefolk]] marks: a teardrop, a closed eye and an open mouth. The Statue Room's empty plinth has a plaque reading "Sartori, who bowed." Whatever stood on it is gone, leaving only scorch marks.

**The Dam Room:** three chains hang from a frozen sluice gate, each ending in a carved stone weight: a teardrop, a closed eye and an open mouth.

- **Solution:** follow the story. Pull the teardrop (she wept), then the closed eye (she slept), then the open mouth (she sang). The ice hums like a far-off voice, and the sluice opens gently, sending meltwater down a channel toward the Wheel Room.
- **Wrong order:** a burst of icy slush. Everyone in the room makes a DC 13 CON save, taking 7 (2d6) Cold damage on a failure or half on a success, and the chains reset.
- **Brute force:** smash the ice dam (AC 13, 30 HP, Fire deals double damage), or rip all three chains at once with a DC 18 STR (Athletics) check (a helper gives Advantage). Either way the flood is wild: everyone in the room makes a DC 14 STR save or is swept 20 ft. down the corridor and knocked Prone. The rest of [[The Rimefolk Maze|the maze]] becomes Difficult Terrain with slush, and the noise means encounters at the forks now happen on a 1 or 2.
- **Stuck?** DC 13 INT (History) recalls a scrap of the Lay, or DC 13 INT (Investigation) notices the teardrop chain is worn smooth from use.

**The Wheel Room:** the water reaches a great wheel, but it won't turn. Its hub is carved with two figures facing each other: a tall stone builder and a woman made of waves. The builder stands proud and straight, and the hub is locked. A frozen bundle of ancient [[The Rimefolk|Rimefolk]] tools is jammed in the gears.

- **Clearing the jam:** pull the bundle free with a DC 12 STR (Athletics) or DEX (Sleight of Hand) check. Among the tools is [[Sartori's Mallet|Sartori's own mason's mallet]], a relic worth a Rare secret back in [[Cinto]].
- **Solution:** press the builder figure down so he bows to the river woman. A character bowing to her themselves works too. The hub clicks, the wheel starts turning, and far off the drawbridge clanks down.
- **Wrong move:** pushing the river woman down spins the wheel backward. Everyone within 10 ft. makes a DC 12 DEX save or takes 3 (1d6) Cold damage from the spray and is knocked Prone.
- **Brute force:** wrench the wheel round with a DC 16 STR (Athletics) check (a helper gives Advantage), or free the gears with thieves' tools or tinker's tools (DC 14 DEX). Fire melts the jam at once, but destroys the old tools. If the Athletics check fails by 5 or more, a spoke snaps: the drawbridge still lowers, but crooked, and crossing it takes a DC 12 DEX (Acrobatics) check. On a failure, the character slides into the moat, takes 3 (1d6) Cold damage and climbs out with a DC 12 STR (Athletics) check.
- **Stuck?** DC 12 INT (History or Religion) reminds them that in old stories, the proud must bow before they're forgiven.

**Encounters:** at each fork, roll a d6. On a 1, roll on this table. Each link between rooms has 3 forks.

- **1.** Encounter: 4 Skeletons. XP: 200.
- **2.** Encounter: 2 Ghouls. XP: 400.
- **3.** Encounter: 1 Minotaur Skeleton. XP: 450.
- **4.** Encounter: 2 Gargoyles posing as statues. XP: 900.

### Rolls in [[The Rimefolk Maze|the Maze]]

- **Understanding the entrance hint.** Roll: INT (Religion). DC: 12. What happens: "Righteous" is a pun: go right.
- **Keeping track without a map.** Roll: INT (Investigation) or WIS (Survival). DC: 15. What happens: Only if nobody draws a map. Chalk marks need no roll.
- **Noticing the Post stamps.** Roll: Passive Perception. DC: 10. What happens: FORWARD on right turns, RETURN TO SENDER on left.
- **Sneaking past an encounter.** Roll: Group DEX (Stealth). DC: 12. What happens: If half the party succeeds, they slip past.
- **Spotting the Gargoyles posing as statues.** Roll: INT (Investigation). DC: 15. What happens: The Gargoyles don't get surprise.
- **Finding the Fallen Sword's inscription.** Roll: INT (Investigation). DC: 10. What happens: The clue for the Dam Room.
- **Reading the Statue Room's scorch marks.** Roll: INT (Nature). DC: 13. What happens: Something burned here, then got up and walked away: [[The Yule Log|the Yule Log]].
- **The dam and the wheel.** Roll: See The dam and the wheel above.
- **Reading [[The Rimefolk|the Rimefolk]] marks.** Roll: INT (History). DC: 15. What happens: They know the marks belong to [[The Rimefolk|the Rimefolk]], the people from the Lay of Sartori.
