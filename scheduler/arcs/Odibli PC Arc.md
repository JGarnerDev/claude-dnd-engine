# Odibli PC Arc

## Logline

A cheerful gnome who'd rather be in danger than bored, [[Odibli]] thinks he's the one and only Odibli, a scholar on an adventure. He's really a runaway copy: the original is still out there, and he and the people who made him want this one back, or gone. Meanwhile he's slowly learning that his recklessness gets other people hurt. *(DM-only: the middle two sentences.)*

## Growth

- **Learns to count the cost.** He takes the back line on his own and keeps the wonder, but stops spending other people for it.
- **Becomes someone, not a copy.** When he learns what he is, he decides the life he's lived since escaping is his own: the party, the journal, the courage. *(DM-only)*
- **Lives it instead of narrating it.** He stops telling his life as a tale he's already read and lives it first-hand.

## Fall

- **A leap that costs a friend.** His recklessness gets a companion badly hurt or killed, and for the first time the narration doesn't help.
- **Goes back.** [[The Citadel]] finds him and he lets them take him, study him, or shut him down, because he decides a copy has no claim to its own life. *(DM-only)*
- **Fights the original for the name.** He decides there can only be one Odibli, and goes after the original. *(DM-only)*

## Next step

- **Aethryn feels familiar.** A street, a phrasing or a smell he knows without knowing why. Small and cheap, and it plants the copy without saying it. He'll explain it away with a tale under his breath; let him.
- **The back line.** Offer it as a `Choice (Odibli):` when a fight opens: lead the charge as always, or hold back and count. The learning Miguel wants to play, chosen rather than forced. Tests Growth 1 against Fall 1.
- **After these:** pages in his own handwriting he doesn't remember writing, then a leap that finally costs a companion. The original detecting his magic runs on the Citadel Clock (`scheduler/campaign/_citadel-clock.md`), never planned. Hold "he learns he is the copy" for a turn of the campaign.
