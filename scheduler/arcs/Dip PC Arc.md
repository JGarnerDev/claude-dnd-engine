# Dip PC Arc

## Logline

Betrayed by neighbors who called them a friend, [[Dip]] now stays and fights for strangers, the opposite of what was done to them. One hope holds them together: that their twin sister survived the fire. One day they may learn that hope was the only thing keeping them standing. *(DM-only: the last sentence.)*

## Growth

- **A place becomes home.** Ashford and the Lioness stop being a town Dip defends and become Dip's town, which means letting people close enough to betray them.
- **Leads the survivors.** Dip gathers Emberhollow's scattered people and gives them somewhere to stand, carrying on the survival Brannoc's fire started.
- **Keeps going by choice.** If the hope about Vessa fails, Dip keeps going because they decided to, not because a belief holds them up.

## Fall

- **Becomes what burned Emberhollow.** Hunting the raiders turns into cruelty, and Dip starts hurting people who only look like betrayers. Beating a man with his own leg (Session 32) is an early sign.
- **Sees townies everywhere.** Dip decides nobody who stays put is worth saving, and leaves a town that needs them, which is the thing they hate.
- **Breaks when the hope breaks.** Vessa turns out dead, monstrous, or never real, and Dip has nothing left to stand on.

## Next step

- **Stay and fight for a town of strangers.** Ashford is where the party lives now, and Sable's talk about a bigger stake in the Lioness is coming. A town that asks Dip to stay, and maybe doesn't deserve it, tests Growth 1 against Fall 2.
- **The hands, and why Vessa's are missing.** The hearth vision at Session 31 already opened this, and Dip has told no one. A vision or a holy-site moment can follow it.
- **After these:** finishing off the raiders who escaped and gathering Emberhollow's survivors, then the search for Vessa herself. Both are big; they wait until the two above have moved.

## Player wants

See [[Dip]] → Looking Ahead (Player Intent): Ben wants a fight, a fortune and the way back, to be known as "the orc who stays", and to find Vessa, hoping she grew into someone worth knowing and fearing she came back a monster. Growth 1 and the first Next step answer "the orc who stays"; Growth 2 answers "the way back"; Fall 3 holds both of Ben's Vessa outcomes open.

## Someday

- Dip goes back to the Sage Enclave with their chapters complete, and has to decide whether to stay.
- Dip stands in Emberhollow's ashes, which turn out to be in Aethryn, and recognizes home.
- Dip meets Vessa, whatever she turns out to be.
