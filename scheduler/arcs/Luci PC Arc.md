# Luci PC Arc

## Logline

Raised alone in an empty temple by the writing on its walls and a kind voice in her head, [[Luci]] is now loose in a crowded world where she can't sit still. She is sure she exists to save one particular lost soul, and will spend anything to do it, even her friends' trust.

## Growth

- **Learns to stay.** She finds a reason to be still somewhere, people rather than a brighter place, and lets the party be her temple.
- **Saves Aedra without burning everyone.** She frees [[Aedra]] in a way that doesn't spend her friends to do it.
- **Finds her own voice.** She works out which beliefs are hers and which came from the temple walls or from [[Sabathiel]], and chooses for herself.

## Fall

- **Spends her friends.** "No expense spared" comes to mean the party: she lies to them, or leads them somewhere deadly, for Aedra.
- **Becomes the light that burns.** The temple walls feared the light as much as they worshipped it. She becomes what they feared, judging and burning whatever isn't "of the light", [[Halsby]] included.
- **Follows the voice too far.** Sabathiel says "Closer" and she goes, wherever it leads, until she can't tell its wants from her own.

## Next step

- **Was she ever in the temple?** Paul wants this answered in play, not on the sheet. Small clues first (a memory that doesn't fit, a word in the Dead Sun tongue she shouldn't know) before any answer. Tests Growth 3.
- **The lost 113 years.** Something she should remember happened while she drifted, or someone she should know has aged a century. Small and personal; it plants her loneliness before the big Aedra story.
- **Ways in (todo item 81):** Augury before a big choice, in Sabathiel's voice; the hedge priest Hesper Mudd asking which god answers her (Growth 3); Sabathiel's warmth going cold near [[The Shadowless Spear]] (Fall 2, check Paul's and Kellan's appetite for PC friction first).
- **After these:** the pocket temple as a board to hack, then freeing Aedra. Hold the vanished sun worshippers until the group wants them reaching Aethryn.
