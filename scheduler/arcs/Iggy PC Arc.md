# Iggy PC Arc

## Logline

A soldier who lived through the end of his whole world, [[Iggy]] feels he owes it to everyone he lost to remember them, and wants to be the hero who saves people this time. He may learn he helped cause the disaster himself. *(DM-only: the last sentence.)*

## Growth

- **Builds a new faith.** With [[Sah]], Iggy founds what comes after Sahism, partly by looking honestly at where the old religion went wrong: too much certainty, and all meaning locked in one Library. Sah changes alongside him.
- **Makes heroes of the people beside him.** He stands in front, and the names he carries slowly become half from this world.
- **Lets a story end.** To keep a friend alive, he gives up the last of what Threnai left in him and lets the old world go. *(DM-only)*

## Fall

- **Can't stop standing his ground.** He plants his feet when he should run, and gets himself or a friend killed proving he'll never run again.
- **Learns the guilt was earned.** He carried the sealed order that broke Threnai, and decides he's no hero, just a man who helped end a world. *(DM-only)*
- **Goes over to the Correctors.** Grief gets to him: a friend he lost could come back, and he lets them try.

## Next step

- **The new faith.** Sah's coherence grows as Iggy does. Moments where Sah gets something wrong, or where Iggy has to decide what the faith keeps and what it drops, build Growth 1.
- **The grief society.** [[The Correctors]] in Aethryn are the wrong answer the new faith has to argue with: frightened people who want their dead back. Talk first; fight only when something comes back wrong. It tests Growth 1 against Fall 3.
- **Nice to have, never planned:** echo moments, scenes that happen to rhyme with one of the Threnai names. Use one when a scene fits.

Iggy's song and viol hooks stay on his PC file (the viol hook is `seeded`) but this PC arc does not point at them. Stories and wisdom carry Threnai here, not the song (Jeff, 2026-11-11).

## Player wants

See [[Iggy]] → Looking Ahead (Player Intent): a fight worth having and answers, the party as home, never running, and the hero who stands in front and makes the people beside him heroes. Growth 2 is that hero; the grief society is the fight Jeff named; Fall 1 is "never runs" turned into a wound.

## Someday

- Ten years on, people are still telling the story of him and the friends he stood in front of.
- He tells Threnai's last story to someone who will carry it, and lets it become theirs.
- He swears the remembrance vow to Sah out loud.
