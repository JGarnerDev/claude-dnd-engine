# Halsby PC Arc

## Logline

Lied to his whole life by the grandmother who loved him, and slowly wasting away from something in his blood, [[Halsby]] will pay anyone any price for the truth. He will calmly do terrible things if they buy him more time or answers.

## Growth

- **A family he didn't have to give up.** The party becomes the thing he named away in the unlit room, and he lets them count for more than the numbers.
- **A price he won't pay.** His arithmetic reaches a cost, a friend or an innocent, that he refuses, and he learns he has a line after all.
- **The truth, and still free.** He learns what he is without trading away his freedom or his relics, and decides for himself what to do about Shar's claim on him.

## Fall

- **Pays the debt with other people.** [[The Nightveil]] calls in what he owes, and he pays it with other people's lives, calmly, because the numbers say so.
- **Becomes what he was raised for.** He learns he was kept as an offering and steps into the role instead of away from it. *(DM-only: the storing and the name.)*
- **Walks away from the party.** He decides the answers are elsewhere, or that the party will never accept his shadow ways, and slips off. Kellan named this exit himself.

## Next step

- **The fade clock bites.** The rule is already set (the rite holds it off 6 days, 8 after a good night, doubled by [[The Shadowless Spear]]). A long stretch in daylight or open country makes it cost him something small at the table, and it is the lever the order pulls later.
- **The bystander.** The scene Kellan asked for: killing a neutral person is the fastest, safest way through, and Halsby proposes it in the flat ferry tone. Whatever the party says tests Growth 1 against Fall 3.
- **After these:** Luci's faith against his shadow ways (check with Paul first, todo item 81), then the order calling in the ledger. Hold the Sharran priest who fears the name "Rivalen" for the turn of the campaign.
