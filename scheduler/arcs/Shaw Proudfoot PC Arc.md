# Shaw Proudfoot PC Arc

Drafted by Ben (DM) on 2026-11-25 from [[Shaw Proudfoot]]'s file and Jeff's handover, for Ben's run, [[A short quick trip to see some family]]. To confirm with Tyler.

## Logline

A fifty-year-old soldier who sold his fists to a brigand band until the jobs turned on children, [[Shaw Proudfoot|Shaw]] walked into the town he'd helped raid and surrendered. Family is the one thing he says he lives for, and the only family he has left just wrote to him from the edge of the world. Now he has to find out whether he can be something other than muscle.

## Growth

- **Finds his family, and makes more.** He reaches his half-brother Choco La, and somewhere on the way lets the party count as family too: "family, family, family" stops meaning one person.
- **Makes it right, in plain sight.** He pays back what he took, the way a straight shooter would: Ashford's trust, and the Post's robbed sack (see [[The Post]]).
- **The rescuer, not the raider.** He saves someone who was taken, which is the one job he always wanted and never got from the band.

## Fall

- **Muscle again.** Money runs short, a shady job comes along, and "a job's a job" carries him right up to his line, then a step past it.
- **The fight he can't walk away from.** Hot-headed and stubborn, he turns every slight (especially about his height) into a confrontation, until the bridges are gone: blacklisted by the Post, wanted by House Cael, followed by the band.
- **Family at any cost.** Choco La first and everyone else after: he spends the party to save his brother.

## Next step

- **The letter.** Tyler's hook says the letter comes from a prison; in Ben's run Choco La is waiting in the Buckle under a vow of silence instead, a mirror of Shaw's own silence in Mara's cell. The reunion in Part 5 pays it. Seed it in Cinto: Choco La passed through, talked about his little brother, and left a bar tab, a deposit box and a coat (Part 1, "Shaw: Choco La got here first").
- **Someone from the band.** Keep Harrow Bane's band offstage for Redwater, but let one watcher reach the north: Shadwell in Cinto, paid to keep an eye out for the deserter, is the easy fit (Part 1, "Before Cinto", thread 7). Tests Fall 1 if the watcher offers him work.
- **Ways in:** the Post's blacklisting (Growth 2: own up, or keep quiet); the reeve's rider at the dock (Fall 2); the Hobbes rescue at the core of the Mountain, which belongs to Choco La but has room for a man who rescues people (Growth 3).
- **After these:** the other two hooks (Ashford wants him hanged; the kidnapping job someone else took) wait for the party's return south, so they stay with Redwater.
