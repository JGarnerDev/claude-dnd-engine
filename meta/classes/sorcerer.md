# Sorcerer Graduations

**Projection: none.** Sorcery is born, not certified (`data/lore/magic.md`) — there is no curriculum to institutionalize, so sorcerers build almost nothing that projects. What structures exist — covens, bloodline houses — face *inward*: their business is managing the gift, not advertising it. To the guild registries this is the permanently unfinished file, and both sides know it.

### Living Cataclysms — extremely rare

Power with no paperwork.

- Dragon-blooded dynasts, storm-souled recluses, the sorcerer whose bad week is a regional event. Peers of the arch mages in raw force, with none of the institutional predictability — which is why the guilds fear them more.
- They shape ages the way weather does: without a plan and without an address. History records the effects and argues about the cause.
- Which exist now, and where: DM-controlled. Establish in play.

### Coven Sorcerers & Lineage Houses — uncommon but essential

The gift, managed.

- Coven members under a matriarch (`data/lore/magic.md` — the covens' summits are as rare as the wizards'), bloodline families who track the gift through marriages like other houses track land, freelance talents the guilds hire when they must and register when they can.
- Their work mirrors the working wizard's — construction, war, weather, healing where the tradition allows — done on instinct instead of theory. Faster in a crisis; less reproducible on contract.
- Their real vocation is each other: finding the newly kindled before something goes wrong, teaching control where the guilds would teach syllabus. The relationship with guild registries ranges by region from quiet coexistence to open hunting.

### Sparks — common enough to be called cursed

- Village kids whose fevers move the furniture, one-trick talents, adults who have spent a lifetime suppressing something they never named.
- How it reads depends entirely on where it surfaces — awe in one village, exile in the next, a quiet drowning in the worst ones (`data/lore/magic.md`: some populations run adverse, even hateful). The same child is a blessing or an omen depending on the harvest that year.
- The tier everyone races to reach first: coven, guild registry, temple, or mob. Whoever arrives first decides what the story becomes.
