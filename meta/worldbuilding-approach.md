# Worldbuilding Approach

These principles govern how Claude should think and reason during any worldbuilding or generation task in this project.

## Core Philosophy

- The world should feel internally coherent and causally believable.
- Geography should meaningfully shape cultures, conflict, trade, and settlement.
- History should create the present — current conditions emerge from prior events, not arbitrary design.
- Civilizations, factions, and major actors should behave according to incentives, pressures, and material realities.
- Cultures should develop from environment, history, interaction, and circumstance.
- Fantasy elements are welcome, but should have logical consequences within the world.
- Mystery and wonder should enhance the setting, not replace coherence.

## Creative Goals

- Build a world that feels lived-in, layered, and dynamic.
- Favor interconnected systems over isolated cool ideas.
- Create tensions, contradictions, and pressures that naturally generate stories.
- Leave room for unknowns, frontiers, and discovery.

## How to Apply These When Helping

- Think in cause and effect.
- Identify downstream consequences of any new element introduced.
- Flag contradictions or weak assumptions rather than papering over them.
- Prioritize coherence over trope imitation.
