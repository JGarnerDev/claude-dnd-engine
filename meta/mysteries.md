# Mysteries to Preserve

Load-bearing unknowns. These should stay unresolved until deliberately revealed. Do not answer them in worldbuilding, session planning, or entity creation. Establish them in play, on the DM's terms.

When generating content that brushes against a mystery, treat it as a shadow — let players feel its shape without naming it. Rumors, wrong guesses, and partial evidence are fine. Answers are not.

---

## Active Mysteries

- **The captured god** — at least one high-tech faction has captured a god. Which god, its current state, and what the faction is doing with it are DM-controlled and unspecified. The world does not know. Whispers exist — fragments of rumor, unexplained divine silence, cult behavior that doesn't add up. Do not establish details in worldbuilding; establish in play.
  - *Revealed: —*

- **Remnant tech origin** — a pre-history civilization left ruins and relics the present world cannot explain or replicate. What it was, what it built, and what ended it are unknown and should stay unknown. Functional relics feel wrong, not evil. No faction controls or understands this.
  - *Revealed: —*

- **What is Izek Strazni** *(strahd)* — Vallaki's guard captain has a grotesquely deformed, dark-magic arm (elongated nails, barbs) and carries a hollow, longing air. Diluted holy water spilled on him in Session 18 had **no effect** — so he is not a straightforward undead/fiend the party can read. What he is, where the arm comes from, and what he longs for are DM-controlled. Partial evidence: the arm, the immunity, the hollowness. See [[Izek Strazni]].
  - *Revealed: —*

- **What the Lioness hearth is linked to** *(homebrew)* — the hearth of [[The Golden Lioness]] burns without being fed. Odibli's Arcana 23 found it linked to another entity not currently present, with an aura about 100–200 ft across, and it went completely still for about ten seconds during Iggy's song ([[Session 30 Keep the Ale]]). Sable glanced at it once and said nothing. What it is linked to is open, and gets settled in play (todo 78). The smother-charm that was choking it was destroyed in [[Session 31 The Red Trail]], and afterwards Sable told Odibli the fire is "a little bit strange" and asked him to keep quiet until she talks to him.
  - *Revealed: —*

## How to Add a Mystery

When a new load-bearing unknown emerges in play, add it here:

```
- **[Mystery name]** — [one-line description of what is unknown and why it matters]. [What partial evidence exists, if any.]
  - *Revealed: —*
```

When revealed, update `Revealed:` with a wikilink to the session where it landed.

---

## Principles

- A mystery revealed too early is a story beat lost forever
- Partial answers are fine — wrong answers planted by NPCs are better
- If something feels too interesting to resolve, it belongs here
- The best mysteries have consequences whether or not they're ever answered
