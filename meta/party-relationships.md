# Party Relationships

Living record of bonds, tensions, and shared history between PCs. Updated as play reveals and deepens these connections. Not canon history — that lives in `historian/` — this is the DM's read on the relational texture of the group.

Use this when planning scenes that benefit from PC-to-PC friction or warmth, or when a spotlight moment could land harder by engaging an existing relationship thread.

## DM Notes

- Don't manufacture tension — surface what's already there
- Relationship moments land hardest when the player doesn't see them coming
- A PC protecting another PC is more interesting than a PC protecting an NPC; build scenes that make this possible
