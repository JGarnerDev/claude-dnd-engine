## Turn

### Ask in this order

1. **Is an ally at 0 HP?** Lay on Hands (bonus action, touch, no slot) or Healing Word (bonus
   action, 60 ft, a 1st-level slot). Then take your action.
2. **Is a big target in reach?** Attack with Oathcaller, and Smite on the hit.
3. **Is an enemy hitting someone fragile (ODB, Halsby)?** Oathcaller's call on it.
4. **Nothing urgent?** Give Bardic Inspiration to whoever acts next.

### Action

- **Attack:** one melee attack with Oathcaller, a +1 rapier: 1d8 + DEX + 1 slashing on a hit.
  Your best default.
- **Bless (concentration):** 3 allies add 1d4 to attacks and saves. Concentration. The best first-round spell in a
  long fight.
- **Faerie Fire (concentration):** a 20 ft cube; anyone who fails a DEX save glows, and attacks on them get
  advantage. Concentration. It also shows invisible creatures.
- **Command:** one word, WIS save. *Grovel* knocks it prone, and a flyer falls. *Drop* disarms.
  *Flee* sends it away for a turn.
- **Dissonant Whispers:** 3d6 psychic, and on a failed save it must use its reaction to run. It
  runs past your allies, and they get opportunity attacks.
- **Heat Metal (concentration):** against anyone in metal armor or holding a metal weapon. 2d8 fire with no
  attack roll, and the damage repeats as a bonus action each turn. Concentration.
- **Vicious Mockery:** a cantrip, 60 ft. On a failed WIS save it takes 2d6 psychic and has
  disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no slot.
- **Radiant Soul (once per long rest):** costs your action. Wings and 30 ft of flight for 1
  minute, and +5 radiant damage on one hit each turn. Worth it in a long fight or when flying
  solves the problem. In a short fight, attack instead.

### Any creature can (costs your action)

- **Dash:** double your speed this turn.
- **Disengage:** your movement provokes no opportunity attacks this turn. Use it to get back to
  ODB or Halsby through a crowd.
- **Dodge:** attacks on you have disadvantage and you have advantage on DEX saves until your next
  turn. Good when you've called an enemy with Oathcaller and want to soak its hits.
- **Help:** give an ally advantage on their next attack against an enemy within 5 ft of you, or
  on a check you could help with.
- **Hide:** out of sight behind cover, make a DC 15 Stealth check. On a success you're
  Invisible: attacks on you have disadvantage and your attacks have advantage, until you attack,
  make noise or get found. Rarely your job.
- **Influence:** talk a creature into something, out of combat or in it (CHA check).
- **Magic:** cast a spell with a casting time of an action, or use a magic item that needs one.
- **Ready (hold your action):** name a trigger ("when it comes through the door") and what you'll
  do. When the trigger happens, you do it with your reaction. A readied spell needs concentration
  and spends its slot even if the trigger never comes.
- **Search / Study:** a WIS check to spot something, or an INT check to recall or work it out.
- **Utilize:** use an object that takes an action (pull a lever, drink a potion yourself).
- **Grapple or shove:** take the Attack action, but instead of a weapon attack the target makes
  a STR or DEX save (DC 8 + your STR + 3). On a failure it's grappled (speed 0), or you push it
  5 ft or knock it prone.

### Bonus action (only one per turn)

- **Divine Smite:** right after a melee hit. 2d8 radiant with a 1st-level slot, +1d8 per slot
  level above that, +1d8 against undead and fiends. One free cast per long rest (Paladin's Smite).
- **Searing Smite:** right after a hit, 1d6 fire, and the target keeps burning each turn until it
  passes a CON save. It costs the same slot as Divine Smite, so use it only when the fight will go
  long.
- **Bardic Inspiration (4 per long rest, d6):** an ally within 60 ft keeps the die for an hour.
  They add it after failing a d20 roll, or (Valor) add it to damage after a hit, or use their
  reaction to add it to AC when hit.
- **Lay on Hands (10-HP pool):** touch an ally and heal them any number of HP from the pool
  (or spend 5 to cure poison). No slot, and it's not a spell, so it doesn't trigger the one-slot
  rule.
- **Healing Word:** an ally within 60 ft heals 2d4 + CHA HP. A 1st-level slot. It's how you get
  someone up from across the room.
- **Oathcaller's call (once per short rest):** one enemy within 30 ft makes a WIS save. On a
  failure it has disadvantage attacking anyone but you until your next turn, and if it attacks
  someone else anyway, you get a reaction attack.

### Reaction

- **Opportunity attack:** when an enemy you can see leaves your reach, make one melee attack
  against it (Oathcaller, 1d8 + DEX + 1).
- **Oathcaller's strike:** when the enemy you called with Oathcaller attacks someone else, make one
  melee attack against it (Oathcaller, 1d8 + DEX + 1). It's not your turn, so you can't Smite
  (that's a bonus action).
- Your allies' Inspiration reactions use *their* reactions, not yours. Give the die before the
  enemy's turn.

### Movement

- Stand between the enemy and ODB or Halsby. Your AC is 16 and they're the party's lowest.
- Stay within 10 ft of Luci when she lights up (see Combos): you resist radiant damage, so you're
  the one ally who can.
- Move before you attack when an enemy is prone. Melee attacks on a prone enemy have advantage.

### Free (no action)

- **Shazzam (once per long rest):** say the word and a random ally heals 1 HP. The first time
  you say it each day, it fires, meant or not. Make a show of it.
- **Talk:** say a few words or shout a warning or a plan, on your turn or off it.
- **One object:** draw or sheathe a weapon, open a door, pick something up, once on your turn as
  part of moving or acting. A second one costs your action (Utilize).
- **Drop it:** let go of something you're holding.
- **Drop prone:** costs no movement. Standing up costs half your speed.
- **Lucky:** spend a point to give your own d20 roll advantage, or an attack on you
  disadvantage. No action needed, even on an enemy's turn.

## Combos

### On your own

- **Big hit:** Attack, and on the hit Divine Smite with a 2nd-level slot (3d8, 4d8 against undead)
  plus Radiant Soul's +5 if your wings are out. Spend a Lucky point on the attack roll.
- **Wall:** Oathcaller's call (bonus action), then Attack. The enemy hits you or takes your
  reaction strike.
- **First round of a long fight:** Bless (action) and Inspiration on the ally with the biggest
  hits, Shaw or ODB.
- **Pick-up:** Lay on Hands on the downed ally, then Bless or Attack. Use Healing Word only when
  they're out of reach, and then your action has to be an attack or a cantrip.

### With Shaw

- **Topple and smite:** Shaw's Flurry topples the target (Open Hand). Then you attack with
  advantage and Smite.
- **Hold it down:** Shaw grapples a runner or a caster, and you call it with Oathcaller so it
  can't hurt anyone else.
- **Faerie Fire for Shaw:** he makes four attacks with Flurry, and advantage on all of them is
  worth more to him than to anyone.
- **Whispers into Shaw:** Dissonant Whispers makes the enemy run past Shaw, who gets an
  opportunity attack.

### With ODB

- **Hold Person, then smite (concentration):** a paralyzed creature takes a critical hit from any melee hit within
  5 ft. A crit doubles the Smite dice: 2nd-level Divine Smite goes from 3d8 to 6d8. It only works
  on humanoids.
- **Hideous Laughter (concentration):** the target falls prone and can't act. Melee advantage again.
- **Shatter:** it's a 10 ft sphere and it hits allies. Step out of the middle before ODB casts.
- **Guard him:** ODB has the fewest HP in the party. Call the enemy who's closing on him.

### With Halsby

- **Bless and Bane (concentration):** Bless your side, Halsby Banes theirs. Both are concentration, yours and his. Every roll moves by a d4 both ways.
- **Darkness (concentration):** Halsby sees through his own Darkness, and you don't. Stay out of it unless the
  enemy relies on sight more than you do.
- **Pass without Trace (concentration):** +10 Stealth for the party. Open the fight with a surprise hit and Smite.
- **His AC is 12:** he's your best target for a Valor Inspiration die (added to AC as a reaction)
  and for the Oathcaller call.

### With Luci

- **Radiant Consumption:** her light burns everyone within 10 ft, allies too. You both resist
  radiant, so you fight beside her while Shaw, ODB and Halsby keep back.
- **Two healers:** you both have Healing Hands and she has Spare the Dying. Agree who picks up
  whom, so you don't spend two turns on the same downed ally.

## Strategies

### Flyers

- **Team:** Halsby's Gust of Wind (concentration) pushes them back; ODB and Halsby hit them with ranged spells.
  Shaw grapples anything that lands.
- **Iggy:** Command *Grovel*: a flying creature knocked prone falls. Or Faerie Fire so the ranged
  attackers hit, or your heavy crossbow. If it's a long fight, Radiant Soul puts you in the air
  with them.

### Spellcasters

- **Team:** close in fast. Damage breaks their concentration. Shaw's Stunning Strike shuts a
  caster down for a turn. ODB's gnome blood gives him advantage on INT, WIS and CHA saves.
- **Iggy:** Bless and Inspiration both work on failed **saves**, so cast Bless before the caster's
  big spell. Vicious Mockery and Dissonant Whispers attack the caster's weak saves (WIS). The
  Oathcaller call doesn't stop spells, only attacks.

### Tanks (high AC, lots of HP)

- **Team:** use saves, not attack rolls: ODB's Hold Person and Hideous Laughter, your Command.
  Topple it so attacks get advantage, and let everyone dig in.
- **Iggy:** Heat Metal if it wears metal armor: damage every turn, no attack roll. Faerie Fire for
  everyone's advantage. Call it with Oathcaller to keep its hits on your AC 16.

### Swarms and hordes

- **Team:** fight at a door or a choke point. ODB's Shatter and Halsby's Ice Knife or Dragon's
  Breath hit groups. Luci's Radiant Consumption burns everything next to her.
- **Iggy:** hold the choke point next to Luci, where her light can't hurt you. Save your slots:
  attack and use Vicious Mockery, and keep Lay on Hands for the ally who falls.

### Undead and fiends

- **Team:** radiant damage is your weapon here. You and Luci both resist necrotic. Holy water
  hurts both undead and fiends.
- **Iggy:** Divine Smite gets +1d8 against them. Radiant Soul adds radiant damage on top. Charms,
  Hold Person and Hideous Laughter often fail on undead (no mind, not humanoid). Read what it is
  before you spend a slot. The silver shortsword is for creatures that resist anything that isn't
  silver.

### Invisible or hidden enemies

- **Team:** Halsby's Blindsight finds anything within 10 ft. Ready actions on where it will
  appear.
- **Iggy:** Faerie Fire outlines invisible creatures, and their glow cancels their advantage.

### Grapplers and brutes

- **Team:** Shaw counters a grapple best. Keep ODB and Halsby out of its reach.
- **Iggy:** Vicious Mockery and the Sap mastery (longsword) give its next attack disadvantage.
  Spend Lucky to give its big hit disadvantage. Call it with Oathcaller, so it fights you, not the
  casters.

## Build

### Where you are (level 5)

- Bard 3 (Valor) / Paladin 2. Proficiency +3, spell save DC 14, spell attack +6.
- Slots: 4 × 1st, 3 × 2nd. Under 2024 rules the paladin's half levels round **up** for
  multiclass slots: 3 bard + 1 paladin makes you a level-4 caster.
- Jeff's plan (2026-10-13) is to stop at Paladin 2 and keep levelling bard.

### Path A: bard from here (Jeff's plan)

- **Level 6 (Bard 4):** ASI or a feat. Slots 4/3/2.
- **Level 7 (Bard 5):** Font of Inspiration (Inspiration comes back on a short rest), the die
  becomes a d8, 3rd-level bard spells. Slots 4/3/3.
- **Level 8 (Bard 6):** Extra Attack, and Valor lets you swap one of the attacks for a cantrip.
  Slots 4/3/3/1.
- **Level 9 (Bard 7):** Countercharm. Slots 4/3/3/2.
- **Level 10 (Bard 8):** ASI or a feat. Slots 4/3/3/3/1.

### Path B: more paladin

- **Level 6 (Paladin 3):** an Oath and Channel Divinity. Slots 4/3/2.
- **Level 7 (Paladin 4):** ASI or a feat. Slots 4/3/2.
- **Level 8 (Paladin 5):** Extra Attack (the same level as path A), and Find Steed always
  prepared. Slots 4/3/3.
- **Level 9 (Paladin 6):** **Aura of Protection:** you and allies within 10 ft add your CHA
  modifier (+4) to every save. It's the strongest single feature on either path.
- **Path B delays Font of Inspiration:** you keep a long-rest Inspiration pool and d6 dice
  longer.

### The fork, in short

- **Path A** (bard): more Inspiration, more and bigger spells, the better support role.
- **Path B** (paladin): Aura of Protection, more Lay on Hands, an Oath, the better frontliner.
- Both get Extra Attack at level 8. Higher-level slots make Divine Smite bigger on either path.

### Feat picks for the ASI

- **CHA to 20:** spell DC and spell attack +1, a fifth Inspiration use. The safe pick.
- **War Caster:** advantage on concentration saves, so Bless and Heat Metal last in melee. Good for
  how you fight.
- **Resilient (CON):** proficiency on concentration saves. Slower to pay off than War Caster, but
  it lasts.

## Roleplay

### Your tells

- **Name a hero on the spot:** hand an ally a heroic title the moment they earn it ("I think I see
  right here a hero now", as with Shaw).
- **Whisper the names:** say one to three Threnai names under your breath while tuning. If anyone
  asks: "Feels important I say them."
- **Hum into silence:** when a room goes quiet, fill it.
- **Hesitate at revivals:** "Make sure they're… truly back."
- **Fix small things:** straighten a crooked sign or pick up a dropped page without a word.

### Shaw

- **Meet his eyes:** kneel or sit to talk to him. Never joke about his height.
- **Soldier to soldier:** you were city guard, so swap war stories and keep it straight talk.
- **Ask what's north:** ask him what he's walking north for, and listen.

### ODB

- **Tale and song:** he has a tale for every occasion and you have a song. Turn his tale into a
  verse on the spot.
- **Keep the record:** his journal matters to him. Offer to put his deeds into a song, so you both
  keep the record.
- **Be his wall:** he leaps to the front, so step up beside him.
- **Your shadow:** it has moved wrong since his ritual saved you (Session 10). Something only the
  two of you share; bring it up.
- **Truly back?** You brought him up from 0 HP (Session 35). Check on him after.
- **Warren O'Ghibli Buffett:** the running joke still lands.

### Halsby

- **You shook his hand first** (Session 30). You can lean on that.
- **Numbers, not feelings:** he wants figures. Answer him with a number, or with a name.
- **No first name:** he never gives one. You're a man of names, so offer him a title instead.
- **Argue endings:** "every story must end" against "nothing is unthinkable". Good campfire talk.

### Luci

- **The act:** you play while she dances (Sessions 1, 30, 31). Offer it whenever there's a crowd.
- **The Lightbearers!** Take up her name for the party and write it a marching song.
- **Fellow Aasimar:** compare notes on your guides and on being Aasimar.
- **The wings:** she egged you to flash them for Jory. Let her talk you into it once.
- **The ford:** ask her what she saw there.

### Bosco
