## What these are built on

**The character.** She was the sole entity in a light-dimension temple, raised by the writing on its walls and a kind voice in her head, then torn out and dropped into a crowded world where she can't sit still. Liquid gold drips from her eyelids. She wears an elaborate headdress built from depictions on the temple walls. She lost 113 years drifting in the Astral Sea. And the hardest fact on the sheet: she **sacrificed Sacred Flame permanently** to bring two party members back.

**The PC arc.** Growth: learns to stay, finds her own voice apart from the walls and the voice, saves the one she exists to save without spending her friends. Fall: spends her friends, becomes the light that burns, follows the voice too far. Each item leans on one of these:

- **Learning to stay:** [[The Stillwater Wraps]], [[The Rooted Sandals]], [[The Kept Stone]]
- **Her own voice:** [[The Self-Built Headdress]]
- **No expense spared:** [[The Spending Darts]], [[The Ransom Bead]]
- **The light that burns:** [[The Judging Staff]], [[The Unshaded Mantle]]
- **The lost years:** [[The Lost-Day Almanac]]

**The player.** Paul plays high-impact and physical. He threw a party member bodily out of a melee, threw a knife into a stranger's neck in the first session, and killed every shadow in one fight. He gave up a permanent spell rather than let two PCs stay dead, then later sacrificed Luci herself to get the party out. He once ran eighty feet out of a live fight to reach help. It is not recklessness: he will spend anything, including permanent character resources, to change an outcome right now.

## Pick order

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[The Self-Built Headdress]]. It rewards the growth her arc points at, and it suits a quiet handover better than a damage item.

**Biggest at the table:** [[The Spending Darts]], which turn "no expense spared" into a choice every fight.

**Hand over with care:** [[The Unshaded Mantle]] is a temptation item, built on a fall. Give it when the story wants that question asked.

## Caveats

- **Monk assumed.** The wraps, darts, staff and sandals assume her monk features (martial-arts unarmed strikes, Step of the Wind). The sheet settled the build as Monk 2 / Warlock 1 / Transcendent 1, but which class took level 5 isn't recorded.
- **The aura.** [[The Unshaded Mantle]] assumes her Scourge radiant aura spares allies at 10 ft. Check against how Paul runs it.
- One attunement slot on this sheet, on the Headdress. She has room.
