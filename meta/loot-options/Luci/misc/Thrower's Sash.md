# Thrower's Sash

- **Misc (sash)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long silk sash, weighted at both ends.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, as part of her bonus-action flurry, she may move one **willing** creature up to 15 ft in any direction including upward. It lands without falling damage from that movement and is not prone.

## Why this character

Mobility is the monk's identity and hers is already turned outward — she moves other people rather than herself.

## Why this player

He has done this without an item, and it cost his friend 2 damage. The sash removes the cost and turns an instinct into a plan.

## At 6–8

20 ft, and the creature need not be willing.

## Notes

Check the table's appetite first. "Paul repositions anyone 15 ft on a bonus action" is strong at tier 1 and will reshape fights. That is the point, but it should be a decision rather than a surprise.
