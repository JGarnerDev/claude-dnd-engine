# The Lost-Day Almanac

- **Misc (small almanac)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thin almanac with every page dated and every date crossed out in the same hand, except the next one.*

## What it does

She always knows the date and the hour. Once per long rest, she has advantage on a History check about something that happened within the last 150 years.

## Why this character

She lost 113 years drifting in the Astral Sea, years she did not live. An almanac that keeps the date for her, and helps with exactly the stretch she missed, is that loss carried in her pocket.

## Why this player

A quiet Common for a loud player, useful in the moments where Paul asks NPCs questions like 'who is Strahd?'.

## At 6–8

Advantage on any History check about the last 150 years, not once per long rest.
