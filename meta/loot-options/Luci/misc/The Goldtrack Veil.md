# The Goldtrack Veil

- **Misc (veil)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A length of fine cloth worn under the headdress, which catches the gold running from her eyes. After a long rest it is heavy with it.*

## What it does

Once before the next long rest she may press it to an ally's brow to give them 1d4 on one ability check, which empties it.

## Why this character

The dripping gold is on her sheet as pure appearance and has never done anything. This makes it renewable, and makes the party's most alien-looking member the one who hands out blessings by touch.

## Why this player

He plays contact and motion. A reward that requires crossing the room and touching somebody fits how he already moves.

## At 6–8

Holds two charges.
