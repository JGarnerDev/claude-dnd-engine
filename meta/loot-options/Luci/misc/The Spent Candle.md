# The Spent Candle

- **Misc (candle)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A white candle that cannot be lit by any means.*

## What it does

Once per long rest she may hold it and name what she gave up; for one round it burns with a flame that sheds no light, and one creature she can see within 30 ft regains 1d4 HP. She takes 1 psychic damage each time.

## Why this character

Sacred Flame is a strikethrough on her sheet and has been for twenty-three sessions. This does not give it back. It gives the loss a voice that speaks once a day, at a small price.

## Why this player

Paul paid a permanent cost for a temporary outcome and has carried the hole ever since. This is the most direct acknowledgement available that the sacrifice happened and mattered.

## At 6–8

2d4, and the psychic damage stops.
