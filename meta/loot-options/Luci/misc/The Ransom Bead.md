# The Ransom Bead

- **Misc (glass bead on a cord)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A clear glass bead on a cord, with a fleck of gold inside that moves when nothing else does.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 ft of her would drop to 0 hit points, she can use her reaction to take the damage instead. She takes all of it; nothing reduces it.

## Why this character

She gave up Sacred Flame for good to bring two friends back. The bead is that sacrifice made repeatable, and it cuts both ways for her arc: spending herself for the party, not spending the party for her cause.

## Why this player

Paul spends permanent resources to change an outcome on the spot. This gives him a clean, dramatic way to do it again, without costing a spell forever.

## At 6–8

She takes the damage with resistance, and the range extends to 60 ft.
