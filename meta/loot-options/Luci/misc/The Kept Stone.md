# The Kept Stone

- **Misc (plain stone)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain stone, smooth from her hand, from somewhere she slept twice.*

## What it does

When she finishes a long rest in a place she has rested before, she regains one extra Hit Die.

## Why this character

The arc's first growth is learning to stay, and the Someday is a place that feels like home. The stone rewards going back to the same place, which is new for her.

## Why this player

A small reward for a choice the player would not otherwise make, which pairs with the Spending Darts and their cost in Hit Dice.

## At 6–8

Two extra Hit Dice, and a short rest in such a place counts one Hit Die as already spent for free.
