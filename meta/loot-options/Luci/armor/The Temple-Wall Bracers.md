# The Temple-Wall Bracers

- **Armor (bracers)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Flat bracers cut to match a relief she has only ever seen as a rendering — the figures on them are a half-step out of proportion, the way a copy of a copy is.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when she is hit by an attack, she may use her reaction to raise them: the light runs, the gold on her face runs with it, and she gains +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss.

## Why this character

She reconstructed her own headdress from depictions. These are the second thing she built the same way, and they only work when she throws them up in front of her face, which is where the gold already is.

## Why this player

He plays forward and eats the consequences. A reaction that converts one incoming hit per short rest is the shape of defense he will actually use, because it costs nothing until the moment he decides it is worth it.

## At 6–8

+4 AC, usable twice per short rest.

## Note

Pairs with, not against, [[The Scroll-Built Headdress]] — but the Headdress is the attuned pick and these are not, so taking both is cheap on slots and expensive on the act's Uncommon budget. Pick one.
