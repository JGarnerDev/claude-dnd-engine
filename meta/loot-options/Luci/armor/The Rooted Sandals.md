# The Rooted Sandals

- **Armor (sandals)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Plain sandals with soles that leave scorched footprints when she stands her ground.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when something would push her, pull her or knock her prone, it doesn't, and she leaves two scorched footprints where she stood.

## Why this character

The first growth line is learning to stay. Sandals that refuse to be moved, on the one turn she chooses, reverse her usual habit of never staying put.

## Why this player

A small, visible, physical moment: the kind Paul's play is built around.

## At 6–8

Twice per long rest, and allies within 5 ft of her have advantage on saves against being moved.
