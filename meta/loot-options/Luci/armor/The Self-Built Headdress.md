# The Self-Built Headdress

- **Armor (headdress)** · Uncommon · requires attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Her headdress, rebuilt piece by piece from things she chose: no copied depictions, nothing from any wall.*

## What it does

While attuned, she has advantage on saving throws against being charmed. Once per long rest, she can cast Augury without expending a spell slot. The answer comes in her own voice.

## Why this character

She wears a headdress built from depictions on temple walls, and the arc's third growth is working out which beliefs are hers and which came from the walls or the voice in her head. Rebuilding the headdress from her own choices is that growth as an object, and Augury in her own voice is the payoff.

## Why this player

Paul wants the temple question answered in play, not on the sheet. This item doesn't answer it, but it lets Luci act on her own judgment while she looks.

## At 6–8

The advantage extends to saves against being frightened, and the free Augury recharges on a short rest.

## Note

Her one attunement slot on this sheet. Augury is already on her list through her warlock invocation; this is an extra casting, and a different voice.
