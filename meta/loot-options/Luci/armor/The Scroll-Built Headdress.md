# The Scroll-Built Headdress

- **Armor (headdress)** · Uncommon · requires attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Her own headdress, reinforced — thin plates copied from depictions she has only ever seen rendered, fitted into the frame.*

## What it does

While attuned and wearing no armor, her AC increases by 1, and she cannot be **blinded** by light.

## Why this character

The headdress is already on her sheet as something she built from scrolls and temple walls, which means she made it from pictures of a home she can no longer reach. Making it protective is making the reconstruction load-bearing.

## Why this player

He plays forward and takes the hits for it. +1 AC on an unarmored monk is a small number that will matter in every single fight, which is the right shape of reward for someone who never stands at the back.

## At 6–8

+1 becomes +2 while unarmored.
