# The Unshaded Mantle

- **Armor (mantle)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A short white mantle with no lining. Light passes through it as if it weren't there.*

## What it does

When she lights her radiant aura, she can widen it from 10 ft to 20 ft. The widened aura doesn't tell friend from foe: each ally in it makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw at the end of her turn or takes the aura's damage.

## Why this character

Her second fall is the light that burns: becoming what the temple walls feared. The mantle is that offer made concrete. A bigger, brighter light, at a cost to everyone near her. Whether she ever widens it is the choice.

## Why this player

Paul will spend anything to change an outcome right now. This item lets him, and makes the price land on the people around him, which is the question his arc asks.

## At 6–8

The widened aura reaches 30 ft, and the DC rises to 15.

## Note

Check this against how Paul's sheet runs the aura. If it already hurts allies within 10 ft, the cost here is only the reach.
