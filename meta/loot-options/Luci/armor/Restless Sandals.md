# Restless Sandals

- **Armor (sandals)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Temple sandals, worn through at the ball of the foot on both sides from a great deal of turning in place.*

## What it does

While wearing no armor, the first 10 ft she moves each turn ignores difficult terrain, and she takes no damage from the first 10 ft of a fall.

## Why this character

She spins on stools while she listens and is always moving through a space rather than standing in it. Footwear is where that goes.

## Why this player

Paul covers ground — he once ran eighty feet out of a live fight to reach help. Removing the first chunk of terrain tax never changes a plan he had; it just stops the floor from deleting it.

## At 6–8

20 ft of each, and standing up from prone costs her 5 ft instead of half her movement.
