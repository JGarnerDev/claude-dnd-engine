# The Stillwater Wraps

- **Weapon (hand wraps)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Plain grey hand wraps, cool to the touch, that never take a stain.*

## What it does

Her unarmed strikes gain +1 to attack and damage rolls while she wears the wraps. If she has not moved this turn, her unarmed strikes also deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage.

## Why this character

She can't sit still; the arc's first growth is learning to stay, with people rather than a brighter place. Wraps that make the restless one strongest when she plants her feet push against her habit without punishing it.

## Why this player

Paul plays high-impact and physical, and he runs: eighty feet out of a live fight to reach help. An item that pays more for standing still gives him a real trade on every turn instead of a flat bonus.

## At 6–8

The extra damage becomes 1d6, and she counts as not having moved if she moved only with Step of the Wind.
