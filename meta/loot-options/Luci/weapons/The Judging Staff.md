# The Judging Staff

- **Weapon (quarterstaff)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A pale quarterstaff that gives off a faint light when it strikes something, and none at rest.*

## What it does

When she hits a creature with the staff, she can make it shed dim light in a 5-ft radius for 1 minute. While it sheds this light, it can't benefit from being hidden or invisible.

## Why this character

One of her falls is becoming the light that burns, judging whatever isn't of the light. The staff is that, small: whatever she hits is marked and seen. It helps the party, and it is a habit worth watching.

## Why this player

Paul plays aggressive and physical. A Common that turns a hit into a team benefit (nothing she strikes can hide) fits how he already fights.

## At 6–8

The light becomes bright light, and the marked creature has disadvantage on Stealth checks for the duration.
