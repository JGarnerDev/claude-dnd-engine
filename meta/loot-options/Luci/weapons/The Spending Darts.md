# The Spending Darts

- **Weapon (five darts)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Five darts with gold-leaf flights. Thrown hard enough, the leaf catches light.*

## What it does

When she hits with one of these darts, she can spend one of her Hit Dice. Roll it, and the target takes extra radiant damage equal to twice the roll. That dart burns up and returns to the set at the next dawn.

## Why this character

Her arc's logline is a woman who will spend anything to do what she believes she exists for. The darts make that a mechanic: power paid for out of her own reserves.

## Why this player

Paul gave up a permanent spell rather than let two PCs stay dead. A player who will spend anything to change an outcome right now should have something worth spending on. The cost is real, and it is his call when to pay it.

## At 6–8

Spent darts return at the next short rest, and she can spend up to two Hit Dice on one hit.

## Note

She carries three poison darts already; these are a separate set.
