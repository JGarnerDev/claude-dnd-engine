# Moatlight Wraps

- **Weapon (hand wraps)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Linen wraps that hold a faint ring of light at the knuckle, bright at the edge and dark in the middle, like something seen from inside it.*

## What it does

Her unarmed strikes count as **magical**. Once per short rest, when she hits an **unwilling** creature with one, she may move it up to 10 ft in any direction she chooses.

## Why this character

Her own sheet describes her as a moat of light adrift in the dark. A moat is a thing that decides where other things are allowed to stand, and that is the mechanic.

## Why this player

Paul throws people. He has thrown a party member out of a melee at a cost, and [[Thrower's Sash]] covers the willing case — this covers the enemy, which is the half he has never had a tool for.

## At 6–8

15 ft, and the creature is knocked prone if it ends the movement on the ground.

## Note

Assumes a **monk** build (unarmored, martial-arts unarmed strikes). Her class read carries a standing reliability flag — confirm with Paul before spending this, per the character README.
