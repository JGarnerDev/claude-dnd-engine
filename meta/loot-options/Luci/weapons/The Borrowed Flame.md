# The Borrowed Flame

- **Weapon (dagger)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thin dagger with a channel down the blade. Held, the channel fills with light the colour of her tears and holds about as much.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when she hits with it, the target takes an extra 1d6 radiant damage and sheds dim light in a 10 ft radius until the end of her next turn. While lit, it cannot benefit from being invisible or hidden.

## Why this character

She gave up Sacred Flame permanently and it is not coming back. This is not that. It is one borrowed mouthful of the same thing, and the difference should be said out loud when it is handed over.

## Why this player

[[The Spent Candle]] acknowledges the cost; this one gives him something to do with what is left. Lighting a hidden thing up so the party can hit it is exactly the trade he makes on instinct — his resource, somebody else's damage.

## At 6–8

2d6 radiant, and the light also prevents the target from taking the Hide action while it lasts.
