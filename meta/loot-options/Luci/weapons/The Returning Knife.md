# The Returning Knife

- **Weapon (dagger)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain, well-balanced throwing knife with a worn grip.*

## What it does

When she throws it, it returns to her hand at the end of her turn. Thrown attacks with it count as **magical**.

## Why this character

She throws things. It is in the record from the first session and it has never had support on the sheet.

## Why this player

The cleanest playstyle match available. Paul throws knives and throws people; a knife that comes back means he can keep doing the thing he does without ever running an inventory check, and the magical rider means it keeps mattering.

## At 6–8

+1, and it returns immediately rather than at end of turn.
