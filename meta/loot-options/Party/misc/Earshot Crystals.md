# Earshot Crystals

- **Misc (crystal set)** · Common, per crystal · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Thumb-sized crystals, all cut from one stone. Each is cloudy until it is touched, then clears.*

## What it does

A set of crystals cut from the same stone. Each holder carries one.

1. **Speaking takes touch.** While touching your crystal, you can speak telepathically to
   other holders of the same set within **300 feet**. You choose one of two targets:
   - **One holder you can see.** Only they hear it.
   - **"All."** Every creature carrying a crystal from the set within 300 feet hears it.
2. **Listening does not.** A holder only has to carry the crystal, on their body, to hear. To
   answer, they have to touch their own.
3. **Words only.** No images, feelings or senses. The listener hears the speaker's own voice.
4. **Past language.** The words are understood whatever language either side speaks.
5. **Unlimited use.** No charges and no daily limit.
6. **"All" means all.** A message sent to "all" reaches every crystal of the set in range,
   including ones the speaker does not know exist. The speaker gets no sense of who heard.

## Why this party

A party split across two worlds and five temperaments has no shared way to plan in the
middle of a fight. This gives them one without spending anyone's turn or anyone's slot, and
it makes split-party scenes playable, so the DM can pull them apart without leaving anyone
out.

## Why this table

Most of the party's planning already happens out loud at the table, where the enemies can't
hear it. This moves that talk into the fiction, with a cost attached: rule 6 means a careless
"all" can be overheard. An item that rewards whispering and punishes broadcasting gives every
player something to do with it, including the quiet ones.

## At 6–8

Re-cut at a workshop the party has built, the stones become a **closed set**: only the
crystals present at the re-cutting still hear each other, and the range grows to **1,000
feet**.

## Note

- **Strong for a Common.** Unlimited telepathy outclasses most Commons. It stays Common
  because it is shared, short-ranged, words-only, and leaky under rule 6. It does not spend
  the act's Uncommon budget.
- **Not on the per-character menus.** A party item sits outside the nine-per-PC composition
  rule. See the `Party/` note in `meta/loot-options/README.md`.
