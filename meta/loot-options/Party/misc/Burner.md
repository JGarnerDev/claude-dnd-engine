# Burner

- **Misc (thrown consumable)** · Common, per grenade · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

## What it does

See the entity: [[Burner]] (`data/items/magic/Burner.md`). In short, a thrown grenade that
deals **2d6 fire** in a 10 ft burst (DC 12 Dex for half). Creatures that fail keep burning for
**1d4** a turn, and the ground burns for a round. It works once.

## Why this party

The [[Breaker]] is for breaking things. This one is for making a space dangerous to stand in:
it holds a doorway, flushes enemies out of cover, or sets a wagon of supplies alight. Its
damage keeps going after the turn it is thrown, which pays off against enemies who stay to
fight.

## Why this table

It gives the table a real choice: big damage up front, or less damage that lasts and changes
the ground. The burning ground makes the battlefield something the table plays around, and
the flammable-objects clause gives inventive players something to try.

## At 6–8

A stronger **Burner**: **4d6 fire** on the blast, clinging fire rises to **2d4**, and the
burning ground lasts **1 minute**.

## Note

- **Sibling item.** It pairs with the [[Breaker]]. Lower up-front damage (2d6 against 3d6) is
  paid back by clinging fire and burning ground.
- **Consumable, not one-per-PC.** Same bundle rule as the Breaker.
