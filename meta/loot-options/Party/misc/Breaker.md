# Breaker

- **Misc (thrown consumable)** · Common, per grenade · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

## What it does

See the entity: [[Breaker]] (`data/items/magic/Breaker.md`). In short, a thrown grenade that
deals **3d6 bludgeoning** in a 10 ft burst (DC 12 Dex for half), plus an extra **2d6 to
constructs and objects**. It is loud and works once.

## Why this party

It gives anyone in the party a turn that hits a group, without a spell slot or a class
feature. The construct bonus turns it into a tool the party reaches for on purpose, against a
golem, a locked gate, or a cart that needs to stop moving, and not only one more damage
number.

## Why this table

A grenade is simple to run. It has one roll, one save and one rule that matters, so nobody at
the table needs to look anything up. The construct clause rewards the player who notices what
they are fighting, and the noise gives the DM an honest cost when the party is trying to stay
quiet.

## At 6–8

A heavier **Breaker**: **5d6 bludgeoning**, constructs and objects take an extra **4d6**, and
the DC rises to **14**. The thrower can choose a **5-foot** blast for close work.

## Note

- **Sibling item.** It pairs with the [[Burner]]. Hand them out as a mixed bundle so the
  choice between them happens mid-fight.
- **Consumable, not one-per-PC.** Unlike the other `Party/` items, these come as a bundle
  (2–4 is right at this bracket), not one copy per holder.
