# The Collector's Sickle

- **Weapon (sickle)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A small black-handled sickle, the blade worn thin from use. It is always clean.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, he can cut a creature within 5 feet that is willing or incapacitated, friend or stranger. It takes 1d10 necrotic damage per level of the spell slot he chooses (1st or 2nd), and he regains one expended spell slot of that level.

## Why this character

The first fall in his PC arc is paying the debt with other people, calmly, because the numbers say so. The sickle makes that trade available every day and never forces it.

## Why this player

Kellan asked for the scene where a bystander's death is the fastest, safest route. This item lets that question come up in miniature, again and again, with a stated price.

## At 6–8

Slots of up to 3rd level.

## Note

A temptation item, built on a fall. Hand it over when the story wants the question asked. [[The Shadow-Lined Coat]] is its mirror.
