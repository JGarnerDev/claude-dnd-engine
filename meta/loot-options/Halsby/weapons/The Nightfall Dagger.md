# The Nightfall Dagger

- **Weapon (dagger)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A dagger of dull dark metal that takes no shine and throws no glint, at any angle, in any light.*

## What it does

In dim light or darkness he gains +1 to attack rolls with it, and drawing it makes no sound.

## Why this character

A sorcerer who walks into dark rooms by preference should be better in them, and the dark is the one place this character is never at a disadvantage.

## Why this player

He wrote a man who tests a thing a hundred times and writes down the results. A weapon with a stated conditional is a rule to test — expect him to find out exactly where "dim" begins and to ask you for the figure.

## At 6–8

+1 becomes +2 in darkness, and it is magical for overcoming resistance.
