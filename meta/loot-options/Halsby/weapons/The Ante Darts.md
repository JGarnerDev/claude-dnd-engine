# The Ante Darts

- **Weapon (darts)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A set of ten darts with flights painted like Three-Dragon Ante cards. The dragons on the losing cards look annoyed.*

## What it does

Before he throws one, he can call a number from 10 to 20. If the attack roll's d20 meets or beats the call, the dart deals extra damage equal to half the call, rounded down, minus 4 (so a call of 18 adds 5). If it falls short of the call, the dart misses and he takes 1d4 psychic damage.

## Why this character

His moral engine is arithmetic: assess, weigh, take the shortest road. Darts that ask him to price every throw suit a man who wants the numbers before he moves.

## Why this player

Kellan engages with a stated price and is bored by a flat bonus, and Three-Dragon Ante is the one tool proficiency on his sheet. He will work out the expected value at the table, which is the point.

## At 6–8

Calls can go up to 25, using the attack's total instead of the d20.
