# The Tallied Sling

- **Weapon (sling)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A sling and a pouch of nineteen stones, each scored with a number. The numbers are not in order and none of them is repeated.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when he hits a creature with it, he learns that creature's **current hit points as an exact figure**. It adds no damage.

## Why this character

His moral engine is arithmetic and he notices when he is handed a feeling instead of a number. This is the only weapon he would describe as useful, and he would describe it that way without mentioning the stones.

## Why this player

He wrote a character who measures, records, and acts on the result. Exact enemy HP turns a fight into a problem with a known remainder, which is the kind of thing he will do the math on out loud and then make a cold call about.

## At 6–8

Once per round, and the figure includes any temporary hit points stated separately.
