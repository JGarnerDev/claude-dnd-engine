# The Ferryman's Pole

- **Weapon (quarterstaff)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long staff, waterlogged at one end and worn smooth at the other, with shallow notches cut along it at even intervals.*

## What it does

When he hits a creature with it, he may push that creature 5 ft directly away from him. The notches are a measure: anything he lays the staff against, he knows the length of exactly.

## Why this character

He will propose something appalling in the tone of a man suggesting they take the ferry. A pole that moves things a known distance, in a straight line, by a stated amount, is that voice as an object.

## Why this player

Kellan tests stated rules. This one has two clauses, both exact, and neither adds a point of damage — expect him to use the measuring half more than the pushing half and to be right to.

## At 6–8

10 ft, and he may use the push on a creature an ally hits while he is within 5 ft of it.
