# The Kin-Knife

- **Weapon (dagger)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain dagger with a horn grip, scratched with five short notches near the guard. Nobody remembers cutting them.*

## What it does

When an ally within 30 feet of him drops to 0 hit points, the next spell he casts that hits or damages the creature responsible deals an extra 1d8 necrotic damage.

## Why this character

He gave up the word *family* in the unlit room. The first growth line in his PC arc is the party becoming that family anyway, counting for more than the numbers. The knife only answers when one of them falls.

## Why this player

Kellan's Halsby keeps his feelings out of his arithmetic. This gives the table a visible moment when they get in anyway.

## At 6–8

The extra damage becomes 2d8.
