## What these are built on

**The character.** His moral engine is arithmetic. He asks for the numbers and will propose something horrifying in the flat tone of a man suggesting they take the ferry. He never gives a first name, avoids fire and lies badly about why, and every dark-moon night names one thing he is giving up in an unlit room. The dark obeys him cheaply; light and fire cost him. His shadow acts on its own.

**The PC arc.** Growth: a family he didn't have to give up, a price he won't pay, the truth while staying free. Fall: pays the debt with other people, walks away from the party. Each item leans on one of these:

- **A family he didn't have to give up:** [[The Kin-Knife]], [[The Kin Stones]]
- **A price he won't pay:** [[The Shadow-Lined Coat]]
- **Pays the debt with other people:** [[The Collector's Sickle]]
- **Walks away:** [[The Kin Stones]]
- **The arithmetic:** [[The Ante Darts]], [[The Book of Sums]]
- **The dark and the rite:** [[The Dusk Mantle]], [[The Unlit Lamp]]
- **No name left behind:** [[The Nameless Collar]]

**The player.** Kellan DMs the Strahd season, and plays like it: he hands the DM levers, engages with a stated price, tests a stated rule, and is bored by a flat bonus. At the table he tested the fox like a laboratory (Perception, Arcana, a subtle Darkness, a false offer), scouted invisible, broadcast through every earshot crystal at once, and thanked "Mr. Foxy" for his new spear.

## Caveats

- **Two gods, one letter apart.** `Shar` is Halsby's greater death deity, `state: active`. `Sah` is Iggy's diminished knowledge-and-grave deity, `state: diminished`. Do not let an item description blur them.
- **The fade.** [[The Unlit Lamp]] uses only the rule Halsby knows (6 days, 8 after a good night). The Spear's doubling stacks on top. Nothing here says what the fade is.
- **No armor proficiency.** Every armor item is cloth or leather with no base AC.
- **No attunement needed anywhere on this sheet.**
