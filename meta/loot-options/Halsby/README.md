## What these are built on

**The character.** His moral engine is arithmetic. He asks for the numbers — how many guards, how long until the patrol returns, what happens to the village if we don't — and he notices when someone hands him a feeling instead of a figure. He will propose something horrifying in the flat tone of a man suggesting they take the ferry. His conscience works fine; he has concluded that feeling bad about a correct decision is a tax rather than an argument, pays it, and moves on.

Two tells: **he avoids fire and lies badly about why**, and **he never gives a first name**. The second reads to everyone as evasion and is the one place he is being completely honest.

Mechanically the important sentence on his sheet is this one: *he can light a wick, but it costs, out of all proportion, while the dark obeys before he finishes asking.* And every dark-moon night he sits in an unlit room and names one thing aloud that he is giving up. He learned it as manners, performs it before he could read, and keeps doing it because he measured the results.

**The player.** Kellan is the only other person at this table who DMs, and the submission shows it: he wrote his own ideal, bond and flaw, built a DM-only section, flagged his own gaps, and left threads loaded for whoever runs him. He hands a DM levers instead of asking for protection. He will engage with a stated price, he will test a stated rule, and he will be bored by a flat bonus.

## Pick order

**First item:** [[Cold Wick]]. Nearly free, validates the sheet's best sentence, and gives a brand-new player at a table of four veterans an immediate reason to be the one who walks into the dark first — a good first impression for this character to make.

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[The Grey Mantle]]. He is a d6-hit-die caster at level 4 with no armor and no table history; the AC is the responsible pick. [[The Nightfall Dagger]] is the pick instead if he builds for melee cantrips or shows up wanting to be dangerous rather than durable.

**Hold for last:** [[Counting Beads]] — wait until he has asked for a number and not got one, then hand it over. The item lands twice as hard once the table has watched him want it.

## Caveats

- **Race and background are both `TBD`** and flagged as mandatory gaps pending Kellan (dashboard item 57). **Nothing here depends on race, subrace, or background** — deliberate, so none of it needs rewriting when he fills them in. Keep it that way; avoid anything race-keyed until then.
- **He has no table history as this PC.** Every player read above comes from how he wrote the backstory and from the fact that he DMs, not from observed play. That is a thinner evidence base than the other four sheets and should be held more loosely — expect to revise this file after he has actually played two sessions.
- **One attunement slot used, and only on [[The Grey Mantle]].** He has room, but a sorcerer's slots fill fast at tier 2; do not stack another attuned item on him in the same stretch.
- **Two gods, one letter apart.** `Shar` is Halsby's greater death deity, `state: active`. `Sah` is Iggy's diminished knowledge-and-grave deity, `state: diminished`. Both are load-bearing for a PC backstory and they are not the same god. Do not let an item description blur them.
- **Nothing on this sheet adds damage output.** Three add conditional AC or damage reduction, one adds a conditional +1 to hit with a dagger, and the sling trades its damage rider for information. That is deliberate: he joins an existing party as a fifth body, and encounter math tuned for the party as it stood should not be moved by his arrival gift.
