# The Unlit Hood

- **Armor (hood)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A deep hood of coarse cloth that holds its own shadow across the face at any angle, including upward into full sun.*

## What it does

While he is in dim light or darkness, he has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. A creature relying on sight cannot read his expression while the hood is up.

## Why this character

He avoids fire and lies badly about why. A hood that works in the dark and hides his face in the light covers both of his tells with one object and settles neither.

## Why this player

The second clause is the one he will use. He wrote a man who gives no first name and reads as evasive while being honest; a hood that makes the misreading worse is an item that argues with its owner, and he will enjoy that.

## At 6–8

Advantage on Stealth extends to bright light, provided he is not the brightest thing in the room.
