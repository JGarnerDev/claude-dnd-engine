# The Nameless Collar

- **Armor (collar)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A high collar of soft black leather that fastens with a hook. Nobody who has seen it can say what colour the hook was.*

## What it does

Anyone who hears his name forgets it within an hour unless he chooses otherwise. Divination that tries to find him by name fails.

## Why this character

His grandmother taught him never to leave a name behind him in a room, and he gives only *Halsby*. The collar makes the habit into something real.

## Why this player

Kellan built his character around names: three of them, and one he never uses. This gives him a tool that plays into that without answering anything.

## At 6–8

He can also let one listener remember a different name of his choosing.
