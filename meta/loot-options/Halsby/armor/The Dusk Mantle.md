# The Dusk Mantle

- **Armor (cloak)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long grey mantle that looks darker indoors and paler outside, as if it always matches the light it would rather be in.*

## What it does

While he is in dim light or darkness, he gains +1 to AC and to saving throws. While he is in bright light, he has disadvantage on Constitution saving throws to keep concentration.

## Why this character

The dark obeys him before he finishes asking; light and fire cost him out of all proportion. The mantle writes that rule down as a bonus and a price.

## Why this player

Kellan tests stated rules. This one has a clear edge to play around: he will put out lights and cast Darkness to stay on the right side of it.

## At 6–8

The bonus becomes +2.
