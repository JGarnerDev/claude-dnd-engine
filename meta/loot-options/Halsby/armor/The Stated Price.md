# The Stated Price

- **Armor (coat, light)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain dark coat with a row of empty stitch-holes down the inside of the lapel, where something was unpicked rather than lost.*

## What it does

It counts as light armor he is proficient in: **AC 11 + Dex**. Once per long rest, when he takes damage, he may name aloud one thing he is giving up and expend a spell slot of 1st level or higher to reduce that damage by 2d6 per level of the slot spent.

## Why this character

Every dark-moon night he sits in an unlit room and names one thing he is giving up. He learned it as manners, kept it because he measured the results, and this coat is the first time the measurement came back immediately.

## Why this player

He engages with a stated price. This is the only item on his sheet that charges him on the spot, in a currency he cares about, for a benefit he chooses the size of — which is the decision he will want to be making every session.

## At 6–8

3d6 per slot level, and a cantrip may be given up for 1d6 instead of a slot.

## Note

Same slot and the same base AC as [[The Grey Mantle]] — pick one. The Mantle is the responsible pick and needs a slot; this one needs none and spends his spells instead.
