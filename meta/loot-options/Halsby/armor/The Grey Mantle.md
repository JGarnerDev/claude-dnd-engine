# The Grey Mantle

- **Armor (mantle, light)** · Uncommon · requires attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A heavy shoulder-mantle in a grey that is not quite any colour, worn over ordinary clothes.*

## What it does

It counts as light armor he is proficient in: **AC 11 + Dex**, and while he is in dim light or darkness that becomes **AC 12 + Dex**.

## Why this character

Grey is the one colour from his four surviving childhood fragments that he has never seen again in waking life. Describe the mantle's colour and say nothing else about it.

## Why this player

Sorcerers have the worst armor situation in the game and he knows it. An AC that improves in the dark gives him a numerical reason to position where the character already wants to be, which is the kind of alignment between fiction and arithmetic he will appreciate out loud.

## At 6–8

AC 12 + Dex, or 13 + Dex in darkness.
