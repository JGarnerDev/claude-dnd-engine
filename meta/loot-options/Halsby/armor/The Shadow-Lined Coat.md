# The Shadow-Lined Coat

- **Armor (coat)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A dark travelling coat whose lining is a shade darker than the cloth and always seems to move a moment after he does.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 feet of him takes damage, he can use his reaction to let his shadow stretch across to them. He takes the damage instead.

## Why this character

The second growth line in his PC arc is finding a price he won't pay. This coat turns the cost onto himself instead of someone else, the mirror of [[The Collector's Sickle]].

## Why this player

His Living Shadow already acts on its own. Here it acts on his behalf, and Kellan decides when.

## At 6–8

Recharges on a short rest.
