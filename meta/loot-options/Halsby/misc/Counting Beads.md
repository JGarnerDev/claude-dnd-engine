# Counting Beads

- **Misc (bead loop)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A short loop of nine black beads on wire, worn at the wrist, worn smooth.*

## What it does

Once per short rest he may ask for one exact figure about something he can currently observe — how many, how far, how long, how heavy — and get the true number rather than an impression.

## Why this character

*He notices when someone hands him a feeling instead of a figure.* That line is on his sheet. This is the figure.

## Why this player

The strongest player-fit option here, and it costs the game almost nothing. Kellan wrote a character who audits the room; handing him a once-a-rest audit tool means the table's most useful question gets asked out loud instead of guessed at.

## At 6–8

Twice per short rest.
