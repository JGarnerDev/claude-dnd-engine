# The Book of Sums

- **Misc (ledger)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A narrow ledger of ruled pages. Figures written in it stay; words written in it fade by morning.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, he can ask the DM one question with a number for an answer, about something he can see or hear right now: how many guards, how long until the patrol returns, how many casks on the cart. The DM answers truly.

## Why this character

He asks for the numbers and notices when someone hands him a feeling instead of a figure. The book gives him one figure a day that he can trust.

## Why this player

Kellan tested the fox like a laboratory: Perception, Arcana, a subtle Darkness, a false offer. An item that answers a precise question will be used precisely.

## At 6–8

Twice per long rest.
