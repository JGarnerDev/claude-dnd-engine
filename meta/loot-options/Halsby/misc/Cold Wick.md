# Cold Wick

- **Misc (candle)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A stub of black candle on an iron pricket.*

## What it does

It lights instantly for him at no cost and burns with a flame that gives off no light at all — within 10 ft of it, light drops one step dimmer. It gutters out in direct sunlight.

## Why this character

The cheapest possible validation of the sheet's most distinctive sentence. He cannot comfortably make fire; here is a wick he can light.

## Why this player

He wrote the fire-avoidance tell and then wrote that Halsby lies badly about why. This lets him keep running the tell in every dark room without having to explain himself to four strangers, which is how the character would prefer to handle it.

## At 6–8

20 ft, and he may snuff and relight it as a free action.
