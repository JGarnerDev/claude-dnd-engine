# The Unlit Lamp

- **Misc (lamp)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A small iron lamp with a black glass chimney. When it's lit, the room gets darker.*

## What it does

When lit, it sheds dim shadow in a 10-foot radius: bright light inside becomes dim, and dim light becomes darkness. A dark-moon rite he performs within its dark always counts as a good night.

## Why this character

Every dark-moon night he sits in an unlit room and names one thing he is giving up, and he has measured that it holds the fading off for six days, eight after a good night. The lamp makes the room easy to find and the good night certain.

## Why this player

Kellan plays Halsby as a man who keeps the rite because of results. This makes the results better, and gives him a light source that suits a sorcerer who avoids fire.

## At 6–8

The radius becomes 30 feet.
