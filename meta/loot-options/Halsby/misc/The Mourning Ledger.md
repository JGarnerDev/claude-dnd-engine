# The Mourning Ledger

- **Misc (book)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A blank book, unlined, bound in grey cloth.*

## What it does

Each long rest he may write down one thing he is giving up. While that entry stands unstruck he has advantage on saving throws against being **charmed** or **frightened**. He may strike an entry out at any time to take the thing back, which costs him one level of exhaustion.

## Why this character

The rite is already the load-bearing structure of his life and currently has no mechanical existence. This gives it a page and a price without changing a word of what he wrote.

## Why this player

He asks for the numbers. A resource with a stated cost in both directions is exactly the object a DM-brained player picks up and starts working.

## At 6–8

Two entries may stand at once.
