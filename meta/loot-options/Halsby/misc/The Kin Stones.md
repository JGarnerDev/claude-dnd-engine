# The Kin Stones

- **Misc (matched stones)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Five smooth grey stones from the same riverbed, each with a matching chip on one edge.*

## What it does

He can give one stone to each companion. He always knows the direction and rough distance to each stone he gave away, and each holder knows the direction and rough distance to him.

## Why this character

The third fall in his PC arc is walking away from the party. The stones mean that if he slips off, they can find him, and he would know they were looking.

## Why this player

Kellan named that exit himself. This item puts the cost of using it on the table without forbidding it.

## At 6–8

A holder can squeeze their stone to let him feel it, once per long rest each.
