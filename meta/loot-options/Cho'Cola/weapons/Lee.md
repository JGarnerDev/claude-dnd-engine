# Lee

- **Weapon (brass knuckles)** · Rare · attunement · bracket: levels 4–5
- Half of a pair with [[Shaw Proudfoot]]'s **Pham** (`meta/loot-options/Shaw/weapons/Pham.md`). Lee pulls; Pham pushes.

*A slim brass knuckle-duster stamped LEE. Things that are far away keep ending up in its fist.*

Draft from the 2026-11-27 meeting (Jeff, Tyler, Tyson). Agreed: +1, pulling, stronger within 15 ft of Pham. The numbers below are a proposal, balanced against Pham (2026-11-27): each has one free once-per-turn rider, one free utility trick and one 1-Focus big trick. Kept equal on purpose (Jeff, 2026-11-27): Cho'Cola is the stronger character (+7 to hit, AC 14 against Shaw's +5, AC 13), and that gap is closed through Shaw's other loot or Cho'Cola's feat fix, not here.

## What it does

Worn on the fist, Lee makes his Unarmed Strikes the weapon, so Martial Arts, Flurry of Blows and Stunning Strike all work with it.

- **+1.** His Unarmed Strikes gain +1 to attack and damage rolls and count as magical.
- **Reel.** Once per turn, when he takes the Attack action, he can replace one attack with a yank at a Large or smaller creature he can see within 30 ft. It makes a Strength save (DC 8 + proficiency + Dexterity, as Martial Arts sets for a Shove; 14 for Cho'Cola) or is pulled up to 10 ft straight toward him. No Focus. It costs an attack and allows a save, which pays for the range.
- **Snatch.** As a Bonus Action, he can pull an unattended object of 10 lb or less within 30 ft into his free hand.
- **Disarm.** As an action, he can spend 1 Focus Point to pull an object a creature within 30 ft is holding: it makes a Strength save (same DC) or the object flies to his hand (or lands at his feet if he has no free hand).

## Together (within 15 ft of Pham)

While Lee's and Pham's wearers are both attuned and within 15 ft of each other, the brass hums:

- **Reel** pulls up to 20 ft (Pham's Shove gains the same +10 ft).
- **Blood Brothers.** When a creature that Lee pulled ends that move within 5 ft of Pham's wearer, Pham's wearer can use a Reaction to make one Unarmed Strike against it. (Pham's push gives Lee's wearer the same.) The Reaction is the same one Deflect Attacks uses.

## Why this character

Cho'Cola cut purses and burgled shops. A fist that drags keys, purses and people out of the dark to him is a thief's tool and a shadow monk's: pull a guard into his own *Darkness*, where only he can see.

## Why this player

Tyson is back after time away, and Lee hands him a clear move with obvious combos: Reel into Darkness, Reel to his brother, Snatch the key. One save, one Focus spend for the disarm, nothing to track.

## At 6–8

Reel can also pull him toward a Large or bigger creature or a solid surface instead (a grapple-line), and Disarm costs no Focus once per short rest (Pham's Wallbreaker gets the same).
