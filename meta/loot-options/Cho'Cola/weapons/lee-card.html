Lee

Weapon (brass knuckles), rare · requires attunement · Cho'Cola

A slim brass knuckle-duster stamped LEE. Things that are far away keep ending up in its fist, and it hums when its twin, Pham, is near.

Rarity
Rare
Attunement
Required
Bonus
+1 unarmed

  • Brass

    While you wear Lee, your Unarmed Strikes gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls and count as magical. Martial Arts, Flurry of Blows and Stunning Strike all work with it.

  • Reel

    Once per turn, when you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a yank at a Large or smaller creature you can see within 30 ft. It makes a Strength save (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier) or is pulled up to 10 ft straight toward you.

  • Snatch

    As a Bonus Action, pull an unattended object of 10 lb or less within 30 ft into your free hand.

  • Disarm

    As an action, spend 1 Focus Point to pull an object a creature within 30 ft is holding. It makes a Strength save (same DC) or the object flies to your hand, or lands at your feet if your hands are full.

  • Blood Brothers

    Within 15 ft of Pham's wearer, Reel pulls up to 20 ft. When a creature you pull ends that move within 5 ft of Pham's wearer, they can use a Reaction to make one Unarmed Strike against it. Pham's push gives you the same. It uses the same Reaction as Deflect Attacks.

One of a matched pair. Lee pulls; Pham pushes. Draft, 2026-11-27.
