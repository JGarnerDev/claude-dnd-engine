# Pham

- **Weapon (brass knuckles)** · Rare · attunement · bracket: levels 4–5
- Half of a pair with [[Cho'Cola]]'s **Lee** (`meta/loot-options/Cho'Cola/weapons/Lee.md`). Pham pushes; Lee pulls.

*A heavy brass knuckle-duster stamped PHAM. It hits like a door being kicked in.*

Draft from the 2026-11-27 meeting (Jeff, Tyler, Tyson). Agreed: +1, pushing and wall-breaking, stronger within 15 ft of Lee. The numbers below are a proposal, balanced against Lee (2026-11-27): each has one free once-per-turn rider, one free utility trick and one 1-Focus big trick. Kept equal on purpose (Jeff, 2026-11-27): Cho'Cola is the stronger character (+7 to hit, AC 14 against Shaw's +5, AC 13), and that gap is closed through Shaw's other loot or Cho'Cola's feat fix, not here.

## What it does

Worn on the fist, Pham makes his Unarmed Strikes the weapon, so Martial Arts, Flurry of Blows and Stunning Strike all work with it.

- **+1.** His Unarmed Strikes gain +1 to attack and damage rolls and count as magical.
- **Shove.** Once per turn, when he hits a Large or smaller creature with an Unarmed Strike, he can push it up to 10 ft straight away from him. No save. Melee only, and it rides on a hit, so it costs nothing.
- **Breaker.** His Unarmed Strikes deal double damage to objects and structures.
- **Wallbreaker.** As an action, he can spend 1 Focus Point to punch a 5-ft hole through a nonmagical door or wall up to 1 ft of stone or 3 ft of wood. It is loud: anyone within 300 ft hears it.

## Together (within 15 ft of Lee)

While Pham's and Lee's wearers are both attuned and within 15 ft of each other, the brass hums:

- **Shove** pushes up to 20 ft (Lee's Reel gains the same +10 ft).
- **Blood Brothers.** When a creature that Pham pushed ends that move within 5 ft of Lee's wearer, Lee's wearer can use a Reaction to make one Unarmed Strike against it. (Lee's pull gives Pham's wearer the same.) The Reaction is the same one Deflect Attacks uses.

## Why this character

Shaw is a front-door fighter: he doesn't make an entrance, he gets stuck in. A knuckle that punches through walls and shoves men off him is the Statham fist-fight in item form, and the pair ties him to the brother whose letter he carries.

## Why this player

Tyler plays action-movie archetypes. Pushing through a wall into the next room, or bouncing a brigand off a pillar into his brother's fist, gives him the set-piece moves without new bookkeeping: one push per turn, one Focus spend for the wall.

## At 6–8

Shove can push a Huge creature, and Wallbreaker costs no Focus once per short rest.
