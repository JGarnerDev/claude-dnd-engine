Pham

Weapon (brass knuckles), rare · requires attunement · Shaw Proudfoot

A heavy brass knuckle-duster stamped PHAM. It hits like a door being kicked in, and it hums when its twin, Lee, is near.

Rarity
Rare
Attunement
Required
Bonus
+1 unarmed

  • Brass

    While you wear Pham, your Unarmed Strikes gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls and count as magical. Martial Arts, Flurry of Blows and Stunning Strike all work with it.

  • Shove

    Once per turn, when you hit a Large or smaller creature with an Unarmed Strike, you can push it up to 10 ft straight away from you. No save.

  • Breaker

    Your Unarmed Strikes deal double damage to objects and structures.

  • Wallbreaker

    As an action, spend 1 Focus Point to punch a 5-ft hole through a nonmagical door or wall, up to 1 ft of stone or 3 ft of wood. It is loud: anyone within 300 ft hears it.

  • Blood Brothers

    Within 15 ft of Lee's wearer, Shove pushes up to 20 ft. When a creature you push ends that move within 5 ft of Lee's wearer, they can use a Reaction to make one Unarmed Strike against it. Lee's pull gives you the same. It uses the same Reaction as Deflect Attacks.

One of a matched pair. Pham pushes; Lee pulls. Draft, 2026-11-27.
