# The Campfire Tin

- **Misc (tinderbox)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A dented tin tinderbox with a sunrise stamped on the lid. It never runs out of dry tinder.*

## What it does

A fire lit with the tin catches on the first strike, even in the wet. During a short rest beside a fire Dip lit with it, each creature that spends Hit Dice regains an extra 1d4 hit points.

## Why this character

Dip's everyday holy sites are souls gathered around a warm campfire. The tin makes Dip the one who lights it, and gives people a reason to gather.

## Why this player

Ben's backstory holds the line about holy sites not being as common as they used to be. This one he can carry.

## At 6–8

The extra healing becomes 1d6, and each creature that rests by the fire also gains advantage on its next saving throw against being frightened.
