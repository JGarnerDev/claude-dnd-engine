# The Mantra Stone

- **Misc (river stone)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A flat river stone worn smooth on one side by a thumb. Held tightly, it is warmer than it should be.*

## What it does

When Dip uses Relentless Endurance, they also gain 1d8 + their level in temporary hit points.

## Why this character

*Keep going. Keep pushing.* The third growth line in their PC arc is keeping going because Dip chose to. The stone makes not falling down pay a little better.

## Why this player

Dip has been killed or downed in four separate sessions and keeps getting up. Ben likes getting back up; the stone makes it a bigger moment.

## At 6–8

The temporary hit points become 2d8 + Dip's level.
