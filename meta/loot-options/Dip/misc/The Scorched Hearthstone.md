# The Scorched Hearthstone

- **Misc (hearthstone)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A fist-sized chunk of hearthstone, burned black on one face.*

## What it does

Advantage on saving throws against being **frightened** while he is inside a building with people in it he means to keep alive.

## Why this character

He watched a town burn from the inside and lost family in it. A piece of somebody's hearth, carried, is the memory turned into a reason to stand in a doorway.

## Why this player

He steps forward at the dangerous moment. A fear-save bonus conditional on there being people behind him is a licence to keep doing it.

## At 6–8

Immunity to frightened under the same condition.
