# The Empty Reliquary

- **Misc (silver case)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thumb-length silver case, hinged, lined in felt, sized for exactly one small toe. It is empty.*

## What it does

It rattles faintly when carried within 30 ft of a severed part of a living creature's body.

## Why this character

The sage in him will absolutely keep a reliquary. That it holds nothing is the joke and the ache at once.

## Why this player

Ben's own bit handed back to him with a completely straight face. He will carry it for a year and introduce it to every NPC.

## At 6–8

The rattle gains a direction.
