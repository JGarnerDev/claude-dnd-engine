# Quiet-Hours Bead

- **Misc (wooden bead)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A worn wooden bead on a thong.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, after a full minute in genuine silence, he takes advantage on his next Perception or Insight check.

## Why this character

The whispers are not cured and this does not cure them. It gives them a use, which is a better thing for a player to hold than an affliction that only ever costs.

## Why this player

He reads undercurrents in character and writes about them out of character. A bead that pays him for sitting still and listening suits a loud character played by a watchful person.

## At 6–8

The minute drops to a round.
