# The Account Book

- **Misc (book)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thick book bound in plain board. Its pages refuse any ink but Dip's; anyone else's writing fades by morning.*

## What it does

If Ben writes an in-character account of something Dip lived through between sessions, Dip starts the next session with Heroic Inspiration.

## Why this character

Dip trained as a sage and carries calligrapher's supplies and a book. They keep their mouth shut about what they see, so the page is where it comes out.

## Why this player

Ben is the table's prose writer: his account of Dip's death is the longest piece of player writing in the record. This pays him for doing what he already does.

## At 6–8

Once per long rest, Dip can read a page aloud to grant one ally Heroic Inspiration.
