# The Counterweight Toe

- **Weapon (greataxe counterweight)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A lead weight fixed to the butt of the haft, cast — at somebody's suggestion, and he has never said whose — in the shape of a toe.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when he misses with the axe, he may reroll that attack roll.

## Why this character

He took the toe off with this axe. Rebalancing the weapon around the thing it cost him is the correction a man actually makes, and it makes the axe swing truer than it did before.

## Why this player

Ben keeps the toe alive as a joke and does not need permission to. This gives the joke a mechanical home without ruling on it — the toe is still unresolved, there is just now a lead copy of it bolted to the murder weapon.

## At 6–8

Twice per long rest, and the reroll may be used on an ally's attack he is flanking with.
