# Varrick's Cart-Whip

- **Weapon (whip)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Twelve feet of plaited ox-hide wound over a core of lead shot, with a brass cap on the handle
worn flat by one man's grip. The popper at the end has been replaced so often the knot is black.*

## What it does

A whip (finesse, reach), so Dip's whip mastery (Slow) applies. The lead core makes it hit the way
it hit them in Varrick's hand: **1d6 slashing** instead of 1d4.

- **Haul.** Once per short rest, when Dip hits a Large or smaller creature with the whip, they can
  drag it up to 10 ft in a straight line toward them. With Extra Attack, that is whip, drag, axe.
- **Drover's crack.** While holding it, Dip has advantage on Animal Handling checks to drive,
  steer, or calm draft animals. The crack carries about 300 ft.

## Where it is

Already in Dip's hand. They took it off Varrick's body in Session 33 after taking his head. It
reads as plain gear until it is used or looked at closely in Session 34.

## Why this character

Dip has whip mastery and no whip, and the gear list carried that gap since Session 29. The drag
puts an enemy where the battleaxe is, which suits the one who steps into the dangerous spot. And
the party's worst driver, who missed the stream crossing and steered a cart into a shed, now
holds the drover's own whip.

## Why this player

Ben named the whip mastery himself at level 5. A weapon he already chose, made better, rather
than a second item to track. The animal-handling line hands him the running cart joke.

## At 6–8

Haul twice per short rest, and up to 15 ft.
