Tithe-Taker

Weapon (whip), uncommon · no attunement · Dip

You took it off a dead man at the waystation. Asher gave it back with the griffon worked into it: four of Tithe's talons set along the end of the lash, hooked back toward your hand. What it catches, it keeps.

  • Bring it down

    Twice per short rest, when you hit a Large or smaller creature with it, you can drag that creature up to 15 feet in a straight line toward you. It's grappled until the end of your next turn (escape DC 13). A flying creature grappled this way has a speed of 0, so it falls unless it can hover.

  • Drover's crack

    While you hold it, you have advantage on Animal Handling checks to drive, steer or calm draft animals. Its crack carries about 300 feet.

Won at the waystation. Reforged by Asher, and given back as a gift.
