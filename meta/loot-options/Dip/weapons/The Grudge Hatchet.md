# The Grudge Hatchet

- **Weapon (handaxe)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A heavy-bladed hatchet with a handle wrapped in cord that was red once. It sits easier in the hand after someone has drawn blood from it.*

## What it does

+1 to attack and damage rolls. It deals an extra 1d6 damage to any creature that has damaged Dip during this combat.

Once per long rest, when Dip drops a creature to 0 hit points with it, each enemy within 30 feet that can see Dip must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + Dip's proficiency bonus + Strength modifier) or be frightened of Dip until the end of its next turn. Anyone else who saw it is wary of Dip: Dip has disadvantage on Charisma checks with them until they have slept.

## Why this character

The first fall in Dip's PC arc is becoming what burned their home, with hunting turning into cruelty. The hatchet rewards the grudge and the ugly kill, and the people Dip is fighting for flinch from them afterward. It never forces the choice.

## Why this player

Ben plays comedy and consequence in the same character: the man beaten with his own leg was funny at the table and is also a warning sign. This item lets the table feel both.

## At 6–8

The extra damage becomes 1d8.

## Note

A temptation item, built on a fall. Hand it over when the story wants the question asked, and not in the same act as [[The Stayer]].
