# Pilgrim's Wrap

- **Weapon (greataxe wrap)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A strip of oiled leather off a pilgrim's walking staff, wound around the haft.*

## What it does

The axe counts as **magical** for overcoming resistance and immunity. Once per long rest, after rolling damage with it, he may reroll one damage die and keep either result.

## Why this character

The sage training and the pilgrimages currently sit on his sheet as pure flavor with no mechanical surface. This is the surface, and it is on the only object he actually uses.

## Why this player

He fights at the front and rolls big dice. "Your axe hits the things that normally shrug it off" is the most useful sentence you can say to a tier-1 barbarian, and it needs no bookkeeping beyond the reroll.

## At 6–8

+1 weapon, and the reroll comes back on a short rest.
