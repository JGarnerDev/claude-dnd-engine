# The Recited Edge

- **Weapon (greataxe)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A greataxe with a line of text chased down the flat of the blade in a script the enclave used for commentary rather than scripture.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, before he attacks, he recites one line aloud about a god reaching into a mortal life — **the player says the line at the table**. If he hits that turn, the attack deals an extra 2d6 damage.

## Why this character

His sage training is in exactly this literature and currently has no surface on his sheet. The axe does not grant him divine favor; it only pays him for having read the books, which is the correct amount of credit.

## Why this player

Ben wrote the longest piece of prose in the record unprompted. This is the one item in the folder that *requires* him to write a line, and it will be a different line every time.

## At 6–8

3d6, and once per short rest he may recite for an ally's attack instead of his own.
