# The Stayer

- **Weapon (greataxe)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A greataxe with a long iron-shod haft, the kind meant for planting butt-first in the ground. The edge has been ground back so many times it has lost a finger's width.*

## What it does

+1 to attack and damage rolls. Once per rage, when an ally within 5 feet of Dip is hit by an attack, Dip can use their reaction to make one weapon attack against the attacker.

## Why this character

Dip wants to be the orc who stays: townies take and leave, Dip stands and fights. The first growth line in their PC arc is a place becoming home. An axe that pays out when Dip holds their ground beside someone puts that on the dice.

## Why this player

Ben steps forward when something is dangerous. This gives him a reason to step forward next to someone rather than away from everyone.

## At 6–8

Twice per rage.
