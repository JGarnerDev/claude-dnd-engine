# The Handy Strap

- **Weapon (grip-wrap)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long leather strap with a buckle at each end, made to cinch tight around whatever is nearest. It has been around a lot of things.*

## What it does

Dip can spend a bonus action to strap it around an object they are holding. While strapped, that object is an improvised weapon Dip is proficient with. It deals 1d6 damage (1d8 if wielded in two hands) of the type that fits it. If thrown, it has a range of 20/60 feet.

## Why this character

Dip fights with whatever is to hand when the axe isn't enough, and keeps fighting.

## Why this player

Ben's best moments are improvised: a man's own leg as a club, his shoe thrown at Luci, the interpretive dance that took out the furniture. Proficiency with the furniture seems fair.

## At 6–8

Rage damage applies to strapped weapons thrown with Strength, and the damage becomes 1d8 (1d10 two-handed).
