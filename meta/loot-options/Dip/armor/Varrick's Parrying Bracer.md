# Varrick's Parrying Bracer

- **Armor (bracer)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A soldier's forearm guard, leather laced over a steel splint, scored with a hundred turned
blades. The lacing has been redone so many times the holes have worn into slots.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, as a reaction when a creature Dip can see hits them with a melee attack,
they add +2 to their AC against that attack, possibly turning the hit into a miss.

## Where it is

On Varrick Tenn's left forearm. It is the drover's **Parry** on the statblock, so the table sees it
work against them before they own it. Taken off his body, or off him as a prisoner. If the camp
is resolved without a fight (heist or parley), it hangs unlaced on his bedroll by the watch-fire.

## Why this character

AC 13 is the softest front line in the party, and Dip is the one who steps into the dangerous
spot. They learned young to swallow trouble rather than start it, so this is a way to take the
blow on the arm and keep going.

## Why this player

Ben picks the moment to step forward. A reaction that rewards seeing the hit coming fits a
watchful player on a loud character, and it is one line of bookkeeping.

## At 6–8

Twice per short rest.
