# Second Hands

- **Armor (vambraces)** · Uncommon · requires attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Battered leather vambraces. While attuned and raging, a faint second pair of hands shows overlaid on his own, gripping where he grips.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when he would drop to 0 HP, he drops to 1 instead.

## Why this character

His visions are full of hands that guide and push him. This is that, made physical and made useful, and he has earned a catch by body count.

## Why this player

Ben wrote the death experience once already. This hands him the sequel and guarantees he gets to narrate it out loud the first time it fires.

## At 6–8

He may instead spend the catch on an ally within 5 ft.
