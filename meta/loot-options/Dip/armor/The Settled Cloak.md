# The Settled Cloak

- **Armor (cloak)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A heavy wool cloak with a deep hood and plain bone toggles. The lining has a pocket over the heart that stays empty until it's needed.*

## What it does

At the end of a long rest in a settlement, Dip can name it home; the cloak takes a scrap of something from that place into its pocket. Dip can have only one home at a time.

While in that settlement, Dip has advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks and on saving throws against being frightened.

## Why this character

The first growth line in their PC arc is a place becoming home, which means letting people close enough to betray them. The cloak pays off only once Dip has picked a place and said so.

## Why this player

Ben reads rooms well in fiction, and Dip keeps quiet about what they see. Advantage on Insight at home gives that sharp read to the place that matters most.

## At 6–8

Dip can name two homes.
