# The Standard Pauldron

- **Armor (pauldron)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A single steel shoulder-plate, too big for most people, with an empty socket on top where a pole or banner once fitted.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when Dip enters a rage, they can choose up to three allies within 30 feet who can see them. Each gains temporary hit points equal to Dip's level.

## Why this character

The second growth line in their PC arc is leading the survivors and giving people somewhere to stand. The pauldron turns Dip's rage into something the people around them can stand on.

## Why this player

Ben steps into the dangerous spot first. This lets the rest of the table feel it when he does.

## At 6–8

Recharges on a short rest.
