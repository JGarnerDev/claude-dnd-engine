# Shrine Leathers

- **Armor (hide)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Hide armor patched with squares cut from the cloth coverings of holy sites, each patch a different weight, the whole thing smelling faintly of old smoke.*

## What it does

While raging, reduce the first damage he takes each turn by 2. Separately, when he enters a building he knows whether anyone has prayed in it within the last day — not who, and not to what.

## Why this character

He is drawn to the places the faithful gather, and he notices a room's undercurrents without saying so. The armor carries both halves: the places, and the noticing.

## Why this player

Comedy and consequence in one object. The damage reduction is for the front-line barbarian; the prayer-sense is for the man who will walk into a tavern, go quiet, and then say something that unsettles everyone.

## At 6–8

Reduce by 4, and the prayer-sense reaches a week back.
