# Walked-Out Boots

- **Armor (boots)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Heavy boots walked thin on pilgrim roads, resoled four times by four different people. One of them is packed slightly oddly on the left.*

## What it does

While raging, he has advantage on saving throws against being knocked **prone** and against being moved against his will.

## Why this character

He has been downed in four separate sessions and has got up every time. Boots that refuse to let the floor have him are the least metaphorical possible version of that.

## Why this player

Ben steps forward at the dangerous moment, which means he is the one who gets shoved, swept, and dragged. This removes the failure mode that wastes the turn he stepped forward to take.

## At 6–8

He also cannot be knocked prone by an effect that allows no saving throw while raging.
