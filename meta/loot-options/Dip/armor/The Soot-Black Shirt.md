# The Soot-Black Shirt

- **Armor (chain shirt)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A chain shirt blackened all through, as if it hung in a chimney for a year. It smells faintly of smoke, however often it is washed.*

## What it does

A chain shirt (AC 13 + Dex modifier, max 2).

Once per long rest, when Dip takes fire damage, they can use their reaction to halve it. If Dip is not raging, they can also enter a rage as part of that reaction without spending a use of Rage.

## Why this character

Dip survived a fire that took their home. When they landed beside a burning tavern, the memory drove them straight into a rage. The shirt makes that reaction something Dip can use.

## Why this player

It swaps for the chain shirt already on Ben's sheet, so it adds nothing to track beyond one reaction.

## At 6–8

Fire damage is halved whenever Dip is raging, and the reaction recharges on a short rest.
