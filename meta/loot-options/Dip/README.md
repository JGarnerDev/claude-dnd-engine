## What these are built on

**The character.** An orc berserker with a sage's training. Neighbors who called their family friends burned their home; Dip now stays and fights for strangers, the opposite of what was done to them. They read a room sharply and keep quiet about it. Their everyday holy sites are a campfire, a tide, a sunrise. The mantra they think and never say: *keep going, keep pushing.* They have been killed or downed in four sessions and keep getting up.

**The PC arc.** Growth: a place becomes home, leads the survivors, keeps going by choice. Fall: becomes what burned their home. Each item leans on one of these:

- **The orc who stays:** [[The Stayer]], [[The Settled Cloak]]
- **Leads the survivors:** [[The Standard Pauldron]], [[The Campfire Tin]]
- **Keeps going by choice:** [[The Mantra Stone]]
- **Becomes what burned their home:** [[The Grudge Hatchet]]
- **The fire:** [[The Soot-Black Shirt]]
- **The player:** [[The Handy Strap]], [[The Account Book]]

**The player.** The table's prose writer: Ben's first-person account of Dip's death is the longest piece of player writing in the record. He plays comedy without flinching (the interpretive dance, the toe, a man beaten with his own leg) and steps forward when something is dangerous. Comedy and consequence in the same character, with prose attached.

## Pick order

**When:** Dip is away on a pilgrimage for Ben's run, and the Cart-Whip covered Session 34. These wait for Dip's return.

**First Uncommon:** [[The Stayer]]. It rewards the growth their PC arc points at.

**Best Common:** [[The Account Book]], which pays Ben for writing he already does.

**Hand over with care:** [[The Grudge Hatchet]] is a temptation item, built on a fall. Never give it in the same act as the Stayer.
