# Loot Options — Dip (Ben)

Half-Orc Barbarian · Sage background (narrative) · level 4 · `level_confirmed: 28`

**Bracket for every option: levels 4–5 (tier 1 into tier 2).** No option here is tied to a plot, a location, or an NPC.

## The options

- **weapons/** — [[Pilgrim's Wrap]] (Uncommon) · [[The Counterweight Toe]] (Common) · [[The Recited Edge]] (Uncommon)
- **armor/** — [[Second Hands]] (Uncommon, attunement) · [[Walked-Out Boots]] (Common) · [[Shrine Leathers]] (Uncommon)
- **misc/** — [[The Empty Reliquary]] (Common) · [[Quiet-Hours Bead]] (Common) · [[The Scorched Hearthstone]] (Common)

## What these are built on

**The character.** Trained at a sage enclave in planar communion and meditation; drawn to the literature of gods intervening in mortal lives and to the holy sites where the faithful gather. Reads a room's undercurrents sharply and keeps his mouth shut about it, because he learned early that a vocal orc invites trouble. His visions are full of guiding hands. He hears whispers when it is quiet. He lost a toe to his own axe and never got it back. He has been killed or downed in four separate sessions and keeps getting up.

**The player.** The table's prose writer — Ben produced a full first-person account of Dip's death experience, unprompted, the longest piece of player writing in the record. He plays comedy without flinching: the jovial entrance calling a tavern's patrons "less than stellar company," the interpretive dance that rolled a natural 1 and took out the furniture, the toe. And he steps forward when something is dangerous — Dip was the one a villain chose to draw out, and the one who finished her in her own cellar. Comedy and consequence in the same character, with prose attached.

## Pick order

**First item:** [[The Empty Reliquary]]. Free, instant, and it rewards a joke the table already owns.

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[Pilgrim's Wrap]] if the season runs anything with damage resistance; [[Second Hands]] if it does not. Both are good — the Wrap is the safer pick and never needs a slot.

**Hold for last:** [[The Scorched Hearthstone]], which means more once he has somewhere he would rather not see burn.

## Caveats

- Race, class, and background are confirmed, but his **full legal name is still unknown** — only "Dip." Nothing here is inscribed.
- The lost toe has **no confirmed mechanical effect** (the session note says confirm with DM). [[The Empty Reliquary]] deliberately does not resolve that — it is a container for a joke, not a ruling.
- The whispers are **dormant in this world**, not gone. [[Quiet-Hours Bead]] assumes they are still reachable as flavor; it works either way, since the mechanic is just advantage on a check.
- [[Second Hands]] is the only attunement cost on the sheet. He is the PC least likely to want to spend a slot — check with Ben before assuming he wants it.
