# The Spine-Bound Coat

- **Armor (coat, light)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A gnome-cut coat whose back seam is bound like a book's spine, in signatures, so it opens flat and lies in a place it should not fit.*

## What it does

It counts as light armor he is proficient in: **AC 11 + Dex**. Once per short rest, when a creature he can see hits him with a melee attack, he may use his reaction to step 10 ft to an unoccupied space he can see — the coat opens, and he is at a different page.

## Why this character

A bound thing opens at more than one place at once, and he has spent his life inside bound things. The escape is a property of the binding, not of the gnome.

## Why this player

Miguel's highlight reel is movement and angles — a 40-foot jump off ten feet of walking. A reaction that gets him out of melee is the defensive option a wizard who plays sideways will actually remember he has.

## At 6–8

15 ft, and he may trigger it on a ranged attack as well.

## Note

Same slot as [[The Inkproof Overrobe]], and the same AC. Pick one — this is the version for a session where he keeps getting cornered, the Overrobe is the version where he keeps losing paper.
