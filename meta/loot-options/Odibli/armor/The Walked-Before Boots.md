# The Walked-Before Boots

- **Armor (boots)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Soft gnome-sized boots with worn heels and a scuff on the left toe. They fit as if they've always been his.*

## What it does

He has advantage on checks to retrace a route he has travelled. Once per day, inside a building, he can ask the boots for the way out, and they turn toward the shortest one.

## Why this character

His PC arc's next step is that Aethryn feels familiar to him. Boots that feel already walked in play that note as a comfort, with no answer attached to it.

## Why this player

Miguel is the party's suspicious one. Boots that seem to remember being worn by someone else will get questions, and the item gives no answers.

## At 6–8

The once-per-day sense works anywhere in a settlement, not just inside a building.

## Note

DM-only: this plays as "I've walked this before". It never confirms the copy, names the original, or says where the boots came from.
