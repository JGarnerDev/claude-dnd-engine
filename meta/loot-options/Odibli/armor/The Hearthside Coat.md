# The Hearthside Coat

- **Armor (coat)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A heavy coat lined in soot-dark wool that smells of woodsmoke. Small char-marks are stitched into the lining, one for each hearth it remembers.*

## What it does

**Warmth.** He is unaffected by extreme cold.

**Remembering a hearth.** If he spends a short rest beside a lit hearth, the lining gains a stitched mark for it. The coat holds up to three marks; a new one replaces the oldest.

**Sending.** Once per long rest, he can drop an object of 1 pound or less into any open flame he can reach, even a candle or a campfire. It comes out of the ashes of a remembered hearth, unburnt and still warm, at any distance on the same plane. If that hearth is cold, the object stays in the flame and burns.

## Why this character

He checks every fireplace he passes, because gnomes pass notes to each other through fire. When he goes cold or afraid, the quiet comes and his narration goes flat. The coat keeps out the cold half of that and turns his fireplace habit into something he can use.

## Why this player

Miguel plays utility sideways. A small courier through fire is the kind of tool he will find three uses for that nobody planned, and the lit-hearth rule gives the DM a clean way to say no.

## At 6–8

Objects of up to 5 pounds, and it works both ways: anything put into a remembered hearth's fire can turn up in one of the coat's pockets.

## Note

Item magic, not his spellcasting. It casts no spell.
