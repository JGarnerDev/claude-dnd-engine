# The Folio Bracers

- **Armor (bracers)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Padded forearm bracers with a reading-strap on each, built so a standing transcriber can hold his place without a desk.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when he fails a saving throw to maintain **concentration**, he may reroll it.

## Why this character

The bracers exist to keep a man's place in a document while both hands are busy. Holding his place is the entire function, and it does not change when the document is a spell.

## Why this player

He has been downed in four separate fights, which means his good spell has dropped in four separate fights. One guaranteed second chance per day protects the clever thing he already did rather than handing him a new one.

## At 6–8

Twice per long rest, and he adds his Intelligence modifier to the reroll.
