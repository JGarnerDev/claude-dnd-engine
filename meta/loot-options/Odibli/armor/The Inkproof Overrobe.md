# The Inkproof Overrobe

- **Armor (robe, light)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A scholar's overrobe in oiled grey, cut for a gnome, with far more inside pockets than the outside suggests.*

## What it does

It counts as light armor he is proficient in: **AC 11 + Dex**. Anything paper carried inside it is immune to water, ink, mud, and ordinary fire.

## Why this character

He transcribes for a living and owns documents he cannot replace. A robe that protects paper first and the gnome second is the correct priority for this man and he would say so.

## Why this player

Miguel plays a squishy caster who has been downed in four separate fights. A modest AC floor with no attunement and no spell slot is the unglamorous thing that keeps him upright long enough to do the clever thing.

## At 6–8

AC 12 + Dex.
