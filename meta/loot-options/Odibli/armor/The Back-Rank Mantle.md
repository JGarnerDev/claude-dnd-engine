# The Back-Rank Mantle

- **Armor (cloak)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A short grey travelling mantle, heavier at the hem than it looks. It hangs still when he does.*

## What it does

If he didn't move closer to any hostile creature on his turn, he gains +2 to AC and to Dexterity saving throws until the start of his next turn.

## Why this character

Counting the cost again, from the defensive side: the mantle protects the gnome who stays back, and gives nothing to the one who charges in.

## Why this player

Mage Armor is on his sheet, so a flat AC item adds little. A conditional bonus that stacks with it gives Miguel a choice about his footwork every turn.

## At 6–8

Allies within 5 feet of him gain +1 to AC while the bonus is active.
