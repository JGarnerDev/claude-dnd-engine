# The Annotated Staff

- **Weapon (quarterstaff)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A gnome-length quarterstaff covered end to end in transcriber's marginalia in his own hand, most of it corrections to texts he will never see again.*

## What it does

It counts as **magical**. Once per short rest, when he hits a creature with it, that creature has disadvantage on its next saving throw against one of his spells before the end of his next turn.

## Why this character

A wizard whose whole life was annotation, carrying a staff he has annotated. The mechanic is the character's actual job — he marks a thing up, and then the thing is easier to read.

## Why this player

He does not play for damage, he plays for setup. A staff that makes his next spell land is a melee attack that a wizard who hates melee will actually want to make.

## At 6–8

+1, and the disadvantage applies to the next two saves.
