# The Naming Rod

- **Weapon (rod)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain rod of dark wood with a blank brass band at the grip. When he names a spell aloud, the name scratches itself into the brass.*

## What it does

He can use it as an arcane focus. After a long rest, he names one damage cantrip he knows; the name appears on the band. When he casts that cantrip through the rod and shouts its name, he can reroll any 1s on its damage dice once.

## Why this character

His PC arc points at becoming someone: a name, a style, a way of doing things that is his alone. A rod that only works when he says his own name for a spell rewards exactly that.

## Why this player

Miguel names his spells. Firebolt is "Diesel Cougar in the Winter", which he decided and the table now lives with. This makes the habit worth something on the dice.

## At 6–8

He can name two cantrips, and rerolls 1s and 2s.
