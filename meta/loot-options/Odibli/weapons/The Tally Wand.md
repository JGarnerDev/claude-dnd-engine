# The Tally Wand

- **Weapon (wand)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A wand of pale ash notched along its length like a tally stick. Most of the notches are old; there is room for more.*

## What it does

He can use it as an arcane focus. If no hostile creature is within 30 feet of him at the start of his turn, he gains +1 to spell attack rolls until the end of that turn, and his cantrips' range increases by 30 feet.

## Why this character

His PC arc's first growth line is learning to count the cost: to stand at the back, keep the wonder, and let the leap go. A wand that works best when he has kept his distance pays him for that without forbidding the leap.

## Why this player

Miguel already finds sideways answers from range: Mage Hand down a well, Minor Illusion against a specter. This gives him a reason to look for the far spot on purpose, and a small, readable bonus for finding it.

## At 6–8

The bonus becomes +2.
