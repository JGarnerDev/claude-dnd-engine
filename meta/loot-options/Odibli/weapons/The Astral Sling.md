# The Astral Sling

- **Weapon (sling)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain leather sling. A stone thrown from it is gone for about half its flight and is somewhere else for that half.*

## What it does

Attacks with it count as **magical** and ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.

## Why this character

His academic focus is the Astral Plane and nothing on his sheet lets him touch it. A stone that takes the short way through is the smallest honest application of a career's worth of study.

## Why this player

He is the player who got a key out of the bottom of a well with Mage Hand. Give him a delivery method that ignores walls and he will spend a session finding out exactly how much of a wall counts as cover.

## At 6–8

Also ignores total cover, provided he knows where the target is.
