# The Headlong Wand

- **Weapon (wand)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A short, blunt wand wrapped in red cord, warm at the striking end. It feels like it wants to be pointed at something close.*

## What it does

He can use it as an arcane focus. He gains +2 to spell attack rolls against creatures within 10 feet of him.

Once per long rest, when he would drop to 0 hit points, he drops to 1 instead. The nearest ally within 30 feet takes the damage he didn't.

## Why this character

His PC arc's first fall is a leap that costs a friend. This wand rewards the leap and then, when it goes wrong, hands the bill to someone standing near him. It never forces the choice; it only makes the reckless line pay better.

## Why this player

Miguel pays costs: the orb was his call. Give him an item whose cost lands on someone else and the table gets to watch whether he keeps using it.

## At 6–8

The 0-hit-point clause recharges on a short rest.

## Note

A temptation item, built on a fall. Hand it over when the story wants the question asked, and not in the same act as [[The Tally Wand]].
