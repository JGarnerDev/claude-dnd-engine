# The Red-Ink Stylus

- **Weapon (dagger)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A short stylus-bladed knife, the kind kept on a transcriber's desk for scraping an error off vellum. The grip is stained red at the thumb.*

## What it does

When he hits a creature with it, he marks it until the end of his next turn. The next attack roll any creature makes against the marked target has +1.

## Why this character

His job was marking other people's mistakes in red so the next reader could see them. The knife does the same job to a person: it does not hurt much, it just makes the error legible.

## Why this player

Miguel does not play for damage, he plays setup — Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, a thrown potion. A melee attack whose whole output is a bonus for somebody else is a turn he will actually be happy to spend.

## At 6–8

+2, and the mark lasts until the end of his *next* turn after that.
