## What these are built on

**The character.** Bookish, excited, a bucket-list adventurer who would rather be in danger than bored. His journal is his spellbook, and he logs his life as he lives it. He checks every fireplace he passes, because gnomes pass notes to each other through fire. When he is cold or afraid, the quiet comes and his narration goes flat. DM-only: he is a copy made at [[The Citadel]] and doesn't know it. Desk-and-scribe items fit only as echoes of the original's life, never as his own past, and no item here confirms the copy.

**The PC arc.** Growth: learns to count the cost, becomes someone rather than a copy, lives it instead of narrating it. Fall: a leap that costs a friend. Each item leans on one of these:

- **Counting the cost:** [[The Tally Wand]], [[The Back-Rank Mantle]], [[The Reckoning Beads]], [[The Patient Glass]]
- **Becoming someone:** [[The Naming Rod]]
- **Living it:** [[The Wonder Jar]]
- **The leap that costs a friend:** [[The Headlong Wand]]
- **The quiet and the fireplaces:** [[The Hearthside Coat]]
- **Aethryn feels familiar:** [[The Walked-Before Boots]]

**The player.** Miguel plays utility sideways. The highlights on his sheet are not damage: a 40-foot jump off 10 feet of movement, Minor Illusion buying twenty seconds against a specter, Mage Hand retrieving a key from the bottom of a well, a thrown healing potion that brought a PC back from the floor. He names his spells: Firebolt is "Diesel Cougar in the Winter". He is the party's suspicious one, and he pays costs: the orb was his call.

## Pick order
