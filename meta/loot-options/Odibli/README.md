## What these are built on

**The character.** Bookish, excited, a bucket-list adventurer who leaps before he looks: wonder over safety. His journal is his spellbook and his truest self, and he logs his life as he lives it. He checks every fireplace he passes, because gnomes pass notes to each other through fire. High Arcana. He absorbed shadow essence from a smashed orb, watched his veins turn black, and spent it bringing two party members back. DM-only (2026-10-25): he is a copy made at [[The Citadel]] for an experiment, loose by accident and unaware of it. The decades at a scribe's desk were the original's, so desk-and-scribe items fit as echoes of that life, never as his own past; see his PC file.

**The player.** Miguel plays utility sideways. The highlights on his sheet are not damage: a 40-foot jump off 10 feet of movement, Minor Illusion buying twenty seconds against a specter, Mage Hand retrieving a key from the bottom of a well, a thrown healing potion that brought a PC back from the floor. He names his spells — Firebolt is "Diesel Cougar in the Winter," which he decided and the table now lives with. He is the party's suspicious one: mistrusted an NPC on sight, interrogated him about a boast, caught the evasion, and stayed mistrustful until the man saved everyone. And he pays costs — the orb was his call.

- **He has spotlight hooks now** (on his PC file, from the 2026-10-25 backstory pass). An item can pay one off, but none here is built to.

If Mage Armor is a regular prepared spell, AC 11 + Dex is worse than what he casts, and the Overrobe becomes a pocket organiser while the Coat becomes a pure escape button. Either is fine; neither should count as his reward on the AC alone.
