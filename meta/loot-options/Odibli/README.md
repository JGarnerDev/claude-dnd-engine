## What these are built on

**The character.** He spent most of his life transcribing scrolls and arcane texts, and his academic focus is the Astral Plane. Adventuring is a bucket-list item, not a career. He checks every fireplace he passes for hidden messages — not a quirk but a professional reflex from a career of finding concealed correspondence in manuscripts. High Arcana. He absorbed shadow essence from a smashed orb, watched his veins turn black, and spent it bringing two party members back.

**The player.** Miguel plays utility sideways. The highlights on his sheet are not damage: a 40-foot jump off 10 feet of movement, Minor Illusion buying twenty seconds against a specter, Mage Hand retrieving a key from the bottom of a well, a thrown healing potion that brought a PC back from the floor. He names his spells — Firebolt is "Diesel Cougar in the Winter," which he decided and the table now lives with. He is the party's suspicious one: mistrusted an NPC on sight, interrogated him about a boast, caught the evasion, and stayed mistrustful until the man saved everyone. And he pays costs — the orb was his call.

## Pick order

**First item:** [[The Flue Ledger]]. It rewards the habit he will display within five minutes of any session starting.

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[Astral Chalk]] to reward how he plays, [[The Annotated Staff]] if he needs his melee turns to stop being wasted. Chalk is the more fun pick; the Staff is the more useful one.

**Hold for last:** [[The Inkproof Overrobe]], which is the least exciting item in this folder and quietly the one most likely to keep him alive.

## Caveats

- **Spelling.** A standing flag on the sheet: notes spell him Odibli, Odible, and ODB, with "Odibli" used in the record and spelling unconfirmed with Miguel. Nothing here is inscribed; keep it that way until confirmed.
- **His background is `Unknown`** and his full name has never been established. No option here depends on either.
- His sheet has **no `spotlight_hooks`** (dashboard item 20 lists him and Luci as the two still missing them). Items do not fix that — the backstory pass does.
- **Check [[The Inkproof Overrobe]] and [[The Spine-Bound Coat]] against whatever he is already wearing.** Both grant AC 11 + Dex and both fill the same slot — pick one, not both. If Mage Armor is a regular prepared spell, AC 11 + Dex is worse than what he casts, and the Overrobe becomes a pocket organiser while the Coat becomes a pure escape button. Either is fine; neither should count as his reward on the AC alone.
- **No attunement needed anywhere on this sheet.**
