# Diesel Cougar

- **Misc (casting sleeve)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A scorched leather sleeve for his casting hand, named by its owner before it was earned.*

## What it does

Once per short rest his Firebolt ignites a flammable object it hits regardless of the attack roll — and he may choose for the bolt to burn **cold**, dealing its damage as normal but shedding no light and leaving frost instead of char.

## Why this character

Firebolt is the only spell on his sheet he gave a name to. Naming it was a declaration that it was his signature, and signatures should do something.

## Why this player

He named it "Diesel Cougar in the Winter" and the table has carried the joke ever since. The cold-burn option is the mechanical pun on the *winter* half — he will notice inside one second.

## At 6–8

The cold burn also douses one ordinary fire within 30 ft.
