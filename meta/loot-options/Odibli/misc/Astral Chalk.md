# Astral Chalk

- **Misc (chalk)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A stub of grey chalk that leaves a line with no thickness.*

## What it does

Once per long rest he may draw a line up to 20 ft across any surface or through open air; for 1 minute any creature can walk on that line as solid ground at any orientation, gravity notwithstanding.

## Why this character

The Astral Plane is his stated academic focus and has never once come up mechanically. This is a piece of it small enough for tier 1.

## Why this player

Every memorable thing he has done was lateral — the jump, the illusion, the mage-hand key, the thrown potion. Twenty feet of walkable nothing is a tool with no combat application printed on it, which is exactly the shape he converts into something nobody planned for.

## At 6–8

40 ft, and the line lasts 10 minutes.
