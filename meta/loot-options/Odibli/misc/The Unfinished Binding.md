# The Unfinished Binding

- **Misc (hand wrap)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long strip of grey linen in faded guild script, sized for a half-orc's hand. On a gnome it
winds to the elbow.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when he rolls initiative or is surprised, he can teleport up to 60 ft to
an unoccupied space he can see. This takes no action, works while surprised, and happens
before the first turn of combat.

## History

**[[Elowen Rimetusk]]** wrapped her casting hand in it for the translocation that moved her
surrounded company, and wore it for decades after. The spell's last order — *move them* — is
woven into the linen and never finished running. She gives it to Odibli herself, with a favor
attached; the scene is in her file.

## Why this character

He is split the way she is: one Odibli here, one left behind at the desk. She sees it, and it
is the only reason she trusts him with it. An item made of an unfinished crossing belongs to
the one PC who is himself the result of one.

## Why this player

Miguel plays utility sideways. A free 60-ft reposition before anyone acts is the kind of tool
he will turn into something nobody planned, and it keeps a d6-hit-die gnome out of the first
volley.

## At 6–8

He can bring one willing creature within 5 ft along with him — the spell remembering it was
built to move a company.

## Note

This is the only item in the folder tied to an NPC and a plot. That breaks the folder's
plot-free rule on purpose: the DM attached its giver and history before handover. It sits as a
fourth misc option, past the three-per-class composition, and as an Uncommon it spends Odibli's
Uncommon for Act 1.
