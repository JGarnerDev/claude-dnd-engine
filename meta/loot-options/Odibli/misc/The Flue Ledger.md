# The Flue Ledger

- **Misc (brass tube)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thin brass tube, open at both ends, scribed inside with transcriber's marks.*

## What it does

Held in a cold hearth or dropped down a chimney, it writes out one legible line of whatever was last burned there. One hearth per long rest, and never a fire that is still lit.

## Why this character

The fireplace habit is the single most established detail about him and it has never once paid out. This makes the reflex correct.

## Why this player

The best player-fit item on this sheet. He checks every fireplace anyway — the Ledger means the rest of the table starts wanting him to.

## At 6–8

A full sentence, and it can read a fire that went out within the hour.

## Notes

This is an information faucet. It has no plot attached, but whatever plot is running will walk into it, so decide how fast it runs before handing it over.
