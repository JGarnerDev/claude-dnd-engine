# The Reckoning Beads

- **Misc (pocket abacus)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A palm-sized abacus of nine wires and dark wooden beads. The beads slide on their own when nobody is touching them.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, he gives the beads a shake. He learns which hostile creature within 60 feet of him has the lowest share of its hit points left, and which ally within 60 feet is closest to dropping.

## Why this character

Counting the cost as a literal count: the abacus tells him what the fight has already cost and who is about to pay for it.

## Why this player

He is the party's suspicious one and its Arcana expert. Miguel will use the information well, and will want to know why it's sometimes wrong (it isn't).

## At 6–8

Twice per long rest, and he can use it as a reaction when an ally takes damage.
