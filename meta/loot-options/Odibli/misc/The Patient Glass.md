# The Patient Glass

- **Misc (sandglass)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A pocket sandglass in a brass frame. The sand runs slower when he's in a hurry.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when he rolls initiative, he can turn the glass and choose to act last in the order. If he does, the save DC of the first spell he casts in that combat increases by 2.

## Why this character

Counting the cost means waiting to see the field before committing. The glass pays the gnome who lets everyone else move first.

## Why this player

Odibli is the leap-first character at a table that knows it. The glass gives Miguel a payoff for not going first.

## At 6–8

Recharges on a short rest.
