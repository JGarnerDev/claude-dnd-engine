# The Wonder Jar

- **Misc (jar)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A small stoppered jar of clear glass that looks empty, though it never sounds empty.*

## What it does

He can uncork the jar to catch one moment he is living through: a sound, a smell, a few seconds of a place. The jar holds one moment; catching a new one lets the old one out.

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, he can play the moment back. One ally within 30 feet who hears it has advantage on their next saving throw against being frightened.

## Why this character

His PC arc's growth includes living it instead of narrating it. The jar only takes what he was there for, so it rewards the moments he lived, not the ones he wrote down.

## Why this player

Miguel is good at the small, funny use. Expect jars of tavern songs, insults and one very loud goat.

## At 6–8

The jar holds two moments, and the advantage covers any save against being frightened or charmed.
