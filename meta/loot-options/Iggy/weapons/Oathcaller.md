# Oathcaller

- **Weapon (long single-edged blade, rapier statistics)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A long, single-edged blade with a slight curve and a grip long enough for a second hand, though it never needs one. It is balanced like a much lighter sword.*

## What it does

A +1 weapon that uses rapier statistics (1d8, finesse, one-handed) and deals slashing damage instead of piercing.

Once per short rest, as a bonus action, Iggy raises the blade and calls out one creature within 30 ft of him that can hear him. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against his spell save DC. On a failure, until the start of Iggy's next turn it has disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but him, and if it attacks someone other than Iggy anyway, he can use his reaction to make one melee weapon attack against it.

## Why this character

He stands in front of people, and his arc's growth is the hero who makes heroes of the people beside him. The challenge does both at once: it calls the danger onto himself and leaves his friends free to work. It is also a small, deliberate taste of his fall, the man who never steps back, so the item asks the arc's question every time he uses it.

## Why this player

Jeff likes his bits to land and plays protector unprompted. A called challenge is a performance with a mechanical payoff: a line he says out loud, a target the table can see, and a punishment if the enemy ignores him.

## At 6–8

Twice per short rest, or he can call out two creatures with one use.

## Note

One-handed, so it pairs with [[The Front-Rank Shield]]. Its form is a saber or katana with a bastard sword's grip; its statistics are a rapier's on purpose, so it keeps Iggy's finesse build.
