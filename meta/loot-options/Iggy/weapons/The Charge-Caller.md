# The Charge-Caller

- **Weapon (saber, rapier statistics)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A cavalry saber with a knuckle-bow worn bright where a hand has gripped it through a lot of shouting.*

## What it does

Uses rapier statistics (1d8, finesse, one-handed). Once per short rest, when Iggy hits a creature with a Divine Smite using this saber, he can shout the charge. Each ally within 30 ft of him who can hear him can use their reaction to move up to half their speed toward that creature. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

## Why this character

Stand in front, and bring the line with you. He is a soldier first, and the arc's hero is the one whose friends follow him in. The smite becomes the signal the whole party moves on.

## Why this player

Jeff likes his moments to land for the table, not just for him. A smite that moves four other people's tokens is a moment everybody plays in.

## At 6–8

Allies who answer the charge also gain temporary hit points equal to Iggy's Charisma modifier.

## Note

Overlaps with [[Oathcaller]] in shape (both curved, one-handed blades). Prefer Oathcaller if the party needs protection, and this one if it needs momentum.
