# The Ovation

- **Weapon (rapier)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A rapier that gathers a thin sound as it is drawn, like a room an instant before it starts clapping.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when Iggy drops a creature to 0 HP with it, he names one ally aloud as the reason it worked. That ally gains 1d4 temporary HP.

## Why this character

He narrates other people's deeds by reflex and keeps a running account of who did what. A kill that pays out to somebody else is the only version of a killing blow that fits him.

## Why this player

Jeff hands out credit unprompted. This makes the credit mechanical without making it mandatory, and it gives him a line to say at the exact moment the table is already looking at him.

## At 6–8

1d6 temporary HP, and he may name two allies.
