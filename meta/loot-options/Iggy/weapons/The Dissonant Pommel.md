# The Dissonant Pommel

- **Weapon (rapier pommel)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A replacement pommel of dark brass. Struck, it sounds a note that is very slightly wrong and stays wrong however it is tuned.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when Iggy lands a Divine Smite, the target must make a Wisdom saving throw against his spell save DC. On a failure it can take no reactions until the end of its next turn.

## Why this character

His smite already reads as radiant with a dissonance underneath. This is that dissonance given a rule — the radiance does the damage, the wrong note does the rest.

## Why this player

He has one smite and he will spend it on the moment that matters. Attaching a rider to the smite rather than to the rapier means the item only fires when he has already decided this is the swing, which is how he plays anyway.

## At 6–8

Once per short rest, and the target also has disadvantage on its next attack roll.
