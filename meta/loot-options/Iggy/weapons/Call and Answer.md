# Call and Answer

- **Weapon (rapier)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A rapier with a plain guard and a second, smaller ring set behind the first, as if the hilt were made for two hands that take turns.*

## What it does

A +1 rapier. When an ally uses one of Iggy's Bardic Inspiration dice on an attack roll and hits, Iggy can use his reaction to make one melee weapon attack with this rapier against the same target, if it is within his reach. On a hit, he adds the number rolled on that Inspiration die to the damage.

## Why this character

He makes heroes of the people beside him, then follows them in. The rapier pays him for letting a friend strike first, which is the arc's growth (the names he carries become half from this world) made into a fighting habit.

## Why this player

Jeff hands out Inspiration and titles as a morale engine. This makes every die he gives away come back as a chance to swing, so being generous is also the strongest play.

## At 6–8

He can use it when an ally uses his Inspiration on any roll that hits or succeeds against a creature within his reach, not only an attack roll.
