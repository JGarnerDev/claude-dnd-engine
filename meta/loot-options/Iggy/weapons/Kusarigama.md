# Kusarigama

A player proposal, added 2026-11-25. Jeff gave Iggy Kusarigama mastery as the second paladin
Weapon Mastery slot (in place of the whip). The weapon itself is homebrew and needs the DM's
approval before Iggy carries one. It sits outside the three-weapon composition rule: it starts
mundane, and this file exists for the upgrade path.

## What it does

A sickle on a long weighted chain. Martial melee weapon, 2 gp, 3 lb.

- **Damage:** 1d4 slashing.
- **Properties:** finesse, reach.
- **Mastery: Topple.** On a hit, the target makes a CON save (DC 8 + DEX modifier + proficiency
  bonus, DC 13 for Iggy now) or falls prone.

Iggy attacks with it at +5 to hit for 1d4 + 2 slashing, and can smite through it from 10 ft.

Table note: a prone target gives advantage to melee attacks from within 5 ft, but attacks from
farther away have disadvantage. Toppling at reach sets the target up for the party's front line
(or for Iggy stepping in with Oathcaller), not for Iggy's own next chain swing.

## Why this character

Iggy's main weapon, Oathcaller, wants Iggy adjacent. The chain covers the gap: it reaches the
enemy one square past the ally Iggy is protecting, carries a smite at 10 ft, and turns a hit into
a prone target the rest of the party can pile onto. It is a support weapon for a support
character: it hits light and opens the door for others.

## Why this player

Jeff picked the mastery before the weapon existed, on the grounds of "let's see what happens".
That is a player who likes to experiment with a build, so the item should reward the experiment
by growing, not stay a reflavoured whip forever. The upgrades below each add one new decision to
a turn rather than a flat number.

## At 6–8

Three steps, each one optional, any of which the DM can skip or reorder.

1. **Weighted chain (Common).** The sickle stays, the weight is rebuilt. Once per turn, when a
   Topple attempt succeeds, Iggy can pull the prone target up to 5 ft toward them. This fixes the
   reach problem: drag the target into Oathcaller range and finish it with advantage.
2. **+1 Kusarigama (Uncommon).** +1 to attack and damage rolls. The Topple DC does not change.
3. **Binding chain (Uncommon, attunement).** +1 weapon as above, plus: once per short rest, when
   Iggy hits with it, Iggy can wrap the target instead of dealing damage, so the target is
   Grappled (escape DC equals the Topple DC) while it is within 10 ft of Iggy and Iggy holds the
   chain. Iggy can't attack with the kusarigama while the grapple lasts. A smite spent on that
   hit still deals its radiant damage.

## Note

Rules check for the DM: as written the kusarigama is a whip with Topple in place of Slow, which
is roughly an even trade (Topple is the stronger mastery, but the 1d4 damage and the reach
disadvantage on prone targets hold it back). Confirm with the DM before it goes on the sheet.
