# The Half-Beat

- **Weapon (rapier)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A rapier whose ring arrives a fraction after the strike, always slightly out of time with the room.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when a creature within 5 ft of Iggy hits one of his allies, he may use his reaction to make one melee attack against that creature.

## Why this character

He puts himself between allies and harm as temperament, not doctrine, and he fights with a rapier. This is the only mechanical expression of that instinct on the sheet.

## Why this player

He plays protector without being asked to. A reaction attack that only exists when somebody else gets hurt pays him for watching his friends instead of the enemy, which is what he is doing anyway.

## At 6–8

Once per short rest becomes once per round, and the trigger extends to 10 ft.
