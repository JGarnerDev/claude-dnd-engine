# Loot Options — Iggy (Jeff)

Aasimar Bard 3 (Valor) / Paladin 2 · Entertainer · level 5 · `level_confirmed: 33`

**Bracket for every option: levels 4–5 (tier 1 into tier 2).** No option here is tied to a plot, a location, or an NPC — the story picks from this list, not the other way round.

Rebuilt 2026-11-11 from his PC arc (`scheduler/arcs/Iggy PC Arc.md`). The arc gives each item its theme; the item still names no person, place or thread. Weapons were reworked with Jeff toward martial blades with a bard's edge (Inspiration, the battle cry, the called challenge), and no song or viol, matching the arc.

## The options

- **weapons/** — [[Oathcaller]] (Uncommon) · [[Call and Answer]] (Uncommon) · [[The Charge-Caller]] (Uncommon)
- **armor/** — [[The Front-Rank Shield]] (Uncommon) · [[The Filed Breastplate]] (Uncommon) · [[The Lined Coat]] (Common)
- **misc/** — [[The Unfinished Catechism]] (Uncommon) · [[The Ledger of Small Deeds]] (Common) · [[The Mourner's Token]] (Common)

## What these are built on

**The character.** A soldier who lived through the end of his whole world and carries the names of everyone he lost. Assigns brief heroic titles to allies and notes their deeds. Front-line protector: offense first, stabilize allies a close second. Rapier and voice; Bardic Inspiration, Healing Hands, Divine Smite.

**The PC arc.** Growth: builds a new faith by looking honestly at the old one's faults, makes heroes of the people beside him, lets a story end. Fall: can't stop standing his ground, learns the guilt was earned, lets grief bring back the dead. Each item leans on one of these:

- **Makes heroes:** [[Call and Answer]], [[The Charge-Caller]], [[The Ledger of Small Deeds]]
- **Stands in front (and never steps back):** [[Oathcaller]], [[The Front-Rank Shield]]
- **The new faith:** [[The Unfinished Catechism]], [[The Filed Breastplate]]
- **The names he carries:** [[The Lined Coat]]
- **Grief's temptation:** [[The Mourner's Token]]

**The player.** A bit-generator and a morale engine. He hands out heroic titles unprompted, plays protector without being asked, and presses NPCs for concrete answers rather than accepting atmosphere. He likes his bits to *land*, so items that reward a performance reach him more than items that add a damage die.

## Pick order

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[The Unfinished Catechism]]. The player writes the faith's tenets in play, which is the arc's first growth line, and it suits a quiet night better than a damage item.

**The martial pick:** [[Oathcaller]], if the Uncommon should change how he fights instead.

**Hand over with care:** [[The Mourner's Token]] is a temptation item, built on a fall.

## Caveats

- **Jeff is the DM.** He is choosing from a menu written for his own PC, which is unavoidable in a rotating-DM group but worth naming — if anything here looks generous, this is the one sheet in the folder with nobody independent checking it. Having another player pick which one he gets is a cheap fix.
- **All three weapons are Uncommon**, at Jeff's call (2026-11-11). Only one Uncommon per act is the budget either way.
- **No attunement anywhere on this sheet**, deliberately — he is a half-caster with two classes' worth of bookkeeping already.
