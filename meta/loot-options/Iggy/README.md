Aasimar Bard 3 / Paladin 1 · Entertainer · level 4 · `level_confirmed: 28`

**Bracket for every option: levels 4–5 (tier 1 into tier 2).** No option here is tied to a plot, a location, or an NPC — the story picks from this list, not the other way round.

## What these are built on

**The character.** A viol that survived a dead plane. Murmured half-remembered names while tuning. Micro-reverence for records — a pause to touch or straighten books, maps, inscriptions. Assigns brief heroic titles to allies and notes their deeds. Front-line protector: offense first, stabilize allies a close second. The unsuppressable **Shazzam** (says the word, one random ally heals 1 HP). Rapier and voice; Healing Hands; a smite that reads as radiant with a dissonance underneath.

**The player.** A bit-generator and a morale engine. Shazzam is a joke made permanent. He hands out heroic titles unprompted. He spun a gold-dragon-turned-raccoon story into a viol jig for a whole tavern. He negotiates — traded a platinum necklace for thieves' tools and hide armor at market. And he presses NPCs for concrete answers rather than accepting atmosphere. He likes his bits to *land*, so items that reward a performance reach him more than items that add a damage die.

## Pick order

**First item:** [[The Shazzam Bell]]. Cheap, instantly funny, and it rewards the one thing he will certainly do within ten minutes of any session.

**The budgeted Uncommon:** [[The Half-Beat]]. The only option here that changes how he stands in a fight, and standing in front of people is the whole character.

**Hold for last:** [[Titleplate of the Named]], which lands best once the party has deeds worth naming.

## Caveats

- **Jeff is the DM.** He is choosing from a menu written for his own PC, which is unavoidable in a rotating-DM group but worth naming — if anything here looks generous, this is the one sheet in the folder with nobody independent checking it. Having another player pick which one he gets is a cheap fix.
- Iggy is the **overdue PC on rotation** — 2 spotlight turns over the last 4 sessions against the other PCs' 4.
- [[The Second String]] and [[The Shazzam Bell]] assume the viol and the rapier survive in his hands. Both are carried items with no entity files.
- **No attunement anywhere on this sheet**, deliberately — he is a half-caster with two classes' worth of bookkeeping already.
