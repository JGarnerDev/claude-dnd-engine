# The Second String

- **Misc (viol string)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A replacement viol string of gut that is not local to anywhere. It keeps sounding a half-beat after he stops playing.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when he grants Bardic Inspiration, he may also make one Insight or History check with advantage.

## Why this character

The viol already produces tones he does not recall composing. This is that, scaled down to something he can use in a conversation.

## Why this player

Attaches to a thing he does every session anyway and turns the inspiration moment into a two-for-one, which is the kind of value he notices.

## At 6–8

Advantage extends to Performance and Persuasion.
