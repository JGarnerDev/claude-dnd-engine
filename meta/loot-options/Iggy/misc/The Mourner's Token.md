# The Mourner's Token

- **Misc (worn copper token)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A copper token worn smooth on one side by a thumb. It is warm when you pick it up, whoever had it last.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, Iggy can touch a creature that died within the last minute while holding the token. He hears one sentence: the last thing it thought.

## Why this character

One of the arc's falls is grief winning: a lost friend could come back, and he lets someone try. The token is a small, harmless taste of reaching back to the dead, which is exactly why it is worth handing to him. Whether he keeps using it is his choice.

## Why this player

Jeff presses for answers. One true sentence from the dead is an answer he can act on, and a cheap, strange item a player will remember.

## At 6–8

The window extends to 10 minutes, and he can ask one yes-or-no question instead of hearing the last thought.
