# The Unfinished Catechism

- **Misc (blank prayer book)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A small bound book of blank pages with a good clasp. The first page has a heading and nothing under it.*

## What it does

The book holds up to three tenets, each one short sentence that Jeff writes in play when Iggy decides what his faith keeps or drops. Once per long rest, when Iggy makes an ability check that acts on one of the written tenets, he has advantage. He can strike out a tenet and write a new one at the end of a long rest.

## Why this character

The arc's first growth line is a new faith built partly by looking at the old one's faults: too much certainty, and all meaning locked in one place. A book with room for only three lines refuses the first fault by design, and the tenets get written by what Iggy actually does.

## Why this player

Jeff likes his bits to land, and he presses for concrete answers. Here the player writes the rules of the faith himself, in play, and the item pays out when he acts on them. It is the one option on this sheet that the player authors.

## At 6–8

The book holds five tenets, and once per long rest he can share the advantage with an ally who acts on the same tenet in the same scene.
