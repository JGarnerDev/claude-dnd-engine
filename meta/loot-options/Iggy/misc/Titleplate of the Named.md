# Titleplate of the Named

- **Misc (brass plate)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A small brass plate with room for one name, the kind a tavern screws to the wall over a regular's seat.*

## What it does

When Iggy gives an ally one of his heroic titles and scratches it in, that ally adds 1d4 to their next saving throw before the scene ends. One name at a time — a new name erases the old. Once per long rest.

## Why this character

He assigns titles naturally and constantly. The plate makes the habit cost something to aim.

## Why this player

The most direct conversion available of a thing Jeff already does into a thing the sheet pays for — and it pulls the rest of the table into his bit, because being named is now worth a d4.

## At 6–8

Holds two names.
