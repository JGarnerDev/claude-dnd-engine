# The Ledger of Small Deeds

- **Misc (pocket ledger)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A pocket ledger in a soldier's hand: one ruled line per deed, the name on the left and what they did on the right.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, Iggy can use an action to read one ally's deed from the ledger aloud. For 1 minute, that ally adds 1d4 to their saving throws.

## Why this character

He gives allies heroic titles and keeps a running account of who did what. The arc's growth is the hero who makes heroes of the people beside him; the ledger turns the account into something the person named can feel.

## Why this player

He hands out titles unprompted. This makes the habit worth an action in a fight, without making it mandatory.

## At 6–8

The read becomes a bonus action, and the bonus lasts until the ally's next long rest instead of 1 minute.
