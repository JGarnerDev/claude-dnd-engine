# The Shazzam Bell

- **Misc (pommel bell)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A thumb-sized bell fixed to the rapier's pommel.*

## What it does

When Shazzam triggers, once per long rest the 1 HP also ends the **frightened** condition on whoever it landed on.

## Why this character

Shazzam is established and cannot be suppressed. The bell does not change that — it means once a day the gag saves someone.

## Why this player

His longest-running bit, finally load-bearing. Expect him to start saying the word with intent, which is the point.

## At 6–8

Also ends charmed.
