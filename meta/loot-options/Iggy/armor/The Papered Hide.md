# The Papered Hide

- **Armor (hide)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*Hide armor relined between layers with pressed paper — playbills, set lists, copied pages, a few in a script nobody reads.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when Iggy takes damage, he may reduce it by 1d6 as the lining gives.

## Why this character

He has worn hide since the market trade, and he cannot pass a written thing without straightening it. Armor made partly of records is the character wearing his own reflex.

## Why this player

Small, always available, and purely his. Expect him to narrate which page took the hit, which is the entire return on this item.

## At 6–8

2d6, twice per long rest.
