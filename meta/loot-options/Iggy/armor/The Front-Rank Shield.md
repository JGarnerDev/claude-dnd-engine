# The Front-Rank Shield

- **Armor (shield)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A plain round shield, scarred on the face and clean on the back, where nobody ever hit it.*

## What it does

Once per short rest, when a creature Iggy can see within 5 ft of him is hit by an attack, he can use his reaction to become the target of that attack instead. He takes the damage with resistance.

## Why this character

He stands in front of people as temperament, not doctrine, and the arc's growth is the hero who makes room for the people beside him by taking what was meant for them. The shield turns that instinct into a rule he can call on.

## Why this player

Jeff plays protector unprompted and likes his moves to land visibly. Stepping into a hit meant for someone else is a moment the whole table sees, and it pays him for watching his friends instead of the enemy.

## At 6–8

Once per short rest becomes twice, and the reach extends to 10 ft (he moves up to 5 ft to take the hit, without provoking).

## Note

Iggy wields a rapier one-handed, so the shield costs him nothing he uses. It does compete with any two-handed option for his hands.
