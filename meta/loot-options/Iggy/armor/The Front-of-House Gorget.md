# The Front-of-House Gorget

- **Armor (gorget)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A performer's throat-plate, thin and well-polished, made to be looked at from the front by a great many people at once.*

## What it does

While at least one ally is within 5 ft of him, Iggy has advantage on saving throws against being **frightened**.

## Why this character

He stands between people and the thing coming at them, and he does it as temperament rather than doctrine. The armor is only brave while somebody is standing behind it, which is the honest shape of his courage.

## Why this player

Jeff plays forward and talks the whole time. A condition-proofing that switches on when a friend is beside him never asks him to change position — it pays the position he was already going to take.

## At 6–8

Extends to 10 ft, and also covers **charmed**.
