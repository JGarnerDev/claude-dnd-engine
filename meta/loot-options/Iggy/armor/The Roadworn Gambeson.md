# The Roadworn Gambeson

- **Armor (gambeson)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A padded coat sewn by a touring company out of whatever each of them could spare, so no two panels match and all of them fit.*

## What it does

While two or more allies are within 10 ft of him, Iggy's AC increases by 1.

## Why this character

He is a company man playing at being a knight. An item that is worse alone and better in an ensemble is the character stated as arithmetic.

## Why this player

A small number that applies in nearly every fight he chooses to be in, with no attunement and no bookkeeping. It also quietly rewards him for dragging the party into formation, which he does by talking.

## At 6–8

+2 AC under the same condition, and difficult terrain costs him nothing while an ally is adjacent.
