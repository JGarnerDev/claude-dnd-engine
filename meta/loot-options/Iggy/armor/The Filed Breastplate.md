# The Filed Breastplate

- **Armor (breastplate)** · Uncommon · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A breastplate with a holy sign filed off the front. The scratches where it used to be are still there, and he has not polished them out.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when Iggy fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead. If he does, he takes 2d6 psychic damage that can't be reduced.

## Why this character

His arc's growth is a faith that looks honestly at where the old one went wrong. A breastplate that keeps the mark of what was removed is that idea worn on the chest, and the cost of the success is the honesty: admitting the fault hurts.

## Why this player

Jeff presses NPCs for concrete answers rather than accepting atmosphere. An item that trades a clean, known cost for a guaranteed result suits a player who wants to know what he is paying.

## At 6–8

The psychic damage drops to 1d6, and once per long rest he can extend the success to one ally within 10 ft who failed the same save.
