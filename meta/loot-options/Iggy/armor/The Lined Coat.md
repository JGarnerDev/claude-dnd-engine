# The Lined Coat

- **Armor (coat worn under armor)** · Common · no attunement · bracket: levels 4–5

*A soldier's coat whose lining is stitched over with small names in several hands, some of them his.*

## What it does

Once per long rest, when Iggy fails a death saving throw, he can reroll it and must use the new roll.

## Why this character

He carries the names of everyone he lost and feels he owes it to them to remember. A coat that holds the names close, and won't let him go quietly while it does, is that debt as a garment.

## Why this player

A Common that only matters on the worst turn of the night. Jeff plays a man who never runs; this is the small mercy that keeps that habit from ending the character on one bad roll.

## At 6–8

The reroll gains a bonus equal to his Charisma modifier.
