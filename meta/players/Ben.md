## Working with

How Claude works with Ben (filled with Ben, 2026-11-25):

- **Works at the desk.** Ben uses the Claude desktop app on his computer, not a phone (for now).
- **Short chat, detail in the doc.** Keep chat answers short and plain, with no walls of text. The detail goes in the campaign doc, where more is welcome: Ben runs sessions from cue cards, so the doc (the Claude Doc "Deorum Sanctum Campaign" and its Word copy in `Desktop\DnD Bosses`, re-saved after every change) should carry everything he needs at the table.
- **Plain words.** No tech or rules jargon without saying what it means.
- **Technical steps: do it, then say what happened.** Claude runs installs, git and terminal work itself where it can, and hands Ben a step only when it has to (a sign-in, a token), one click at a time, in plain words.
- **One question at a time,** as options he can pick from, with room for a note (root `CLAUDE.md` → Working Rules).
- **When he dislikes a beat, NPC or idea,** drop it and offer three numbered alternatives.
- **Never delete without asking.** Ask before removing anything.
- **Builds maps in Roll20** from the linear, gridded map sheets in `Desktop\DnD Bosses\Maps`.
- **When he's a player, not the DM** (Strahd seasons, and Jeff's Aethryn runs), no spoilers: keep DM-only plans, trackers and secrets away from him.
