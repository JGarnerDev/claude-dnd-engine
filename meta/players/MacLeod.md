## Planned Cameo

**The organizing wizard of Tassad's crusade.** Spending the party's Barovian coin (Strahd's face,
a currency nobody here knows) reads as forgery. The escalation ends with Tassad, where the real
church is Waukeen's, sending a tiny extranational crusade into Velmora. MacLeod plays the wizard
who organizes it. It is a guest seat for an arc beat, not a party member. Not canon until it is
planned.

- **How it gets there:** Silas refuses the coin → Velmora's crown sends a coin-warden (minting is
  one of the few powers its weak crown still holds) → a coin drifts to Tassad, where forgery is
  heresy against Waukeen as well as a crime → the crusade.
- **Why a wizard leads it:** Tassad's wizard guilds run like cartels, and the Templar Triumvirate
  sits over its harbor. To them an untraceable "mint" is a rival magic as much as a heresy.
