## Working with

How Claude works with Jeff (moved here from Claude's local memory, 2026-11-11):

- **Reads on a phone, answers by number.** He often works over remote control. Use plain words and numbered lists, keep the numbers stable once shown, and expect replies like "push 3" or "2 ok, cut 4". About six items a list at most.
- **One question at a time,** as options he can pick from, with room for a note (root `CLAUDE.md` → Working Rules).
- **When he dislikes a beat,** offer three numbered alternatives rather than defending it.
- **Lines he can use cold.** NPC lines say their point plainly and have a cue in the scene; fix a vague line by rewording it, never by adding an explanation under it (`/review` Pass 1).
- **Repo files over artifacts** for anything he'll study: he zooms in and reads them beside their sources.
- **Builds maps by hand in Roll20,** using renders only as layout references (`maps/battlemaps/CLAUDE.md`).
- **When he's a player, not the DM** (Strahd seasons, and Ben's Aethryn run), he contributes seeds, not solved mysteries: keep hooks and secrets underdeveloped and don't show him DM-only material.
