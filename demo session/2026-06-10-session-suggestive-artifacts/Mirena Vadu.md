Location: [[The Old Svalich Road]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

Race: Human

## The Stead

The Vadu Stead sits at the midpoint of [[The Old Svalich Road]] — woodpile bigger than the house, lamplit windows, a made path to the door. Travelers between [[Barovia Village]] and [[Vallaki]] have slept under its roof for as long as anyone bothers to remember. There is always room. There is always too much food on the table for a woodcutter's household, and nobody in the family eats much of it.

## The Debt

The cart stops at the stead, seasonally. The family pays. None of them — Mirena included — can tell you what they hand over; the question slides off her like water, and it is not an act. Upstairs there is a third bed, kept made, in a house with two sleepers; no Vadu can say who sleeps in it. On the pantry doorframe there are tally-marks, one cut each season; each Vadu assumes another Vadu cuts them, and none of them does, as far as any can say.

(The cart is consistent with [[The Dream Pastry Woman]]'s bargain pattern. This stays unconfirmed on screen, and what the family pays stays unanswered — it is her dread, not a puzzle.)

## The Kindness

Someone kindly told her, once, that sheltering travelers counts against what's owed. She would never put it that way — to her it is just prudence, the way a good housewife salts meat before winter. So she finds gentle reasons for guests to stay one more night: the ford will be high, you look pale, the wolves are bad this week. Everyone local knows her warmth has prudence under it. Nobody minds. It is still real warmth — that is the part that doesn't resolve.
