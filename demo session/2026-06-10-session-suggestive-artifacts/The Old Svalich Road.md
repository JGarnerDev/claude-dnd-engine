Region: [[Barovia]] | Campaign: [[Curse of Strahd]]

## The Road

The only way west from [[Barovia Village]] to [[Vallaki]] — two to three days of packed dirt and old stone through the Svalich Woods, under a sky that never fully commits to daylight. It is not abandoned. That is the strange part. The shrines to the Morninglord lean but never fall. The mile-markers' names are worn to grooves, but there is always fresh bread on one or two of them, and it is always gone by morning, and nobody — including travelers keeping deliberate watch — sees it go.

## Customs

You leave bread on the markers. You don't ask. ([[Ismark Kolyanovich]] knows the custom; he does not know its origin, and neither does anyone else alive.)

## The Wolves

Wolf packs pace travelers at the treeline — not hunting, not fleeing, keeping a tally. They scatter if attacked and return within the hour. When the count comes out, they arrive all at once. Whether they belong to the land's master or to the road itself is not established — and should not be.

## The Crows

Crows sit one to a mile-marker, always exactly too far away to matter. They watch. They do not land on the road. No check reveals anything; as far as anyone can tell, they are just crows.

## Who Lives Here

The Vadu Stead sits at the road's midpoint — woodcutters, and the unofficial last roof before Vallaki. See [[Mirena Vadu]]. Households along the road have arrangements they do not discuss: the cart of [[The Dream Pastry Woman]] is known to stop at some of them, seasonally, and what is handed over is not.
