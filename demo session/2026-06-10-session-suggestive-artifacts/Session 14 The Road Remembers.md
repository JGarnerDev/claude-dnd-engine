## Orientation

The party (all level 3) departs [[Barovia Village]] the morning after burying Kolyan, with [[Ireena Kolyana]] and [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] as traveling companions. Dip still hears whispers when it's quiet — and the Svalich Woods are very quiet. Iggy's shadow still moves wrong, which firelight makes hard to ignore. Luci is without Sacred Flame, permanently. Odibli is intact. Mood out of Session 13: victory-flavored failure — they buried a man with honor, then watched a hag take a child and leave anyway. Resources mostly full after a night's rest.

Unresolved `new_entities` from last session to acknowledge:
- [[Father Donavich]] and [[Doru]] — left behind at the church; the screaming under the floor is not resolved
- [[The Dream Pastry Woman]] — at large, plane-shifted, child in tow
- [[Bosco]] — Iggy's raccoon; first full session as a party member

## Scenes

#### Scene 1 — Out the West Gate

- **Trigger:** Session open; departure morning.
- **Location:** [[Barovia Village]], west edge — grey light, shuttered windows, the road bending into the woods.
- **NPCs present:** Ismark (goal: get moving before attention gathers; voice: warm, brisk, the only easy laugh in Barovia), Ireena (goal: leave without looking back, failing at it; voice: composed, shorter sentences than usual since the burial).
- **Beats:**
  - Crows line the lychgate and rooftops as the party leaves. They watch. None land on the road itself. No check reveals anything — they are just crows, as far as anyone can tell.
  - Villagers who paid the hag watch the party go from doorways. One woman touches her doorframe as they pass. Nobody waves.
  - Ireena, if engaged about Donavich's revelation, deflects practically: "I am Kolyan's daughter where it matters." (Insight DC 12: she's been awake all night with it.)
  - Ismark sets the route: two nights on the road, "and there's a stead at the halfway — the Vadus. Good people. They always have room." He says it like a fact everyone agrees on. (He's right. That's the problem.)
- **Transition:** The village drops behind the trees. The woods close in.

#### Scene 2 — The Road That Counts

- **Trigger:** First travel day, through to camp.
- **Location:** [[The Old Svalich Road]] — mile-markers with names worn to grooves, tilted Morninglord shrines no one maintains, a sky that never fully commits to daylight.
- **NPCs present:** Ismark, Ireena (escort banter, road lore).
- **Beats:**
  - Travel montage with texture stops: bread left on a mile-marker (fresh; nobody around for miles). If the party watches a marker through the night — the bread is gone by morning and no one saw it go, including whoever kept watch (no roll; it simply happens).
  - Wolves appear at the treeline mid-afternoon. They pace the party. They do not approach, do not growl, do not leave. If attacked, they scatter without fighting — and are back within the hour. The intended feeling: not hunted, *counted*.
  - Dip's whisper affliction: the woods are the quietest place Dip has ever been. DM's discretion on content — fragments, never sentences. Iggy's shadow at the campfire: one beat where it doesn't match the flame's direction; Bosco notices first, refuses to sit on that side.
  - Ismark road lore (if asked about the bread): "You leave it for the markers. You don't ask." He's not evasive — it's genuinely all he knows. Everyone does it; no one started it.
- **Transition:** Late second day — woodsmoke, a fence line, the Vadu Stead. The wolves at the treeline stop and sit down, like the party crossed into a different ledger.

#### Scene 3 — The Last Roof Before Vallaki

- **Trigger:** Arrival at the stead, evening of day two.
- **Location:** [[The Old Svalich Road]] — the Vadu Stead at the road's midpoint: woodpile bigger than the house, lamplit windows, a made path to the door.
- **NPCs present:** Mirena Vadu (goal: feed them, keep them, one more night; voice: easy laugh, presses seconds before plates are empty, smiles one beat too long when the road west is mentioned), Tomas Vadu (background — answers in single words, kind ones), Ismark, Ireena.
- **Beats:**
  - Hospitality that outpaces means: too much food on the table for a woodcutter's household, and nobody in the family eating much of it.
  - Discoverable quiet details (no prompting — reward poking around): a third bed upstairs, made, in a house with two sleepers. Tally-marks on the pantry doorframe, dozens, one cut each season; if asked, each Vadu assumes another Vadu cuts them. None of them does, as far as any can say.
  - The residue: if the party mentions pastries, the hag, or the road's debts — the room doesn't go cold, it goes *kind*. Mirena: "The cart comes when it comes. We manage. More bread?" Everyone local knows. Nobody minds. Minding costs more.
  - The hook under the warmth: Mirena finds gentle reasons the party should stay tomorrow too — the ford will be high, Ireena looks pale, the wolves are bad this week. (She believes hosting travelers counts against what's owed. She'd never say it that way — to her it's just prudence, the way you salt meat before winter.)
  - Ireena beat: she recognizes something in Mirena — a person being collected slowly, politely. She doesn't say Strahd's name. The players will. *Branch: if the party presses Mirena hard about what the family pays → she genuinely cannot answer; the question slides off her. This is not a puzzle the session solves. Do not resolve it.*
- **Transition:** The party beds down — or refuses to and camps in the yard. Either way: night two.

#### Scene 4 — The Tally Completes

- **Trigger:** Deep night at the stead.
- **Location:** The Vadu Stead, yard and fence line.
- **NPCs present:** Mirena (doorway only), Ismark (fights), Ireena (protected).
- **Beats:**
  - Bosco wakes Iggy. The treeline is full of eyes — the pacing wolves, finally arrived all at once. The count, it seems, came out.
  - **Combat — medium:** wolf pack (suggested: 4 wolves + 1 dire wolf against four level-3 PCs plus Ismark, veteran; tune live — difficulty preferences are unfilled, so this is judgment, not policy). The dire wolf goes for Ireena, not the strongest fighter — this is a collection attempt, framing unclear by design (Strahd's attention or the road's; do not specify).
  - The Vadus stand in the doorway through all of it. They do not help. They hold the lamp up so the party can see, and Mirena says, kindly, "Mind the fence." Afterward she apologizes — for the noise, not the wolves — and feeds everyone again.
  - *Branch: if the party loses the fight badly → the wolves take nothing and withdraw; whatever was being counted, the number changed. Worse than losing.*
- **Transition:** Grey morning, day three. The Vadus pack the party off warmly.

#### Scene 5 — Bells Before the Walls

- **Trigger:** Final stretch, day three.
- **Location:** [[The Old Svalich Road]] approaching [[Vallaki]].
- **NPCs present:** Ismark, Ireena.
- **Beats:**
  - Mirena's parting gift: bread "for the markers," pressed into someone's pack. She won't explain. If refused, it's in the pack anyway.
  - The bells arrive before the town does — [[Vallaki]]'s church bells through the trees, faint, then constant. Ismark brightens; Ireena doesn't. Ask her player-facing question through her silence: safer, or just walled in?
  - The gates: guards, wagon queue, painted slogans about happiness (seed Vallaki's texture without spending it).
  - Land the closing image: one crow on the gatehouse. Three days of crows that watched and never landed — this one is looking directly at them.
- **Transition:** Session end at the gates. Vallaki itself is next session's country.

## NPC Quick-Reference

- **[[Mirena Vadu]]** *(free entity — new this session)*
  - **Motivation:** keep the family whole one more season; kindness as prudence against a debt she can't name.
  - **Knows:** the cart stops here seasonally; hosting travelers helps somehow (she was told kindly, can't say by whom); everyone on the road owes or is owed; nothing about the hag's nature, the child, or what the family pays.
  - **Voice:** easy warmth, food as punctuation, one-beat-too-long smile at any mention of the road west; questions about the debt slide off her like water.
- **[[Ismark Kolyanovich]]** *(canon)*
  - **Motivation:** Ireena behind Vallaki's walls, whatever it costs him.
  - **Knows:** road customs (bread on the markers — "you don't ask"); the Vadus by reputation; Vallaki's gate procedures; nothing about the hag's network.
  - **Voice:** warm and direct, the only easy laugh in Barovia; goes quiet exactly when reassurance would be a lie — he doesn't lie.
- **[[Ireena Kolyana]]** *(canon)*
  - **Motivation:** reach Vallaki; outrun the question of what she is, now that "Kolyan's daughter" is off the table.
  - **Knows:** the lineage revelation and Donavich's "trials that await her" (won't elaborate — she can't); what being slowly collected feels like (she recognizes it in Mirena).
  - **Voice:** composed, shorter sentences than before the burial; deflects with practicalities.
- **[[The Dream Pastry Woman]]** *(canon — does NOT appear)*
  - Residue only: the Vadus' debt structure matches her bargain pattern. Whether the cart is hers stays unconfirmed on screen. Her motives remain canonically unknown — keep them that way.

## Contingencies

- **The party tries to hunt the hag now.** The trail is residue, not footprints — she plane-shifted and the road only knows the cart. Redirect: a Vallaki rumor next session can pick the thread up; tonight the road only offers the Vadus.
- **The party decides Mirena is the hag in disguise.** Let them test it — insight, detect magic, outright accusation. Everything comes back human and genuinely kind. The horror is that she's real. If they attack anyway, Ismark physically intervenes — these are good people and he's known of them his whole life.
- **The party refuses the stead and pushes through the night.** Allowed. The wolf encounter happens on the open road instead — harder, no fence, no lamp — and they pass the stead at dawn, where Mirena is already at the gate with bread, as if she knew when they'd walk by.

## Closing

- Land the hook on the image, not a reveal: bells still ringing, gate queue shuffling, one crow on the gatehouse turning its head to watch them arrive. Say it last and end the session there.
- Leave unresolved, deliberately: what the Vadus pay and to whom; the stolen child's fate; whether the wolves were Strahd's or something the road itself keeps; what Donavich meant by Ireena's "trials"; Doru, still screaming under a church floor a day behind them.
