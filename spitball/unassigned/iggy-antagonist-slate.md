# Iggy antagonist slate

One idea per lens. Each lists the hooks it serves, any data entity it could fill (pool first), its scale, and a ladder: touch, then beat, then arc. "(new)" means invented here; "(from canon)" means drawn from existing files.

Iggy already has a big villain on file: the grief society, its three tempted, and the thing beneath them (from canon, [[The Correctors]]). This slate uses that material and fills the lenses it leaves open. It does not repeat it.

## Pressure points

1. The Correctors and the Returned, loose in Aethryn as a grief society. (from canon)
2. Caelion's bridge: what came up the road, and whether she died, was taken or Returned. (from canon)
3. The sealed order. Iggy may have carried the Failed Intervention plan himself. (from canon)
4. Survivor's guilt. He ran once and never runs now. (from canon)
5. The final song. Finishing it ends Threnai for good. (from canon)
6. Remembrance: deeds told true, "before they write it false and new." (from canon)
7. Sah is changing, and his old certainty was flawed. (from canon)

## Ideas

1. **Wound-maker: the First Corrector, Convincingly Returned.** (new twist on canon) The First Corrector died in the ritual. They came back as a Convincing Return, intact but slow, with flat affect, and they don't know it. In Aethryn they still talk calmly about mercy, and they are sliding down the same stages they caused. They are their own proof that the idea fails, and they can't see it.
   - Hooks: confront the Correctors; face a Returned and recognize the corruption.
   - Pool fit: [[The First Corrector]] (fate deliberately open). [inferred: that they died and Returned]
   - Scale: season villain, or the mask the thing beneath wears.
   - Ladder: touch: a grief-society pamphlet uses the Corrector's exact phrase, "Nothing that must end can be called just." Beat: Iggy meets them and notices the delay before every answer. Arc: Iggy's line, "Nothing that refuses to end remains true," spoken to the person it was written against.

2. **Dark mirror: TBD.** Killed so far, don't re-propose: the Glossator (a bard who sells false legends) and the Redactor (a Threnai Finalizer who unwrites grief, and unwrote Vessa).

3. **Ideology foe: the Patron Who Won't Die.** (from canon seed) The second of the three tempted: a lord or merchant-prince who funds the grief society quietly and wants the lich-path for himself. He isn't grieving. He is afraid. He wants his own story never to end, which is the Correctors' idea at its most selfish.
   - Hooks: confront the Correctors; meet the grief society; "every story must end."
   - Pool fit: the unnamed patron in [[The Correctors]]. Kept apart from [[Lady Sorcha Marlow]] on purpose, since Dip's slate already uses her. [missing: name, seat; [[Tassad]]'s death-date-less grave markers (from canon) could be his fashion]
   - Scale: mid-season, a step toward the thing beneath.
   - Ladder: touch: an expensive grave marker with no death date. Beat: Iggy learns who pays for the society's parlor. Arc: the patron's vessel, and the choice to break it.

4. **Twisted hope: Caelion Marr, holding the wrong bridge.** (from canon, fate open) Caelion came back. She holds a crossing in Aethryn the way she held the river gate, except now she holds it for the Correctors, against the living. She still waves people across with the same joke, a beat too late. Iggy's dream is to hold a bridge like hers and walk back across it. Here, the crossing is hers, and she won't let him through.
   - Hooks: what came up the road at Caelion's bridge; face a Returned; the echo moment of a crossing held like Caelion's.
   - Pool fit: none. Caelion is text-only in Iggy's file, so she would need a file. [missing: which Returned stage; where the bridge is]
   - Scale: one big arc beat, possibly the act climax.
   - Ladder: touch: someone in Aethryn tells "the same bad joke." Beat: Iggy hears "The Count at the River Gate" hummed by a stranger. Arc: the bridge, and whether he can end her story kindly.
   - Cross-slate clash: Dip's idea 4 (Vessa, Returned) is the same shape. Use only one, or make them clearly different, such as Caelion at Convincing Return and Vessa at Degradation.

5. **Truth threat: the Archivist with the ledger.** (from canon, unnamed) The ink-stained archivist who copied the sealed order survived, with her ledger. *"If this goes wrong, someone should know we chose it."* She has decided the Failed Intervention, Sah's plan, is what really killed Threnai, not the Correctors. She is not a cultist. She wants the truth told: Sah chose it, and Iggy carried it.
   - Hooks: recover the sealed order; whether Iggy carried the plan; survivor's guilt; why Sah saved him.
   - Pool fit: none. She has no name, only a description in Iggy's file. [missing: how she survived; whether she's right]
   - Scale: recurring. Can become an ally once the truth is out.
   - Ladder: touch: a ledger page in Threnai script turns up for sale. Beat: she finds Iggy and reads him the order aloud. Arc: Iggy has to decide what he owes for it, in front of Sah.

6. **Institution's price: TBD.** Killed so far, don't re-propose: Old Sah (Sah's rigid old self opposing the vow-break) and Velhareth (the Great Library as an absorbing collective that is also Dip's guiding hands).

## Combinations

7. **The thing beneath the grief society (from canon) and Dip's idea 5 (the Hand That Lies) could be one being.** It is "learning to sound like the people they lost" (from canon). That means Vessa's voice for Dip and Caelion's for Iggy. One season villain, two PCs, and a shared spotlight for both.
8. **The healer link.** The third tempted is "a good healer tired of losing patients" (from canon). [[Wick]]'s guild is hunting a vampire that is Ashford's risky healer (from canon, DM-only). [inferred: they could be the same person.] That would put the grief society in Act 1 now.
9. **1 and 3 fit together.** The Returned First Corrector is what the Patron pays to become, without seeing what it costs.
