# Dip antagonist slate

One idea per lens. Each lists the hooks it serves, any data entity it could fill (pool first), its scale, and a ladder: touch, then beat, then arc. "(new)" means invented here; "(from canon)" means drawn from existing files.

## Pressure points

1. Some raiders escaped Brannoc's fire. Someone hired the band. Nobody knows who. (from canon)
2. Friends can smile at you for months and then turn. (from canon)
3. Vessa: hoped-for, feared as a monster, or never real. (from canon)
4. The guiding hands, and Vessa's missing from them. (from canon)
5. Townies who bleed a town dry, against "the orc who stays." (from canon)
6. The Enclave took Dip's anger and sorrow. (from canon)
7. Dip does not know Aethryn is home. (from canon)
8. The survivors' movement born from the fire. (from canon)

## Ideas

1. **Wound-maker: the Smiling Guest.** (new) The leader of the raiders who got out of the fire. Burn-scarred, warm and patient, still doing the same work: move into a town, become everyone's friend, then open the door for the killers. He has learned one Emberhollow child lived, and he wants to finish the job.
   - Hooks: finish off the raiders who escaped; uncover and confront the raiders.
   - Pool fit: [[Unknown Deceiver A]], a charming man selling belonging, whose route "someone has been tracking." That someone could be Dip. Fills the leader slot in [[The Hamlet Raiders]]. [missing: who hired him; inferred: he is the Deceiver]
   - Scale: recurring, then season villain.
   - Ladder: touch: a Golden Lioness raider carries something Dip last saw in Emberhollow. Beat: Dip meets a kind stranger in a new town and only later sees the burn scars. Arc: the fight, and the name of whoever paid for Emberhollow.
   - Link: his employer could be the same unnamed go-between who carries [[Lady Sorcha Marlow]]'s coin to [[Harrow Bane]] (from canon: the go-between exists). That would tie Emberhollow to the Ashford act.

2. **Dark mirror: the Ashkeeper.** (new) A fellow Emberhollow survivor, perhaps Brannoc's grandchild, who now leads the survivors' movement. "Survival against any odds" has turned into "strike first." The movement now raids towns it judges dangerous before they can turn on it, which is what the raiders did. Dip wants to reunite their people. These are their people.
   - Hooks: reunite Emberhollow's survivors; "the orc who stays" (the Ashkeeper takes and leaves).
   - Pool fit: [[Unknown Tragic A]], who was once right and whom old friends don't speak of with hatred. [missing: name, where the movement is based]
   - Scale: recurring. Can be redeemed or killed.
   - Ladder: touch: someone with an Emberhollow ember-mark helps the party and won't say why. Beat: Dip is invited to join. Arc: Dip stands between the movement and a town of strangers it plans to burn.

3. **Ideology foe: Lady Sorcha Marlow as a townie.** (from canon) No new villain. Marlow already bleeds Ashford through Harrow Bane's band, over a debt she thinks she is owed. Seen through Dip's eyes, she is the townie they hate: she takes from a town that gave her everything. She is also a foe an axe can't fix.
   - Hooks: stay and fight for a town of strangers; "don't call me a townie."
   - Pool fit: [[Lady Sorcha Marlow]], already in the active act. [inferred: that she fits Ben's townie picture; check against her file]
   - Scale: act villain. Already placed.
   - Ladder: touch: Dip hears Ashford folk call her a pillar of the town. Beat: Dip learns where the money goes. Arc: Ashford needs someone to stay when she pulls out.

4. **Twisted hope: Vessa, Returned.** (from canon link) Vessa died in the fire. When the Correctors' ritual broke the boundary of death, she came back as one of the corrupted Returned. That's the version Dip fears. The raiders' bloodlust and the "monsters" behind their smiles could be Returned too, so the Correctors may be the ideology behind Emberhollow.
   - Hooks: find Vessa; face the possibility of her as a monster; her hands absent from the visions (the Returned sit outside the dead the hands come from).
   - Pool fit: [[Dip's Twin Sister]], plus [[The Correctors]] and [[The First Corrector]] (Iggy's backstory). [inferred: the Returned reach Aethryn; missing: how Threnai's breach touches Aethryn]
   - Scale: arc payoff. Probably season-ending.
   - Ladder: touch: Iggy recognizes something Corrector-made in the raiders' gear. Beat: a Returned speaks with Dip's own voice-patterns. Arc: Dip finds her.
   - Cross-PC: a shared Dip and Iggy two-hander.

5. **Truth threat: the Hand That Lies.** (new) One of the guiding hands is not a friendly soul. It is part of the "something much larger" from Dip's Session 7 death (from canon), and it has been steering Dip for years toward a festival weak point it wants opened. The whispers Dip hears when it's quiet (from canon: since Session 10) are its voice. It works to convince Dip that Vessa never existed, because a Dip with no Vessa follows the hands without question.
   - Hooks: interpret the guiding hands; face the possibility that Vessa never existed; pilgrimage to grasp the divine-mortal bond.
   - Pool fit: none. Net-new; ask before creating. [missing: what it is, what is behind the weak point]
   - Scale: slow-burn season villain. Rarely seen.
   - Ladder: touch: the whispers say something true that Dip never told anyone. Beat: at a holy site, the hands push Dip toward harm. Arc: Dip must choose between the hands and Vessa.
   - Cross-PC: the whispers began with [[Odibli]]'s shadow ritual (from canon). That makes this a Dip and Odibli two-hander, a good fit for the shared-secret hook.

6. **Institution's price: the Abbot of the Well.** (new) The Enclave's calm is paid for. The anger and sorrow its pupils give up are not gone. They are kept in a well beneath the Enclave, and the Abbot tends it. The well is cracking. The Abbot needs more grief to seal it, so the Enclave quietly sends its pupils out on "personal matters" to gather more. Dip's own anger is down there and has started to take a shape. It could even be what Dip remembers as Vessa.
   - Hooks: return to the Enclave with their chapters complete.
   - Pool fit: [[The Sage Enclave]] (Ben's faction, canon). The Abbot is new. [missing: Enclave leadership; contradicts nothing yet. Check with Ben, since he authored the Enclave as a kind place]
   - Scale: late-season, or next season's homecoming.
   - Ladder: touch: a letter from the Enclave asks how Dip's chapters are going. Beat: another pupil, out on "personal matters," is leaving ruin behind them. Arc: Dip goes home.

## Combinations

7. **1 and 3 fit together.** Same go-between, so Emberhollow becomes an earlier job in Marlow's line. Lowest cost: it uses the active act.
8. **4 and 5 compete.** Each gives a different answer to "where is Vessa." Pick one as true; the other can be the lie the villain tells.
