## Origin

Miguel made the word up at the table on 2026-11-18: **Gnomaha** = Gnome + Omaha Beach, the bloodiest D-Day landing in 1944.

## The idea (new)

A gnome invasion fleet hits a fortified beach at dawn. Landing boats drop their ramps, tiny soldiers wade in under fire, and the cliffs above are lined with guns. The party is either in one of those boats, ankle-deep in surf with a gnome sergeant screaming orders, or defending the cliffs as the boats come in. The joke comes from playing it completely straight: gnome-sized hedgehogs, gnome-sized bunkers, a gnome chaplain, and real stakes.

## Open questions

1. Which side is the party on: landing with the gnomes, holding the beach against them, or caught in the middle?
2. Is it a one-shot, a single session in Redwater, or the opening of a whole act?
3. Who holds the beach, and why do the gnomes need it so badly?

## Riff 1 (2026-11-18): the party lands with the gnomes

Answer to question 1: **the party lands with the gnomes.** They are giants in a gnome army.

4. **Carry them in (new).** Water that comes up to a PC's knees is over a gnome's head. The boats ground too far out, so the PCs wade in carrying gnomes on their shoulders and in their arms, under fire.
5. **Walking cover (new).** On the sand, gnomes pack in behind each PC. A simple running count of gnomes alive behind each PC makes every choice to move, duck or charge cost something.
6. **Sergeant on the ramp (new).** A gnome sergeant gives every order at full volume and treats the PCs as recruits, not heroes: "You! Tall one! Your job is to be shot at. Do your job!"
7. **Obstacles sized for gnomes (new).** Iron hedgehogs and wire knee-high to a PC are walls to the gnomes. The PCs can tear them out and open lanes, which is the quickest way to save lives.
8. **The defenders were warned (new).** The cliff guns were built for gnomes, but someone told the defenders big ones were coming. One heavy gun is waiting for the PCs, and finding out who leaked it is the thread that carries on.
9. **Objective: the cliff gun (new).** The landing works only if the heavy gun goes silent. The gnomes have a demolition charge, and only a PC is tall enough to climb the cliff and plant it.

## Riff 2 (2026-11-18): small squad command

Jeff's addition: each PC is trying to get up the beach, but **the real fun is the small squad of gnomes each PC commands** to help the landing. This builds on 5: the gnomes behind each PC are now that PC's squad.

10. **One squad per PC (from Jeff).** Each player commands a handful of gnomes, around five. The PC's own advance matters, but the decisions that count are what the squad does.
11. **Short order menu (new).** The squad acts on its PC's turn and takes one order: Advance, Covering fire, Dig in, Breach (wire or hedgehogs), or Carry (wounded, the charge, a fallen flag). It's quick to call, and the table can learn it in one round.
12. **A named gnome in every squad (new).** Each squad has one specialist with a name and a trick (sapper, medic, sniper, flag-bearer), and every squad's is different. That's what makes a player's squad feel like their own.
13. **Squad nerve (new).** A squad holds as long as its PC leads from the front. If the PC hangs back or goes down, the gnomes freeze or scatter. Brave play earns the gnomes' loyalty.
14. **The survivors stick around (new).** Gnomes who live through the beach can come back later as recurring NPCs who owe that PC their lives. That fits 8's leak thread if this becomes an act.

## Riff 3 (2026-11-18): part of a larger story

Answer to question 2: **not a one-off.** Gnomaha goes into a campaign or mission later on, as a set piece inside a bigger story. Which campaign, and which DM runs it, are still open.

What that asks of the beach (new):

15. **A reason to be in the boat.** The party needs its own stake in the landing: a debt the gnomes call in, someone they need who is on the far side, or the beach being the only way in to where the story is going.
16. **The day after.** The beach is one big session, and the mission goes on past it. Holding the beachhead, pushing inland, or running down item 8's leak are all things to do next.
17. **Seeding the gnomes early.** If gnomes and their war show up in passing a few sessions before the landing (a recruiting poster, a gnome veteran in a tavern, rumors of a fleet), the landing feels earned instead of dropped in.

## Riff 4 (2026-11-18): defenders left open

Answer to question 3: **left open on purpose.** Who holds the cliffs gets decided by whichever campaign takes this on. Options raised and not chosen: something bigger than gnomes (kobolds, goblins, an ogre warlord), or a rival gnome clan in a civil war.
