Jeff and Paul fixed who Nul and Farzrig are on 2026-11-27 (see `data/deities/Nul.md`), but left two questions open: how soon Farzrig is seen, and how the finale plays. These are the proposals offered, none chosen.

## How soon Farzrig is seen

- **Slow eclipse (proposed).** Felt from level 5, the steed seen at 9 to 13, the rider at 14, touchable at 15, the finale at 18 to 20. Late arrival with steady pressure.
- **Long shadow.** Only absences until level 17: blank places, missing stars. The first sighting opens the finale. Most dread, but it risks feeling absent.
- **Early glimpse, late fight.** One impossible sighting at level 8 or 9, high in the sky and gone, then silence until 14. The players know what is coming.

### The slow-eclipse ladder

Levels 5 to 8, echoes. Nothing is named; the world develops blank spots.

- A patch of Aethryn stops being finished: grass without blades, a well with no sound. Iggy starts humming before he notices. Luci's radiance gutters inside it.
- A Returned from the grief society reaches the Intrusion stage. For a moment something looks out through it, and its pupils are black suns.
- Luci finds Dead Sun script in Aethryn, carved somewhere it shouldn't be, on the same subject as her temple walls.

Levels 9 to 13, omens. The herald's shadow, and its people.

- A shard of Fida goes missing: a hole in the night sky that stars don't show through.
- The Void Serpent is sighted, scouting.
- The Absolvers appear. Their line is "let them rest": shrines quietly closed, the old allowed to die, a god's last priest talked out of praying.
- Around level 12 or 13, the Astral Dragon arrives to weigh Luci. It knows what Farzrig used to be, and tells the truth at a price.

Level 14, arrival. The Huzzar rides into Aethryn's sky.

- It isn't a fight. It passes over a place the party loves and unfinishes it. People are left as sketches who don't remember their names.
- The party survives because Iggy sings: the blank road again, with an audience.
- Farzrig looks straight at both Aasimar and knows them for unfinished business.

Levels 15 to 17, the hunt for what can hurt it.

- Luci's road: reach the Sun Temple through Fida and run Paul's hackable board toward Aedra.
- Iggy's road: the Tier 3 memories, the sealed order, Caelion's bridge and what came up the road.
- The Astral Dragon's terms, and a Returned Caelion: a crossing held the right way, the echo moment.

## The finale

- **Three chosen endings (proposed).** Iggy finishes the song, and Threnai's story ends by choice, which is meaning Nul can't eat. Luci frees Aedra, creation flows again, and Farzrig remembers it was a balance, not a hunger, and turns from herald back to balancer. Nul's prophet is left alone with the creed.
- **Slay the herald, then Nul.** A level-18 set-piece fight with Farzrig, then the BBEG at 20. Big fights, less thematic payoff.
- **Farzrig defects.** At level 17, once Aedra stirs, Farzrig turns on Nul and becomes a terrifying, reluctant ally for the last act. Needs Aedra freed earlier.
