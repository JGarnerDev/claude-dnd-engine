## The idea

Odibli needs his own level-up moment in Session 30, the way Dip got the rage and Luci got the
moon. Halsby levels at the Spear and Iggy at the hearth. The duty-ledger beat is out.

**How to give notes:** write under any option's **Notes:** line, or just reply by number ("3 yes,
but after the fight"). The numbers won't change.

**Timing rule for every option:** if Odibli levels **before** the camp fight, he brings Fireball
to a fight built around crowds, and with Halsby and Iggy also at 5 the camp drops from Hard to
Medium. Either level him after the camp, or add a body to the camp. Each option says which side
it falls on.

## Options

1. **He signs the deed** (Scene 3, `S2_BOON`, night, before the fights). The Boon goes dead in
   his hand and the way home closes. Sable slides the deed across. Forty years of copying other
   people's names, and this is the first document he signs as himself. He's a copy, so his
   signature can be forged by definition, and he signs anyway. Level up as the ink dries. It fits
   his bond: "the first fellowship I've ever been *in*." Only works if the party takes the deed
   that night.
   - **Notes:**

2. **The first page of Aethryn** (Scene 3, end of night, before the fights). The others are
   asleep. He checks the Lioness hearth out of habit, the way he checks every fire. For the first
   time nothing looks back: no Strahd, no Palimpsest, only a fire that burns without anyone
   feeding it. He opens the journal (his spellbook and his truest self) and writes the first
   entry of a story he's *in* rather than copying. Level up on the first line. A quiet moment,
   and Miguel gets to say the line.
   - **Notes:** Jeff, 2026-10-07: liked best so far. It should be built around the hearth.
     Variants are 7–12 below.

3. **The cast that's sensed** (Scene 7, mid-fight at the camp). His magic is unreliable here, and
   every cast risks the original feeling it. Late in the fight, after the morale break, he
   reaches for something big anyway, it catches, and it goes off bigger than it should. Level up
   mid-cast, like Dip's rage. **DM only:** somewhere far off, the original Odibli looks up from
   his desk. This pays out the hook "the real Odibli detects his magic in Aethryn". Coming late
   in the fight keeps the camp at Hard.
   - **Notes:**

4. **The archer looks back again** (Scene 7, at the gully, end of the fight). In Session 29
   Nella looked back at him from the cart before she vanished. Now she's crossing the rope bridge
   and cutting it behind her. She never shoots casters, so he's the one person she lets come
   close. He gets to the edge and leaps before he looks, his flaw, and this time being bold
   actually pays off (he lands it, or grabs the tally as it falls). Level up mid-air. This sets
   up a Nella and Odibli thread.
   - **Notes:**

5. **A line he didn't write** (Scene 7/8, the long rest at the waystation, after the fights).
   The 24-hour window reopens, so a level here lines up with a real long rest. He writes up the
   day, and when he reads it back there's one line in his own handwriting that he doesn't
   remember writing. Level up as he reads it. It's mechanically the cleanest option and it opens
   the hook "pages in his own handwriting turn up". You'd need to decide what the line says.
   - **Notes:**

6. **Knowing drink** (Scene 3, `S2_ODIBLI`, before the fights). Sable's thimble test. He names
   the honey and the strange sweetness of the spring water, which is Asher under the floor,
   though he doesn't know that. Sable raises an eyebrow, which from her is applause. His Vallaki
   wine-conquest joke finally pays off. The lightest option of the six, and listed mostly for
   completeness.
   - **Notes:**

## Hearth variants (from 2)

What these build on. Odibli checks every fire for mail because of the **hearth-channel**: burn a
page to send it, read the reply in the embers. The channel runs through the plane of [[The
Palimpsest]], and older erased messages sometimes ghost up beneath new ones. **DM only:** Strahd
taps what he sends out. The Lioness hearth burns without anyone feeding it. Under it lives Asher,
a lonely being whose memory is held in the water, who prizes *a true story told aloud* because
the telling gets remembered, and whose memory the feud's blood is eating away.

**Iggy's beat already uses this hearth** (it goes still during his song). So Odibli's moment
comes later, once the room has emptied, and can call back to that stillness.

7. **The fire won't take the page.** Out of habit he writes a short note ("alive, somewhere
   new") and puts it in the fire to send it. The page sits on the coals and doesn't burn. He tries
   again, and the fire turns it away again. Nothing in this hearth leaves the room. **DM only:**
   Strahd's tap can't reach a message that never goes out. Level up when he stops trying and
   writes the line in the journal instead, as an entry he keeps rather than one he sends.
   - **Notes:**

8. **Something else answers.** He sends a page and it burns normally. The reply in the embers
   isn't the Palimpsest's double meanings. It comes slow and wet, like writing on fogged glass,
   and it asks a question: *"Who are you?"* Nobody has ever asked the copy that. He answers in
   writing, truthfully, and the fire reads it. Level up when he finishes. Asher gets a true story,
   and Odibli is truly known by something that isn't Strahd. This is a strong counterweight to the
   hook "Strahd offers to be truly known". **Cost:** it opens a direct line between Asher and a
   PC, which is a big lore commitment.
   - **Notes:**

9. **The hum stops.** Every fire since the Death House has had a faint pressure behind it, so
   constant he stopped noticing it. He looks into the Lioness hearth and the pressure is gone. His
   head is quiet for the first time in 28 sessions. He doesn't know what it was. **DM only:** it
   was Strahd listening, and this hearth belongs to someone else. Level up on the silence. This is
   option 2 with a hidden reason underneath.
   - **Notes:**

10. **A ghost in the embers.** Aethryn sits closer to the Citadel, so the channel is louder
    here. A message ghosts up under the coals, half-erased, in a hand he knows. It could be the
    Colleague's, or a smear of static that feels like his own (the original). He copies it into
    the journal before it fades, because he can't leave a text unread. Level up on the last word.
    This pays out the hook "an erased message ghosts up beneath a fresh one in the embers". The
    darkest of these, and you'd need to write the message.
    - **Notes:**

11. **The tale for the hearth.** Sable is still counting stock and everyone else is asleep.
    Odibli narrates his life under his breath as he always does, but tonight he tells the whole
    night aloud to the fire: the fall, the cart, the archer, as a story he was in. The flame leans
    toward him while he talks. **DM only:** Asher is listening, and keeps it. Level up when the
    tale ends. Miguel improvises the telling, so it's the best option for the table.
    - **Notes:**

12. **Sable catches him at it.** At 3 a.m. she finds him crouched at the hearth. *"It doesn't
    need feeding."* — *"I wasn't feeding it. I was checking for mail."* She sits down beside him
    and asks nothing. She might pour him the thimble from option 6. Level up on whatever he says
    next. Option 2 shared with one other person, which also builds toward the deed.
    - **Notes:**

**Combining them:** 9 + 11 (the quiet, then the tale) or 7 + 12 (the refused page, then Sable)
pair naturally. All of them happen at night, **before the camp fight**, so the timing rule above
still applies.

## Hearth deeds (13–15)

Jeff, 2026-10-07: levels should land right before or right after **a cool deed**, not on a moment
of drama alone. Options 7–12 are drama moments. These three give Odibli something to *do* at the
hearth, and need a new beat added to the plan or an existing one changed.

13. **The hearth goes out, and he relights it.** New beat, late in the night. The fire that never
    needs feeding gutters and starts to die. The raid's blood went into the yard drain and down
    into the spring, and Asher is reeling (his memory lives in the water, and blood fouls it).
    Sable, for the first time, looks frightened. Odibli is the evoker, the man who named his Fire
    Bolt "Diesel Cougar in the Winter". He reads the fire with Arcana and finds it isn't a fire at
    all: it's something under the floor, breathing up. Then he keeps it alight by hand while it
    recovers, as a short skill challenge (Arcana, then cantrips and a spent slot to hold the
    flame). Level up when it catches on its own. **Deed:** he saves the Lioness's heart. **Cost:**
    he spends a slot before the camp, which partly offsets the level. Each cast risks the original
    sensing him, and this is a big, sustained cast.
    - **Notes:**

14. **Tallis's last charm, in the coals.** This one changes `S2_ODIBLI` or `S2_BOON`. Tallis, the
    raiders' hedge-mage, sold charms at the fair, so the town knows him. During the raid he dropped
    a written charm into the old core's hearth: a delayed burn, meant to finish the building the
    new wing started. It's still smouldering, a page curled in the coals with a glyph slowly
    brightening. Nobody would ever find it except the man who checks every fire for mail. It can't
    be pulled out (it's bound to the fire) or erased (it's written). A scribe can do one thing
    with it, though: **write over it.** He works his pen into the coals and turns the glyph into
    something else, a palimpsest, which is what he is. It plays as a tense 3-check sequence
    (Investigation to spot it, Arcana to read it, calligrapher's supplies or INT to overwrite it),
    with the glyph ticking up a step on each failure. Level up when the last stroke lands.
    **Deed:** he saves the building and everyone asleep in it. It uses his scribe skill, his
    fireplace habit and his own nature at the same time.
    - **Notes:**

15. **A coal from the Lioness, at the camp.** This changes `S2_FIGHT`. When they ride at dawn,
    Sable hands him a coal from the hearth in a lidded tin: *"It doesn't go out. Don't ask me how."*
    That night, Nella is invisible on her ridge, picking people off. Odibli throws the tin into
    the watch-fire, or into the brush below her blind, and the fire flares as if it were
    breathing up from somewhere, lighting the whole ridge. Her cover is gone. Level up right
    after, mid-fight. **Deed:** he breaks the sniper. Coming late in the fight keeps the camp at
    Hard. **Clash:** lighting the camp turns off Halsby's 15-ft Spear reach, which only works in
    the dark. That could be a deliberate trade, or a reason to choose 13 or 14 instead.
    - **Notes:**

## The pick: 13 + 14 merged (16)

Jeff, 2026-10-07: mix 13 and 14.

16. **The hearth is dying, and Tallis's charm is the reason.** The fire going out (13) is the
    symptom; the charm in the coals (14) is the cause. Saving the hearth means holding the flame up
    and overwriting the glyph at the same time.

### Where it goes

- **A new beat, `S2_HEARTH`**, after `S2_BOON` and just before they sleep. The room has emptied.
  This keeps it away from Iggy's song, where the same hearth goes still for a different reason.
- `S2_ODIBLI` (the drink test) stays as a light warm-up, or gets cut if the night runs long.

### What's going on (DM only)

- During the raid, **Tallis** (the hedge-mage who sold charms at the fair) dropped a written charm
  into the old core's hearth. It's a **smother-charm**: it drinks the fire, stores the heat, and
  when the flame finally dies, it lets all of it go at once. The new wing burned, and this was
  meant to finish the old core while everyone slept in it.
- The fire it is drinking is Asher's breath coming up from under the floor. Asher feels it as
  suffocation. **He remembers who saved it.**

### How it plays

- **The symptom.** The fire that never needs feeding gutters. Sable stops mid-pour, and for the
  first time tonight she looks afraid: *"It doesn't do that. It has never done that."* Wood won't
  help; this fire has never taken wood.
- **Finding it.** Odibli checks the fire for mail, as he does every fire (advantage, or let him
  find it automatically). There's a page curled in the coals that won't burn, with a glyph slowly
  brightening on it.
- **Reading it** (Arcana DC 13). It's a smother-charm. It can't be pulled out, because it's bound
  to the fire, and it can't be erased, because it's written. **It can be written over.** And the
  fire has to stay alight while he does it, or the charm wins.
- **The deed, run in rounds:**
  - **The flame track: 3 → 2 → 1 → 0.** At 0, the charm lets go.
  - **Each round, someone feeds the fire magic, or it drops one step.** A fire cantrip (Fire Bolt
    into the hearth, Produce Flame, Prestidigitation) holds it for one round. Burning a spell slot
    into it holds it for that many rounds. **This is the party's job**, and Halsby and Odibli can
    both do it.
  - **Each round, Odibli makes one stroke**: INT with calligrapher's supplies, DC 14 (he's at +7).
    A failed stroke also drops the flame one step.
  - **Three successful strokes** overwrite the glyph.
- **If the flame hits 0:** the charm bursts. 2d6 fire to everyone at the hearth (DEX 13 for half),
  and the old core catches. The party puts it out, but Sable's last safe building is scorched and
  the hearth is cold until Asher recovers. That's a real cost, but the session can carry on.
- **The last stroke.** **Miguel picks the word he writes over the glyph.** A suggestion if he wants
  one: the charm says *consume*, and he turns it into *kindle*. The fire comes back up the chimney
  in one breath. Sable lets go of the bar. Then **level up.**
- **One line from Sable, after:** *"That fire was lit before I got here."* A beat. *"…Don't tell
  anyone it went out."* (Check this against Sable's canon before using it.)

### What it gives the session

- **The deed:** he saves the building, everyone asleep in it, and, though he doesn't know it,
  Asher's breath.
- **It's all Odibli:** the habit of checking every fireplace, the scribe's pen, calligrapher's
  supplies, and being a palimpsest himself.
- **Other PCs get a role too:** holding the flame up gives Halsby and anyone else something to do.
- **A seed:** the charm paper matches the charms Tallis sold at the fair, and Mara knows his name.
  That's evidence the raid was planned past the fight, and someone may ask who taught him.
- **His Aethryn rule:** a sustained, hours-late cast is exactly what the original Odibli might
  feel. Roll for it if that rule has odds by then.

### Effect on the camp fight

- With Halsby, Iggy and Odibli all at 5, the party is all level 5: Hard 3,750, Deadly 5,500. The
  camp, at 3,625 adjusted XP, drops to **Medium**.
- **Fix: add a third Thug cask-hand at the cart.** That's 1,550 XP across 9 creatures, ×2.5 =
  **3,875, Hard**. A Bandit isn't enough (3,688, still Medium). Pack Tactics at the cart gets
  sharper, so keep the crew on its stations.
- Any slots Odibli or Halsby burn into the fire are also gone for the camp. The short rest at dawn
  doesn't bring them back, except through Arcane Recovery.
