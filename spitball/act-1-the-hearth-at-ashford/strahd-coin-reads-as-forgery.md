## The idea

The party spends Barovian coin in Aethryn. It has a face on it that nobody here recognizes, so to
everyone who handles it, it looks forged. That starts trouble, and the trouble grows.

## What we have

1. **The coin (from canon).** Iggy carries 130 ep stamped with Strahd's profile, from the chest
   at the winery in Session 27. He also has 131 gp and 409 sp, which may carry Barovian marks too.
   That is unconfirmed.
2. **Coin here is plain (from canon).** Velmora uses gold, silver and copper, and no electrum is
   mentioned. A coin with a stranger's face is wrong twice over.
3. **Silas is the first to notice (from Jeff).** He handles every coin in Ashford. He would be
   right to worry that regional authorities will come looking for whoever is making it, and that
   anyone caught passing it gets the blame.
4. **His line (from the Session 29 draft):** *"Who's the fellow on this? Handsome. Looks like he'd
   foreclose on you."* The question stays a joke until he works out what it means for him.

## Ways it could escalate (new)

5. Silas won't take it, or quietly pulls it out of circulation, and asks the party to stop
   spending it in his town.
6. A coin has already left town, in change or in Nella's take, and turns up somewhere it should
   not.
7. A tax-man or assayer from Lord Cael asks where the "false electrum" came from. A wrong answer
   makes the party suspected counterfeiters.
8. The wizard guilds in Tassad run like cartels (from canon). An unknown minting magic would draw
   them in.
9. Silas is neutral and trades with anyone (from canon). He could just as easily melt the coins
   down and sell the metal to the wrong person.

## Open questions

10. Is it only the electrum, or all of their Barovian coin? **Answered: all of it (Jeff).**
11. Who comes first: Lord Cael's officials, a guild, or a crime ring that wants the "mint"?
    **Answered: the state of the nation the party is in (Jeff).** That is **Velmora**, not Tassad
    (from canon). Tassad is a city in a different nation, Blue, across the bay.
12. Does the face ever matter? Someone in Aethryn could know Strahd by another name. **Open.** It
    depends on how well extra-planar beings know Strahd. See 16–19.

## Who comes looking: Velmora (developed)

13. **A weak crown guards its coin hardest (new).** Velmora is a weak crown over powerful landed
    families (from canon). Minting may be one of the few powers it still holds outright. So
    false coin isn't a local nuisance. It is an attack on the one thing the crown still controls,
    and the crown answers it, even though it sends nothing about the raids. Ashford would notice
    that.
14. **Who actually arrives (new).** A royal coin-warden or assayer, someone with a writ and an
    escort. Lord Cael is honest and doesn't come (from canon), so the crown goes past him, and that
    may embarrass him.
15. **Tassad as a second front (new).** Tassad's real church is Waukeen, the coin-goddess (from
    canon). If a coin drifts there, forgery is heresy as well as a crime. That would be a later
    rung, and a different nation.

## The endgame: Tassad's little crusade (from Jeff)

20. **Eventually Tassad steps in (from Jeff).** Not Velmora's crown this time, but a group from
    Tassad crossing into Velmora as an insurgency: a very small crusade in Waukeen's name against
    false coin. It answers to a foreign faith, not to Velmora's law, so it is trouble for the
    party and for Velmora too.
21. **MacLeod cameos as the wizard who organizes it (from Jeff).** Ask MacLeod to play the
    organizer. A one-off guest seat, not a party member. His player file is
    `meta/players/MacLeod.md`.
22. **Why a wizard leads it (new).** Tassad's wizard guilds run like cartels (from canon), and
    [[The Templar Triumvirate]] already sits over the harbor. An unknown "mint" is a rival magic
    to them as much as it is heresy, so faith and guild interest point the same way.
23. **The order of escalation (new):** Silas quietly refuses the coin (5) → Velmora's
    coin-warden (14) → a coin reaches Tassad (15) → the crusade (20). Each rung is a separate
    session's worth of trouble, never all at once.

## How notorious is Strahd? (new, for the DM to decide)

16. **In the D&D lore Barovia comes from:** Barovia is one of the Domains of Dread. They sit in
    the Shadowfell, sealed by the Mists. Most worlds have never heard of Strahd. The few who know
    are planar scholars, vampire hunters (Van Richten's writings), the Vistani, and powers that
    watch the Shadowfell.
17. **[[The Nightveil]] (Halsby's order) is the strongest link.** They are Sharran and tied to the
    Shadowfell (from canon), the plane Barovia sits in. If anyone in Aethryn knows that face, it is
    them. A coin reaching a Sharran could be the moment Halsby's world and Barovia touch.
18. **[[Wick]]'s guild hunts vampires for a living (from canon).** A guild like that might keep
    Strahd in its archive the way Van Richten wrote him down: as the first vampire, a legend.
    Wick seeing that coin is a scene.
19. **Recommendation:** unknown to the public and to the state, known to a few. Velmora only sees
    a forgery. The Nightveil and Wick's guild might see the face. That keeps 11 and 12 separate:
    the crown hunts a counterfeiter, and someone else hunts the face.
