## The idea

Part of Act 1 ties the present feud back to the old war fought on this land. A tortured spirit, in
the shape of a huge predator, sleeps where the fighting was worst. The party's first real meeting
with Asher is him asking them to lay it to rest, and he asks Dip by name. Jorn, the druid in Harrow
Bane's band, is trying to wake it and bind it to his will.

Numbering was cleaned up on 2026-10-04 at the DM's request. These numbers are the ones to use.

## Core

1. **Asher picks Dip (new).** Dip's Sage Enclave training lets him feel weak spots between planes
   (from canon, backstory). Asher wants that sense, not the barbarian's axe.
2. **Jorn's reason (new, built on canon).** Jorn hates the fouling of the river and wants the feud's
   war kept off his sacred stretch of wild (from canon). A woken spirit would be the perfect guard
   dog for that ground.
3. **Asher and Jorn want the same thing (from canon).** Both are angry about the fouled water, and
   neither knows it. Here they pull opposite ways on the same spirit. The party could find out they
   are on the same side.
4. **Echo of Dip's past (new).** The spirit could feel like the "many hands" in Dip's visions and
   his Session 7 death. It might even mention a hand that is missing, which points at his sister.

## The meeting with Asher

5. **The job is the meeting (new). DM: yes.** The first time the party stands before Asher, this
   is what he wants. Session 29's "never meets him" covers only that session; they meet him later
   in Act 1 (DM).
6. **Where (from canon).** The Golden Lioness cellar spring drops into Asher's aquifer. The party
   goes down to the water and he rises: a slit-eyed blue-green bulk, monstrous to look at.
7. **Why the spirit hurts him (from canon, extended).** Asher remembers through the water, and the
   feud's blood eats his mind (from canon). The old war's blood did the same long ago, and the
   spirit is that memory gone sour (new). He cannot digest it.
8. **He talks to Dip first (new).** Asher feels Dip's sense for thin places the moment he is near,
   and speaks straight into Dip's head. The rest of the party hears only half of it.
9. **A story first (from canon).** Asher's custom is a true story told aloud over the water. He asks
   for one before he asks for anything. Dip telling the hamlet fire would land hard.
10. **His bait (new). DM: keep.** Asher hints he has tasted something of Dip's sister in his water,
    true or not. Dip can hardly refuse, and it leaves a hook for later.
11. **Jorn in the picture (new).** Asher knows a druid has been at the old field. He does not know
    Jorn shares his anger. The party can find that out on their own.

## The battle

12. **The last stand at the border-river (from canon, extended).** In the old war Cael and Marlow
    fought as allies, and Cael's grandfather fell holding the border-river (from canon). This is
    that stand: a field above the ford where the line broke and the blood ran into the water (new).
    Richmond the smith was there (from canon), so he is the person to ask.

## The beast

DM wants the spirit to be a huge or dire predator animal.

13. **Option A, a war-beast (new).** One of the houses rode a bred war-predator, a huge sabre-tooth
    or dire wolf. It died with its handler, still fighting, and never got the order to stop.
    Careful: if it was Marlow's beast dying for Cael, it brushes against "the shield that turned,"
    which is meant to be a reveal for the curious.
14. **Option B, the wild's top predator (new).** A huge cave bear or great cat ruled that stretch of
    wild before the armies came. It ate the dead, drank the bloody water and went mad. Jorn sees it
    as the rightful guardian of his sacred ground.
15. **Option C, many souls in one body (new).** The soldiers' souls poured into the beast. Up close
    it is made of hands, claws and faces, like Dip's visions and his Session 7 death (from canon).
    Dip feels it as something he knows.
16. **They combine (new).** Option 15 can sit on top of 13 or 14: the beast is the shell, the dead
    are inside.
17. **Stats (new).** The party is level 4, so a plain sabre-toothed tiger is too weak for a boss.
    Make it Huge with ghost traits (half damage from normal weapons, moves through trees and
    stone), about CR 5. Jorn's partial control could add a second phase where it fights for him.

## The beast, chosen (DM: 13 + 15)

21. **The shape (DM).** A noble's war-beast. It grew huge by soaking up the souls of the noble's
    dead kin who fell on that field. The bloodline is inside the animal.
22. **Whose beast: Cael (new, fits canon).** Cael's grandfather fell holding the border-river (from
    canon). He rode the beast, and his kin died around him. Lord Corren Cael is the grandson (from
    canon), so laying the spirit to rest means putting his own family down a second time. Big
    personal stake for the town's lord.
23. **Whose beast: Marlow (new, riskier).** Marlow is "the house that once died for Cael" (from
    canon). Their dead inside the beast would make Lady Sorcha's grudge visible. But it points
    hard at "the shield that turned," which is meant to stay a reveal for the curious.
24. **Why it is still angry (new).** The kin inside it died believing the line would hold. The
    beast keeps fighting a battle that ended long ago, and it guards the ford against anyone.
25. **Why Jorn wants it (new).** A beast that guards the ford against all comers is exactly the
    wall Jorn wants between the feud and his wild.
26. **Laying it to rest (new).** Killing the beast is not enough. Someone has to tell the dead the
    battle is over. That could be Dip through the planes, or Corren himself, or both.

**DM: 22, Cael's beast. 23 dropped.** Why: it adds sadness and a chance at redemption in the bigger
arc, and it can echo later if the party starts dealing with the existing nobility.

## Riff — making it echo

27. **Corren's redemption (new).** House Cael can't defend its own town (from canon). Standing at
    the field and releasing his family is the one brave act Corren gets to do himself. Whether he
    shows up is the test.
28. **A token from the beast (new).** When it goes down, something of the grandfather's is left: a
    collar, a war-standard, a signet. Carried to the right noble later, it opens a door.
29. **Richmond's guilt (new).** Richmond was there and chose the forge after (from canon). Maybe he
    saw the beast fall and never told the family it was still out there.
30. **A pattern for later (new).** If other old houses lost kin in the same war, other beasts or
    spirits could be out there. Not needed now; a seed for when the party meets more nobility.

**DM: keep 27–30.**
