# Emberhollow in Bane's band

From the 2026-10-25 backstory-to-plans review (item 14). Ben's main wish for Dip is to finish off
the raiders who escaped Emberhollow and reunite the survivors. The Hamlet Raiders arrived friendly,
earned the town's trust over many shared meals, then turned. Most burned in Brannoc's fire; a few
got out. DM-only: Emberhollow is in Aethryn, and Dip doesn't know it.

Two versions, not exclusive:

1. **The method, not the men.** Bane's band uses the Emberhollow trick: a friendly newcomer in
   Ashford, liked by everyone, turns out to be the band's inside man. Dip reads the pattern before
   anyone else does. Ties Dip's past to the act's villain without saying Bane was there. Cheap.
2. **One of the men.** A raider who got out of Brannoc's fire rides with Bane now. The first real
   step toward "finish them off". Bigger: it puts Emberhollow close enough to Ashford to matter.

Open questions:

3. Who learned the trick, and from whom? (Version 1 needs a teacher, or it's coincidence.)
4. Does Dip recognize a face, a habit, or only the shape of it?
5. Does it collide with Tyler's surrendered raider? A man who quit the band could be the one who
   carries the Emberhollow memory, or the one Dip suspects wrongly.
